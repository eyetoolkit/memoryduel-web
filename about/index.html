About MemoryDuel

What is this?

MemoryDuel is a knowledge battle platform with instant duels across multiple categories. Free to play, privacy-first, supports 1v1/multiplayer online battles, AI training, and personal rank system. Click to play, no registration required.

Game Modes

  • Knowledge Battle — 1v1 instant duel with trivia questions (science, tech, art, history, lifestyle, etc.). Winner is determined by speed and accuracy.
  • Memory Training — Card matching, number flash memory, multiple difficulty levels. Personal bests saved locally.
  • AI Training — Battle against AI bots, simulating real duel rhythm.

Why Play Knowledge Battles?

Research shows active recall is the most effective memory consolidation method. Knowledge battles use timed Q&A + competitive pressure to activate active recall, providing 3-5x better retention than passive reading.

10 minutes of daily battles across trivia, science, art, history and more. Long-term benefits include:

  • Broaden knowledge and keep your brain active
  • Improve quick reaction and decision-making skills
  • Meet knowledge enthusiasts from around the world

Tech Stack

Pure frontend HTML5 + CSS3 + JavaScript. Data stored in browser localStorage. Real-time duel sync provided by Cloudflare Worker WebSocket service. No login required, open and play.
