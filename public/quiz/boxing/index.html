Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which boxer is widely regarded as 'The Greatest' and held the heavyweight title three times?

    Answer: Muhammad Ali

    Muhammad Ali, known as 'The Greatest', is a three-time world heavyweight champion and one of the most celebrated athletes of the 20th century.

  2. Which athlete is known as 'The Greatest' in boxing?

    Answer: Muhammad Ali

    Muhammad Ali was famously nicknamed 'The Greatest' and is widely considered one of the best boxers of all time.

  3. Which boxer is known as 'The Greatest'?

    Answer: Muhammad Ali

    Muhammad Ali famously referred to himself as 'The Greatest' and is widely considered one of the greatest boxers of all time.

  4. In which Olympic Games did women's boxing first become an official event?

    Answer: 2012 London

    Women's boxing debuted at the 2012 Summer Olympics in London.

  5. In Olympic boxing, how many rounds are fought in a standard amateur match?

    Answer: 3

    Olympic boxing matches are fought over three rounds.

  6. Which of these athletes is known as 'The Greatest'?

    Answer: Muhammad Ali

    Muhammad Ali, the legendary professional boxer, famously nicknamed himself 'The Greatest'.

  7. In professional boxing, which weight division includes the world heavyweight title?

    Answer: Heavyweight

    The heavyweight division contests the world heavyweight championship.

  8. Which boxer is widely regarded as 'The Greatest' and was known for his quick footwork and taunting style?

    Answer: Muhammad Ali

    Muhammad Ali (Cassius Clay) famously called himself 'The Greatest' and was known for his distinctive boxing style.

  9. Which athlete is widely considered the greatest boxer of all time and was nicknamed 'The Greatest'?

    Answer: Muhammad Ali

    Muhammad Ali, born Cassius Clay, is widely regarded as one of the greatest heavyweight boxers and was known as 'The Greatest'.

  10. Which boxer holds the record for the longest reign as undisputed heavyweight champion (over 11 years)?

    Answer: Joe Louis

    Joe Louis held the world heavyweight championship for nearly 12 consecutive years (1937–1949), the longest reign in boxing history.

  11. Which boxer is widely regarded as 'The Greatest' and won Olympic gold for the United States in 1960?

    Answer: Muhammad Ali

    Muhammad Ali (born Cassius Clay) won an Olympic gold medal in light heavyweight boxing at the 1960 Rome Games.

  12. Who is the only boxer in history to win world titles in five different weight divisions?

    Answer: Manny Pacquiao

    Manny Pacquiao of the Philippines won world titles in five weight classes (flyweight, featherweight, super featherweight, lightweight, and light welterweight).

  13. Which of these athletes is widely regarded as the greatest boxer of all time, with a 56-0-1 professional record?

    Answer: Muhammad Ali

    Muhammad Ali retired with an official professional record of 56 wins and 1 loss (later amended to 56-0-1 for an early career bout), making him one of the greatest boxers ever.

  14. What is the maximum duration of a standard professional boxing match?

    Answer: 12 rounds

    Since 1987, world championship professional boxing matches have been limited to a maximum of twelve rounds.

  15. Which boxer holds the record for holding world titles in eight different weight divisions?

    Answer: Manny Pacquiao

    Manny Pacquiao of the Philippines is the only boxer in history to win world titles in eight different weight divisions.
