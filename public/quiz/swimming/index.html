Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which country has won the most Olympic gold medals in swimming (as of 2020)?

    Answer: USA

    The United States has historically accumulated the highest number of Olympic swimming gold medals.

  2. In Olympic swimming, what is the distance of the freestyle sprint event commonly known as the '100 free'?

    Answer: 100 meters

    The '100 free' is the 100-meter freestyle event, a standard Olympic swimming distance.

  3. What is the standard length of an Olympic swimming pool?

    Answer: 50 meters

    Olympic swimming competitions are held in a 50-meter long-course pool.

  4. In which Olympic sport do athletes compete in an event called the 'medley relay'?

    Answer: Swimming

    The medley relay is a swimming event where four swimmers each use a different stroke.

  5. In swimming, which stroke requires the swimmer's body to remain on their back?

    Answer: Backstroke

    In the backstroke, swimmers lie on their backs and alternate their arms while using a flutter kick.

  6. In Olympic swimming, how many lanes are used in the final?

    Answer: 8

    Olympic swimming finals typically feature eight lanes.

  7. Which swimming stroke is commonly referred to as "the crawl"?

    Answer: Freestyle

    The crawl is the most common freestyle technique and is often simply called freestyle.

  8. In which sport would you perform a 'butterfly' stroke?

    Answer: Butterfly

    The butterfly stroke is one of the four main swimming strokes used in competitive swimming.

  9. Which swimming stroke is NOT an official Olympic competitive stroke?

    Answer: Sidestroke

    Sidestroke is not an Olympic competitive stroke; the four official strokes are freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, and butterfly.

  10. Which swimmer holds the world record in the men's 200m freestyle as of 2023?

    Answer: David Popovici

    David Popovici set the 200m freestyle world record at the 2022 World Championships.

  11. In which Olympics did Michael Phelps win his first individual gold medal?

    Answer: Athens 2004

    Michael Phelps won his first individual Olympic gold in the 200m butterfly at the 2004 Athens Games.

  12. Which swimming stroke involves an alternating overwater arm recovery and is generally the fastest?

    Answer: Freestyle (Front Crawl)

    Front crawl (often raced as freestyle) is the fastest and most common competitive stroke.

  13. In swimming, which stroke is characterized by simultaneous overhead arm movements and a flutter kick?

    Answer: Butterfly

    The butterfly stroke involves simultaneous arm recovery over the water and a dolphin kick.

  14. Which swimmer holds the record for the most Olympic gold medals of all time, across all sports?

    Answer: Michael Phelps

    American swimmer Michael Phelps won 23 Olympic gold medals, the most by any athlete in Olympic history.

  15. Which of these swimming strokes is NOT used in the individual medley (IM) event?

    Answer: Sidestroke

    The individual medley consists of four strokes in this order: butterfly, backstroke, breaststroke, and freestyle. Sidestroke is not an Olympic event stroke.
