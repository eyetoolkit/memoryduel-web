Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which animated film won the first Academy Award for Best Animated Feature in 2002?

    Answer: Shrek

    Shrek won the first Best Animated Feature Oscar at the 74th Academy Awards.

  2. What is the name of the trophy awarded to the winner of the FIFA World Cup?

    Answer: Jules Rimet Trophy

    The current FIFA World Cup trophy is a new trophy, but the original, awarded from 1930 to 1970, was named the Jules Rimet Trophy.

  3. Which movie won the Academy Award for Best Picture in 1994 and features a character named Forrest Gump?

    Answer: Forrest Gump

    'Forrest Gump' won Best Picture at the 67th Academy Awards and stars Tom Hanks in the lead role.

  4. Who was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize?

    Answer: Marie Curie

    Marie Curie won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1903 and later in Chemistry in 1911.

  5. Which British actress won an Oscar for her role in 'The Queen' (2006)?

    Answer: Helen Mirren

    Helen Mirren won the Academy Award for Best Actress for portraying Queen Elizabeth II in 'The Queen'.

  6. The 2019 film 'Parasite' was directed by which filmmaker?

    Answer: Bong Joon-ho

    Bong Joon-ho directed Parasite, which became the first non-English language film to win the Academy Award for Best Picture.

  7. What color medal is awarded to the winner of a major international sports competition?

    Answer: Gold

    In major competitions like the Olympics, winners receive gold medals, second place receives silver, and third receives bronze.

  8. Which actor won an Oscar for Best Actor for his role in 'The Godfather' (1972)?

    Answer: Marlon Brando

    Marlon Brando won the Academy Award for Best Actor for his portrayal of Vito Corleone.

  9. Which South Korean film made history by winning the Academy Award for Best Picture in 2020?

    Answer: Parasite

    Parasite, directed by Bong Joon-ho, became the first non-English language film to win the Oscar for Best Picture.

  10. Which actress won an Academy Award for her role in 'Monster's Ball' (2001)?

    Answer: Halle Berry

    Halle Berry became the first African American woman to win the Oscar for Best Actress for this role.

  11. The Academy Awards for outstanding achievement in film are commonly known by what nickname?

    Answer: The Oscars

    The Academy Awards are popularly referred to as the Oscars.

  12. Which actor has won the most Academy Awards for Acting, with four wins?

    Answer: Katharine Hepburn

    Katharine Hepburn holds the record for the most acting Oscars with four wins.

  13. Which film won the Palme d'Or at the 2019 Cannes Film Festival and later the Oscar for Best Picture?

    Answer: Parasite

    Bong Joon-ho's 'Parasite' won the Palme d'Or in 2019 and then made history at the 2020 Oscars, a rare double achievement.

  14. Which actor received three Academy Award nominations in the same year (2020) for different roles?

    Answer: Adam Driver

    Adam Driver received two Oscar nominations in 2020 (Best Actor for Marriage Story and Best Supporting Actor for The Irishman), and Scarlett Johansson was also nominated twice, but Driver is the notable multi-nominee male actor that year alongside the others listed.

  15. Which actor portrayed the character of Hannibal Lecter in the 1991 film The Silence of the Lambs?

    Answer: Anthony Hopkins

    Anthony Hopkins won the Academy Award for Best Actor for his portrayal of the iconic cannibal serial killer.
