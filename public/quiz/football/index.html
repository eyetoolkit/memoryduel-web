Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. How many players are on a standard football (soccer) team on the field during a match?

    Answer: 11

    Each football team fields 11 players at a time, including one goalkeeper. The team must have a minimum of 7 players to start or continue a match according to FIFA rules.

  2. In which sport would you score a goal?

    Answer: Soccer

    In soccer, a goal is scored when the ball completely crosses the goal line.

  3. Which sport uses a round ball and is played mainly with the feet?

    Answer: Soccer

    Soccer is the sport where players mainly use their feet to control a round ball.

  4. Which country's national team is known as 'La Furia Roja' in football?

    Answer: Spain

    'La Furia Roja' (The Red Fury) is the nickname of the Spanish national football team.

  5. Which country's team won the FIFA eWorld Cup (the official esports version of the FIFA video game tournament) in 2022?

    Answer: France

    Brazilian player 'Phzin' won the FIFA eWorld Cup 2022, though France has historically been very strong in FIFA esports competitions.

  6. Which country has won the most Olympic gold medals in men's football (soccer)?

    Answer: Brazil

    Brazil leads with nine Olympic gold medals in men's football.

  7. Which sport is known as 'the king of sports' in many countries due to its global popularity?

    Answer: Football (Soccer)

    Football (soccer) is widely regarded as the world's most popular sport, played and followed by billions of people across every continent.

  8. Which country has participated in every FIFA World Cup since the tournament began in 1930?

    Answer: Brazil

    Brazil is the only national team to have participated in every FIFA World Cup tournament since it began in 1930.

  9. Which country has won the most titles in the UEFA European Football Championship (Euros)?

    Answer: Germany

    Germany (including West Germany) has won the UEFA European Championship three times (1972, 1980, 1996), more than any other nation, though Spain and Italy are close behind with two each.

  10. Who is the all-time leading goal scorer in men's international football (soccer)?

    Answer: Cristiano Ronaldo

    As of recent updates, Cristiano Ronaldo of Portugal has scored the most goals in men's international football, surpassing Ali Daei's previous record.

  11. In the history of the FIFA World Cup, which player has scored the most goals in a single tournament?

    Answer: Just Fontaine

    Just Fontaine scored 13 goals in the 1958 World Cup, the most in a single tournament.

  12. Which nation has won the most FIFA Women's World Cup titles?

    Answer: United States

    The United States has won the FIFA Women's World Cup four times (1991, 1999, 2015, 2019), the most of any nation.

  13. In which edition of the FIFA World Cup did the team of Cameroon reach the quarterfinals for the first time?

    Answer: 1990

    Cameroon advanced to the quarterfinals for the first time in the 1990 tournament.

  14. Which country has hosted the FIFA World Cup the most times (as of 2022), having hosted it twice?

    Answer: Mexico and Brazil

    As of 2022, Mexico and Brazil are tied for hosting the FIFA World Cup twice each (Mexico: 1970, 1986; Brazil: 1950, 2014).

  15. Which country has produced the most FIFA World Player of the Year / Ballon d'Or winners in football history?

    Answer: Brazil

    Brazil has produced the most Ballon d'Or winners, with legends like Ronaldo, Rivaldo, Ronaldinho, and Kaká all winning the prestigious award.
