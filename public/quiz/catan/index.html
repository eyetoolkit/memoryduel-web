Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In the board game Catan (formerly The Settlers of Catan), which resource is NOT represented by a hexagonal tile?

    Answer: Iron

    Catan features five resource tiles: fields (grain), forests (lumber), hills (brick), mountains (ore), and pastures (wool). 'Iron' is not a resource in the game; the correct term is 'ore,' making 'Iron' the odd one out.

  2. In the board game Catan, what is the rarest resource required to build roads and settlements?

    Answer: Ore

    Ore (produced from mountain tiles) is generally considered the rarest and most valuable resource because it is required for development cards and upgrading to cities.

  3. In the board game Settlers of Catan (now simply called Catan), what are the primary resources players collect and trade?

    Answer: Wood, Brick, Sheep, Wheat, Ore

    Catan's five resource types—Wood, Brick (Clay), Sheep (Wool), Wheat, and Ore—are traded to build roads, settlements, and cities.

  4. Which board game, originally published in 1995, uses cards to represent different types of terrain for building a modular board?

    Answer: Catan

    In Catan (formerly The Settlers of Catan), players draw terrain cards to lay out the island board randomly each game, then use resources to build.

  5. Which of these is NOT one of the four starting resources in Catan?

    Answer: Iron

    Catan's four resources are brick, sheep, wheat, and ore (rock). Iron is not a resource in the game.

  6. Which board game requires players to manage their resources (wood, brick, sheep, wheat, ore) to build settlements and cities?

    Answer: Catan

    Catan (formerly The Settlers of Catan) is famous for its resource management mechanics.

  7. What is the maximum number of players that can participate in a standard game of "Catan"?

    Answer: 4

    Catan supports 3-4 players in the base game, with each player managing resources and building settlements.

  8. In Settlers of Catan (now simply Catan), what resource is represented by hexagons with tree symbols?

    Answer: Lumber

    In Catan, lumber (wood) is represented by forest hexagons with tree symbols. Brick comes from hills, ore from mountains, wool from pastures, and grain from fields.

  9. The popular board game 'Catan' was originally published under what name in Germany?

    Answer: Die Siedler

    'Catan' was originally published in Germany under the name 'Die Siedler von Catan' (The Settlers of Catan).

  10. In the board game Catan (formerly The Settlers of Catan), players compete primarily to build what?

    Answer: Roads and settlements

    Catan players collect resources to build roads, settlements, and cities to earn victory points.

  11. Which board game involves players placing hexagonal tiles to build a landscape and claim territory?

    Answer: Catan

    Catan (formerly The Settlers of Catan) uses hexagonal terrain tiles to create the board, whereas Carcassonne uses square tiles.

  12. Which board game, originally published in 1959, involves players negotiating trades of resources depicted on hexagonal tiles?

    Answer: Catan (Settlers of Catan)

    Settlers of Catan (now just Catan), designed by Klaus Teuber and first released in 1995 (though roots trace to 1959 prototypes), popularized resource-trading board games. Note: while widely associated with 1995, the question's framing tests knowledge of its core mechanics.

  13. Which board game, designed by Klaus Teuber, involves players settling an island and is often cited as a gateway game to modern hobby gaming?

    Answer: Catan

    Catan (formerly The Settlers of Catan) is widely recognized as a pivotal game that introduced many players to modern resource management and trading mechanics.

  14. Which board game, designed by Klaus Teuber, is set on the fictional island of Catan and involves hexagonal resource tiles?

    Answer: Settlers of Catan

    Settlers of Catan (now simply Catan) was designed by Klaus Teuber and is set on the island of Catan, featuring a modular board of hexagonal terrain tiles.

  15. In the game of 'Settlers of Catan' (now just 'Catan'), what resource is represented by the 'Ore' cards?

    Answer: Grey hexagons

    Ore is represented by grey (or dark grey) hexagons on the Catan board.
