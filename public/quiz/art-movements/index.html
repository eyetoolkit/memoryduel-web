Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which art movement was Pablo Picasso closely associated with?

    Answer: Cubism

    Picasso co-founded Cubism, a revolutionary art movement that broke subjects into geometric forms.

  2. What did Pablo Picasso co-found in the early 20th century?

    Answer: Cubism

    Picasso, along with Georges Braque, co-founded Cubism, revolutionizing modern art by depicting multiple viewpoints simultaneously.

  3. Which of these artists was a leading figure of the Surrealist art movement?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí was one of the most prominent Surrealists. His painting 'The Persistence of Memory' with its melting clocks is iconic.

  4. Which artist is a founder of the Cubist movement alongside Georges Braque?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso and Georges Braque developed Cubism in the early 1900s, revolutionizing how objects are represented in art.

  5. The abstract painting style known as 'Cubism' was co-founded by Pablo Picasso alongside which French artist?

    Answer: Georges Braque

    Pablo Picasso and Georges Braque co-founded Cubism in the early 1900s, revolutionizing European painting and sculpture by depicting subjects from multiple viewpoints.

  6. Which art movement did Claude Monet help pioneer?

    Answer: Impressionism

    Claude Monet was a founder of Impressionism, a 19th-century movement characterized by capturing light and color through visible brushstrokes.

  7. Which Spanish painter is known for co-founding the Cubist movement?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso, along with Georges Braque, developed Cubism in the early 20th century, revolutionizing European painting and sculpture.

  8. Which artist's style is known for using bold colors and expressive brushstrokes, as seen in 'The Starry Night'?

    Answer: Post-Impressionism

    Van Gogh's work is classified as Post-Impressionism, emphasizing emotional expression over realism.

  9. Which artist painted 'Les Demoiselles d'Avignon', a landmark work of Cubism?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Les Demoiselles d'Avignon' in 1907. Along with Georges Braque, he developed Cubism, revolutionizing 20th-century art.

  10. Which 19th-century French painter is credited with the birth of Impressionism?

    Answer: Claude Monet

    Claude Monet is considered the most consistent and prolific practitioner of the Impressionist philosophy, and his painting 'Impression, Sunrise' gave the movement its name.

  11. Which 1903 painting by Henri Matisse, featuring a nude woman in bold colors, helped launch Fauvism?

    Answer: Woman with a Hat

    Matisse's 'Woman with a Hat' (1905) was displayed at the Salon d'Automne in Paris and shocked critics with its non-naturalistic color palette. Critics called Matisse and his peers 'les Fauves' (the wild beasts), giving Fauvism its name.

  12. Which painter was a central figure in the Baroque period and painted 'Las Meninas'?

    Answer: A) Diego Velázquez

    Diego Velázquez, the Spanish court painter, created 'Las Meninas' in 1656.

  13. Which artist is credited with pioneering the use of geometric abstraction in art?

    Answer: Wassily Kandinsky

    Wassily Kandinsky is widely regarded as one of the pioneers of abstract art.

  14. Which artist created the 'Campbell's Soup Cans' artwork?

    Answer: Andy Warhol

    Andy Warhol created Campbell's Soup Cans in 1962, a landmark of Pop Art.

  15. Which artist, a leader of Cubism, also co-founded the Blue and Rose periods before developing Synthetic Cubism?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso co-founded Cubism with Georges Braque and is known for his Blue Period, Rose Period, and later Synthetic Cubism works.
