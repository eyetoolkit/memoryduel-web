Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Who painted the famous artwork 'Starry Night'?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    'The Starry Night' was painted by Dutch Post-Impressionist artist Vincent van Gogh in 1889, while he was staying at an asylum in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, France.

  2. Which post-impressionist artist cut off part of his own ear?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh famously cut off a portion of his left ear in December 1888 during a period of mental illness in Arles, France.

  3. Which famous painting depicts a group of sailors on a raft in distress?

    Answer: The Raft of the Medusa

    The Raft of the Medusa was painted by Théodore Géricault in 1818–1819. It depicts the aftermath of a real shipwreck and is a landmark of French Romanticism.

  4. The Renaissance, a period of great artistic and scientific advancement, began in which country?

    Answer: Italy

    The Renaissance started in Italy in the 14th century before spreading to the rest of Europe.

  5. Which painter is famous for using thick, swirling brushstrokes and vibrant colors?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Van Gogh's expressive, impasto brushwork is a hallmark of his Post-Impressionist style.

  6. Which Spanish painter created 'Guernica', a powerful anti-war masterpiece depicting the bombing of a Basque town?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in 1937 in response to the bombing of the town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War. It remains one of the most powerful anti-war statements in art.

  7. Which Dutch painter created the famous series of paintings depicting sunflowers?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh painted several still-life works of sunflowers between 1886 and 1889. The series is among his most recognizable works and showcases his bold use of color.

  8. In which century did Leonardo da Vinci paint the Mona Lisa?

    Answer: 16th century

    Leonardo began the Mona Lisa around 1503, placing it in the early 16th century.

  9. Which famous painting features a group of people eating dinner but is not 'The Last Supper'?

    Answer: The Night Watch

    The Night Watch (1642) is a famous painting by Dutch artist Rembrandt van Rijn depicting a militia company preparing to march, not a dinner scene. The School of Athens depicts philosophers gathering, not eating dinner.

  10. Which artist painted 'The Garden of Earthly Delights'?

    Answer: Hieronymus Bosch

    This triptych is a famous and enigmatic work by the Dutch painter Hieronymus Bosch.

  11. Which artist painted 'The Persistence of Memory' and was associated with Surrealism?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí was a Spanish Surrealist artist famous for his technical skill and bizarre, dreamlike imagery. 'The Persistence of Memory' (1931), featuring melting clocks, is his most recognized work and a defining piece of the Surrealist movement.

  12. Which artist painted 'The Scream', a work depicting an agonized figure against a turbulent sky?

    Answer: Edvard Munch

    Edvard Munch painted 'The Scream' in 1893. It is considered a precursor to Expressionism and a symbol of modern anxiety.

  13. Which painter is credited with co-founding the Cubist art movement alongside Pablo Picasso?

    Answer: Georges Braque

    Georges Braque and Pablo Picasso jointly developed Cubism between 1907 and 1914. Cubism revolutionized European painting by fragmenting objects and showing multiple perspectives simultaneously.

  14. The Benin Bronzes, a collection of thousands of metal plaques and sculptures, originated from which African kingdom?

    Answer: Benin Empire (modern-day Nigeria)

    The Benin Bronzes were created by the Benin Empire (in present-day Nigeria) beginning in the 13th century and were looted by British forces during the punitive expedition of 1897.

  15. Which painting, created by Edvard Munch, is often associated with existential anxiety?

    Answer: The Scream

    'The Scream' (1893) by Edvard Munch is an iconic Expressionist work symbolizing modern anxiety and dread.
