Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which country is Pablo Picasso most associated with?

    Answer: Spain

    Pablo Picasso was born in Málaga, Spain. Although he spent much of his career in France, he is most closely associated with his Spanish roots and co-founded Cubism.

  2. Who painted the famous 'Starry Night'?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh painted 'The Starry Night' in 1889 while staying at an asylum in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, France.

  3. In which country was the painter Frida Kahlo born?

    Answer: Mexico

    Frida Kahlo was born in Mexico in 1907; she is one of the country's most celebrated artists.

  4. Which artist is known for painting 'Water Lilies'?

    Answer: Claude Monet

    Claude Monet painted the Water Lilies series, consisting of approximately 250 oil paintings. They depict his flower garden at his home in Giverny and are a quintessential example of Impressionism.

  5. Which Spanish painter is known for his surrealistic art style?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí was a leading figure in the Surrealism movement.

  6. Which artist painted 'The Persistence of Memory' in 1931?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí painted 'The Persistence of Memory' in 1931, featuring melting clocks.

  7. Which painter is known for 'Guernica', a large anti-war painting?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted Guernica in 1937 in response to the bombing of the town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War.

  8. Which art movement is Claude Monet most closely associated with?

    Answer: Impressionism

    Claude Monet was a founder of French Impressionism, characterized by small, visible brushstrokes and emphasis on light.

  9. Which Spanish artist is famous for co-founding the Cubist movement?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso, along with Georges Braque, co-founded Cubism in the early 20th century, revolutionizing how artists depict three-dimensional objects.

  10. Which famous painting by Salvador Dalí features melting clocks draped over a barren landscape?

    Answer: The Persistence of Memory

    Salvador Dalí painted 'The Persistence of Memory' in 1931. Its iconic melting clocks have become a symbol of Surrealism.

  11. Which artist painted 'The Garden of Earthly Delights'?

    Answer: Hieronymus Bosch

    Hieronymus Bosch painted 'The Garden of Earthly Delights' between 1490 and 1510. It is famous for its complex, fantastical imagery.

  12. Which famous painting depicts the 1804 coronation of Napoleon?

    Answer: The Coronation of Napoleon

    The Coronation of Napoleon (1805-1807) is a massive painting by French artist Jacques-Louis David depicting Napoleon's coronation ceremony at Notre-Dame Cathedral in Paris.

  13. Which artist created the famous 'Campbell's Soup Cans' artwork?

    Answer: Andy Warhol

    Andy Warhol created his iconic Campbell's Soup Cans series in 1962, becoming a defining figure of the Pop Art movement.

  14. Which painter created the controversial work 'Les Demoiselles d'Avignon'?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Les Demoiselles d'Avignon' in 1907. It is considered a foundational work of Cubism and modern art.

  15. Which artist's self-portraits, characterized by intense color and emotional brushwork, are among the most recognized in Western art?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Van Gogh painted over 30 self-portraits, using bold colors and expressive brushwork to convey emotion.
