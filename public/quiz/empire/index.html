Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Who was the first Emperor of China?

    Answer: Qin Shi Huang

    Qin Shi Huang unified China in 221 BC and became its first emperor.

  2. Which empire was ruled by Genghis Khan in the 13th century?

    Answer: Mongol Empire

    Genghis Khan founded and led the Mongol Empire, which expanded across Asia in the early 13th century.

  3. Which empire was the largest contiguous land empire in world history?

    Answer: Mongol Empire

    At its height in the 13th–14th centuries, the Mongol Empire under Genghis Khan and his successors became the largest contiguous land empire ever, spanning from Eastern Europe to East Asia.

  4. The 'Mughal Empire' at its height controlled most of which modern-day region?

    Answer: South Asia (Indian subcontinent)

    The Mughal Empire, founded by Babur in 1526, controlled much of the Indian subcontinent at its peak under Emperor Akbar and later Aurangzeb.

  5. Which major European empire fell in 1922, leading to the establishment of the Republic of Turkey?

    Answer: The Ottoman Empire

    The Ottoman Empire, which had ruled for over 600 years, dissolved after World War I, giving way to modern Turkey under Mustafa Kemal Atatürk.

  6. After Genghis Khan, which of his successors continued the Mongol expansion into Eastern Europe, culminating in the sack of Kiev?

    Answer: Batu Khan

    Batu Khan led the Mongol Golden Horde’s invasion of Europe, including the destruction of Kiev in 1240.

  7. During which century did the Maurya Empire reach its greatest territorial extent under Emperor Ashoka?

    Answer: 3rd century BCE

    After Ashoka’s conversion to Buddhism following the Kalinga War, he expanded the empire across most of the Indian subcontinent in the 3rd century BCE.

  8. The Battle of Hastings in 1066 was fought between William the Conqueror and which English king?

    Answer: King Harold II

    Harold II led the English forces against William’s Norman army at Hastings.

  9. The Byzantine Empire, the eastern continuation of the Roman Empire, fell with the conquest of its capital in 1453 by which empire?

    Answer: Ottoman Empire

    Constantinople, the capital of the Byzantine Empire, was conquered by the Ottoman Empire under Sultan Mehmed II in 1453, ending the Byzantine state.

  10. According to the 'migration period' theory, what was the primary cause of the fall of the Western Roman Empire?

    Answer: Barbarian invasions

    The migration period theory emphasizes the pressure of barbarian migrations and invasions as a key factor.

  11. The 'Gunpowder Empires' of the early modern period refers to which three empires?

    Answer: Ottoman, Safavid, Mughal

    The term 'Gunpowder Empires' describes the Ottoman, Safavid, and Mughal empires, which rose to power between the 15th and 18th centuries by effectively adopting gunpowder technology for warfare and state-building.

  12. Which empire, at its height under Suleiman the Magnificent, controlled much of Southeastern Europe, Western Asia, and North Africa?

    Answer: The Ottoman Empire

    The Ottoman Empire reached its zenith under Suleiman the Magnificent (1520-1566), becoming a dominant world power with a vast multi-ethnic and multi-religious territory.

  13. The Qin Dynasty unified which country?

    Answer: China

    The Qin Dynasty unified China in 221 BCE, laying the foundation for the Great Wall and a centralized imperial system.

  14. Who was the first ruler of the Maurya Empire in ancient India?

    Answer: Chandragupta Maurya

    Chandragupta Maurya founded the Maurya Empire around 322 BCE, uniting much of the Indian subcontinent.

  15. The ancient city of Constantinople, now Istanbul, was the capital of which empire for over a thousand years?

    Answer: Byzantine Empire

    Constantinople served as the capital of the Byzantine (Eastern Roman) Empire from 330 CE until its fall to the Ottomans in 1453.
