Answer key

All twelve questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which animal is known as the 'ship of the desert'?

    Answer: Camel

    Camels are adapted to desert life, able to travel long distances without water.

  2. On which continent is the Sahara Desert located?

    Answer: Africa

    The Sahara Desert, the world's largest hot desert, is located in North Africa.

  3. Which desert, the largest hot desert in the world, stretches across North Africa?

    Answer: Sahara Desert

    The Sahara covers about 9.2 million square kilometers across much of North Africa, making it the largest hot desert on Earth.

  4. Which country contains the Atacama Desert, one of the driest places on Earth?

    Answer: Chile

    The Atacama Desert stretches along the Pacific coast of Chile and parts of Peru and Bolivia. Some weather stations in the Atacama have never recorded rainfall.

  5. What is the largest desert in the world (excluding polar regions)?

    Answer: Sahara

    The Sahara is the largest hot desert in the world, covering about 9.2 million square kilometers.

  6. Which country is home to the largest desert in the world (by area, hot desert)?

    Answer: Spans multiple countries in North Africa

    The Sahara is the world's largest hot desert, covering about 9.2 million square kilometers across many North African countries.

  7. Which desert, the world's largest hot desert, is located in northern Africa?

    Answer: Sahara

    The Sahara Desert, covering about 9.2 million square kilometers, is the world's largest hot desert, spanning much of northern Africa.

  8. What is the largest desert in Asia?

    Answer: Arabian

    The Arabian Desert covers roughly 2.33 million km², making it the largest in Asia.

  9. Which two countries does the Atacama Desert lie between, and what is it known for?

    Answer: Chile and Peru, the world's driest non-polar desert

    The Atacama Desert stretches along the Pacific coast of Chile and southern Peru, and is recognized as the driest desert on Earth outside the polar regions.

  10. What is the largest desert on Earth — not by heat, but by classification?

    Answer: Antarctic Desert

    The Antarctic Desert is the largest desert on Earth at about 14.2 million km². A desert is defined by low precipitation, not temperature — Antarctica receives very little rainfall.

  11. The Gobi Desert spans which two countries?

    Answer: China and Mongolia

    The Gobi, a large cold desert, stretches across southern Mongolia and northern China, covering over 1.2 million square kilometers.

  12. What is the largest desert in the world (including hot and cold deserts)?

    Answer: Antarctic

    Antarctica is the largest desert in the world at about 14.2 million square kilometers, since a desert is defined by low precipitation, not temperature.
