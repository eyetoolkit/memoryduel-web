Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which Beatles album features the song 'Let It Be' on its original tracklist?

    Answer: Let It Be

    'Let It Be' is the title track of The Beatles' final released album, 'Let It Be' (1970), famously recorded in the rooftop session atop Apple Corps' London office.

  2. Who is the American pop star famous for hits like 'Bad Guy' and 'Happier Than Ever'?

    Answer: Billie Eilish

    Billie Eilish rose to global fame with 'Bad Guy' and continues to dominate pop charts.

  3. Which famous band wrote the song 'Bohemian Rhapsody'?

    Answer: Queen

    Bohemian Rhapsody was written by Freddie Mercury and performed by Queen, first released in 1975 on the album 'A Night at the Opera'.

  4. Which country is the Beatles originally from?

    Answer: United Kingdom

    The Beatles were formed in Liverpool, England, United Kingdom, in 1960.

  5. Who is the lead singer of the British rock band The Beatles?

    Answer: John Lennon

    John Lennon was a primary vocalist and songwriter for The Beatles.

  6. What is the name of the Beatles' home city?

    Answer: Liverpool

    The Beatles famously hailed from Liverpool, England.

  7. Which artist is known for the song 'Like a Rolling Stone', released in 1965?

    Answer: Bob Dylan

    Bob Dylan wrote and performed 'Like a Rolling Stone', which is often cited as one of the greatest songs ever.

  8. Which genre of music originated in Jamaica in the late 1960s?

    Answer: Reggae

    Reggae developed in Jamaica in the late 1960s, popularized worldwide by Bob Marley. Ska is a precursor to reggae.

  9. Which singer is famous for the song 'Rolling in the Deep' and the album '21'?

    Answer: Adele

    Adele's album '21' (2011) became one of the best-selling albums of the 21st century, featuring 'Rolling in the Deep'.

  10. Which singer released the album '1989', which marked her full transition to pop music?

    Answer: Taylor Swift

    Taylor Swift's fifth studio album '1989' (2014) represented her shift from country to mainstream pop music and was a massive commercial success.

  11. Which 1971 song by Marvin Gaye addresses social and political issues, often cited as a landmark in concept albums?

    Answer: What's Going On

    Marvin Gaye's 'What's Going On' (1971) was a groundbreaking concept album addressing war, poverty, and injustice, and is frequently ranked among the greatest albums of all time.

  12. Which of these artists is known for a musical style that blends country, pop, and rock, and is associated with the song 'Jolene'?

    Answer: Dolly Parton

    Dolly Parton wrote and released 'Jolene' in 1973. The song has been covered by numerous artists and remains one of her signature songs.

  13. Which of these artists holds the record for the most Grammy Awards won by a single individual?

    Answer: Beyoncé

    Beyoncé has won 35 Grammy Awards (as of 2024), surpassing conductor Georg Solti, who held the record for decades with 31 wins.

  14. Which American singer and songwriter, known as the 'Queen of Soul', performed the iconic song 'Respect'?

    Answer: Aretha Franklin

    Aretha Franklin's 1967 rendition of 'Respect' became an anthem for both the civil rights and women's movements, cementing her legacy as the Queen of Soul.

  15. Which artist is known for the painting-like concept album 'Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band'?

    Answer: The Beatles

    The Beatles released 'Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band' in 1967, a groundbreaking album that helped define the concept album genre and the psychedelic era.
