Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In which country is the TV series 'Squid Game' set?

    Answer: South Korea

    Squid Game is a South Korean survival drama series created by Hwang Dong-hyuk.

  2. In which country is the famous film festival 'Cannes' held annually?

    Answer: France

    The Cannes Film Festival is held in Cannes, France, and is one of the most prestigious film festivals in the world.

  3. In which country was the TV series 'Money Heist' (La Casa de Papel) originally produced?

    Answer: Spain

    Money Heist is a Spanish heist crime drama created by Álex Pina. It became a global Netflix sensation after being picked up for international distribution.

  4. The Olympic Games are typically held every how many years?

    Answer: 4 years

    The Summer and Winter Olympics are held on a four-year cycle, alternating between Summer and Winter editions.

  5. Which major international sport is played with a round ball on a court and a net, popularized in its modern form in the United States?

    Answer: Basketball

    Basketball, invented in 1891, is played worldwide and is a major Olympic sport.

  6. Which country produced the famous animated film 'Spirited Away'?

    Answer: Japan

    'Spirited Away' is a Japanese animated fantasy film written and directed by Hayao Miyazaki.

  7. What is the name of the tile-based game originating from China that uses 144 tiles?

    Answer: Mahjong

    Mahjong originated in China and uses 144 tiles marked with Chinese characters and symbols.

  8. Which French film won the Palme d'Or at the 2023 Cannes Film Festival and later the Academy Award for Best Picture?

    Answer: Anatomy of a Fall

    Justine Triet's 'Anatomy of a Fall' won the Palme d'Or in 2023. Note: 'Parasite' (2019) was the first non-English language film to win Best Picture; 'Anatomy of a Fall' was nominated but did not win Best Picture, though it won Best Original Screenplay. The correct globally notable pairing of Palme d'Or + Best Picture is 'Parasite'.

  9. Which country produced the Academy Award-winning film 'Parasite' (2019)?

    Answer: South Korea

    'Parasite', directed by Bong Joon-ho, is a South Korean film that won Best Picture at the 2020 Oscars.

  10. Which Indian film, directed by Satyajit Ray, is the first part of the 'Apu Trilogy'?

    Answer: Pather Panchali

    Pather Panchali (1955) is the first film in Satyajit Ray's acclaimed Apu Trilogy, widely regarded as one of the greatest film series ever made.

  11. Which Japanese filmmaker, known for movies like 'Seven Samurai' and 'Rashomon', is considered one of the most influential directors in cinema history?

    Answer: Akira Kurosawa

    Akira Kurosawa (1910-1998) is one of the most important and influential filmmakers in cinema history, with works like 'Seven Samurai' (1954) and 'Rashomon' (1950) having a profound impact on world cinema, including Western directors like George Lucas and Steven Spielberg.

  12. Which Swedish actor is known for playing the title character in David Fincher's 'The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo' (2011)?

    Answer: Daniel Craig

    Daniel Craig, the James Bond actor, played Mikael Blomkvist in the English-language adaptation of The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo.

  13. As of 2023, which non‑English language film has earned the highest worldwide box‑office total?

    Answer: Wolf Warrior 2

    Wolf Warrior 2 (2017) holds the record for the highest‑grossing non‑English language film worldwide.

  14. Which country's team won the first-ever FIFA eWorld Cup (FIFA esports) in 2018?

    Answer: Germany

    Germany's 'deto' won the inaugural FIFA eWorld Cup 2018, showcasing the growth of football esports.

  15. Which Danish film, directed by Thomas Vinterberg, won the Oscar for Best International Feature Film in 2021?

    Answer: Another Round

    'Another Round' (Druk) won Best International Feature Film at the 93rd Academy Awards.
