Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What do bees primarily collect from flowers?

    Answer: Nectar

    Bees collect nectar from flowers, which they convert into honey, while also inadvertently transferring pollen and aiding pollination.

  2. What is the hard outer covering of an insect called?

    Answer: Exoskeleton

    Insects have an exoskeleton made of chitin that provides support and protection.

  3. What do bees produce that humans commonly consume?

    Answer: Honey

    Bees collect nectar from flowers and convert it into honey, which they store in their hives.

  4. Which animal is known to undergo complete metamorphosis?

    Answer: Butterfly

    Butterflies pass through egg, larva (caterpillar), pupa (chrysalis), and adult stages—complete metamorphosis—unlike grasshoppers, which undergo incomplete metamorphosis.

  5. What is the process called when a caterpillar turns into a butterfly?

    Answer: Metamorphosis

    Metamorphosis is a biological process by which an animal physically develops after birth or hatching.

  6. What is the average lifespan of a housefly?

    Answer: About 28 days

    Houseflies typically live for about 28 days on average, though their lifespan can vary with environmental conditions.

  7. How many legs does a spider have?

    Answer: Eight

    Spiders are arachnids and always have eight legs. Insects, by contrast, have six.

  8. Which of these creatures is an insect?

    Answer: Ant

    Insects have three body segments (head, thorax, abdomen), three pairs of jointed legs, compound eyes, and one pair of antennae. Ants fit this description, while spiders (arachnids) have 8 legs and 2 body parts.

  9. What is the primary role of bees in the ecosystem?

    Answer: Pollination

    Bees are crucial pollinators, transferring pollen between flowers which allows plants to reproduce.

  10. Which of these animals is known to carry out a long-distance migration that is one of the longest in the animal kingdom?

    Answer: Monarch Butterfly

    Monarch butterflies travel up to 4,800 kilometers (3,000 miles) during their annual migration to warmer climates, which is remarkable for an insect of its size.

  11. What is the primary cause of the current global decline in insect populations?

    Answer: Habitat loss and pesticide use

    While climate change plays a role, the primary drivers of insect decline are habitat loss (due to urbanization and agriculture) and the widespread use of pesticides.

  12. Which of these animals is known to exhibit 'eusocial' behavior, with a queen, workers, and drones?

    Answer: Honeybees

    Honeybees are eusocial insects with a complex caste system: a single queen, many female workers, and male drones, all working together for the colony's survival.

  13. What phenomenon describes the decline of insect pollinators that threatens global food production?

    Answer: Insect Apocalypse

    The 'Insect Apocalypse' refers to the dramatic global decline in insect populations, driven by pesticides, habitat loss, and climate change, endangering pollination services critical to agriculture.

  14. Which group of insects is the primary pollinator of many economically important crops such as apples and almonds?

    Answer: C) Bees

    Bees are the most efficient pollinators for these fruit crops due to their body structure and foraging behavior.

  15. What phenomenon describes the decline in insect populations worldwide, threatening pollination and food webs?

    Answer: Insect apocalypse

    The 'insect apocalypse' refers to documented global declines in insect biomass and diversity, driven by habitat loss, pesticides, and climate change.
