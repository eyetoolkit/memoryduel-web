Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the name of the fictional African nation in the 'Black Panther' films?

    Answer: Wakanda

    Wakanda is the technologically advanced nation ruled by T'Challa, the Black Panther.

  2. Which country produced the Oscar-winning film 'Parasite' (2019)?

    Answer: South Korea

    'Parasite', directed by Bong Joon-ho, is a South Korean film that won four Academy Awards including Best Picture.

  3. Who played the character of Iron Man/Tony Stark in the Marvel Cinematic Universe?

    Answer: Robert Downey Jr.

    Robert Downey Jr. portrayed Tony Stark (Iron Man) in the MCU starting with the 2008 film.

  4. Which actor plays the title character in the 'Indiana Jones' film series?

    Answer: Harrison Ford

    Harrison Ford is famously known for playing the adventurous archaeologist Indiana Jones in multiple films since 1981.

  5. Who played the character of Jack in the movie 'Titanic' (1997)?

    Answer: Leonardo DiCaprio

    Leonardo DiCaprio played Jack Dawson, the male lead in James Cameron's Titanic.

  6. Which 1993 Steven Spielberg film depicts the horrors of the Holocaust through the story of Oskar Schindler?

    Answer: Schindler's List

    Schindler's List won seven Academy Awards, including Best Picture and Best Director for Spielberg.

  7. Which director is known for films such as 'Pulp Fiction', 'Kill Bill', and 'Inglourious Basterds'?

    Answer: Quentin Tarantino

    Quentin Tarantino is an American filmmaker famous for his distinctive style, non-linear storytelling, and sharp dialogue.

  8. Who is the director of the 'Lord of the Rings' film trilogy?

    Answer: Peter Jackson

    Peter Jackson directed the acclaimed Lord of the Rings trilogy, filmed in his native New Zealand.

  9. Which 1999 sci-fi film features a red pill and a blue pill?

    Answer: The Matrix

    In 'The Matrix', Morpheus offers Neo the choice between the red pill (truth) and the blue pill (blissful ignorance).

  10. What is the name of the fictional city protected by Batman in Gotham?

    Answer: Gotham City

    Batman protects Gotham City, while Superman protects Metropolis.

  11. In which film does the character 'Rose' say, 'I'll never let go, Jack'?

    Answer: Titanic

    Kate Winslet's character Rose says this line in James Cameron's Titanic (1997).

  12. Which 1972 film, directed by Francis Ford Coppola, is widely regarded as one of the greatest films ever made?

    Answer: The Godfather

    'The Godfather' (1972) is Coppola's adaptation of Mario Puzo's novel about the Corleone crime family, starring Marlon Brando and Al Pacino.

  13. Which director created the animated films 'Spirited Away', 'My Neighbor Totoro', and 'Princess Mononoke'?

    Answer: Hayao Miyazaki

    Hayao Miyazaki is the co-founder of Studio Ghibli and director of those acclaimed animated films.

  14. Which actor is famous for portraying the Joker in the 2008 film 'The Dark Knight'?

    Answer: Heath Ledger

    Heath Ledger played the Joker in The Dark Knight and was posthumously awarded an Oscar for the role. Joaquin Phoenix played a different version in the 2019 film Joker.

  15. Which Japanese filmmaker directed the 1954 classic 'Seven Samurai'?

    Answer: Akira Kurosawa

    Akira Kurosawa's Seven Samurai profoundly influenced global cinema and was later remade as 'The Magnificent Seven'.
