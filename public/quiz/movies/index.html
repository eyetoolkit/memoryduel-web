Movie Quiz

Film trivia splits into three kinds of memory, and this quiz tests all three. There is the director question — who was behind the camera. There is the casting question — who stood in front of it. And there is the line question, where four words are enough to place an entire film.

The questions avoid release dates and box-office takings, because those are the facts that go stale and the ones people argue about. What is left is the stuff you either know or you do not: Han Solo's ship, the first fully computer-animated feature, the actor who won a posthumous Oscar for a Batman film.

The complete answer key sits below the quiz, followed by the ground the twenty questions cover and short answers to the questions people search for most about these films.

Answer key

All 20 questions, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Who directed the 1994 film Pulp Fiction?

    Answer: Quentin Tarantino

    Pulp Fiction was Tarantino's second feature and won the Palme d'Or at Cannes before becoming a cult reference point.

  2. Which 1975 Steven Spielberg film is credited with inventing the summer blockbuster?

    Answer: Jaws

    Jaws opened wide in summer 1975 and proved a film could earn most of its money in a few weeks rather than over months.

  3. Who directed Avatar, released in 2009?

    Answer: James Cameron

    James Cameron wrote and directed Avatar, and returned for the sequels decades later after developing the underwater capture technology.

  4. Who directed Inception, released in 2010?

    Answer: Christopher Nolan

    Inception was written and directed by Christopher Nolan, who had pitched the idea a decade before he was able to make it.

  5. Which film contains the line 'Life is like a box of chocolates'?

    Answer: Forrest Gump

    Tom Hanks says it as Forrest on a park bench. The line is often misquoted as 'Life is a box of chocolates'.

  6. Who played Jack Dawson in the 1997 film Titanic?

    Answer: Leonardo DiCaprio

    Leonardo DiCaprio played Jack opposite Kate Winslet's Rose. Matthew McConaughey was among those considered for the part.

  7. Who directed the Lord of the Rings trilogy?

    Answer: Peter Jackson

    Peter Jackson shot all three films in one continuous production in New Zealand between 1999 and 2000.

  8. Which film won Best Picture at the 1995 Academy Awards, for films of 1994?

    Answer: Forrest Gump

    Forrest Gump took Best Picture. Pulp Fiction won the original screenplay award but lost the top prize.

  9. Who played the T-800 in the 1984 film The Terminator?

    Answer: Arnold Schwarzenegger

    Arnold Schwarzenegger played the Terminator, a role originally offered to O. J. Simpson, who was judged too recognisable as a killer.

  10. Who played James Bond in Casino Royale, released in 2006?

    Answer: Daniel Craig

    Daniel Craig's first Bond film was a reboot of the series, and it opened with the black-and-white bathroom fight that defined his version.

  11. What is the name of Han Solo's spaceship in Star Wars?

    Answer: Millennium Falcon

    The Millennium Falcon is a heavily modified YT-1300 freighter, and its 'twelve parsecs' claim is about distance, not speed.

  12. In which Star Wars film does Darth Vader reveal he is Luke's father?

    Answer: The Empire Strikes Back

    The reveal comes at the end of The Empire Strikes Back, in a film that spends most of its running time splitting the heroes apart.

  13. Who directed The Matrix, released in 1999?

    Answer: The Wachowskis

    Lana and Lilly Wachowski wrote and directed it. They had pitched the idea with a comic-book storyboard before anyone would fund it.

  14. Which studio made Toy Story, the first fully computer-animated feature?

    Answer: Pixar

    Toy Story was Pixar's first feature, released in 1995. Disney distributed it and later bought the studio outright.

  15. Who played the Joker in The Dark Knight, released in 2008?

    Answer: Heath Ledger

    Heath Ledger won a posthumous Academy Award for the role. He died in January 2008, six months before the film opened.

  16. Which film contains the line 'Here's looking at you, kid'?

    Answer: Casablanca

    Humphrey Bogart says it to Ingrid Bergman in Casablanca. The line was ad-libbed from a phrase Bogart used at the poker table.

  17. Who directed the 1960 film Psycho?

    Answer: Alfred Hitchcock

    Hitchcock shot Psycho on a television budget and insisted cinemas not admit anyone after the film had started, to protect the shower scene.

  18. Which actor played Hermione Granger in the Harry Potter films?

    Answer: Emma Watson

    Emma Watson played Hermione across all eight films, starting in 2001 when she was eleven years old.

  19. Who played Vito Corleone in The Godfather, released in 1972?

    Answer: Marlon Brando

    Marlon Brando played the older Vito; Robert De Niro played the same character as a young man in Part II, and also won an Oscar for it.

  20. Who directed Citizen Kane, released in 1941?

    Answer: Orson Welles

    Citizen Kane was Orson Welles's first feature, made when he was twenty-five after arriving from radio and theatre.

What this quiz covers

Nothing here depends on a number that moves. No budgets, no box-office records, no 'highest-grossing film of all time' — those answers change every few years and make a quiz feel broken when they do.

The hard lane leans on craft rather than fame: who actually directed a film people assume was directed by someone else, and which year a line was first spoken rather than which film is most quoted.

  • Directors and their signature films: Tarantino, Spielberg, Cameron, Nolan, Jackson, Hitchcock and the Wachowskis.
  • Casting: who played Jack Dawson, Hermione Granger, the T-800, James Bond in 2006, and the Joker in 2008.
  • Lines and where they come from — 'Life is like a box of chocolates' and 'Here's looking at you, kid'.
  • Milestones: the first fully computer-animated feature, and the film credited with inventing the summer blockbuster.
  • Franchise furniture: Han Solo's ship, the Star Wars film that delivers the famous reveal, and the studio behind Toy Story.

Frequently asked questions

Who directed Pulp Fiction?
Quentin Tarantino, in 1994. It was his second feature after Reservoir Dogs and won the Palme d'Or at Cannes.
Which film first used computer animation for an entire feature?
Toy Story, released by Pixar in 1995. Every earlier animated feature was drawn or shot frame by frame by hand.
Who played the Joker in The Dark Knight?
Heath Ledger, who won a posthumous Academy Award for the role. He died in January 2008, before the film was released.
Who directed the Lord of the Rings trilogy?
Peter Jackson, who shot all three films together in New Zealand. The trilogy was released between 2001 and 2003.
What is Han Solo's spaceship called?
The Millennium Falcon, a modified YT-1300 freighter. It appears first in A New Hope and is described as having made the Kessel Run in less than twelve parsecs.
