Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. How many chambers does a typical human heart have?

    Answer: 4

    The human heart has four chambers: two atria (upper) and two ventricles (lower).

  2. What is the main function of red blood cells in the human body?

    Answer: Carry oxygen

    Red blood cells contain hemoglobin, which binds to oxygen in the lungs and transports it to tissues throughout the body.

  3. What is the primary gas that humans need to breathe in to survive?

    Answer: Oxygen

    Oxygen is essential for cellular respiration, the process that generates energy in the body.

  4. Which part of the human body contains the strongest muscle relative to its size?

    Answer: Jaw (Masseter)

    The masseter (jaw) muscle can exert a force of up to 200 pounds, the highest relative to its size.

  5. Which gas do humans primarily exhale during respiration?

    Answer: Carbon Dioxide

    During cellular respiration, humans consume oxygen and produce carbon dioxide as a waste product.

  6. Which sense organ is used for seeing?

    Answer: Eyes

    The eyes are the sensory organs responsible for vision.

  7. What is the world record for the longest fingernails on a single hand (combined length, ever recorded)?

    Answer: About 28 feet (8.6 m)

    Shridhar Chillal of India grew the fingernails on his left hand to a combined length of 28 feet 4.5 inches (8.65 m), a Guinness World Record.

  8. Which human sense relies on the olfactory epithelium?

    Answer: Smell

    The olfactory epithelium is the tissue in the nasal cavity responsible for the sense of smell.

  9. Which blood type is considered the universal donor?

    Answer: O-

    O- blood lacks A, B antigens and Rh factor, making it compatible with all blood types.

  10. What is the fastest recorded speed of a human sneeze?

    Answer: 100 mph

    The fastest recorded human sneeze reached approximately 100 mph (160 km/h), as documented in the Guinness Book of World Records.

  11. How many bones are there in the human body?

    Answer: 206

    An adult human body typically contains 206 bones.

  12. Which human organ contains the greatest number of cells?

    Answer: Liver

    The liver is estimated to contain about 2–3 trillion cells, more than any other organ.

  13. How many bones does a newborn baby typically have?

    Answer: 270

    Newborns are born with about 270 bones, many of which fuse together during childhood to form the 206 bones found in adults.

  14. What is the largest internal organ in the human body by mass?

    Answer: Liver

    The liver weighs about 1.5 kg (3.3 lbs) in adults, making it the largest internal organ by mass. The skin is the largest organ overall.

  15. What is the world record for the most time spent holding one's breath (static apnea)?

    Answer: About 24 minutes

    Budimir Šobat of Croatia holds the record at 24 minutes 37 seconds (2021).
