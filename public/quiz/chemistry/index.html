Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the smallest unit of matter that retains the chemical properties of an element?

    Answer: Atom

    An atom is the fundamental unit of an element. While it can be divided into subatomic particles (protons, neutrons, electrons), the atom is the smallest unit that still behaves chemically like that specific element.

  2. What is the chemical symbol for gold?

    Answer: Au

    Gold’s symbol comes from the Latin 'aurum' and is Au.

  3. Which of these elements does water (H2O) contain?

    Answer: Hydrogen and Oxygen

    Water molecules consist of two hydrogen atoms bonded to one oxygen atom, hence the chemical formula H2O.

  4. What is the boiling point of water at sea level in degrees Celsius?

    Answer: 100°C

    At standard atmospheric pressure (sea level), water boils at exactly 100°C (212°F).

  5. Which gas makes up approximately 78% of Earth's atmosphere by volume?

    Answer: Nitrogen

    Nitrogen (N₂) is the dominant component of Earth's atmosphere, constituting about 78% of the total volume.

  6. Which chemical element is the most abundant metal in the Earth's crust?

    Answer: Aluminum

    Aluminum is the most abundant metal in Earth's crust, making up about 8% by weight.

  7. What pH value indicates a neutral solution?

    Answer: 7

    A pH of 7 is neutral; values below 7 are acidic and above 7 are basic.

  8. Which element has the atomic number 79?

    Answer: Gold

    Gold's atomic number is 79, placing it among the transition metals on the periodic table.

  9. In organic chemistry, which functional group is characterized by a carbon‑carbon double bond?

    Answer: Alkene

    An alkene contains at least one C=C double bond, distinguishing it from other functional groups.

  10. Which type of bond is formed when electrons are shared equally between two atoms?

    Answer: Nonpolar covalent bond

    Equal sharing of electron density creates a non‑polar covalent bond.

  11. Which of these is an example of an endothermic reaction?

    Answer: Photosynthesis

    Photosynthesis absorbs energy (sunlight) to convert CO2 and water into glucose, making it endothermic. Combustion and rusting release heat (exothermic); freezing releases heat.

  12. What is the hybridization of carbon atoms in benzene?

    Answer: sp^2

    In benzene each carbon is sp² hybridized, forming a planar ring with delocalized π electrons.

  13. Which statement about the rate‑determining step of a multi‑step reaction mechanism is correct?

    Answer: It involves the highest activation energy

    The rate‑determining step is the slowest step and usually has the highest activation energy barrier in the mechanism.

  14. Which element has the highest known melting point?

    Answer: Tungsten

    Tungsten melts at 3,422 °C (6,192 °F), the highest melting point among all elements under standard pressure.

  15. What is the oxidation state of sulfur in H₂SO₄?

    Answer: B) +6

    In H₂SO₄ the sum of oxidation numbers equals zero; hydrogen is +1, oxygen is -2, so sulfur must be +6.
