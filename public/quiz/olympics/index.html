Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the standard length of an Olympic swimming pool?

    Answer: 50 meters

    Olympic swimming competitions are held in a 50-meter long-course pool.

  2. Which Olympic Games were famously boycotted by the United States and several other nations in 1980?

    Answer: Moscow

    The US led a boycott of the 1980 Moscow Olympics to protest the Soviet invasion of Afghanistan.

  3. What is the name of the main event of the Olympic Games that includes the 100m sprint?

    Answer: Track and field

    The 100m sprint is part of track and field (athletics), the centerpiece of the Olympic Games.

  4. In which Olympic sport do athletes compete for gold in the '100 metres' event?

    Answer: Athletics (Track and Field)

    The 100 metres is one of the most prestigious sprint events in track and field athletics.

  5. Which Olympic sport involves athletes jumping over a bar using a long pole?

    Answer: Pole vault

    The pole vault uses a long flexible pole to help athletes clear a high bar.

  6. Which Olympic Games are held every four years in the same year as the Summer Olympics?

    Answer: Winter Olympics

    The Winter Olympics occur in the same calendar year as the Summer Games, alternating every two years.

  7. Which of these sports is NOT part of the traditional Summer Olympic Games programme?

    Answer: Cricket

    Cricket has historically not been part of the Summer Olympics (though it was included in 1900 and returned for 2028), unlike Rowing, Boxing, and Cycling.

  8. In Olympic basketball, how many points is a free throw worth?

    Answer: 1 point

    A free throw is always worth 1 point in FIBA (and Olympic) basketball rules, awarded after certain fouls.

  9. Which of these countries did NOT win a medal at the 2020 Tokyo Olympics?

    Answer: Egypt

    Egypt did not win any medals at the 2020 Tokyo Olympics, while the other three countries did.

  10. In swimming, which stroke is NOT typically swum at the Olympic Games as an individual event?

    Answer: Sidestroke

    The four competitive Olympic swimming strokes are freestyle (front crawl), backstroke, breaststroke, and butterfly. Sidestroke is not competitive at this level.

  11. How many rings make up the Olympic symbol?

    Answer: 5

    The Olympic rings consist of five interlocking rings representing the five inhabited continents.

  12. In which year did the modern Olympic Games first take place?

    Answer: 1896

    The first modern Olympic Games were held in Athens, Greece, in 1896, reviving the ancient tradition.

  13. Which country has hosted both the Summer Olympic Games and the FIFA World Cup three times each?

    Answer: Italy

    Italy has hosted the Summer Olympics twice (1960, 2006 Winter) and the FIFA World Cup three times (1934, 1938, 1990); however, no country has hosted the Summer Olympics three times. Germany hosted the Summer Olympics twice (1936, 1972) and the World Cup as West Germany/Germany three times (1974, 2006, and shared 1974) — this question is often debated. The intended answer based on commonly cited trivia is Germany (hosted World Cup 1974, 2006; shared 1974; Olympics 1936, 1972). This is complex — let me note Italy hosted the Olympics in 1960 (Summer) and 2006 (Winter), and World Cup three times.

  14. Which legendary swimmer holds the record for the most Olympic gold medals won by an individual athlete, with a total of 23?

    Answer: Michael Phelps

    Michael Phelps, an American swimmer, won 23 Olympic gold medals across four Games (2000-2016), the most by any individual athlete in history.

  15. The 'Olympic motto' consists of three Latin words. What are they?

    Answer: Citius, Altius, Fortius

    The Olympic motto is 'Citius, Altius, Fortius' (Faster, Higher, Stronger), adopted in 1894. 'Veni, vidi, vici' is Julius Caesar's famous phrase, unrelated to sport.
