Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which animal is known as the largest living land animal?

    Answer: Elephant

    The African elephant is the largest living land animal, weighing up to 6,000 kg.

  2. How many players are on the field for a standard team in soccer (association football)?

    Answer: 11

    A standard soccer team consists of 11 players on the field, including the goalkeeper.

  3. What is the chemical symbol for water?

    Answer: H2O

    Water is composed of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom, giving it the chemical formula H2O.

  4. Which of these colors is NOT one of the three primary colors of light (additive color model)?

    Answer: Yellow

    The three primary colors of light are red, green, and blue (RGB). Yellow is a secondary color in the additive model, formed by mixing red and green.

  5. Which human organ pumps blood through the body?

    Answer: Heart

    The heart is a muscular organ that pumps blood, delivering oxygen and nutrients throughout the body.

  6. Which of these is a continent?

    Answer: Antarctica

    Antarctica is one of the seven continents on Earth.

  7. What is the term for a group of musicians playing together?

    Answer: Ensemble

    An ensemble is a group of musicians who perform together. The term is used across classical, jazz, and popular music.

  8. Which planet in our solar system has the most moons (as of recent counts)?

    Answer: Saturn

    Saturn has overtaken Jupiter in confirmed moon counts, with over 140 confirmed moons as of 2023-2024.

  9. What is the longest river in the world?

    Answer: Nile River

    The Nile River is approximately 6,650 km long, flowing northward through northeastern Africa to the Mediterranean Sea. However, some measurements suggest the Amazon might be longer.

  10. Which is the driest continent on Earth?

    Answer: Antarctica

    Antarctica is a desert by precipitation standards, receiving less than 50 mm of precipitation per year in its interior.

  11. Which blood type is known as the universal donor?

    Answer: Type O negative

    O negative blood lacks A, B, and Rh antigens, so it can be transfused to people of any blood type in emergencies.

  12. Which animal is capable of changing its color to blend in with its surroundings and is also one of the most intelligent invertebrates?

    Answer: Cuttlefish

    Cuttlefish are cephalopods renowned for their rapid and complex color-changing abilities using specialized skin cells called chromatophores, and they demonstrate advanced problem-solving skills.

  13. How many times does the Earth rotate on its axis in a year?

    Answer: 365.25

    The Earth rotates about 365.25 times per year, which is why we add a leap day every 4 years to keep calendars aligned.

  14. Which country spans the most time zones in the world?

    Answer: France

    France includes numerous overseas territories, giving it 12 distinct time zones, more than any other nation.

  15. Which element makes up approximately 78% of Earth's atmosphere by volume?

    Answer: Nitrogen

    Nitrogen makes up about 78% of Earth's atmosphere, while oxygen makes up about 21%.
