Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which river, the longest in Africa, flows through 11 countries and empties into the Mediterranean Sea?

    Answer: Nile

    The Nile is widely recognized as the longest river in the world, stretching approximately 6,650 km through northeastern Africa before reaching its delta in Egypt.

  2. Which country is home to the city of Cairo?

    Answer: Egypt

    Cairo is the capital of Egypt and the largest city in Africa.

  3. Which of the following is the largest land animal alive today?

    Answer: African elephant

    The African elephant is the largest living land animal, with males weighing up to about 6,000 kg.

  4. Which of these animals is known for its black and white stripes?

    Answer: Zebra

    Zebras are African equids famous for their distinctive striped coats, unique to each individual.

  5. What is the capital of Morocco?

    Answer: Rabat

    Rabat is the capital of Morocco, while Casablanca is its largest city.

  6. What is the capital of Nigeria?

    Answer: Abuja

    Abuja is the capital of Nigeria. It replaced Lagos as the capital in 1991.

  7. The Congo River is the second longest river in Africa. What is unique about its basin compared to the Nile?

    Answer: It has the most volume discharge of any African river

    Although shorter than the Nile, the Congo has by far the greatest water volume of any African river and the second largest in the world after the Amazon.

  8. Which country contains the highest mountain in Africa, Mount Kilimanjaro?

    Answer: Tanzania

    Mount Kilimanjaro (5,895 m) is located in northeastern Tanzania, near the Kenyan border.

  9. Nelson Mandela became the first Black president of which country?

    Answer: South Africa

    Nelson Mandela was elected in 1994, marking the end of apartheid in South Africa.

  10. Which African country is known for its tradition of 'griots'—storytellers who preserve oral history through music and narrative?

    Answer: Senegal

    Griots are West African storytellers, praise singers, and musicians who maintain a tradition of oral history passed down through generations, particularly prominent in Senegal and Mali.

  11. The Congo River is notable for being the world's deepest river. It flows through which two countries' capitals?

    Answer: Kinshasa and Brazzaville

    The Congo River separates Kinshasa (capital of DR Congo) and Brazzaville (capital of Republic of the Congo) — the two closest national capitals in the world.

  12. Which river, the second longest in Africa, empties into the Atlantic Ocean near Douala?

    Answer: Congo River

    The Congo River is the second longest river in Africa and the second most voluminous in the world, flowing into the Atlantic Ocean near Douala, Cameroon.

  13. In the Yoruba religion, which originated in West Africa, what is the term for the spirits or deities that act as intermediaries between humans and the supreme god Olodumare?

    Answer: Orisha

    In Yoruba religion, Orishas are spirits/deities that reflect one of the manifestations of Olodumare (God). This concept spread to the Americas via the Atlantic slave trade, evolving into figures in Candomblé and Santería.

  14. The concept of 'Ubuntu' originates from which region and roughly translates to 'I am because we are'?

    Answer: Sub-Saharan Africa

    Ubuntu is a Nguni Bantu term meaning 'humanity'. It is sometimes translated as 'I am because we are', expressing a belief in a universal bond of sharing that connects all humanity.

  15. The policy of 'Apartheid' in South Africa was officially abolished in which year?

    Answer: 1994

    Apartheid was dismantled in the early 1990s, culminating in the first fully democratic elections in April 1994, when Nelson Mandela became president.
