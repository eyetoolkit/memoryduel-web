Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which video game franchise features a character named Link who must save the princess Zelda?

    Answer: The Legend of Zelda

    The Legend of Zelda is a long-running action-adventure series by Nintendo where Link is the main protagonist and Princess Zelda is a key character.

  2. In the video game Fortnite, what mechanic allows players to build structures?

    Answer: Resource harvesting and building

    Fortnite's signature mechanic is harvesting wood, stone, and metal to build walls, ramps, and other structures during battle.

  3. Which country is home to the headquarters of video game giant Nintendo?

    Answer: Japan

    Nintendo was founded in Kyoto, Japan, and is still headquartered there.

  4. What is the name of the video game character famous for collecting gold rings?

    Answer: Sonic the Hedgehog

    Sonic the Hedgehog, created by Sega, uses gold rings as health; losing them upon taking damage.

  5. In the popular video game series The Legend of Zelda, what is the name of the main protagonist?

    Answer: Link

    The main playable character in The Legend of Zelda series is Link. Zelda is the princess he is often trying to save.

  6. In esports, which game is famous for its 'battle royale' mode where 100 players fight to be the last one standing?

    Answer: Fortnite

    Fortnite popularized the battle royale genre where players compete until one remains.

  7. Which video game genre does the title 'The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild' primarily belong to?

    Answer: Action-adventure

    Breath of the Wild is an open-world action-adventure game, emphasizing exploration, puzzle-solving, and combat.

  8. In the game 'Rocket League', what is the primary objective?

    Answer: To score goals using rocket-powered cars

    Rocket League is a hybrid of arcade-style soccer and vehicular chaos, where players use rocket-powered cars to hit a ball into a goal.

  9. What is the name of the currency used in the video game 'Roblox' to buy virtual items?

    Answer: Robux

    Robux is the virtual currency used within the Roblox platform.

  10. What was the first video game to be played in outer space?

    Answer: Tetris

    Tetris became the first video game played in outer space when NASA astronaut Shannon Lucid played it on a Game Boy aboard the Space Shuttle in 1993. This occurred during her stay aboard the Russian Space Station Mir.

  11. Which video game series features the recurring antagonist 'Sephiroth'?

    Answer: Final Fantasy

    Sephiroth is the iconic villain of Final Fantasy VII, a major installment in the Final Fantasy series.

  12. Which of these is the highest-selling video game of all time (including bundled copies)?

    Answer: Minecraft

    Minecraft holds the record as the best-selling video game of all time with over 300 million copies sold across platforms.

  13. Which video game company was founded by Hideki Kamiya and is known for 'Devil May Cry' and 'Bayonetta'?

    Answer: PlatinumGames

    PlatinumGames, founded in 2006, was co-founded by Hideki Kamiya and is renowned for action games like Devil May Cry and Bayonetta.

  14. In the video game Dark Souls, what term is used to describe the act of being invaded by another player's phantom into your world?

    Answer: Being invaded

    In Dark Souls, when another player enters your world as a hostile phantom using a Black Spirit Orb, it is called 'being invaded' — a core multiplayer mechanic.

  15. Which video game, developed by FromSoftware and released in 2019, is set in a fictionalized Sengoku-era Japan and features the Shinobi protagonist Sekiro?

    Answer: Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice

    Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice won Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2019. Ghost of Tsushima was developed by Sucker Punch, and Tenchu was a different FromSoftware series.
