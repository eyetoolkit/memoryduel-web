Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which playwright wrote the tragedy "Hamlet"?

    Answer: William Shakespeare

    William Shakespeare authored "Hamlet" around 1600, one of his most celebrated plays.

  2. Who directed the classic 1994 film 'The Shawshank Redemption'?

    Answer: Frank Darabont

    Frank Darabont directed 'The Shawshank Redemption', based on a Stephen King novella.

  3. Which TV series features a chemistry teacher turned methamphetamine producer named Walter White?

    Answer: Breaking Bad

    Breaking Bad follows Walter White, played by Bryan Cranston, a chemistry teacher diagnosed with cancer who turns to making meth.

  4. Which actor portrayed Jack Dawson in the 1997 film Titanic?

    Answer: Leonardo DiCaprio

    Leonardo DiCaprio played Jack Dawson, the male lead in Titanic.

  5. Which actor played the title role in the 2000 film 'Gladiator'?

    Answer: Russell Crowe

    Russell Crowe won the Academy Award for Best Actor for his portrayal of Maximus in 'Gladiator'.

  6. Who won the Academy Award for Best Actor for the 2010 film "The King's Speech"?

    Answer: Colin Firth

    Colin Firth received the Oscar for Best Actor for his portrayal of King George VI in The King's Speech.

  7. Which actor portrays Walter White in the television series 'Breaking Bad'?

    Answer: Bryan Cranston

    Bryan Cranston won multiple Emmy Awards for his portrayal of Walter White, a chemistry teacher turned methamphetamine manufacturer.

  8. Which actress received the Academy Award for Best Actress for her role in the 2016 film La La Land?

    Answer: Emma Stone

    Emma Stone won Best Actress for her performance as Mia Dolan in La La Land at the 2017 Oscars.

  9. The TV series 'The Sopranos' primarily focuses on a New Jersey mob boss and his struggles with what?

    Answer: His crime family and personal life

    The groundbreaking HBO series 'The Sopranos' follows Tony Soprano as he navigates the dangerous world of the Italian-American Mafia while dealing with family and mental health issues.

  10. What is the name of the fictional police department featured in the long-running TV show 'Law & Order'?

    Answer: NYPD

    The original 'Law & Order' and its many spin-offs are primarily set within the New York City Police Department (NYPD).

  11. In which decade did the TV series 'Twin Peaks' originally premiere?

    Answer: 1990s

    Twin Peaks debuted in 1990, making its premiere in the 1990s.

  12. In which year did the TV series 'Game of Thrones' win its first Emmy Award for Outstanding Drama Series?

    Answer: 2012

    Game of Thrones first won Outstanding Drama Series at the 2012 Primetime Emmy Awards.

  13. Which director won the Palme d’Or at the 2011 Cannes Film Festival for The Tree of Life?

    Answer: Terrence Malick

    Terrence Malick was awarded the Palme d’Or for The Tree of Life at the 2011 Cannes Film Festival.

  14. Which 1994 film features characters named Forrest Gump, Lieutenant Dan, and Jenny Curran?

    Answer: Forrest Gump

    Forrest Gump, directed by Robert Zemeckis and starring Tom Hanks, is a historical drama covering several decades of American history through the life of the titular character.

  15. Which 1960 French New Wave film is credited with popularizing the jump cut editing technique?

    Answer: Breathless (À bout de souffle)

    Jean‑Luc Godard's 1960 film Breathless introduced the jump cut to mainstream cinema.
