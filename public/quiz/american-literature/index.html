Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which poet wrote the poem 'The Road Not Taken'?

    Answer: Robert Frost

    Robert Frost authored the poem, which appears in his collection 'Mountain Interval' (1916).

  2. In which novel does the character Atticus Finch appear?

    Answer: To Kill a Mockingbird

    Atticus Finch is the father and main moral figure in Harper Lee's 1960 novel To Kill a Mockingbird.

  3. Who authored the novel "The Great Gatsby"?

    Answer: F. Scott Fitzgerald

    F. Scott Fitzgerald wrote The Great Gatsby, a classic of 20th‑century American fiction.

  4. Who wrote the poem 'The Raven'?

    Answer: C: Edgar Allan Poe

    Poe's haunting narrative poem was first published in 1845.

  5. Which American novelist wrote 'The Great Gatsby', a classic of American literature?

    Answer: B) F. Scott Fitzgerald

    F. Scott Fitzgerald authored The Great Gatsby, published in 1925, depicting the Jazz Age.

  6. Which novel is famous for the line 'Call me Ishmael'?

    Answer: Moby-Dick

    Herman Melville's 'Moby-Dick' (1851) opens with the famous line 'Call me Ishmael', spoken by the narrator of the whaling epic.

  7. Who is the author of 'To Kill a Mockingbird'?

    Answer: Harper Lee

    Harper Lee wrote the Pulitzer Prize-winning novel 'To Kill a Mockingbird' (1960).

  8. Which novel features the character Jay Gatsby?

    Answer: The Great Gatsby

    'The Great Gatsby' by F. Scott Fitzgerald, published in 1925, tells the story of the mysterious millionaire Jay Gatsby and his obsession with Daisy Buchanan. It is a defining novel of the Jazz Age.

  9. Who wrote 'The Old Man and the Sea'?

    Answer: Ernest Hemingway

    Ernest Hemingway published 'The Old Man and the Sea' in 1952, for which he won the Pulitzer Prize and later the Nobel Prize in Literature.

  10. Which 19th-century American poet authored "Leaves of Grass"?

    Answer: Walt Whitman

    Walt Whitman published "Leaves of Grass" in 1855.

  11. Which American poet wrote the long poem 'Song of Myself' in 'Leaves of Grass'?

    Answer: B) Walt Whitman

    Walt Whitman's 'Song of Myself' is a central poem in his 1855 collection Leaves of Grass.

  12. Who wrote the novel 'The Catcher in the Rye'?

    Answer: J.D. Salinger

    J.D. Salinger published 'The Catcher in the Rye' in 1951. The novel is narrated by the cynical and alienated teenager Holden Caulfield and has become a touchstone for its portrayal of teenage angst.

  13. In F. Scott Fitzgerald's 'The Great Gatsby', what is the first name of Gatsby?

    Answer: James

    Jay Gatsby's real name is revealed to be James Gatz, who reinvented himself as Jay Gatsby.

  14. Which 20th‑century American playwright authored the two‑part drama 'Angels in America'?

    Answer: Tony Kushner

    Kushner's play premiered in 1991 and explores the AIDS crisis and American politics.

  15. Which modernist author wrote the novel 'The Sound and the Fury' and is also known for his short story 'A Rose for Emily'?

    Answer: William Faulkner

    William Faulkner authored 'The Sound and the Fury' (1929) and the short story 'A Rose for Emily' (1930).
