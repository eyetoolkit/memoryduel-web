Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which is the tallest mountain on Earth?

    Answer: Mount Everest

    Mount Everest stands at 8,849 meters on the border of Nepal and China.

  2. In which country is Mount Kilimanjaro located?

    Answer: Tanzania

    Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa's highest peak at 5,895 meters, is located in northeastern Tanzania near the border with Kenya. It is a dormant stratovolcano and one of the Seven Summits.

  3. Which mountain range is home to Mount Everest?

    Answer: Himalayas

    Mount Everest, the highest peak on Earth at 8,849 meters, is located in the Himalayas on the border of Nepal and Tibet.

  4. Mount Everest lies on the border of which two countries?

    Answer: Nepal and China

    Mount Everest straddles the border between Nepal and the Tibet Autonomous Region of China.

  5. Which mountain range forms a natural border between France and Spain?

    Answer: Pyrenees

    The Pyrenees mountain range runs along the border between France and Spain, stretching about 430 km.

  6. Which mountain range separates Europe from Asia?

    Answer: Ural Mountains

    The Ural Mountains are traditionally considered the geographical boundary between Europe and Asia.

  7. The Andes Mountains run along which continent?

    Answer: South America

    The Andes are the longest continental mountain range, running along the western coast of South America.

  8. Which mountain range contains Mont Blanc?

    Answer: Alps

    Mont Blanc, at 4,810 m, is the highest peak in the Alps, located on the French-Italian border.

  9. Which plateau is often referred to as the 'Roof of the World'?

    Answer: Tibetan Plateau

    The Tibetan Plateau averages over 4,500 m in elevation and is commonly called the Roof of the World.

  10. The Ural Mountains traditionally form the boundary between which two continents?

    Answer: Europe and Asia

    The Ural Mountains run approximately north-south through western Russia and are the traditional boundary between Europe and Asia.

  11. Which mountain peak is the highest in the world?

    Answer: Mount Everest

    Mount Everest, at 8,849 m, stands on the border of Nepal and Tibet (China) and is the highest point on Earth.

  12. Which mountain range runs along the western coast of South America?

    Answer: Andes

    The Andes are the longest continental mountain range in the world, running along the western coast of South America through seven countries. The Rockies are in North America.

  13. Which is the largest desert in the world?

    Answer: Antarctic Desert

    Antarctica is the largest desert, covering about 14 million km² with extremely low precipitation.

  14. The Karakoram Range, home to K2, is primarily located in which region?

    Answer: India (Ladakh) and Pakistan

    The Karakoram Range spans northern India (Ladakh) and northern Pakistan, with K2 sitting on the Pakistan-China border. It is distinct from the Himalayas.

  15. The Hindu Kush mountain range spans which two countries?

    Answer: Afghanistan and Pakistan

    The Hindu Kush range stretches approximately 800 kilometers from central Afghanistan to northern Pakistan. It is a significant segment of the 'Pamir Knot,' a junction of several of Asia's great mountain ranges.
