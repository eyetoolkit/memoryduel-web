Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which card game often uses a standard 52-card deck and involves betting and bluffing?

    Answer: Poker

    Poker is a family of card games involving betting and individual play, typically played with a standard 52-card deck.

  2. In standard poker, what is the highest-ranking hand?

    Answer: Royal flush

    A royal flush (ace, king, queen, jack, ten, all of the same suit) is the highest possible hand in standard poker.

  3. Which board game requires players to 'bluff' about the cards in their hand to avoid losing rounds?

    Answer: Poker

    Poker (and bluffing variants like Liar's Poker) centers on deceiving opponents about hand strength, though poker is also a card game.

  4. In the card game Poker, which hand ranks higher: a Flush or a Straight?

    Answer: Flush

    In standard poker hand rankings, a Flush (five cards of the same suit) ranks higher than a Straight (five consecutive cards).

  5. In poker, what hand consists of five cards in numerical order but not of the same suit?

    Answer: Straight

    A straight is five sequential cards of mixed suits, while a flush is five cards of the same suit in any order.

  6. In poker, which hand beats a straight but loses to a flush?

    Answer: Full House

    A Full House (three of a kind plus a pair) ranks above a Flush in standard poker hand rankings.

  7. In the game 'Poker', what hand consists of three cards of the same rank?

    Answer: Three of a Kind

    Three of a Kind is a poker hand consisting of three cards with the same rank.

  8. In poker, what hand ranks immediately below a straight flush?

    Answer: Four of a kind

    The standard poker hand order is: royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair, high card.

  9. In poker, which hand ranks higher: a full house or a flush?

    Answer: Full house

    A full house (three of a kind plus a pair) outranks a flush (five cards of the same suit) in standard poker hand rankings.

  10. In the card game Poker, what is the name of the highest possible five-card hand?

    Answer: Royal Flush

    A Royal Flush (Ace, King, Queen, Jack, Ten of the same suit) is the unbeatable top hand in standard poker.

  11. In poker, what is the name of the hand consisting of five cards of the same suit, not in sequence?

    Answer: Flush

    A flush is five cards of the same suit that are not in consecutive order. A straight is five cards in sequence but of mixed suits.

  12. What is the maximum number of players allowed at a standard 'Texas Hold'em' poker table in a casino?

    Answer: 10

    Standard casino Texas Hold'em tables typically accommodate a maximum of 10 players.

  13. Which board game uses a 52-card deck and involves players betting on the strength of their hand?

    Answer: Poker

    Poker is a family of card games involving betting and individual play, using a standard 52-card deck.

  14. Which of these is NOT a standard phase in the popular board game 'Poker'?

    Answer: The Deal

    In Texas Hold'em Poker, the community cards are revealed in stages called the Flop, the Turn, and the River. 'The Deal' is the action of distributing cards, not a named betting phase.

  15. What is the maximum number of players that can participate in a standard game of 'Poker' at a single table in most casinos?

    Answer: 10

    Most casino poker tables accommodate a maximum of 10 players, though 9 is common for Texas Hold'em.
