Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Who directed the 'Harry Potter' film series (2001-2011)?

    Answer: Chris Columbus

    Chris Columbus directed the first two Harry Potter films ('Philosopher's Stone' and 'Chamber of Secrets').

  2. In the movie Titanic (1997), who directed the film?

    Answer: James Cameron

    James Cameron wrote and directed Titanic, which became the highest-grossing film of its time.

  3. Who directed the sci-fi film 'Inception' (2010)?

    Answer: Christopher Nolan

    Christopher Nolan wrote and directed 'Inception', a film about entering dreams within dreams.

  4. Who directed the 1994 classic film 'Pulp Fiction'?

    Answer: Quentin Tarantino

    Quentin Tarantino is famous for his distinct dialogue-driven style and directed 'Pulp Fiction', which won the Palme d'Or.

  5. Which 1975 Steven Spielberg film is credited with inventing the summer blockbuster?

    Answer: Jaws

    Jaws (1975) is widely credited with creating the summer blockbuster phenomenon. Its wide-release marketing strategy and record-breaking box office performance fundamentally changed how Hollywood distributed and marketed films.

  6. Who directed the animated film 'Finding Nemo' (2003)?

    Answer: Andrew Stanton

    Andrew Stanton directed 'Finding Nemo' for Pixar; he also voiced the character Crush the sea turtle.

  7. Which 1994 Quentin Tarantino film features interweaving stories of criminals in Los Angeles and is known for its nonlinear narrative?

    Answer: Pulp Fiction

    Pulp Fiction, starring John Travolta, Samuel L. Jackson, and Uma Thurman, is celebrated for its dialogue, structure, and influence on 1990s cinema.

  8. Which director made the 1972 classic 'The Godfather', starring Marlon Brando and Al Pacino?

    Answer: Francis Ford Coppola

    Francis Ford Coppola directed 'The Godfather', widely regarded as one of the greatest films ever made.

  9. Which animated film studio produced 'Spirited Away' (2001)?

    Answer: Studio Ghibli

    Spirited Away, directed by Hayao Miyazaki, was produced by Japan's Studio Ghibli and won the Oscar for Best Animated Feature.

  10. Which director is famous for the 'Lord of the Rings' film trilogy?

    Answer: Peter Jackson

    Peter Jackson directed the 'Lord of the Rings' trilogy, winning multiple Academy Awards.

  11. Which director has won the Academy Award for Best Director in consecutive years?

    Answer: Alejandro G. Iñárritu

    Alejandro G. Iñárritu won Best Director for Birdman (2014) and The Revenant (2015).

  12. Who directed the 1960 psychological horror film 'Psycho'?

    Answer: Alfred Hitchcock

    Alfred Hitchcock, known as the 'Master of Suspense', shocked audiences with 'Psycho' and its famous shower scene.

  13. Which director helmed the 1993 film 'Schindler's List'?

    Answer: Steven Spielberg

    Steven Spielberg directed 'Schindler's List', which won seven Academy Awards including Best Picture and Best Director.

  14. Which Japanese film director is known for works such as 'Seven Samurai', 'Rashomon', and 'Ikiru'?

    Answer: Akira Kurosawa

    Akira Kurosawa is one of the most influential filmmakers in cinema history, known globally for 'Seven Samurai' and 'Rashomon'.

  15. Which director is known for the 'Three Colors' trilogy (Blue, White, Red)?

    Answer: Krzysztof Kieślowski

    Polish director Krzysztof Kieślowski created the acclaimed Three Colors trilogy, exploring the French tricolor ideals of liberty, equality, and fraternity.
