Answer key

All thirteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which event marked the beginning of World War I?

    Answer: The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand

    The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary in Sarajevo on June 28, 1914, triggered a chain of events leading to World War I.

  2. Which war was fought between the Allied Powers and the Central Powers from 1914 to 1918?

    Answer: World War I

    World War I (1914–1918) pitted the Allies (Britain, France, Russia, later the US) against the Central Powers (Germany, Austria-Hungary, Ottoman Empire).

  3. The Treaty of Versailles (1919) primarily dealt with the aftermath of:

    Answer: World War I

    The Treaty of Versailles formally ended World War I, imposing territorial losses and heavy reparations on Germany, conditions many historians link to the rise of World War II.

  4. Which war is often called the 'Great War' before it was renamed World War I?

    Answer: World War I

    World War I (1914–1918) was originally called 'The Great War' before being renamed after WWII.

  5. Which event is considered the immediate trigger for the outbreak of World War I?

    Answer: Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand

    The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria in Sarajevo in 1914 set off a chain of events leading to WWI.

  6. Which event is widely considered the spark that triggered World War I?

    Answer: Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand

    The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria in Sarajevo on June 28, 1914, set off a chain of alliances that led to World War I.

  7. The Treaty of Versailles, which ended World War I, was signed in which year?

    Answer: 1919

    The Treaty of Versailles was signed on June 28, 1919, formally ending the state of war between Germany and the Allied Powers.

  8. Which treaty, signed in 1919, officially ended World War I and established the League of Nations?

    Answer: Treaty of Versailles

    The Treaty of Versailles was signed on June 28, 1919, formally ending the state of war between Germany and the Allied Powers. It imposed heavy reparations on Germany and created the League of Nations.

  9. The Treaty of Versailles, signed in 1919, officially ended which conflict?

    Answer: World War I

    The Treaty of Versailles was the peace treaty signed in 1919 that officially ended World War I between the Allied Powers and Germany.

  10. Which event is commonly considered the immediate cause of World War I?

    Answer: The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand

    The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria in 1914 triggered a chain of alliances that led to WWI.

  11. Which war was formally ended by the Treaty of Versailles in 1919?

    Answer: World War I

    The Treaty of Versailles officially ended World War I between Germany and the Allied Powers.

  12. Which event is often considered the start of World War I?

    Answer: Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand

    The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria in Sarajevo in 1914 triggered a chain of events leading to WWI.

  13. Which treaty, signed in 1919, officially ended World War I and imposed heavy reparations on Germany?

    Answer: Treaty of Versailles

    The Treaty of Versailles was signed in 1919, ending World War I and imposing significant territorial losses and reparations on Germany.
