Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Who painted the Mona Lisa?

    Answer: Leonardo da Vinci

    The Mona Lisa is a half-length portrait painting by the Italian Renaissance artist Leonardo da Vinci, considered an archetypal masterpiece.

  2. What is the name of the famous painting by Edvard Munch that shows a figure screaming?

    Answer: The Scream

    The Scream was painted by Norwegian artist Edvard Munch in 1893. It is one of the most recognizable images in modern art.

  3. Which painter is famous for cutting off part of his own ear?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh famously cut off part of his left ear in December 1888 while living in Arles, France.

  4. Which famous painting features a woman with an enigmatic smile?

    Answer: Mona Lisa

    The Mona Lisa by Leonardo da Vinci is renowned for the subject's mysterious and enigmatic smile.

  5. Which artist is famous for painting 'The Persistence of Memory' with melting clocks?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí painted 'The Persistence of Memory' in 1931, an iconic work of surrealism featuring melting clocks.

  6. Which artist painted 'Guernica', depicting the horrors of war?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in 1937 as a response to the bombing of the town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War.

  7. Which famous artist is known for painting water lilies in a series of Impressionist works?

    Answer: Claude Monet

    Claude Monet is famous for his series of paintings depicting his flower garden and its water lilies at Giverny.

  8. Which artist is known for using a distinctive 'drip' painting technique?

    Answer: Jackson Pollock

    Jackson Pollock became famous for his 'drip paintings' of the late 1940s and 1950s, created by dripping or pouring paint onto a canvas laid on the floor.

  9. Which artist painted the ceiling of the Sistine Chapel in Vatican City?

    Answer: Michelangelo

    Michelangelo painted the Sistine Chapel ceiling between 1508 and 1512, commissioned by Pope Julius II. It includes the iconic 'Creation of Adam' scene.

  10. Which artist painted 'Les Demoiselles d'Avignon', a work that helped launch the Cubist movement?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted Les Demoiselles d'Avignon in 1907, marking a radical break from traditional Western art.

  11. Which painting by Johannes Vermeer depicts a girl wearing a pearl earring?

    Answer: Girl with a Pearl Earring

    'Girl with a Pearl Earring' is an oil painting by Johannes Vermeer, created around 1665, often called the 'Mona Lisa of the North.'

  12. Which artist sculpted 'The Thinker', originally part of 'The Gates of Hell'?

    Answer: Auguste Rodin

    Auguste Rodin created The Thinker in 1904, originally as part of a larger sculptural group called The Gates of Hell, inspired by Dante's Divine Comedy. It has become a universal symbol of philosophy and contemplation.

  13. Which artist painted The Garden of Earthly Delights, a famous triptych?

    Answer: Hieronymus Bosch

    Hieronymus Bosch painted The Garden of Earthly Delights (c. 1490–1510), a surreal and intricate triptych now in the Prado Museum.

  14. Which artist created the 'Campbell's Soup Cans' artwork?

    Answer: Andy Warhol

    Andy Warhol created 32 Campbell's Soup Cans in 1962, one for each variety of soup the company sold at the time.

  15. Which painter was a leading figure of the Dutch Golden Age and is famous for 'Girl with a Pearl Earring'?

    Answer: Johannes Vermeer

    Johannes Vermeer, active in Delft during the Dutch Golden Age, painted 'Girl with a Pearl Earring' around 1665. Vermeer is renowned for his masterful use of light and his intimate domestic scenes, though only about 34-36 of his paintings survive.
