Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. How many continents are there on Earth?

    Answer: 7

    There are 7 continents: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Australia, Europe, North America, and South America.

  2. How many bones are in the adult human body?

    Answer: 206

    Adults have 206 bones. Babies are born with about 270, but many fuse together during growth.

  3. How many colors are in a traditional rainbow?

    Answer: 7

    The traditional rainbow has 7 colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet (ROYGBIV).

  4. On average, how many steps does a typical adult take each day?

    Answer: 7,500

    Studies suggest the average adult walks about 7,500 steps per day.

  5. How many hearts does an octopus have?

    Answer: 3

    An octopus possesses three hearts: two pump blood to the gills, and one pumps it to the body.

  6. Which human organ can regenerate itself if part of it is removed?

    Answer: Liver

    The liver is the only internal organ capable of regenerating lost tissue, which is why living donor liver transplants are possible.

  7. Which bird is known for its ability to mimic human speech?

    Answer: Parrot

    Parrots, especially species like the African Grey, are famous for their ability to mimic sounds including human speech.

  8. Which of these everyday items is the most numerous manufactured object on Earth (by units produced annually)?

    Answer: Ballpoint pens

    An estimated 100 billion ballpoint pens are manufactured worldwide each year, making them one of the most mass-produced objects in history.

  9. Which language is the most spoken in the world by total number of speakers?

    Answer: A. English

    English has the largest combined total of native and second‑language speakers worldwide.

  10. Which everyday item was originally invented as a wallpaper cleaner?

    Answer: Play-Doh

    Play-Doh was originally marketed as a wallpaper cleaner in the 1930s. It was only later repurposed as a children's toy when teachers noticed its educational value.

  11. What percentage of the Earth's water is freshwater?

    Answer: About 2.5%

    Only about 2.5% of Earth's water is freshwater, and most of that is locked in glaciers and ice caps. Less than 1% is easily accessible in lakes, rivers, and underground sources for human use.

  12. How many time zones does China officially use?

    Answer: One

    Despite spanning five geographic time zones, China officially uses a single time zone (Beijing Time) nationwide.

  13. Which country produces the most coffee in the world?

    Answer: Brazil

    Brazil is the world's largest coffee producer, responsible for roughly one-third of global coffee production.

  14. What is the most abundant gas in Earth's atmosphere?

    Answer: Nitrogen

    Nitrogen makes up about 78% of Earth's atmosphere, with oxygen comprising about 21%.

  15. What is the only prime number that is also even?

    Answer: Two

    Two is the only even prime number. All other even numbers are divisible by 2, making them composite rather than prime.
