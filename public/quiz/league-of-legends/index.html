Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In esports, which team won the first League of Legends World Championship in 2011?

    Answer: Fnatic

    Fnatic won the inaugural League of Legends World Championship in June 2011.

  2. Which esports title is known for its 'Summoner's Rift' map?

    Answer: League of Legends

    Summoner's Rift is the primary competitive map in League of Legends, the world's most popular MOBA (Multiplayer Online Battle Arena) game.

  3. In the popular esports game 'League of Legends', what is the name of the company that developed it?

    Answer: Riot Games

    Riot Games is the developer and publisher of League of Legends, one of the world's most popular MOBA games.

  4. The esports team 'T1' is most famous for its dominance in which video game, with legendary player Faker?

    Answer: League of Legends

    T1, formerly SK Telecom T1, is a South Korean esports organization renowned for its League of Legends team, which includes the iconic player Lee 'Faker' Sang-hyeok.

  5. Which sport is considered the most-watched esports discipline globally by viewership?

    Answer: League of Legends

    League of Legends consistently draws the largest global viewership of any esports title, especially for its annual World Championship.

  6. Which esports organization is widely known by its Korean abbreviation 'T1' and has a legendary partnership with Faker?

    Answer: SK Telecom T1

    SK Telecom T1 (now simply T1) is a South Korean esports organization famous for its League of Legends team, which features the most decorated player, Faker.

  7. Which esports title is known for its professional league featuring teams named T1, Cloud9, and G2 Esports competing in a 5v5 MOBA format?

    Answer: League of Legends

    League of Legends is a popular MOBA (Multiplayer Online Battle Arena) where teams of five compete, and T1, Cloud9, and G2 are prominent professional organizations.

  8. Which esports organization is known for its yellow and black branding and is often associated with South Korean League of Legends?

    Answer: T1

    T1 (formerly SK Telecom T1) is a legendary South Korean esports organization, especially dominant in League of Legends.

  9. Which esports tournament is widely considered the most prestigious event in professional 'League of Legends'?

    Answer: The World Championship

    The League of Legends World Championship (often called 'Worlds') is the premier annual tournament for the game, hosted by Riot Games.

  10. What popular esports title is developed by Riot Games and features a MOBA (Multiplayer Online Battle Arena) format?

    Answer: League of Legends

    League of Legends, developed by Riot Games, is one of the most-played MOBA games globally.

  11. In League of Legends, what is the name of the currency used to buy items during a match?

    Answer: Gold

    Gold is the primary in-game currency in MOBA games like League of Legends, earned by killing minions and champions to buy gear.

  12. In competitive League of Legends, how many players are on each team?

    Answer: 5

    League of Legends features 5v5 gameplay, with each team controlling a champion in a battle over the map.

  13. In the video game 'League of Legends', which role is primarily responsible for supporting the 'Attack Damage Carry' (ADC) in the bottom lane?

    Answer: Support

    The Support role protects and assists the ADC (who scales well into the late game) during the laning phase and team fights.

  14. Which country hosted the 2023 League of Legends World Championship Finals, where T1 defeated WBG?

    Answer: China

    The 2023 League of Legends World Championship was held in South Korea, with the finals taking place at the Gocheok Sky Dome in Seoul, where T1 won.

  15. Which esports player, known as 'Faker', is widely regarded as the greatest League of Legends player of all time and plays for T1?

    Answer: Lee 'Faker' Sang-hyeok

    Lee 'Faker' Sang-hyeok is a South Korean mid-laner who plays for T1 and is widely considered the greatest LoL player in history, winning multiple World Championships.
