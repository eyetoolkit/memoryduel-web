Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Who painted the portrait "Girl with a Pearl Earring"?

    Answer: Johannes Vermeer

    The portrait was created by Dutch Golden Age painter Johannes Vermeer around 1665.

  2. Which of these is a famous painting by Pablo Picasso?

    Answer: Guernica

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in 1937 as a response to the bombing of the Basque town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War.

  3. Which artist is known for painting the Mona Lisa?

    Answer: Leonardo da Vinci

    Leonardo da Vinci painted the Mona Lisa, which is now displayed in the Louvre Museum in Paris.

  4. Which famous artwork features a woman with an enigmatic smile?

    Answer: Mona Lisa

    The Mona Lisa by Leonardo da Vinci is renowned for the subject's mysterious smile, which has fascinated viewers for centuries.

  5. Which Spanish painter painted 'Las Meninas'?

    Answer: Diego Velázquez

    Diego Velázquez created the iconic Baroque painting 'Las Meninas'.

  6. Which Spanish painter is associated with the surrealist work 'The Persistence of Memory', featuring melting clocks?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí painted 'The Persistence of Memory' in 1931, one of the most iconic Surrealist paintings.

  7. Which art movement did Claude Monet help found?

    Answer: Impressionism

    Claude Monet was a founder of French Impressionist painting, known for his series paintings such as the Water Lilies.

  8. Which American painter is associated with the 'American Gothic' painting?

    Answer: Grant Wood

    Grant Wood painted 'American Gothic', depicting a farmer and his daughter.

  9. Which 19th‑century French painter is known for the Realist work 'The Gleaners'?

    Answer: Jean‑François Millet

    Jean‑François Millet's 1857 painting 'The Gleaners' depicts rural laborers and epitomizes Realist social commentary.

  10. Which painting by Édouard Manet caused a scandal at the 1863 Salon des Refusés for its unflinching realism?

    Answer: Le Déjeuner sur l'herbe

    Le Déjeuner sur l'herbe (The Luncheon on the Grass) shocked 19th-century audiences with a nude woman picnicking with clothed men. (Note: 'Olympia' also caused scandal in 1865, but Le Déjeuner sur l'herbe is the 1863 Salon des Refusés work.)

  11. Which artist painted 'The Garden of Earthly Delights,' a triptych famous for its surreal and crowded imagery?

    Answer: Hieronymus Bosch

    Hieronymus Bosch painted 'The Garden of Earthly Delights' between 1490 and 1510. The triptych is filled with fantastical, symbolic imagery and remains one of the most studied works of the Northern Renaissance.

  12. Who painted 'Campbell's Soup Cans'?

    Answer: C: Andy Warhol

    Warhol's 1962 series of thirty‑two canvases each depicting a variety of Campbell's soup flavors.

  13. Which Japanese ukiyo‑e artist created the woodblock print series 'Thirty‑Six Views of Mount Fuji', including 'The Great Wave off Kanagawa'?

    Answer: Katsushika Hokusai

    Katsushika Hokusai produced the famous series in the early 1830s.

  14. Which painter of 'Les Demoiselles d'Avignon' is widely regarded as one of the most influential artists of the 20th century?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Les Demoiselles d'Avignon' in 1907. The work is considered a foundational piece of Cubism and a major turning point in the history of modern art.

  15. Which 17th‑century Dutch painter is known for the domestic interior painting 'The Milkmaid'?

    Answer: Johannes Vermeer

    Vermeer painted The Milkmaid about 1658‑1661, notable for its depiction of light and domestic space.
