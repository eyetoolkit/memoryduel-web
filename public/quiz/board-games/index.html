Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the objective of the board game 'Risk'?

    Answer: Conquering territories and eliminating opponents

    Risk is a strategy game focused on world domination by capturing territories and defeating other players.

  2. What color are the 'Go' stones typically used by the first player (Black) in a standard game?

    Answer: Black

    In Go, the first player traditionally plays with black stones, while the second player uses white.

  3. In Monopoly, what is the name of the prison space?

    Answer: Jail

    The Jail square is where players are sent when they land on 'Go to Jail' or roll three doubles.

  4. In chess, what is it called when a player wins in just two moves?

    Answer: Fool's mate

    Fool's mate is the fastest possible checkmate, occurring after just two moves by each side.

  5. In the game Monopoly, what color are the properties on Boardwalk and Park Place?

    Answer: Dark Blue

    Boardwalk and Park Place are the most expensive properties in Monopoly and belong to the dark blue color group.

  6. How many squares are on a standard Scrabble board?

    Answer: 225

    A standard Scrabble board consists of a 15x15 grid, totaling 225 squares.

  7. In the game of Monopoly, what do players collect when they pass 'Go'?

    Answer: Money

    Players collect $200 (or equivalent in local currency) when they pass 'Go' in Monopoly.

  8. In the game of Go, what is the goal of the game?

    Answer: Score the most points by surrounding territory

    In Go, players take turns placing black or white stones on a grid, aiming to surround more territory than their opponent.

  9. In the video game chess variant or digital adaptations, which piece is often considered the most powerful in standard chess?

    Answer: Queen

    In chess, the Queen combines the movement abilities of both the rook and the bishop, making it the most powerful piece on the board. It can move any number of squares vertically, horizontally, or diagonally.

  10. What is the total number of unique tiles in the standard Mahjong set used in most international competitions?

    Answer: 144

    A standard Mahjong set contains 144 tiles, including suits, honor tiles, flowers, and seasons.

  11. In the game of Chess, which piece can move any number of squares in any direction, but cannot jump over other pieces?

    Answer: Queen

    The Queen combines the movement of the Rook and Bishop, moving any number of squares horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, but cannot jump over pieces.

  12. Which Japanese company is credited with creating the modern trading card game (TCG) genre with the launch of Magic: The Gathering in 1993?

    Answer: Wizards of the Coast

    Wizards of the Coast created Magic: The Gathering, the first modern trading card game, which revolutionized tabletop gaming and spawned an entire genre.

  13. Which of these board games is designed for exactly two players and involves a grid where each player tries to surround the other's stones?

    Answer: Go

    Go (also known as Weiqi or Baduk) is a two-player strategy board game originating from ancient China. Players place black and white stones on a grid, aiming to surround territory and capture opponent stones.

  14. In chess, what is the term for a move that simultaneously checks the opponent's king and attacks another piece?

    Answer: Fork

    A fork is a tactic where a single piece attacks two or more enemy pieces simultaneously, often including the king.

  15. Which chess opening is characterized by the moves 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5?

    Answer: Ruy López (Spanish Game)

    The Ruy López, also known as the Spanish Game, begins with those three moves and is one of the oldest and most popular chess openings.
