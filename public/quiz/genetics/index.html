Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which molecule carries genetic information in most organisms?

    Answer: DNA

    DNA stores the genetic blueprint in the form of a double helix.

  2. What is the process called when animals and plants naturally adapt to their environment over many generations?

    Answer: Evolution

    Evolution is the gradual process by which populations of living organisms change over time through natural selection and genetic variation.

  3. In genetics, what term describes the observable physical characteristics of an organism?

    Answer: Phenotype

    Phenotype refers to the observable traits (e.g., eye color). Genotype is the genetic makeup, an allele is a variant form of a gene, and chromosomes are DNA structures.

  4. What is the process by which a cell divides to produce two identical daughter cells?

    Answer: Mitosis

    Mitosis produces two genetically identical daughter cells, each with the same chromosome number as the parent cell.

  5. In genetics, what does DNA stand for?

    Answer: Deoxyribonucleic Acid

    DNA stands for Deoxyribonucleic Acid, the molecule that carries genetic information in living organisms.

  6. Which organelle contains DNA in eukaryotic cells?

    Answer: Nucleus

    The nucleus houses most of the cell's DNA; mitochondria and chloroplasts also have their own DNA.

  7. What is the powerhouse of the human body's genetic information?

    Answer: Nucleus

    The nucleus contains DNA, the body's genetic blueprint.

  8. What is the term for the diversity of genes in a population?

    Answer: Genetic diversity

    Genetic diversity refers to the total variety of genes within a species, crucial for adaptation.

  9. What is the term for an animal or plant that has been genetically modified to express a trait from another species?

    Answer: Transgenic Organism

    A transgenic organism contains DNA from a different species.

  10. In the lac operon of E. coli, the presence of lactose leads to the binding of the lac repressor protein to the operator, resulting in which outcome?

    Answer: Activation of transcription

    Lactose binding inactivates the repressor, allowing RNA polymerase to initiate transcription of the lac genes.

  11. What is the primary function of the enzyme telomerase?

    Answer: Adding repetitive nucleotide sequences to chromosome ends

    Telomerase adds telomere repeats (TTAGGG in humans) to chromosome ends, preventing them from shortening during cell division and playing a key role in aging and cancer.

  12. Which term describes the variety of genetic information within a species?

    Answer: Genetic diversity

    Genetic diversity is the total number of genetic characteristics in the genetic makeup of a species, which serves as a way for populations to adapt to changing environments.

  13. In eukaryotic cells, which molecular complex catalyzes the removal of introns from pre‑mRNA?

    Answer: Spliceosome

    The spliceosome, a ribonucleoprotein complex, performs splicing by excising introns from pre‑mRNA.

  14. What is the term for the rapid evolution of traits in response to changes in the environment, often seen in invasive species or pathogens?

    Answer: Rapid Evolution

    Rapid evolution refers to evolutionary changes that occur over a relatively short period, sometimes observable in real-time, as species adapt to new environmental pressures.

  15. Which enzyme unwinds the DNA double helix during replication?

    Answer: Helicase

    Helicase breaks the hydrogen bonds between base pairs, unwinding the double helix into single strands.
