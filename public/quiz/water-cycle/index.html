Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the process by which water vapor becomes liquid water in the atmosphere?

    Answer: Condensation

    Condensation occurs when water vapor cools and changes into liquid droplets, forming clouds and dew.

  2. What is the term for the process by which water changes from a liquid to a gas in the environment?

    Answer: Evaporation

    Evaporation describes the phase change of liquid water into water vapor due to heat energy.

  3. What is the process by which water moves from the ground and plants back into the atmosphere?

    Answer: Transpiration

    Transpiration is the process where water is carried through plants from roots to small pores on the underside of leaves, where it changes to vapor and is released.

  4. What is the term for the process by which water evaporates from the surface of oceans, lakes, and soil into the atmosphere?

    Answer: Evaporation

    Evaporation is the phase change from liquid to gas, moving water into the atmosphere.

  5. What is the primary source of water vapor in the atmosphere?

    Answer: B) Ocean evaporation

    Evaporation from the ocean surface is the largest contributor to atmospheric water vapor.

  6. Which of these processes contributes to the water cycle?

    Answer: Condensation

    Condensation turns water vapor into liquid, forming clouds and precipitation.

  7. Which of the following processes is responsible for the formation of clouds?

    Answer: Evaporation and condensation

    Water evaporates, rises, cools, and condenses into tiny droplets that form clouds.

  8. What percentage of the Earth's water is freshwater, and of that, what portion is locked in glaciers and ice caps?

    Answer: About 3% is freshwater; roughly 69% of it is in ice

    Only about 3% of Earth's water is freshwater, and approximately 69% of that freshwater is trapped in glaciers and ice caps, leaving relatively little readily accessible for human use.

  9. What is the process called when water evaporates, rises, cools, and falls back to Earth as rain?

    Answer: The Water Cycle

    The water cycle describes the continuous movement of water on, above, and below the surface of the Earth.

  10. What is the name of the process where plants lose water vapor through their leaves?

    Answer: Transpiration

    Transpiration is the release of water vapor from plant leaves through small pores called stomata.

  11. What is the name of the process by which water moves from the Earth's surface into the atmosphere as a gas?

    Answer: Transpiration

    Transpiration is the process where plants release water vapor from their leaves into the atmosphere. It is a major component of the water cycle, especially from forests.

  12. What is the main driver of the water cycle?

    Answer: The Sun

    Solar energy heats water, causing evaporation, which powers the movement of water through the atmosphere and back to Earth.

  13. What is the process called when water vapor cools and forms clouds?

    Answer: Condensation

    Condensation is the process where water vapor cools down and changes into liquid water droplets, forming clouds.

  14. What is the term for the natural process of water moving from the earth to the atmosphere and back again?

    Answer: Water Cycle

    The Water Cycle (or Hydrologic Cycle) describes the continuous movement of water through evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection.

  15. What is the primary mechanism by which plants regulate water loss through their leaves?

    Answer: Transpiration

    Transpiration is the process by which water is drawn up from the roots and released as vapor through stomata in the leaves, helping regulate the plant's water balance.
