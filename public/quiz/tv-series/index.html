Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which TV series is set in the fictional country of Westeros?

    Answer: Game of Thrones

    'Game of Thrones' is the epic fantasy series primarily set on the continent of Westeros, focusing on the power struggles among noble families.

  2. Which actor played the protagonist in the TV series "Breaking Bad"?

    Answer: Bryan Cranston

    Bryan Cranston portrayed Walter White, the main protagonist of Breaking Bad.

  3. In which country was the TV series 'Money Heist' (La Casa de Papel) originally produced?

    Answer: Spain

    Money Heist is a Spanish heist crime drama created by Álex Pina. It became a global Netflix sensation after being picked up for international distribution.

  4. Which TV series is known for the quote 'Winter is coming'?

    Answer: Game of Thrones

    'Winter is coming' is the motto of House Stark in 'Game of Thrones'.

  5. In the TV series 'Friends', which city do the main characters live in?

    Answer: New York

    'Friends' is set in New York City, primarily in Manhattan.

  6. In which TV series does the character Tyrion Lannister appear?

    Answer: Game of Thrones

    Tyrion Lannister is a central character in Game of Thrones (2011-2019), portrayed by Peter Dinklage. The character is known for his wit, intelligence, and complex moral journey throughout the series.

  7. Which TV series features characters named Walter White and Jesse Pinkman?

    Answer: Breaking Bad

    Breaking Bad (2008–2013) follows chemistry teacher Walter White and his former student Jesse Pinkman.

  8. Which TV series features the famous house sigil of a direwolf on a wintery shield?

    Answer: Game of Thrones

    House Stark, one of the Great Houses of Westeros in Game of Thrones, uses the direwolf as its sigil. The motto 'Winter is Coming' is associated with this house.

  9. In which TV series did Bryan Cranston play the chemistry teacher turned drug lord Walter White?

    Answer: Breaking Bad

    Bryan Cranston starred as Walter White in Breaking Bad, which aired from 2008 to 2013 on AMC.

  10. In the TV series 'Game of Thrones', which family's sigil is a roaring lion?

    Answer: Lannister

    House Lannister's sigil is a golden lion on a crimson field, and their words are 'Hear Me Roar!' (though 'A Lannister always pays his debts' is more famous).

  11. Which TV series, set in a medieval fantasy world, was based on George R.R. Martin's book series 'A Song of Ice and Fire'?

    Answer: Game of Thrones

    'Game of Thrones' aired on HBO from 2011 to 2019 and became a global cultural phenomenon.

  12. Which British actor is known for playing the 13th incarnation of the Doctor in 'Doctor Who' and the Tenth Doctor in the same series?

    Answer: David Tennant

    David Tennant played the Tenth Doctor (2005–2010) and later returned as the Fourteenth Doctor in 2023, making him unique for incarnating two numbered Doctors in the long-running BBC series.

  13. Which TV series, created by David Chase, is often regarded as one of the greatest shows of all time and follows Tony Soprano?

    Answer: The Sopranos

    David Chase created 'The Sopranos' (1999–2007), widely considered a landmark in television.

  14. Which drama series holds the record for the most Primetime Emmy Awards won by a drama?

    Answer: Game of Thrones

    Game of Thrones has won 12 Primetime Emmy Awards for drama, the most for that category.

  15. Which streaming service produced the historical drama series 'Bridgerton'?

    Answer: Netflix

    Netflix produced 'Bridgerton,' based on Julia Quinn's novels, which became one of Netflix's most-watched series and is created by Chris Van Dusen with Shonda Rhimes as executive producer.
