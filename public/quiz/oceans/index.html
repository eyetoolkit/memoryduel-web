Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the primary cause of ocean acidification?

    Answer: Increased CO2 absorption

    Oceans absorb about 30% of the CO2 released into the atmosphere, which dissolves to form carbonic acid, lowering the pH of seawater.

  2. What is the largest ocean on Earth?

    Answer: Pacific

    The Pacific Ocean covers about 63 million square miles, more than any other ocean.

  3. What is the primary source of Earth's oxygen?

    Answer: Ocean phytoplankton

    Marine phytoplankton—microscopic ocean organisms—produce roughly half of Earth's oxygen through photosynthesis.

  4. Which ocean is the largest?

    Answer: Pacific

    The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean, covering more area than all landmasses combined.

  5. Which of these countries is the only one in the world to border both the Atlantic and Indian Oceans?

    Answer: South Africa

    South Africa's coastline spans from the Atlantic Ocean on its west coast to the Indian Ocean on its east coast, making it the only country to border both.

  6. Which ocean lies between Africa and Australia?

    Answer: Indian Ocean

    The Indian Ocean lies between the eastern coast of Africa and the western coast of Australia.

  7. Which of the following is a mammal?

    Answer: Dolphin

    Dolphins are marine mammals. They breathe air, are warm-blooded, and nurse their young with milk.

  8. Which of the following is the world's largest coral reef system?

    Answer: Great Barrier Reef

    Australia's Great Barrier Reef stretches over 2,300 km and is the largest coral reef system in the world.

  9. Which type of ecosystem is found in the intertidal zone where land meets the ocean?

    Answer: Rocky shore

    Rocky shores are the intertidal ecosystems exposed to both tidal and atmospheric conditions.

  10. Which of these is a major cause of coral bleaching?

    Answer: Rising sea temperatures

    Rising sea temperatures cause corals to expel their symbiotic algae, leading to coral bleaching.

  11. Which of these processes removes carbon dioxide from the atmosphere naturally?

    Answer: Ocean absorption

    The oceans act as a major carbon sink, absorbing significant amounts of atmospheric CO2 through physical and biological processes.

  12. Which term describes the gradual increase in the acidity of ocean water due to increased atmospheric CO2?

    Answer: Ocean Acidification

    Ocean acidification occurs when CO2 dissolves in seawater, forming carbonic acid and lowering pH, threatening shell-forming organisms.

  13. Which ocean current is known as the 'global conveyor belt'?

    Answer: Thermohaline Circulation

    Thermohaline circulation, driven by temperature and salinity differences, moves water throughout the world's oceans in a conveyor-like pattern.

  14. Which sea has no coastline?

    Answer: Sargasso Sea

    The Sargasso Sea, in the North Atlantic, is bounded by ocean currents rather than land, making it the only sea without a coastline.

  15. The Amazon River empties into which ocean?

    Answer: Atlantic Ocean

    The Amazon River flows eastward across South America and empties into the Atlantic Ocean in Brazil.
