Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which river, the longest in the world, flows through the Sahara Desert and empties into the Mediterranean Sea?

    Answer: Nile River

    The Nile is generally recognized as the longest river in the world, stretching approximately 6,650 km through northeastern Africa before reaching the Mediterranean Sea in Egypt. The Amazon is often cited as the largest by volume, but the Nile holds the length record.

  2. Which river is central to the rise of ancient Egyptian civilization?

    Answer: The Nile

    Ancient Egypt flourished along the Nile River, whose annual floods deposited fertile silt that supported agriculture.

  3. Which river, the longest in Africa, flows through eleven countries including Egypt, Sudan, and Uganda?

    Answer: Nile River

    The Nile is widely recognized as the longest river in the world and flows through 11 countries in northeastern Africa before emptying into the Mediterranean Sea in Egypt.

  4. The Mekong River flows through six countries. Which capital city, known for its temples, sits directly on its banks?

    Answer: Vientiane

    Vientiane, the capital of Laos, is situated on the banks of the Mekong River, which serves as a major part of the country's border with Thailand.

  5. The Congo River is the second-longest river in Africa. In which country is its mouth located?

    Answer: Republic of the Congo

    The Congo River empties into the Atlantic Ocean at the border between the Democratic Republic of the Congo and the Republic of the Congo, with its mouth primarily in the Republic of the Congo.

  6. After the Amazon, which river is the second longest in South America?

    Answer: Paraná

    The Paraná River is about 4,880 km long, making it the second longest river on the continent after the Amazon.

  7. Which is the world's longest river system by combined length of its tributaries?

    Answer: Amazon River

    The Amazon River system is considered the world's longest river system when measured by the combined length of its main stem and tributaries, extending approximately 6,992 kilometers.

  8. Which of the following rivers has the largest drainage basin?

    Answer: Amazon

    The Amazon Basin covers roughly 7 million km², the largest contiguous drainage system on Earth.

  9. Which river forms part of the border between the United States and Mexico, flowing into the Gulf of Mexico?

    Answer: Rio Grande

    The Rio Grande (Río Bravo del Norte in Mexico) forms much of the US–Mexico border, running from the San Juan Mountains in Colorado to the Gulf of Mexico.

  10. The Ganges River is sacred in which religion and flows primarily through which country?

    Answer: Hinduism and India

    The Ganges is considered sacred in Hinduism, and the majority of its course flows through India. Millions of Hindus perform rituals along its banks.

  11. Which river is the most voluminous in the world by average discharge?

    Answer: Amazon

    The Amazon discharges roughly 209,000 m³/s of water, more than the next seven largest rivers combined.

  12. The Ganges, Indus, and Brahmaputra rivers all drain into which body of water?

    Answer: Bay of Bengal

    All three major South Asian rivers empty into the Bay of Bengal through vast deltas, notably the Ganges-Brahmaputra delta.

  13. Which is the world's deepest lake?

    Answer: Lake Baikal

    Lake Baikal in Siberia reaches a maximum depth of 1,642 meters, holds about 20% of the world's unfrozen surface freshwater, and is also the oldest lake.

  14. Which European river, flowing through multiple countries, is known for the Blue Danube?

    Answer: Danube

    The Danube flows through ten countries in Europe, second only to the Volga in length on the continent.

  15. Which African country is the source of the Nile River?

    Answer: Tanzania

    The source of the Nile is generally considered to be Lake Victoria in Tanzania (though the lake is shared with Uganda and Kenya), specifically at Jinja, Uganda where the White Nile begins.
