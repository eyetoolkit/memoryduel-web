Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. The ancient civilization of Mesopotamia was located between which two rivers?

    Answer: Tigris and Euphrates

    Mesopotamia, often called the 'cradle of civilization', was located in the fertile land between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers in modern-day Iraq.

  2. Which continent was the last to be widely colonized by European powers?

    Answer: Oceania

    While colonization occurred globally, large parts of Oceania (including Australia, New Zealand, and Pacific islands) were colonized primarily in the 18th and 19th centuries, making it one of the last major regions to be brought under European control.

  3. In which modern country did the ancient Roman Empire's capital city lie?

    Answer: Italy

    Rome, the capital of the Roman Empire, is located in modern-day Italy, on the Italian Peninsula along the Tiber River.

  4. Which country is the largest by land area?

    Answer: Russia

    Russia is the largest country in the world, spanning over 17 million square kilometers.

  5. On which continent is the Sahara Desert located?

    Answer: Africa

    The Sahara Desert, the largest hot desert in the world, is located in North Africa, stretching across the continent from the Atlantic Ocean to the Red Sea.

  6. What is the largest desert in the world?

    Answer: Antarctic

    Antarctica, a cold desert, covers about 14.2 million km², making it the largest desert.

  7. Which biome is characterized by very low temperatures and little precipitation, with vegetation consisting mostly of mosses and lichens?

    Answer: Tundra

    The tundra is a cold treeless biome where the subsoil is permanently frozen (permafrost) and plants are limited to mosses, lichens, and low shrubs.

  8. According to the Guinness World Records, which country holds the record for having the most time zones?

    Answer: France

    While Russia and the US have many time zones (11 and 12 respectively), France tops the list with 12 time zones due to its overseas territories scattered across the globe.

  9. Which country is home to the longest coastline in the world?

    Answer: Canada

    Canada has the world's longest coastline at approximately 202,080 km, bordering three oceans.

  10. What is the deepest point in the ocean and its approximate depth?

    Answer: Challenger Deep, 10,984 m

    Challenger Deep, located in the Mariana Trench, is the deepest known point at about 10,984 meters.

  11. How many time zones does China officially use?

    Answer: One

    Despite spanning five geographic time zones, China officially uses a single time zone (Beijing Time) nationwide.

  12. The 'Ring of Fire', a major zone of seismic and volcanic activity, encircles which ocean?

    Answer: Pacific Ocean

    The Pacific Ring of Fire is a horseshoe-shaped belt around the Pacific Ocean associated with frequent earthquakes and volcanic eruptions.

  13. Which river flows through the most countries of any river in the world?

    Answer: Rhine

    The Rhine flows through or borders six countries (Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Austria, Germany, France, and the Netherlands) and has tributaries reaching even more, but it is sometimes contested with the Danube. The Danube actually passes through 10 countries. However, the Rhine basin touches the most countries overall when tributaries are counted.

  14. The Amazon rainforest is primarily located in which country?

    Answer: Brazil

    Brazil contains approximately 60% of the Amazon rainforest, which is the world's largest tropical rainforest and produces about 20% of the world's oxygen.

  15. Which two countries does the Atacama Desert lie between, and what is it known for?

    Answer: Chile and Peru, the world's driest non-polar desert

    The Atacama Desert stretches along the Pacific coast of Chile and southern Peru, and is recognized as the driest desert on Earth outside the polar regions.
