Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the main religious festival celebrated by Muslims during the ninth month of the Islamic lunar calendar, involving fasting from dawn to sunset?

    Answer: Ramadan

    Ramadan is the holy month of fasting, prayer, and reflection in Islam, preceding the festival of Eid al-Fitr.

  2. Which festival marks the beginning of the Islamic new year and is also known as the Muslim New Year?

    Answer: Muharram

    Muharram marks the first day of the Islamic lunar calendar year. Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha are the two major Islamic festivals, and Laylat al-Qadr is the 'Night of Decree' during Ramadan.

  3. What is the holy book of Islam?

    Answer: The Quran

    The Quran is the central religious text of Islam, believed by Muslims to be a revelation from God.

  4. Which religion observes the holy month of Ramadan?

    Answer: Islam

    Ramadan is the ninth month of the Islamic calendar, during which Muslims fast from dawn to sunset in observance of the Quran's revelation.

  5. What is the name of the annual pilgrimage to Mecca that all Muslims are required to perform at least once in their lifetime?

    Answer: Hajj

    The Hajj is one of the Five Pillars of Islam and is a mandatory religious duty for Muslims to undertake at least once in their lifetime if they are able to do so.

  6. Which of these is a core pillar of Islam?

    Answer: Pilgrimage to Mecca

    The Hajj, or pilgrimage to Mecca, is one of the Five Pillars of Islam that every able-bodied Muslim must perform at least once in their lifetime.

  7. Which religion observes the 'Five Pillars' as a framework for daily life?

    Answer: Islam

    The Five Pillars of Islam are the faith's most important practices: Shahada, Salat, Zakat, Sawm, and Hajj.

  8. In Islamic culture, what is the name of the holy month of fasting, prayer, and reflection?

    Answer: Ramadan

    Ramadan is the ninth month of the Islamic calendar, observed by Muslims worldwide as a month of fasting.

  9. Which Islamic holiday commemorates the willingness of Prophet Ibrahim (Abraham) to sacrifice his son, observed with the slaughter of an animal and sharing of meat with the needy?

    Answer: Eid al‑Adha

    Eid al‑Adha marks Ibrahim’s test of faith and is observed by sacrificing an animal and distributing its meat.

  10. The 'Festival of Breaking the Fast' (Eid al-Fitr) marks the end of which Islamic observance?

    Answer: The month of Ramadan

    Eid al-Fitr celebrates the conclusion of Ramadan, the holy month of fasting, prayer, and reflection for Muslims worldwide.

  11. What is the significance of the 'Hajj' in Islam?

    Answer: The annual pilgrimage to Mecca

    The Hajj is the annual Islamic pilgrimage to Mecca, one of the Five Pillars of Islam, and is obligatory for all Muslims who are physically and financially able.

  12. What is the theological concept of 'Tawhid' in Islam?

    Answer: The absolute oneness and indivisibility of God

    Tawhid is the Islamic concept of monotheism, asserting that God (Allah) is one, unique, and indivisible, without partners or equals.

  13. The 'Feast of Sacrifice' (Eid al-Adha) commemorates which event in Abrahamic religions?

    Answer: Abraham's willingness to sacrifice his son

    Eid al-Adha honors the willingness of the Prophet Ibrahim (Abraham) to sacrifice his son, Ishmael, as an act of obedience to God, before God provided a ram to sacrifice instead.

  14. Which Sufi tradition involves devotional music and spinning dance to achieve spiritual ecstasy?

    Answer: Mevlevi Order (Whirling Dervishes)

    The Mevlevi Order, founded by Rumi's followers, practices the 'sema' ceremony of whirling dervishes as a form of devotional meditation.

  15. During Ramadan, what is the name of the pre‑dawn meal before the daily fast?

    Answer: Sahur

    Sahur is the meal eaten before sunrise, giving energy for the day’s fast.
