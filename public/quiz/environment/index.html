Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What gas do plants primarily absorb from the atmosphere for photosynthesis?

    Answer: Carbon Dioxide

    Plants absorb carbon dioxide (CO2) and use sunlight to convert it into sugars, releasing oxygen as a byproduct.

  2. What is the main greenhouse gas released by burning fossil fuels?

    Answer: Carbon dioxide

    Burning coal, oil, and gas releases large amounts of carbon dioxide, the primary driver of climate change.

  3. What gas do humans primarily exhale as a waste product of cellular respiration?

    Answer: Carbon dioxide

    Humans inhale oxygen and exhale carbon dioxide, which is produced during the metabolic breakdown of nutrients in cells.

  4. What is the main cause of global warming according to scientific consensus?

    Answer: Increased greenhouse gas emissions

    The burning of fossil fuels releases greenhouse gases like CO2, which trap heat and drive global warming.

  5. What is the process by which water vapor cools, condenses, and falls as rain?

    Answer: The water cycle

    The water cycle includes evaporation, condensation, and precipitation.

  6. Which of these processes removes carbon dioxide from the atmosphere naturally?

    Answer: Ocean absorption

    The oceans act as a major carbon sink, absorbing significant amounts of atmospheric CO2 through physical and biological processes.

  7. Which country is home to the world's largest rainforest?

    Answer: Brazil

    The Amazon Rainforest, the world's largest tropical rainforest, spans approximately 60% of Brazil, though it extends into other South American countries.

  8. Which of the following is considered a 'keystone species' in many ecosystems?

    Answer: Sea Otter

    Sea otters are a classic example of a keystone species because they control sea urchin populations, which in turn protects kelp forests.

  9. What is coral bleaching primarily caused by?

    Answer: Increased water temperatures

    Coral bleaching occurs when corals are stressed by changes in conditions such as temperature, light, or nutrients. They expel the symbiotic algae living in their tissues, causing them to turn completely white.

  10. Which of these biomes is characterized by very low precipitation and extreme temperature fluctuations?

    Answer: Desert

    Deserts receive less than 25 cm (10 inches) of rain per year and often experience scorching days and freezing nights.

  11. What is the primary role of decomposers like fungi and bacteria in an ecosystem?

    Answer: Breaking down dead matter

    Decomposers recycle nutrients by breaking down organic waste and dead organisms, returning essential elements to the soil.

  12. What is the name of the process by which nitrogen is converted into a form plants can absorb?

    Answer: Nitrogen fixation

    Nitrogen-fixing bacteria convert atmospheric N₂ into ammonia, which plants can use.

  13. What is the name of the hypothesis that suggests Earth's current geological era is significantly shaped by human impact?

    Answer: Anthropocene

    The Anthropocene is a proposed geological epoch dating from the commencement of significant human impact on Earth's geology and ecosystems.

  14. What is the term for the variety of life on Earth at all levels, from genes to ecosystems?

    Answer: Biodiversity

    Biodiversity refers to the variety of life forms on Earth, encompassing genetic, species, and ecosystem diversity.

  15. Which gas makes up the greatest percentage of Earth's atmosphere?

    Answer: Nitrogen

    Nitrogen makes up about 78% of the atmosphere, while oxygen accounts for roughly 21%.
