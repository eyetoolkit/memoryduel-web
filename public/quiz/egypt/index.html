Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which ancient civilization built the Great Pyramid of Giza?

    Answer: Egyptians

    The Great Pyramid of Giza was built around 2560 BCE during the reign of Pharaoh Khufu, as part of the funerary complex of the ancient Egyptian civilization.

  2. Which country is home to the city of Cairo?

    Answer: Egypt

    Cairo is the capital of Egypt and the largest city in Africa.

  3. In which country did the ancient civilization of the Pharaohs flourish?

    Answer: Egypt

    The ancient Egyptian civilization, famous for its Pharaohs, pyramids, and hieroglyphs, was located in northeastern Africa along the Nile River.

  4. What is the name of the ancient writing system used by the Egyptians, consisting of stylized images?

    Answer: Hieroglyphics

    Hieroglyphics were the formal writing system of ancient Egypt, used for religious and royal texts.

  5. The Nile River flows through which country on its way to the Mediterranean?

    Answer: Egypt

    The Nile flows through multiple African countries but is most famously associated with Egypt, where it forms a fertile delta before entering the Mediterranean.

  6. The ancient civilization of Egypt developed along which river?

    Answer: Nile

    Egyptian civilization flourished along the Nile River, which provided fertile soil and a reliable water source.

  7. The Rosetta Stone, which helped decipher Egyptian hieroglyphs, was discovered during which conflict?

    Answer: Napoleonic Wars

    The Rosetta Stone was found in 1799 by French soldiers during Napoleon's campaign in Egypt.

  8. Which ancient civilization developed a system of writing using symbols known as hieroglyphs?

    Answer: Ancient Egyptians

    Hieroglyphs were the formal writing system used in Ancient Egypt for religious and monumental inscriptions.

  9. The Rosetta Stone was crucial in deciphering which ancient script?

    Answer: Hieroglyphics

    The Rosetta Stone allowed scholars to finally decode Egyptian hieroglyphics.

  10. Who was the first female pharaoh of ancient Egypt?

    Answer: Hatshepsut

    Hatshepsut ruled as pharaoh from approximately 1479 to 1458 BC, one of the few female pharaohs of ancient Egypt.

  11. What is the capital of Egypt?

    Answer: Cairo

    Cairo is the capital of Egypt and one of the largest cities in Africa and the Middle East.

  12. Which ancient wonder was located in the city of Alexandria, Egypt?

    Answer: The Lighthouse of Alexandria

    The Lighthouse of Alexandria (Pharos of Alexandria) was one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, built around 280 BCE.

  13. In ancient Egypt, what was the 'Weighing of the Heart' ceremony?

    Answer: A judgment where the heart was weighed against a feather

    In Egyptian mythology, the 'Weighing of the Heart' was part of the afterlife journey. The deceased's heart was weighed against the feather of Ma'at (truth). If the heart was lighter or equal, the soul could enter the afterlife.

  14. Which river, the world's second longest by most measurements, flows through Cairo?

    Answer: Nile

    The Nile River flows through Cairo, Egypt's capital. The question's framing about 'second longest' refers to some measurements placing the Amazon first, but the Nile is the correct answer for Cairo.

  15. Who was the Pharaoh who ruled Egypt during the New Kingdom and is famous for his relatively intact tomb discovered in 1922?

    Answer: Tutankhamun

    Tutankhamun was an 18th-dynasty pharaoh whose nearly intact tomb (KV62) was discovered by Howard Carter in 1922 in the Valley of the Kings.
