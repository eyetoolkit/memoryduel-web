Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which country is the capital city Paris located in?

    Answer: France

    Paris is the capital and largest city of France.

  2. The ancient city of Pompeii was destroyed by the eruption of which volcano?

    Answer: Mount Vesuvius

    Mount Vesuvius erupted in 79 AD, burying the Roman city of Pompeii in ash and pumice.

  3. What is the name of the ancient Greek city-state known for its military discipline and the Battle of Thermopylae?

    Answer: Sparta

    Sparta was a prominent Greek city-state known for its militaristic society and was the home of the famous 300 Spartans at the Battle of Thermopylae.

  4. The Magna Carta, a foundational document for modern legal rights, was signed in which country?

    Answer: England

    The Magna Carta was sealed by King John of England in 1215, establishing that the king was subject to the law.

  5. What event is widely considered the start of World War I?

    Answer: Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand

    The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary in Sarajevo on June 28, 1914 triggered a chain of events leading to WWI.

  6. What is the smallest country in the world by land area?

    Answer: Vatican City

    Vatican City is the world's smallest country at approximately 0.44 square kilometers.

  7. Who was the first female Prime Minister of a European country?

    Answer: Margaret Thatcher

    Margaret Thatcher became the first female Prime Minister of the United Kingdom (and a European country) in 1979.

  8. Which is the longest river flowing through Europe?

    Answer: Volga

    The Volga is the longest river in Europe, flowing through central Russia before emptying into the Caspian Sea.

  9. Which country celebrates 'Oktoberfest', a world-famous beer festival?

    Answer: Germany

    Oktoberfest is a massive folk festival held annually in Munich, Germany.

  10. Which mountain range forms a natural border between Europe and Asia?

    Answer: Urals

    The Ural Mountains in Russia are traditionally used as the boundary between Europe and Asia.

  11. Which of these rivers does NOT flow through Germany?

    Answer: Vistula

    The Vistula River flows entirely through Poland, while the Rhine, Elbe, and Danube all pass through Germany.

  12. Which river is the longest that flows entirely within one country?

    Answer: Volga River

    The Volga River at 3,530 km is the longest river flowing entirely within one country (Russia).

  13. Which is the highest mountain in Europe (conventionally, west of the Caucasus divide)?

    Answer: Mont Blanc

    Mont Blanc (4,808 m) is the highest peak in the Alps and traditionally considered the highest in Europe west of the Caucasus; Elbrus is higher if the Caucasus is counted as Europe.

  14. The Treaty of Westphalia (1648), which ended the Thirty Years' War, established what key principle of international relations?

    Answer: State sovereignty

    The Peace of Westphalia established the principle of state sovereignty, meaning each state has exclusive sovereignty over its territory and domestic affairs, forming the basis of the modern international system.

  15. The Thirty Years' War (1618–1648) was primarily fought over what issue?

    Answer: Religious conflict in Europe

    The Thirty Years' War began as a religious conflict between Protestants and Catholics in the Holy Roman Empire but evolved into a broader European political struggle.
