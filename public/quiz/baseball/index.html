Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the main color of the ball used in a standard baseball game?

    Answer: White

    Standard baseballs are white with red stitching.

  2. In which sport is a 'home run' scored?

    Answer: Baseball

    A home run is a fundamental scoring play in baseball, where a batter circles all bases after hitting the ball.

  3. In baseball, what is the goal for a batter after hitting the ball?

    Answer: Run to four bases

    The batter must hit the ball and run around four bases to score a run before being tagged out.

  4. Which two sports are combined in the word 'baseketball' in the famous movie 'Baseketball'?

    Answer: Baseball and Basketball

    The 1998 comedy film 'Baseketball' invented a fictional sport that combines elements of baseball and basketball.

  5. In which sport do teams compete to score by hitting a ball with a bat and running around bases?

    Answer: Baseball

    Baseball is played by two teams where batters hit the ball and run around four bases to score runs.

  6. What is the shape of a traditional baseball?

    Answer: Spherical

    A baseball is a round, spherical ball.

  7. In baseball, how many players from one team are on the field at a time when they are on defense?

    Answer: 9

    A defensive team fields 9 players: pitcher, catcher, four infielders, and three outfielders.

  8. In baseball, how many strikes result in a batter being called out (a strikeout)?

    Answer: 3

    A batter is out after accumulating three strikes, known as a strikeout.

  9. In baseball, how many bases must a runner touch to score a run?

    Answer: 4

    A batter must touch first, second, and third base, then home plate (four bases total) to score a run.

  10. In baseball's World Series, how many games must a team win to claim the championship?

    Answer: 4

    The World Series is a best-of-seven format; the first team to win 4 games is declared the champion.

  11. In baseball, how many stitches are on a standard MLB baseball?

    Answer: 108

    A standard MLB baseball has 108 double stitches (216 total stitches).

  12. Which sport uses the term 'bullpen' to describe the area where relief pitchers warm up?

    Answer: Baseball

    In baseball, the 'bullpen' is the designated area where relief pitchers warm up before entering the game.

  13. Which pitcher holds the record for the most career strikeouts in Major League Baseball?

    Answer: Nolan Ryan

    Nolan Ryan recorded 5,714 career strikeouts, the MLB record.

  14. In baseball, what is the maximum number of balls a pitcher can throw before a 'ball' is called against them?

    Answer: 4

    In baseball, four balls constitute a 'walk', advancing the batter to first base.

  15. Which sport was removed from the Olympic program after the 2008 Beijing Games and returned for Tokyo 2020?

    Answer: Baseball

    Baseball was dropped from the Olympics after 2008 but was reintroduced as a medal sport at the Tokyo 2020 Games (held in 2021).
