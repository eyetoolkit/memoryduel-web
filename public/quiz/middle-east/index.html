Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which modern country was historically known as 'Mesopotamia', often called the 'Cradle of Civilization'?

    Answer: Iraq

    Mesopotamia, meaning 'land between the rivers', lies between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers in what is now Iraq. It was home to Sumer, Babylon, and Assyria and is considered one of the earliest cradles of civilization.

  2. The ancient city of Petra, famous for its rock-cut architecture, was built by which civilization?

    Answer: Nabataeans

    Petra was the capital of the Nabataean Kingdom and a major trading hub in ancient Arabia.

  3. What is the significance of the 'Hamsa' symbol in Middle Eastern and North African cultures?

    Answer: A protective charm against evil

    The Hamsa is a hand-shaped amulet widely used in Middle Eastern and North African cultures as a protective symbol against the evil eye.

  4. Which river flows through Baghdad?

    Answer: Tigris

    Baghdad, the capital of Iraq, lies on the banks of the Tigris River.

  5. The ancient city of Petra is located in which modern-day country?

    Answer: Jordan

    Petra, the rose-red city carved into sandstone cliffs, is located in modern-day Jordan and was the capital of the Nabataean kingdom.

  6. Which ancient civilization is credited with developing one of the earliest known writing systems, cuneiform?

    Answer: Mesopotamia

    Cuneiform was developed by the Sumerians in Mesopotamia (modern-day Iraq) around 3400 BCE.

  7. Which river is considered the lifeline of Egypt?

    Answer: Nile

    The Nile River provides nearly all of Egypt's fresh water and has been essential to agriculture and civilization in the region for thousands of years.

  8. Which empire was ruled by Suleiman the Magnate in the 16th century?

    Answer: The Ottoman Empire

    Suleiman the Magnificent (r. 1520–1566) presided over the golden age of the Ottoman Empire, expanding it across southeastern Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa, and overseeing major legal and cultural achievements.

  9. Which of these countries is NOT located on the African continent?

    Answer: Jordan

    Jordan is located in the Middle East in Asia, while Morocco, Ethiopia, and Tanzania are all African nations.

  10. The fall of Constantinople in 1453 marked the end of which empire?

    Answer: Byzantine Empire

    Constantinople fell to the Ottoman Turks in 1453, ending the Byzantine Empire, the eastern continuation of the Roman Empire.

  11. The Ottoman Empire finally collapsed after which war?

    Answer: World War I

    The Ottoman Empire sided with the Central Powers in WWI and formally dissolved in 1922.

  12. Which major European empire fell in 1922, leading to the establishment of the Republic of Turkey?

    Answer: The Ottoman Empire

    The Ottoman Empire, which had ruled for over 600 years, dissolved after World War I, giving way to modern Turkey under Mustafa Kemal Atatürk.

  13. Which ancient Mesopotamian code of law is one of the earliest written legal codes known to history?

    Answer: Code of Hammurabi

    The Code of Hammurabi was issued by the Babylonian king Hammurabi around 1754 BC and establishes the principle of 'an eye for an eye'.

  14. Which of these countries does NOT border the Mediterranean Sea?

    Answer: Jordan

    Jordan has no Mediterranean coastline; it borders the Red Sea instead.

  15. The Code of Hammurabi originated in which ancient civilization?

    Answer: Babylon

    The Code of Hammurabi was promulgated by King Hammurabi of Babylon around 1754 BC, one of the earliest written legal codes.
