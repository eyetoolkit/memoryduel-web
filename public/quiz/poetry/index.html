Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. How many lines does a typical sonnet have?

    Answer: 14

    A sonnet is a 14-line poetic form, often set to music.

  2. Which English poet wrote the lyric 'I Wandered Lonely as a Cloud', featuring daffodils?

    Answer: William Wordsworth

    William Wordsworth composed 'I Wandered Lonely as a Cloud', celebrating the beauty of daffodils.

  3. Which Japanese poet is known for his haiku, including the famous poem about a frog and an old pond?

    Answer: Matsuo Bashō

    Matsuo Bashō's haiku 'An old silent pond...' is among the most celebrated in the genre.

  4. Which Indian poet wrote the collection 'Gitanjali' and won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913?

    Answer: B) Rabindranath Tagore

    Rabindranath Tagore, an Indian poet, received the Nobel Prize for Literature for his Gitanjali (Song Offerings).

  5. Which 19th‑century poet wrote 'The Raven'?

    Answer: Edgar Allan Poe

    Poe's 'The Raven' (1845) is a famous narrative poem about a talking raven.

  6. Which Tang dynasty poet is celebrated for vivid nature poems such as 'Quiet Night Thought' and 'The River Merchant's Wife: A Letter'?

    Answer: Li Bai

    Li Bai (also known as Li Po) is renowned for his lyrical and imaginative nature verse.

  7. Which Romantic poet wrote 'Ode to a Nightingale'?

    Answer: John Keats

    Keats' 1819 ode explores the transcendent power of the nightingale's song.

  8. Which poet was known for the 'Beat Generation' and wrote 'Howl'?

    Answer: Allen Ginsberg

    Ginsberg's 'Howl' (1956) is a landmark of Beat poetry, critiquing American society.

  9. Who wrote 'The Odyssey'?

    Answer: Homer

    Homer is traditionally credited as the author of 'The Odyssey', an ancient Greek epic poem composed around the 8th century BCE. It follows the hero Odysseus on his journey home after the Trojan War.

  10. Which poet is credited with writing the first sonnet in the English language?

    Answer: Thomas Wyatt

    Thomas Wyatt introduced the sonnet form to English and is often credited with the first English sonnet.

  11. Which poet wrote "The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock"?

    Answer: T.S. Eliot

    T.S. Eliot wrote "The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock", a seminal modernist poem published in 1915.

  12. Which American poet wrote "The Road Not Taken"?

    Answer: Robert Frost

    Robert Frost wrote The Road Not Taken, one of the most quoted poems in American literature.

  13. Which writer authored the epic poem 'Paradise Lost'?

    Answer: A) John Milton

    John Milton wrote the epic poem Paradise Lost in the 17th century.

  14. Which poet is associated with the Harlem Renaissance and the poem "The Weary Blues"?

    Answer: Langston Hughes

    Langston Hughes, a leading figure of the Harlem Renaissance, wrote "The Weary Blues" in 1925.

  15. Which Italian poet composed the narrative epic 'The Divine Comedy'?

    Answer: Dante Alighieri

    Dante wrote The Divine Comedy in the early 14th century, detailing the afterlife.
