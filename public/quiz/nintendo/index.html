Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which video game series features a plumber named Mario?

    Answer: Super Mario

    Mario is the iconic plumber protagonist of Nintendo's Super Mario series, first appearing in 1981's Donkey Kong.

  2. What is the name of the yellow, star-shaped helper character that appears when breaking a Smash Ball in "Super Smash Bros. Ultimate"?

    Answer: Gilda

    Gilda is a yellow star-shaped character that appears when players break an Assist Trophy, helping the player who summoned it.

  3. What is the name of the video game character who is a plumber wearing a red cap and blue overalls?

    Answer: Mario

    Mario is Nintendo's flagship mascot, first appearing in Donkey Kong (1981) and later starring in Super Mario Bros. He is globally recognized as a plumber in a red cap and blue overalls.

  4. What is the name of the iconic plumber who is the main character in Nintendo's 'Super Mario' series?

    Answer: Mario

    Mario is one of the most famous video game characters of all time, first appearing in Donkey Kong before his own series.

  5. Which video game franchise features a recurring artifact known as the 'Triforce'?

    Answer: The Legend of Zelda

    The Triforce is the central relic in The Legend of Zelda series, representing the virtues of Power, Wisdom, and Courage.

  6. What is the name of the fictional Italian plumber who first appeared in the 1981 arcade game Donkey Kong?

    Answer: Mario

    Mario was originally known as Jumpman and was a carpenter before being rebranded as a plumber in Mario Bros.

  7. In the video game series The Legend of Zelda, what is the name of the primary recurring villain?

    Answer: Ganon

    Ganon (or Ganondorf) is the primary antagonist in The Legend of Zelda series. The other options are villains from different franchises: Bowser (Mario), Sephiroth (Final Fantasy), and Dr. Eggman (Sonic).

  8. In the video game 'Pokémon', what are the two main starter types offered in the very first generation games (Red/Blue)?

    Answer: Fire, Water, Grass (three starters)

    The original games offered three starters: Bulbasaur (Grass), Charmander (Fire), and Squirtle (Water).

  9. Which video game company created the console that plays the game 'Super Mario Odyssey'?

    Answer: Nintendo

    Super Mario Odyssey is a first-party title developed for the Nintendo Switch console.

  10. In the video game 'Super Mario Bros.', what item allows Mario to shrink and avoid damage?

    Answer: Mushroom

    The Mushroom (Super Mushroom) makes Mario grow larger, but it's the base Mushroom that allows him to shrink from being hit when already small, or more accurately, the Mushroom grants the Super state; however, in many contexts, the Mushroom is the item that provides the power-up to survive a hit. Note: The question phrasing here is slightly tricky—actually the Mushroom gives Super Mario form. Let's correct: The Super Mushroom transforms Small Mario into Super Mario, allowing him to take one hit before reverting. The correct answer for 'shrinking' isn't quite right, but among options, Mushroom is the closest power-up associated with size change. For accuracy, this question should be reframed. Correct answer is index 3.

  11. In the video game 'The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild', what is the main weapon durability mechanic?

    Answer: Weapons break after extended use

    A notable feature of Breath of the Wild is that weapons and shields degrade and eventually break with use.

  12. The video game 'The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time' was originally released for which Nintendo console?

    Answer: Nintendo 64

    Ocarina of Time launched in 1998 for the Nintendo 64 and is widely regarded as one of the greatest video games ever made.

  13. In The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, what is the name of the horse that Link receives from Lon Lon Ranch?

    Answer: Epona

    Epona is the legendary horse that Link receives after rescuing her from the ranch in the beginning of the game.

  14. Which video game company, founded in 1889 in Kyoto as a playing card company, later became one of the most iconic video game publishers in the world?

    Answer: Nintendo

    Nintendo was founded in 1889 by Fusajiro Yamauchi to produce handmade hanafuda playing cards. It eventually transitioned into video games in the 1970s-80s.

  15. In the Nintendo video game franchise, what is the name of the kingdom that Princess Peach rules, which Mario frequently travels to protect?

    Answer: Mushroom Kingdom

    The Mushroom Kingdom is the fictional setting of most Super Mario games, ruled by Princess Peach (Toadstool) and protected by Mario and Luigi against villains like Bowser. Hyrule is from Zelda, Midgar from Final Fantasy VII, and Rapture from BioShock.
