Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which of these is the largest string instrument in a standard orchestra?

    Answer: Double bass

    The double bass is the largest and lowest-pitched string instrument in the orchestra.

  2. Which instrument is primarily associated with jazz music?

    Answer: Trumpet

    The trumpet is one of the most iconic instruments in jazz, played by legends such as Louis Armstrong and Miles Davis.

  3. Which of these instruments has 88 keys?

    Answer: Piano

    The standard modern piano has 88 keys (52 white and 36 black), spanning over seven octaves.

  4. What instrument does a concert pianist primarily play?

    Answer: Piano

    A pianist plays the piano, a keyboard instrument central to both classical and popular music.

  5. Which instrument has 88 keys, with white keys representing the natural notes?

    Answer: Piano

    The standard piano has 88 keys (52 white, 36 black), making it one of the most versatile and widely used instruments.

  6. Which of these instruments belongs to the string family and is typically played with a bow?

    Answer: Violin

    The violin is the smallest and highest-pitched bowed string instrument and is a staple of classical orchestras.

  7. Which instrument is the violin a member of?

    Answer: String family

    The violin produces sound by the vibration of strings and belongs to the string family (bowed strings).

  8. Which instrument is a young prodigy typically associated with if they play 'Flight of the Bumblebee' with extreme speed?

    Answer: Piano

    While 'Flight of the Bumblebee' was composed for piano by Rimsky-Korsakov, it is frequently performed on various instruments, but the piano remains a classic demonstration of virtuosity for this piece.

  9. Which instrument family does the trumpet, trombone, and French horn belong to?

    Answer: Brass

    The trumpet, trombone, and French horn are all brass instruments, made primarily of brass and played by buzzing the lips into a mouthpiece.

  10. Which famous guitarist is known for playing a right-handed guitar upside down (left-handed) with the strings reversed?

    Answer: Jimi Hendrix

    Jimi Hendrix was left-handed but played a right-handed Fender Stratocaster flipped over, restrung upside down.

  11. Which instrument is the main instrument in a standard jazz quartet rhythm section along with bass and drums?

    Answer: Piano

    The piano (or keyboard) typically fills the harmonic role in a jazz rhythm section, alongside bass and drums.

  12. Which instrument is the standard choice for playing Bach's Cello Suites?

    Answer: Cello

    Johann Sebastian Bach composed his famous Cello Suites specifically for the violoncello.

  13. Which musical instrument belongs to the woodwind family and is made of brass?

    Answer: Saxophone

    The saxophone is made of brass but is classified as a woodwind instrument because it uses a reed.

  14. Which instrument did Miles Davis famously play?

    Answer: Trumpet

    Miles Davis was a legendary jazz trumpeter who pioneered numerous subgenres including cool jazz, modal jazz, and jazz fusion.

  15. What is the highest standard note on a standard 88-key piano?

    Answer: C8

    The top key on a standard piano is C8, also known as 'C8' or 'c'''''. The lowest is A0.
