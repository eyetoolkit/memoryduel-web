Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which ball is used in the sport of basketball?

    Answer: Orange sphere

    Basketballs are typically orange, inflated spheres, though they can be other colors.

  2. In which sport is a slam dunk most commonly associated?

    Answer: Basketball

    A slam dunk is a move in basketball where a player jumps and thrusts the ball through the hoop.

  3. How many players from one team are on the court during a standard basketball game?

    Answer: 5

    Basketball is played with 5 players per team on the court at a time.

  4. In basketball, how many points is a shot worth if it is taken from behind the three-point line?

    Answer: 3

    Shots made from beyond the three-point arc are awarded three points.

  5. In which city were the 2020 Summer Olympics basketball events primarily held?

    Answer: Tokyo

    The basketball competitions took place in Tokyo, Japan, the host city of the 2020 Games.

  6. Which two sports are combined in the word 'baseketball' in the famous movie 'Baseketball'?

    Answer: Baseball and Basketball

    The 1998 comedy film 'Baseketball' invented a fictional sport that combines elements of baseball and basketball.

  7. Which NBA player holds the record for the most career assists in league history?

    Answer: John Stockton

    John Stockton amassed 15,806 assists over 19 seasons, the all‑time NBA record.

  8. Which professional basketball league is considered the top men's league in the world?

    Answer: NBA

    The NBA (National Basketball Association) in North America is widely regarded as the premier basketball league globally.

  9. What is the name of the major professional basketball league in North America?

    Answer: NBA

    The NBA (National Basketball Association) is the premier men's professional basketball league in North America.

  10. In which NBA season did Michael Jordan score 69 points in a single game?

    Answer: 1989-90

    Jordan recorded 69 points on March 28, 1990, during the 1989-90 regular season.

  11. Which sport features the 'Final Four' in its most prestigious annual tournament?

    Answer: College Basketball (NCAA)

    The 'Final Four' is the semifinal round of the NCAA Division I Men's Basketball Tournament.

  12. In basketball, what is the diameter of a standard NBA basketball rim?

    Answer: 18 inches

    A standard NBA basketball rim has an inner diameter of 18 inches (45.72 cm), and the ball itself has a diameter of about 9.43 inches.

  13. Which country has won the most Olympic gold medals in men's basketball through the 'Dream Team' era and beyond?

    Answer: United States

    The United States has dominated men's Olympic basketball, winning 17 gold medals overall, including during the famous Dream Team era starting in 1992.

  14. Who became the NBA's all‑time leading scorer, surpassing Kareem Abdul‑Jabbar, in February 2023?

    Answer: LeBron James

    LeBron James overtook Kareem Abdul‑Jabbar's career points total to become the NBA's all‑time leading scorer.

  15. Which player holds the NBA's all‑time highest points per game average (minimum 1,000 games)?

    Answer: Michael Jordan

    Jordan averaged 30.12 points per game over his career, the highest ever.
