Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which movie features the famous line, 'May the Force be with you'?

    Answer: Star Wars

    This iconic phrase is a staple of the Star Wars franchise.

  2. In which movie does the character Darth Vader first appear?

    Answer: A New Hope

    Darth Vader first appeared in Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope (1977).

  3. In which year was the original 'Star Wars' (Episode IV: A New Hope) released?

    Answer: 1977

    Star Wars: Episode IV premiered in 1977.

  4. Who directed the sci-fi movie 'Jurassic Park' (1993)?

    Answer: Steven Spielberg

    Steven Spielberg directed 'Jurassic Park', based on Michael Crichton's novel.

  5. What is the name of the fictional metal in the Marvel Cinematic Universe that Captain America's shield is made of?

    Answer: Vibranium

    Vibranium is a rare, extraterrestrial metal found primarily in Wakanda.

  6. Which actor played the character 'Neo' in 'The Matrix'?

    Answer: Keanu Reeves

    Keanu Reeves starred as Neo (Thomas Anderson) in the original 'Matrix' trilogy and the 2021 sequel.

  7. Which film franchise features the character Katniss Everdeen?

    Answer: The Hunger Games

    Katniss Everdeen is the protagonist of 'The Hunger Games' film series, based on the novels by Suzanne Collins.

  8. Which actress starred as the lead character in the 2010 film Inception?

    Answer: Elliot Page

    Elliot Page (credited as Ellen Page at the time) played Ariadne in Christopher Nolan's Inception.

  9. In the TV series 'Stranger Things', what is the name of the alternate dimension?

    Answer: The Upside Down

    The Upside Down is a dark, parallel dimension that serves as the main antagonist force in Netflix's 'Stranger Things'.

  10. Which film is the highest-grossing movie of all time (unadjusted for inflation)?

    Answer: Avatar

    James Cameron's 'Avatar' (2009) holds the record for highest-grossing film of all time worldwide.

  11. Which 1999 science fiction film, directed by the Wachowskis, is famous for the 'red pill or blue pill' scene?

    Answer: The Matrix

    The Matrix (1999), directed by Lana and Lilly Wachowski, features the iconic choice between a red pill (truth) and a blue pill (blissful ignorance), offered by Morpheus to Neo.

  12. Which director is known for the films 'Inception', 'Interstellar', and 'Oppenheimer'?

    Answer: Christopher Nolan

    Christopher Nolan is a British-American director famous for his complex, large-scale films and practical effects.

  13. Which 1999 film introduced the character 'The Matrix' and its virtual world?

    Answer: The Matrix

    The 1999 sci‑fi film The Matrix debuted the concept of a simulated reality.

  14. In the board game Twilight Imperium (4th edition), how many victory points are required to win the game?

    Answer: 10

    In Twilight Imperium Fourth Edition, players must accumulate 10 victory points to win.

  15. Which director helmed the 1982 sci-fi classic 'Blade Runner' and its 2017 sequel 'Blade Runner 2049'?

    Answer: Ridley Scott

    Ridley Scott directed the original 'Blade Runner' (1982) and served as executive producer on the sequel directed by Denis Villeneuve.
