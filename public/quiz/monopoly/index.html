Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the main objective in the classic board game Monopoly?

    Answer: To acquire as much wealth and bankrupt all opponents

    The goal of Monopoly is to drive other players into bankruptcy by acquiring properties and charging rent.

  2. In the game Monopoly, what color are the properties on Boardwalk and Park Place?

    Answer: Dark Blue

    Boardwalk and Park Place are the most expensive properties in Monopoly and belong to the dark blue color group.

  3. How many squares are there on a standard Monopoly board?

    Answer: 40

    A standard Monopoly board has 40 squares, including 28 property squares and 12 non-property squares.

  4. In the game Monopoly, what is the name of the dog playing piece?

    Answer: Scottie

    The dog token in Monopoly is traditionally known as the Scottie dog.

  5. In the game Monopoly, what is the color of the 'Go' square where players collect $200?

    Answer: White

    The 'Go' square in Monopoly is located in the bottom-right corner and has a white background.

  6. Which board game features a 'Get Out of Jail Free' card?

    Answer: Monopoly

    Monopoly's Chance and Community Chest decks include a 'Get Out of Jail Free' card.

  7. In Monopoly, what is the name of the free parking space?

    Answer: Free Parking

    Free Parking is the red corner square in the standard Monopoly board.

  8. Which traditional board game involves players moving around a board, buying properties, and paying rent, while trying to bankrupt opponents?

    Answer: Monopoly

    Monopoly is one of the best-selling board games in history, centered on buying, trading, and developing properties to bankrupt opponents.

  9. In the game Monopoly, how much money does each player start with in the standard edition?

    Answer: $1500

    In standard Monopoly, each player begins with $1500: two $500s, four $100s, one $50, one $20, two $10s, five $5s, and five $1s.

  10. In Monopoly, what happens when a player lands on the 'Go to Jail' space?

    Answer: They go directly to Jail without collecting $200

    Landing on 'Go to Jail' sends the player straight to Jail without passing Go, so they do not collect the $200 salary.

  11. Which board game requires players to move tokens around a board based on dice rolls to acquire properties?

    Answer: Monopoly

    Monopoly involves rolling dice to move around the board and buying properties.

  12. Which board game features the phrase 'Do not pass GO, do not collect $200'?

    Answer: Monopoly

    This is a well-known penalty in Monopoly when a player lands on or passes through the 'Go to Jail' space.

  13. Which of the following is the best-selling board game of all time, known for its simple mechanics of buying, renting, and selling properties?

    Answer: Monopoly

    Monopoly, originally based on The Landlord's Game, is the best-selling proprietary board game in history, with hundreds of millions of copies sold worldwide since its 1935 release by Parker Brothers.

  14. Which board game features players moving around a board collecting properties and building houses and hotels?

    Answer: Monopoly

    Monopoly is a real-estate trading game where players buy, sell, and develop properties, aiming to bankrupt opponents through rent collection.

  15. Which board game, originally published in 1935, is based on buying and selling properties in Atlantic City?

    Answer: Monopoly

    Monopoly was originally based on Atlantic City, New Jersey, and was first published by Parker Brothers in 1935.
