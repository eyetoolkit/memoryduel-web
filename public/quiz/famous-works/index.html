Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which instrument is most prominently featured in Ludwig van Beethoven's 'Moonlight Sonata'?

    Answer: Piano

    Beethoven's 'Moonlight Sonata' (Piano Sonata No. 14) is a piano piece, one of the most famous works in the classical piano repertoire.

  2. Which famous classical piece begins with four iconic notes often described as 'da-da-da-DUM'?

    Answer: Symphony No. 5 (Beethoven)

    Beethoven's Symphony No. 5 opens with one of the most recognizable motifs in all of classical music.

  3. Which artist painted the famous work 'Starry Night'?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh painted 'The Starry Night' in 1889 while staying at an asylum in France.

  4. Who composed the ballet music for 'Swan Lake'?

    Answer: Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky

    Tchaikovsky composed Swan Lake in 1876, and it remains one of the most famous ballets in the world.

  5. Which composer wrote the famous piece 'Für Elise'?

    Answer: Ludwig van Beethoven

    'Für Elise' is a well-known bagatelle for solo piano composed by Ludwig van Beethoven around 1810.

  6. What is the name of the famous classical piece composed by Antonio Vivaldi, consisting of four violin concertos depicting the seasons?

    Answer: The Four Seasons

    Vivaldi's Le Quattro Stagioni (The Four Seasons) is one of the most recognized works of Baroque music.

  7. Which artist painted 'Guernica', a powerful anti-war painting?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in 1937 in response to the bombing of the Basque town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War.

  8. Which song by Queen features the lyrics 'We are the champions'?

    Answer: We Are the Champions

    'We Are the Champions' was released in 1977 on the album 'News of the World' and is one of Queen's most iconic anthems.

  9. Which famous band released the album 'The Dark Side of the Moon'?

    Answer: Pink Floyd

    'The Dark Side of the Moon' is the eighth studio album by the English rock band Pink Floyd, released in 1973.

  10. Beethoven's Symphony No. 9 is famous for which movement?

    Answer: The Ode to Joy

    The fourth movement of Beethoven's 9th features the 'Ode to Joy' theme.

  11. Which rock band performed the song 'Hotel California'?

    Answer: The Eagles

    'Hotel California' is one of The Eagles' most iconic songs, released on their 1976 album of the same name.

  12. Which composer became deaf later in life yet continued to compose masterpieces, including his Ninth Symphony?

    Answer: Ludwig van Beethoven

    Beethoven began losing his hearing in his late 20s and was almost completely deaf by the time he composed his Ninth Symphony (1824), one of the greatest works in Western music.

  13. Which artist composed the ballet score 'The Rite of Spring'?

    Answer: Igor Stravinsky

    Igor Stravinsky composed 'The Rite of Spring' (1913), a work famous for its revolutionary rhythm and the riots it caused at its premiere.

  14. Which composer is known for his 'Symphony No. 9' which includes the 'Ode to Joy' chorus?

    Answer: Ludwig van Beethoven

    Beethoven's Ninth Symphony is famous for being one of the first major symphonies to feature vocal soloists and a chorus in its final movement.

  15. Which opera by Giacomo Puccini features the aria 'Nessun Dorma'?

    Answer: Turandot

    'Nessun Dorma' is from Puccini's opera 'Turandot'; it was popularized by tenor Luciano Pavarotti.
