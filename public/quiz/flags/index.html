Flags of the World Quiz

A flag is a design problem solved under brutal constraints: it has to be recognisable at a distance, from the wrong side, and while moving. That is why most of them fail into the same three shapes — stripes, a cross, or a canton with something in the corner.

This quiz tests the exceptions. The flag that is not a rectangle, the one with a dragon on it, the two countries whose flags are near-identical, and the oldest national flag still flying. Twenty questions, each with an explanation of where the design came from.

Answer key

All 20 questions, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which country's flag features a red maple leaf?

    Answer: Canada

    Canada adopted the red-and-white maple leaf flag in 1965, replacing a design that carried the Union Jack in the canton.

  2. Which country's flag shows a green cedar tree?

    Answer: Lebanon

    The cedar of Lebanon is on the flag because the tree is mentioned in the country's name and appears in its oldest literature.

  3. What is the Japanese flag's white field with a red disc called?

    Answer: Hinomaru

    Hinomaru means circle of the sun. The design has been used on banners for centuries; it was formally designated for national use in 1870 and confirmed as the national flag by law in 1999.

  4. How many stars are on the flag of the United States?

    Answer: 50

    Fifty stars for fifty states. The flag is redesigned whenever a state joins, which has happened 27 times.

  5. Which country has the only national flag that is not a rectangle?

    Answer: Nepal

    Nepal's flag is two stacked pennants. Switzerland and Vatican City are square, but square is still a rectangle.

  6. Which country's flag features a white dragon?

    Answer: Bhutan

    The Druk, or thunder dragon, is the national symbol of Bhutan. The flag is split diagonally into yellow and orange.

  7. Which country's flag is green with a white crescent and star?

    Answer: Pakistan

    Pakistan's flag has a white vertical band alongside the green field, which carries a crescent and a five-pointed star.

  8. In France's flag, what is the order of colours from the flagpole outwards?

    Answer: Blue, white, red

    French blue sits at the hoist and red flies free. Reversing them gives you the Dutch flag, which is horizontal anyway.

  9. Which country's flag is a yellow cross on a blue field?

    Answer: Sweden

    Sweden uses a yellow Nordic cross on blue. Finland's is blue on white, and Denmark's is white on red.

  10. Which country flies the oldest national flag still in use?

    Answer: Denmark

    Denmark's Dannebrog is traditionally traced to 1219, though the modern design was fixed in the nineteenth century.

  11. Which country's flag is red with a white crescent and star?

    Answer: Turkey

    Turkey's flag keeps the crescent and star of the Ottoman tradition on a plain red field, with the star turned outward.

  12. Which country's flag is a single large yellow star on a red field?

    Answer: Vietnam

    Vietnam's star has five points and sits centred on red. China's flag has five stars, one large and four small.

  13. Which country's flag has horizontal bands of black, red and gold?

    Answer: Germany

    Germany's flag runs black, red and gold horizontally. Belgium uses the same colours vertically.

  14. Which country's flag is blue with a red cross outlined in white?

    Answer: Iceland

    Iceland combined the Norwegian colour scheme with a white outline so the red cross would not vanish against the blue.

  15. Which country's flag shows a lion holding a sword?

    Answer: Sri Lanka

    Sri Lanka's flag has a gold lion on a maroon field, flanked by green and orange stripes for the country's other communities.

  16. Which two countries have a plain red-over-white horizontal flag?

    Answer: Monaco and Indonesia

    Monaco and Indonesia are both red over white; only the proportions differ. Poland's is white over red, which is the reverse.

  17. How many stars appear on the flag of China?

    Answer: Five

    China's flag has five yellow stars — one large star with four smaller ones arranged in an arc beside it.

  18. Which country replaced the Union Jack on its national flag in 1965?

    Answer: Canada

    Canada's maple leaf flag was adopted in February 1965 after a debate known as the Great Flag Debate, which ran for months.

  19. What is the wheel at the centre of India's flag called?

    Answer: Ashoka Chakra

    The Ashoka Chakra is a 24-spoke wheel taken from the Lion Capital at Sarnath, and it replaced the spinning wheel of the pre-independence design.

  20. Which country's flag is square rather than rectangular?

    Answer: Switzerland

    Switzerland's flag is square for both the national flag and the civil ensign, which is unusual — most countries use a longer rectangle at sea.

What this quiz covers

Flag questions are unforgiving in one specific way: a wrong option is usually a real flag that nearly fits. That is deliberate here — the distractors are countries with similar designs, so knowing the answer means knowing why the design belongs to that country.

Nothing in this quiz depends on a flag that changed recently. Where a country has altered its flag within living memory, the question is written about the change itself rather than the current design.

  • Plants and animals on flags: the maple leaf, the cedar of Lebanon, and the dragon of Bhutan.
  • The non-rectangular flag, and the square one that never adopted the rectangle in the first place.
  • Colour systems: the vertical tricolour of France, the black-red-gold of Germany, and the Nordic cross in Sweden and Iceland.
  • Religious and political symbols: the crescent and star of Pakistan and Turkey, the Ashoka Chakra of India, and the five stars of China.
  • Flag history: which national flag is the oldest still in use, and which country replaced the Union Jack on its flag in 1965.

Frequently asked questions

Which country has the only non-rectangular national flag?
Nepal. Its flag is a double pennant made of two stacked triangles, representing the Himalayas and the two main religions of the country. It is the only national flag that is not a quadrilateral.
What is the oldest national flag still in use?
The Dannebrog of Denmark, traditionally dated to 1219. The legend places its origin at the Battle of Lyndanisse, though the design as used today settled much later.
Which two countries have almost identical flags?
Monaco and Indonesia both fly a simple horizontal red over white. The difference is proportion — Monaco's is 4:5 and Indonesia's is 2:3 — which is impossible to spot on a flagpole.
Why does the United States flag have 50 stars?
One for each state. The number has changed with every admission, which is why the flag has been redesigned 27 times since 1777.
What does the maple leaf on the Canadian flag stand for?
The eleven-point maple leaf is a national symbol rather than a regional one; it was chosen in 1964 after a debate that ran for months and produced thousands of submissions.
