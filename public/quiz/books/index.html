Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Who wrote 'Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone'?

    Answer: J.K. Rowling

    J.K. Rowling wrote the first Harry Potter book, published in 1997.

  2. Which book series features a character named Harry Potter?

    Answer: Harry Potter

    The Harry Potter series, written by British author J.K. Rowling, follows the life of a young wizard, Harry Potter, and his friends at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

  3. Which book begins with the line 'It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a single man in possession of a good fortune, must be in want of a wife'?

    Answer: Pride and Prejudice

    This famous opening line is from Jane Austen's 'Pride and Prejudice' (1813).

  4. Which book is written by George Orwell and set in a dystopian future?

    Answer: 1984

    George Orwell's 1984 depicts a totalitarian society under constant surveillance, a defining dystopian novel.

  5. Who wrote the book 'The Great Gatsby'?

    Answer: F. Scott Fitzgerald

    F. Scott Fitzgerald wrote 'The Great Gatsby', published in 1925, a novel set in the Jazz Age that explores themes of wealth and the American Dream.

  6. Who wrote the novel '1984', which introduced the concept of 'Big Brother'?

    Answer: George Orwell

    George Orwell's '1984' is a dystopian novel that explores themes of totalitarianism and surveillance, coined with the term 'Big Brother'.

  7. The TV series 'Game of Thrones' is based on a book series by which author?

    Answer: George R.R. Martin

    George R.R. Martin wrote 'A Song of Ice and Fire', the series adapted into 'Game of Thrones'.

  8. Who wrote 'The Odyssey', one of the foundational works of ancient Greek literature?

    Answer: Homer

    'The Odyssey' is attributed to Homer and was composed around the 8th century BCE. It follows the hero Odysseus on his journey home after the Trojan War.

  9. The novel 'One Hundred Years of Solitude' by Gabriel García Márquez is a landmark work of which literary genre?

    Answer: Magical Realism

    Gabriel García Márquez's masterpiece is a foundational text of the literary genre known as magical realism, blending realistic narrative with surreal elements.

  10. Which novel is widely considered the first modern novel and follows the adventures of a young knight who sets out to revive chivalry?

    Answer: Don Quixote

    Don Quixote by Miguel de Cervantes, published in the early 17th century, is often called the first modern novel and follows the deluded knight Don Quixote and his squire Sancho Panza.

  11. The epic poem 'The Odyssey' is attributed to which ancient Greek poet?

    Answer: Homer

    'The Odyssey' is one of two major ancient Greek epic poems attributed to Homer, telling the story of Odysseus's journey home after the Trojan War.

  12. Who wrote the modernist novel 'Ulysses', set over a single day in Dublin?

    Answer: James Joyce

    James Joyce published Ulysses in 1922; its stream-of-consciousness technique and dense allusions made it a landmark of modernist fiction.

  13. Which author wrote the novel 'Things Fall Apart', a landmark work of African literature?

    Answer: Chinua Achebe

    Chinua Achebe of Nigeria published 'Things Fall Apart' in 1958. It has been translated into over 50 languages and is one of the most widely read works of African literature.

  14. Which writer authored the novel 'The Master and Margarita'?

    Answer: Mikhail Bulgakov

    The novel was written by Mikhail Bulgakov, although it was not published until after his death in 1966‑67.

  15. Which writer coined the term 'Big Brother' in his dystopian novel?

    Answer: George Orwell

    George Orwell introduced 'Big Brother' in his 1949 novel '1984'. The phrase has since entered everyday language to describe surveillance and authoritarian control.
