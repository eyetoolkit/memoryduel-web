Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the largest animal on Earth by weight?

    Answer: Blue whale

    The blue whale can reach over 150 tons, making it the heaviest animal known to have ever lived.

  2. Which of these animals is a marsupial?

    Answer: Kangaroo

    Kangaroos are marsupials, native to Australia, carrying their young in a pouch.

  3. Which animal is known for its black and white coloring and eats bamboo?

    Answer: Giant panda

    The giant panda is native to south central China and has a diet that is almost entirely bamboo.

  4. What is the tallest mammal?

    Answer: B) Giraffe

    Giraffes can reach heights of about 5–6 meters, making them the tallest mammals.

  5. Which of these is a mammal?

    Answer: Dolphin

    Dolphins are warm-blooded marine mammals that breathe air, nurse their young with milk, and have hair at some stage of life.

  6. Which of these animals is a mammal?

    Answer: Dolphin

    Dolphins are marine mammals that breathe air, give birth to live young, and produce milk.

  7. Which big cat can survive at altitudes above 6,000 m in the Himalayas?

    Answer: Snow leopard

    Snow leopards are adapted to high‑altitude, cold environments of Central Asia.

  8. Which of the following is the fastest land animal?

    Answer: Cheetah

    The Cheetah can reach speeds of up to 112 km/h (70 mph) in short bursts, making it the fastest land animal.

  9. Which of these animals is an example of a marsupial?

    Answer: Kangaroo

    Kangaroos are marsupials, a group of mammals characterized by giving birth to relatively undeveloped young that typically continue to develop in a pouch.

  10. Which of these animals is known for its ability to enter a state of torpor or hibernation during winter?

    Answer: Arctic fox

    Arctic foxes can enter a state of torpor, lowering their metabolic rate to conserve energy in harsh winters.

  11. Which animal is known for having the longest migration among mammals?

    Answer: Humpback whale

    Humpback whales migrate over 8,000 km annually between feeding and breeding grounds.

  12. What is the largest terrestrial carnivore?

    Answer: Polar bear

    Polar bears are the largest land carnivores, weighing up to 700 kg.

  13. Which of the following animals is a marsupial?

    Answer: Kangaroo

    Marsupials are mammals that carry their young in a pouch. Kangaroos are the most well-known example.

  14. Which of these is the largest mammal in the ocean?

    Answer: Blue Whale

    The blue whale is the largest mammal in the ocean and also the largest animal ever known to have lived.

  15. Which of these animals is capable of true flight?

    Answer: Bat

    Bats are the only mammals capable of true, sustained flight. Other animals like flying squirrels and sugar gliders can only glide for short distances.
