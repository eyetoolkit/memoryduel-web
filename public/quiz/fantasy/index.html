Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In the Harry Potter film series, which actor plays the role of Albus Dumbledore?

    Answer: Richard Harris and Michael Gambon

    Richard Harris played Dumbledore in the first two films, and after his death, Michael Gambon took over the role for the remaining movies.

  2. Which movie franchise features a character named Harry Potter?

    Answer: Harry Potter

    The Harry Potter film series is based on J.K. Rowling's novels about a young wizard attending Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

  3. The line 'Winter is coming' is a famous quote from which television series?

    Answer: Game of Thrones

    The Stark family's motto appears throughout HBO's 'Game of Thrones'.

  4. Who wrote the book 'Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone'?

    Answer: J.K. Rowling

    J.K. Rowling wrote the Harry Potter series, which became one of the best-selling book series in history, translated into over 80 languages.

  5. What is the name of the fictional magical school in the 'Harry Potter' series?

    Answer: Hogwarts

    Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry is the primary setting for the Harry Potter series, where young witches and wizards learn magic.

  6. In the TV series 'Game of Thrones', which family's sigil features a three-headed dragon?

    Answer: House Targaryen

    House Targaryen's sigil is a red three-headed dragon on a black field, representing their dragons and ancestral words 'Fire and Blood'.

  7. In what year did the original 'Star Wars' (Episode IV: A New Hope) premiere in theaters?

    Answer: 1977

    The original Star Wars was released in 1977, launching the iconic saga.

  8. Which TV show, set in a fantasy world, is based on George R.R. Martin's book series 'A Song of Ice and Fire'?

    Answer: Game of Thrones

    'Game of Thrones' aired on HBO from 2011 to 2019 and was adapted from Martin's epic fantasy book series.

  9. Which director is famous for the 'Lord of the Rings' trilogy and 'The Hobbit' trilogy?

    Answer: Peter Jackson

    Peter Jackson directed both trilogies based on J.R.R. Tolkien's works, winning multiple Oscars for 'The Return of the King'.

  10. What is the original runtime (in minutes) of the extended edition of "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King"?

    Answer: 263

    The extended edition of The Return of the King runs 263 minutes, making it the longest of the three extended LOTR films.

  11. In the board game Gloomhaven, what is the maximum number of players supported in a standard game?

    Answer: 4

    Gloomhaven supports 1 to 4 players in its standard configuration, with optional rules for more players.

  12. Which film trilogy was directed by Peter Jackson and based on works by J.R.R. Tolkien?

    Answer: The Lord of the Rings

    Peter Jackson directed 'The Lord of the Rings' trilogy (2001–2003), which won 17 Academy Awards including Best Picture for 'The Return of the King'.

  13. Who directed the 2006 film 'Pan's Labyrinth' (El laberinto del fauno)?

    Answer: Guillermo del Toro

    Mexican director Guillermo del Toro wrote and directed this dark fantasy set during the Spanish Civil War.

  14. In the video game series The Legend of Zelda, what is the name of the main antagonist?

    Answer: Ganondorf

    Ganondorf is the primary recurring villain in The Legend of Zelda series, often seeking the Triforce and threatening the kingdom of Hyrule.

  15. Which actress received an Oscar nomination for her role in "The Shape of Water" but did not win?

    Answer: Sally Hawkins

    Sally Hawkins was nominated for Best Actress for her lead performance in The Shape of Water, but the award went to Frances McDormand.
