Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. The Renaissance movement originated in which country?

    Answer: Italy

    The Renaissance began in Italy in the 14th century, particularly in Florence, before spreading to the rest of Europe.

  2. Which battle in 1066 marked the Norman conquest of England?

    Answer: Battle of Hastings

    The Battle of Hastings, fought on October 14, 1066, resulted in the Norman victory.

  3. Which empire was led by Genghis Khan?

    Answer: Mongol Empire

    Genghis Khan founded the Mongol Empire in 1206, which became the largest contiguous land empire in history.

  4. The Battle of Hastings in 1066 was fought between which two sides?

    Answer: Normans vs Saxons

    William the Conqueror led the Normans against the Anglo‑Saxon forces of King Harold.

  5. The Black Death pandemic in Europe occurred primarily during which century?

    Answer: The 14th century

    The Black Death, caused by the bubonic plague, swept through Europe between 1347 and 1351, decimating the population.

  6. Which document, signed in 1215, limited the power of the English monarchy and is considered a foundation of constitutional law?

    Answer: Magna Carta

    The Magna Carta (1215) established that the king was not above the law, influencing constitutional development worldwide.

  7. The 'Magna Carta', a charter of rights, was signed in which country in 1215?

    Answer: England

    The Magna Carta was sealed by King John of England at Runnymede, establishing the principle that everyone was subject to the law.

  8. In what year did Constantinople fall to the Ottoman Turks?

    Answer: 1453

    Constantinople fell on May 29, 1453, when Sultan Mehmed II conquered the city, ending the 1,100-year Byzantine Empire and establishing the Ottoman Empire as a major power.

  9. During which decade did the Ming dynasty's treasure voyages under Zheng He occur?

    Answer: 1400–1410

    Zheng He's first voyage set out in 1405, placing the voyages primarily in the early 15th century.

  10. Which battle in 732 CE is often considered the high‑point of the Umayyad Caliphate's expansion into Western Europe before its reversal?

    Answer: Battle of Tours

    The Frankish victory at Tours (also called Poitiers) halted the Islamic expansion into Gaul.

  11. The Battle of Hastings in 1066 determined the new king of which country?

    Answer: England

    The Battle of Hastings in 1066 resulted in the defeat of King Harold II by William the Conqueror, making William the new King of England.

  12. Which document, signed in 1215, established that the king was not above the law in England?

    Answer: Magna Carta

    The Magna Carta was a charter of rights agreed to by King John of England, limiting royal authority.

  13. The ancient city of Ani, once known as the 'City of 1,001 Churches,' was located on the border of which two modern countries?

    Answer: Turkey and Armenia

    Ani was the capital of the Bagratid Kingdom of Armenia and is located on the modern Turkish-Armenian border. It was a major trading center on the Silk Road.

  14. Who wrote 'The Divine Comedy', an epic poem describing a journey through Hell, Purgatory, and Paradise?

    Answer: Dante Alighieri

    Italian poet Dante Alighieri wrote 'The Divine Comedy' in the early 14th century. It is considered one of the greatest works of world literature and a cornerstone of the Italian language.

  15. Which religious schism in 1054 AD permanently divided the Christian Church into two major branches?

    Answer: The East-West Schism

    The East-West Schism, also known as the Great Schism, separated the Roman Catholic Church in the West from the Eastern Orthodox Church.
