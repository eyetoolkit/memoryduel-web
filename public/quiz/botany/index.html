Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which part of a plant is primarily responsible for absorbing water from the soil?

    Answer: Root

    Roots anchor the plant and absorb water and minerals from the soil.

  2. Which gas do plants primarily take in from the atmosphere for photosynthesis?

    Answer: C) Carbon dioxide

    Carbon dioxide is the carbon source used in the light‑independent reactions of photosynthesis.

  3. What is the main function of the root system in plants?

    Answer: Water and nutrient uptake

    Roots absorb water and minerals from the soil for the plant.

  4. In desert environments, which seed dispersal mechanism is most common among annual plants?

    Answer: A. Wind dispersal (anemochory)

    Many desert annuals produce lightweight seeds that are carried by wind to new locations.

  5. What is the process by which green plants convert sunlight into chemical energy?

    Answer: Photosynthesis

    Photosynthesis uses sunlight to synthesize nutrients from carbon dioxide and water.

  6. What is the only fruit that grows naturally on all continents except Antarctica?

    Answer: Strawberry

    Strawberries grow naturally on every continent except Antarctica.

  7. Cluster roots, found in some plants like the Proteaceae, are specialized for:

    Answer: Phosphorus acquisition

    Cluster roots exude large amounts of carboxylates and phosphatases to mobilize and uptake phosphate from impoverished soils.

  8. What is the process called when a plant grows toward a light source?

    Answer: Phototropism

    Phototropism is the directional growth of a plant in response to light, driven by the hormone auxin.

  9. Which type of pollination involves the transfer of pollen by water?

    Answer: B. Hydrophily

    Hydrophily is water‑mediated pollen transport, seen in some aquatic plant species.

  10. Which process is responsible for transporting sugars from photosynthetic leaves to other parts of a plant?

    Answer: Phloem translocation

    Phloem translocation moves photosynthates from sources to sinks throughout the plant.

  11. Which photoreceptor protein mediates plant phototropism by triggering asymmetric auxin distribution?

    Answer: Phototropin

    Phototropin perceives blue light and leads to lateral transport of auxin.

  12. Which plant hormone is primarily responsible for stimulating cell elongation in response to light (phototropism)?

    Answer: Auxin

    Auxin redistributes to the shaded side of a stem, promoting longer cell elongation and bending toward light.

  13. Which enzyme is the primary carbon-fixing enzyme in C4 plants?

    Answer: PEP carboxylase

    PEP carboxylase fixes CO2 into a four-carbon compound in C4 photosynthesis.

  14. What is the 'photoperiod' effect on plants?

    Answer: The effect of day length on plant processes like flowering

    The photoperiod is the length of daily exposure to light. Many plants use changes in day length as a signal to trigger processes such as flowering or dormancy.

  15. Early‑successional species in post‑fire succession most likely exhibit which combination of traits?

    Answer: Rapid growth, high light tolerance, prolific seed production

    Early successional plants prioritize quick colonization and reproduction in high‑light, disturbed environments.
