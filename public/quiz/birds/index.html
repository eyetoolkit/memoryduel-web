Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which bird is famous for its ability to mimic human speech?

    Answer: Parrot

    Parrots have specialized vocal learning abilities that enable them to imitate human words and sounds.

  2. What is the primary purpose of a bird's feathers?

    Answer: Thermoregulation

    While feathers assist in flight and display, their primary evolutionary function was insulation for thermoregulation.

  3. Which of these is a bird?

    Answer: Penguin

    Penguins are flightless birds, distinguished by feathers, beaks, and laying eggs.

  4. Which of these is NOT a real bird?

    Answer: Phoenix

    The phoenix is a mythical bird from ancient legends, while the others are real species.

  5. Which of these is NOT a mammal?

    Answer: Penguin

    Penguins are birds, whereas dolphins, bats, and whales are all mammals, meaning they are warm-blooded and nurse their young with milk.

  6. Which of these birds is known for migrating the longest distances?

    Answer: Arctic tern

    The Arctic tern migrates from Arctic to Antarctic and back each year, covering about 70,000 km.

  7. Which of these birds is incapable of flying?

    Answer: Penguin

    Penguins are flightless birds that have adapted their wings into flippers for swimming rather than flying.

  8. Which animal is known for making the longest annual migration of any animal?

    Answer: Arctic Tern

    The Arctic Tern makes the longest migration of any animal, traveling from its breeding grounds in the Arctic to winter in the Antarctic and back, a distance of about 44,000 miles annually.

  9. Which living bird has the largest wingspan?

    Answer: Wandering albatross

    The wandering albatross can reach a wingspan of up to 3.7 meters, the greatest of any extant bird.

  10. Which animal group is characterized by having feathers?

    Answer: Birds

    Feathers are unique to birds, serving functions in flight, insulation, and display.

  11. Which bird is known for its incredible migration, traveling from the Arctic to the Antarctic and back?

    Answer: Arctic Tern

    The Arctic Tern has the longest migration of any animal, traveling pole to pole.

  12. Which of these animals is known to travel the longest distance during migration?

    Answer: Arctic Tern

    The Arctic Tern migrates from Arctic to Antarctic and back annually, covering roughly 70,000 km each year.

  13. Which animal holds the record for the longest continuous non‑stop flight?

    Answer: B) Common swift

    The common swift can stay airborne for up to 200 days without landing.

  14. Which bird can fly backward?

    Answer: Hummingbird

    Hummingbirds are the only birds capable of sustained backward flight, thanks to their unique shoulder joint rotation.

  15. Which animal is capable of true powered flight?

    Answer: Frigatebird

    While flying squirrels, sugar gliders, and colugos can glide, only birds like the frigatebird, along with bats and insects, are capable of true powered flight.
