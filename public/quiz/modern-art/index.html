Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What did Pablo Picasso co-found in the early 20th century?

    Answer: Cubism

    Picasso, along with Georges Braque, co-founded Cubism, revolutionizing modern art by depicting multiple viewpoints simultaneously.

  2. Which of these is a famous painting by Pablo Picasso depicting the bombing of a town during the Spanish Civil War?

    Answer: Guernica

    Guernica (1937) is Picasso's monumental anti-war masterpiece depicting the bombing of the Basque town of Guernica by Nazi Germany.

  3. In which art form did Pablo Picasso primarily work?

    Answer: Painting

    While Picasso also sculpted and made prints, he is most renowned as a painter and one of the co-founders of the Cubist movement.

  4. Which Norwegian painter created the iconic expressionist work 'The Scream'?

    Answer: Edvard Munch

    Edvard Munch, a Norwegian artist, created 'The Scream', a seminal expressionist painting.

  5. Which Spanish painter is known for co-founding the Cubist movement?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso, along with Georges Braque, co-founded Cubism, revolutionizing 20th-century art.

  6. Which artist co-founded Cubism with Georges Braque?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso and Georges Braque co-founded Cubism in the early 1900s.

  7. In which art movement is Salvador Dalí primarily associated with, known for melting clocks?

    Answer: Surrealism

    Salvador Dalí was a leading figure in Surrealism, famous for dream-like and bizarre imagery like the melting clocks in 'The Persistence of Memory'.

  8. Which artist is known for his 'Blue Period' paintings?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso's Blue Period (1901-1904) featured somber works dominated by shades of blue and blue-green.

  9. Which artist painted 'Les Demoiselles d'Avignon', a groundbreaking work of modern art?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    'Les Demoiselles d'Avignon' (1907) is a painting by Spanish artist Pablo Picasso, widely considered one of the most influential works of 20th-century art and a precursor to Cubism.

  10. Frida Kahlo, known for her striking self-portraits, was from which country?

    Answer: Mexico

    Frida Kahlo was a Mexican painter celebrated for her deeply personal self-portraits exploring identity, pain, and Mexican culture. She is one of the most prominent Latin American artists in history.

  11. Which artist is famous for using bold colors and cut-out shapes in works like 'The Snail'?

    Answer: Henri Matisse

    Henri Matisse, in his later years, created vibrant paper cut-out compositions, including 'The Snail' (1953), due to failing health.

  12. Which painter of 'Les Demoiselles d'Avignon' is widely regarded as one of the most influential artists of the 20th century?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Les Demoiselles d'Avignon' in 1907. The work is considered a foundational piece of Cubism and a major turning point in the history of modern art.

  13. Which artist created the famous sculpture 'The Thinker'?

    Answer: Auguste Rodin

    Auguste Rodin created 'The Thinker' originally in 1902. The bronze sculpture depicts a man in deep contemplation resting his chin on his hand, and has become an iconic symbol of philosophy and intellectual thought.

  14. Which artist painted the large mural 'Guernica'?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in 1937 in response to the bombing of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War.

  15. Which artist's 1917 work, an ordinary porcelain urinal submitted to an art exhibition under a pseudonym, became one of the most iconic pieces of 20th-century art?

    Answer: Marcel Duchamp — Fountain

    Marcel Duchamp submitted Fountain, a signed porcelain urinal, to the Society of Independent Artists exhibition in 1917 under the name 'R. Mutt.' It challenged conventional definitions of art and is a foundational work of Dada and conceptual art.
