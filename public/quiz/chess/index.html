Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In the game Chess, what is the maximum value a pawn can be promoted to?

    Answer: Queen

    When a pawn reaches the opposite end of the board, it can be promoted to any piece except a king, most commonly a queen.

  2. In chess, what is the term for moving the king two squares toward a rook and moving the rook next to the king?

    Answer: Castling

    Castling is a special move in chess that involves the king and a rook.

  3. In the game of Chess, which piece is worth the most points?

    Answer: Queen

    The Queen is the most powerful piece in Chess, typically valued at 9 points.

  4. In the board game Chess, how many squares does a standard board have?

    Answer: 64

    A standard chess board is an 8x8 grid, totaling 64 squares.

  5. Which traditional board game originated in India around the 6th century and involves capturing the opponent's king?

    Answer: Chess

    Chess originated in India around the 6th century as 'Chaturanga' and evolved into its modern form, with the goal of checkmating the opponent's king.

  6. In chess, which piece can only move diagonally across the board?

    Answer: Bishop

    The bishop moves any number of squares diagonally, staying on squares of the same color throughout the game.

  7. In chess, which piece can jump over other pieces in an 'L' shape?

    Answer: Knight

    The Knight is the only chess piece that can jump over other pieces, moving in an L-shape pattern.

  8. In chess notation, what does 'O-O' represent?

    Answer: Castling kingside

    'O-O' is the standard algebraic notation for kingside castling, while 'O-O-O' represents queenside castling.

  9. In chess, what is the term for a move that simultaneously checks the opponent's king and attacks another piece?

    Answer: Fork

    A fork is a tactic where a single piece attacks two or more enemy pieces simultaneously, often including the king.

  10. In the chess opening known as the 'Ruy Lopez', which piece is moved first by White?

    Answer: King's Pawn

    The Ruy Lopez (Spanish Game) begins with 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5, starting with White's King's Pawn to e4.

  11. In competitive chess, what is the minimum number of moves required to force checkmate from the starting position with optimal play from both sides (the 'God's number' for chess)?

    Answer: There is no guaranteed forced mate

    With perfect play from both sides, chess from the starting position is a draw, so there is no forced checkmate.

  12. What is the official governing body for chess, headquartered in Lausanne, Switzerland?

    Answer: FIDE

    FIDE (Fédération Internationale des Échecs) is the international organization connecting national chess federations and running world championships.

  13. Which board game uses a 64-square checkered board and was referenced in Lewis Carroll's 'Through the Looking-Glass'?

    Answer: Chess

    Through the Looking-Glass uses a chessboard as its structural framework, with Alice moving across squares as a white pawn.

  14. Which company developed the game Chess, and in what century did it originate?

    Answer: No single company; originated in India in the 6th century

    Chess originated in India around the 6th century AD as 'Chaturanga' and evolved over centuries through Persia and the Arab world before reaching Europe. It was not created by any single company or individual.

  15. In chess, what is the term for a situation where a player's king is under attack and the king cannot escape?

    Answer: Checkmate

    Checkmate occurs when a king is in check and there is no legal move to escape, ending the game. Stalemate is when a player is not in check but has no legal moves, resulting in a draw.
