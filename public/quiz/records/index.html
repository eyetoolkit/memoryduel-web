Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the best-selling album of all time worldwide?

    Answer: Thriller by Michael Jackson

    Michael Jackson's 'Thriller' has sold over 70 million copies worldwide, making it the best-selling album of all time.

  2. Which pop artist holds the record for the most Grammy Awards won in a single night?

    Answer: Beyoncé

    Beyoncé holds the record with 6 Grammy Awards won in a single night in 2010.

  3. Which country has won the most FIFA World Cup titles as of 2024?

    Answer: Brazil

    Brazil has won the FIFA World Cup five times (1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002), more than any other nation. Germany and Italy have four each, and Argentina has three (as of 2024).

  4. What is the record for the most viewed YouTube video (as of 2024)?

    Answer: Baby Shark Dance

    Baby Shark Dance became the most viewed YouTube video, surpassing Despacito.

  5. Which of these animals is known to sleep the most hours per day?

    Answer: Brown Bat

    Brown bats are among the sleepiest mammals, often sleeping nearly 20 hours a day during daylight.

  6. What is the tallest animal on Earth?

    Answer: Giraffe

    Adult giraffes can grow up to about 5.7 metres (18.7 ft) tall, making them the tallest living terrestrial animals.

  7. Which athlete holds the world record for the men's 100 metres sprint with a time of 9.58 seconds?

    Answer: Usain Bolt

    Usain Bolt of Jamaica set the 100m world record of 9.58 seconds at the 2009 World Championships in Berlin.

  8. What is the largest desert in the world?

    Answer: Antarctic Desert

    Antarctica is technically the largest desert at about 14 million km², since a desert is defined by low precipitation, not temperature.

  9. Which is the fastest recorded land animal in terms of top speed?

    Answer: Cheetah

    The Cheetah is the fastest land animal, capable of reaching speeds up to 70 mph (112 km/h).

  10. Which country has won the most Olympic gold medals in men's 4x100m freestyle relay swimming history?

    Answer: United States

    The United States has historically dominated the men's 4x100m freestyle relay, winning the event more times than any other nation.

  11. In Formula One racing, which country has produced the most World Drivers' Championship winners?

    Answer: United Kingdom

    The United Kingdom has produced the most Formula One World Drivers' Championship winners, including legends like Lewis Hamilton, Jackie Stewart, and Jim Clark.

  12. Which athlete holds the world record for the most Olympic gold medals won by an individual?

    Answer: Michael Phelps

    Michael Phelps holds the record with 23 Olympic gold medals, the most by any individual athlete.

  13. Which esports tournament holds the record for the largest prize pool in esports history (as of 2023)?

    Answer: The International (Dota 2)

    The International for Dota 2, funded by community crowdfunding, has repeatedly set records for the highest prize pool in esports history.

  14. In which sport did Florence Griffith-Joyner set the women's 100m world record that still stands?

    Answer: Athletics

    Florence Griffith-Joyner set the women's 100m world record of 10.49 seconds in 1988, a record that remains unbroken.

  15. In Formula One racing, which driver holds the record for most career race wins as of 2024?

    Answer: Lewis Hamilton

    Lewis Hamilton holds the record for the most Grand Prix wins in Formula One history, surpassing Michael Schumacher's previous record of 91 wins.
