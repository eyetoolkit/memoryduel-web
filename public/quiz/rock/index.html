Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which British band is fronted by Bono?

    Answer: U2

    Bono is the lead vocalist of U2, formed in Dublin in 1976, known for hits like 'With or Without You' and 'One.'

  2. Which band released the 1971 album 'Stairway to Heaven' on their untitled fourth album?

    Answer: Led Zeppelin

    'Stairway to Heaven' is one of the most iconic rock songs ever recorded, featured on Led Zeppelin IV (1971).

  3. What is the name of the famous rock band that performed 'Bohemian Rhapsody'?

    Answer: Queen

    'Bohemian Rhapsody' was released by Queen in 1975 and became one of the best-known rock songs ever.

  4. Which famous pop song by Queen is often heard at sporting events and features a long, operatic section in the middle?

    Answer: 'Bohemian Rhapsody'

    'Bohemian Rhapsody' is renowned for its complex structure, including a distinctive operatic passage, making it a stadium anthem.

  5. Which band is famous for the 1991 grunge anthem 'Smells Like Teen Spirit'?

    Answer: Nirvana

    Nirvana's 'Smells Like Teen Spirit' defined the early 1990s grunge movement.

  6. Which artist is famous for the 1969 album 'Abbey Road'?

    Answer: The Beatles

    'Abbey Road' is the eleventh studio album by the English rock band The Beatles.

  7. Which famous guitar brand is associated with Jimi Hendrix's iconic white Stratocaster?

    Answer: Fender

    Jimi Hendrix famously played a Fender Stratocaster, including the white one he set alight at Monterey.

  8. Which group's members included Freddie Mercury, Brian May, Roger Taylor and John Deacon?

    Answer: Queen

    Those four formed the core lineup of Queen.

  9. Which song features the famous lyrics 'Is this the real life? Is this just fantasy?'

    Answer: 'Bohemian Rhapsody' by Queen

    These iconic opening lyrics belong to Queen's 'Bohemian Rhapsody', written by Freddie Mercury.

  10. Which legendary guitarist is best known as the frontman of the band Queen?

    Answer: Freddie Mercury

    Freddie Mercury was the charismatic lead vocalist and pianist of the British rock band Queen.

  11. Which band's original lineup included Roger Waters, Nick Mason, and Syd Barrett?

    Answer: Pink Floyd

    Pink Floyd was formed in London in 1965 by Waters, Mason, Barrett, and Richard Wright.

  12. In which year did The Beatles release their debut album 'Please Please Me'?

    Answer: 1963

    Please Please Me was released on 22 January 1963.

  13. Which guitarist is famous for his performance of 'Star-Spangled Banner' at Woodstock in 1969?

    Answer: Jimi Hendrix

    Jimi Hendrix's electric guitar rendition of 'The Star-Spangled Banner' at Woodstock became one of the most iconic moments in rock history.

  14. Which of these is the correct chronological order of these landmark albums (earliest to latest)?

    Answer: The Dark Side of the Moon → Abbey Road → Thriller → Nevermind

    The Dark Side of the Moon (1973) came first, then Abbey Road (1969) is earlier—actually Abbey Road was 1969, Dark Side 1973, Thriller 1982, Nevermind 1991. Correct order: Abbey Road (1969) → Dark Side of the Moon (1973) → Thriller (1982) → Nevermind (1991).

  15. Which artist released the concept album 'The Rise and Fall of Ziggy Stardust and the Spiders from Mars' (1972)?

    Answer: David Bowie

    David Bowie created the Ziggy Stardust persona for this landmark glam rock album.
