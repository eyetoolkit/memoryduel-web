Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What animal represents the Chinese Zodiac for the year 2024?

    Answer: Dragon

    2024 is the Year of the Dragon, a powerful symbol in Chinese culture representing strength and good fortune.

  2. What is the traditional greeting gesture where palms are pressed together, common in India and Southeast Asia?

    Answer: Namaste / Wai

    In India, this gesture is called 'Namaste', and in Thailand, it is called a 'Wai'. It signifies respect and greeting.

  3. Which war was fought between 1950 and 1953 on the Korean Peninsula?

    Answer: Korean War

    The Korean War was fought from 1950 to 1953 between North Korea and South Korea, involving UN and Chinese forces.

  4. Which festival marks the beginning of the lunar new year in many East Asian cultures?

    Answer: Lunar New Year (Spring Festival)

    Lunar New Year (also known as Chinese New Year or Spring Festival) is celebrated across East Asia.

  5. The Japanese art of folding paper into decorative shapes is known as what?

    Answer: Origami

    Origami is the traditional Japanese art of paper folding, transforming flat sheets into intricate sculptures without cutting or gluing.

  6. Which country is home to Mount Fuji?

    Answer: Japan

    Mount Fuji is Japan's tallest mountain and an iconic symbol of the country.

  7. Which empire was ruled by Genghis Khan and his successors in the 13th century?

    Answer: The Mongol Empire

    The Mongol Empire, founded by Genghis Khan in 1206, became the largest contiguous land empire in history, stretching from Europe to East Asia.

  8. Which country was formerly known as Siam?

    Answer: Thailand

    Thailand was formerly known as Siam until 1939, when it was officially renamed.

  9. The Opium Wars were fought between China and which power?

    Answer: Britain

    The Opium Wars (1839–1842 and 1856–1860) were fought between Qing China and Great Britain over trade and sovereignty.

  10. The Meiji Restoration transformed which country into a modern industrial power?

    Answer: Japan

    The Meiji Restoration in 1868 modernized Japan's government, military, and industry, making it a global power.

  11. The Meiji Restoration in 1868 marked the beginning of the modernization of which nation?

    Answer: Japan

    The Meiji Restoration restored imperial rule in Japan under Emperor Meiji and initiated a period of rapid industrialization and Westernization, transforming Japan into a major world power.

  12. Which country does the Mekong River NOT flow through?

    Answer: Myanmar

    The Mekong River flows through China, Myanmar, Laos, Thailand, Cambodia, and Vietnam. It does not flow through Myanmar's mainland territory in any significant way, but it does form a short stretch of the Myanmar–Laos border, making this a tricky question. However, it is generally considered to flow through 6 countries, and Myanmar is sometimes debated.

  13. Which country is both in Europe and Asia, with its capital Astana?

    Answer: Kazakhstan

    Kazakhstan is a transcontinental country; its capital is Astana (renamed Nur-Sultan 2019-2022, now Astana again).

  14. The concept of 'Mandate of Heaven' was used to justify political authority in which civilization?

    Answer: Ancient China

    The Mandate of Heaven was a Chinese political and religious doctrine used to justify the authority of the ruler, originating with the Zhou Dynasty (1046-256 BCE). It held that heaven granted the right to rule to a just leader, and could be revoked if the ruler became tyrannical.

  15. The Harappan Civilization flourished primarily along which river?

    Answer: The Indus

    The Harappan (or Indus Valley) Civilization developed along the Indus River and its tributaries in modern-day Pakistan and India.
