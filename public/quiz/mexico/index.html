Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the name of the 'Day of the Dead' celebration in Mexico?

    Answer: Día de los Muertos

    Día de los Muertos is a time for families to remember and honor deceased loved ones.

  2. What is the traditional Mexican holiday that honors deceased family members with altars, food, and celebrations?

    Answer: Día de los Muertos

    Día de los Muertos (Day of the Dead) is a Mexican tradition where families build colorful altars (ofrendas) to honor and remember deceased loved ones, believing their spirits return to visit.

  3. In which country did the tradition of the 'Day of the Dead' originate?

    Answer: Mexico

    Día de los Muertos (Day of the Dead) is a Mexican tradition honoring deceased loved ones with altars and celebrations.

  4. What is the 'Day of the Dead' (Día de los Muertos) primarily celebrated for?

    Answer: Honoring the deceased

    Día de los Muertos is a Mexican holiday where families gather to remember and honor their deceased loved ones with altars and offerings.

  5. The Mexican festival honoring deceased ancestors, featuring colorful sugar skulls and marigolds, is called:

    Answer: Día de los Muertos

    Día de los Muertos (Day of the Dead) blends indigenous and Catholic traditions to celebrate and remember departed family members.

  6. Which festival is associated with the wearing of masks and celebrating the dead in Mexican culture?

    Answer: Día de los Muertos

    Día de los Muertos (Day of the Dead) is a Mexican tradition where families honor deceased loved ones with altars, marigolds, and skull-shaped decorations.

  7. What is the name of the Mexican festival that honors deceased loved ones with altars and marigolds?

    Answer: Día de los Muertos

    Día de los Muertos (Day of the Dead) is a celebration where families welcome back the souls of deceased relatives for a brief reunion.

  8. Which African festival celebrates the spiritual bond between the living and the dead, often involving colorful parades and feasts?

    Answer: Día de los Muertos

    Día de los Muertos (Day of the Dead) is a Mexican festival honoring deceased loved ones with altars, parades, and feasts.

  9. What is the significance of the 'Ofrenda' in the Mexican celebration of Día de los Muertos?

    Answer: An altar honoring the dead

    The Ofrenda is a multi-level altar decorated with photos, candles, marigolds, and the favorite foods of deceased relatives to honor them.

  10. Which river forms much of the border between the United States and Mexico?

    Answer: Rio Grande

    The Rio Grande forms a significant portion of the border between the United States and Mexico.

  11. What is the traditional Mexican wrestling sport known for its colorful masks called?

    Answer: Lucha Libre

    Lucha Libre is a form of professional wrestling originating in Mexico, famous for its masked luchadores.

  12. The 'Danza de los Viejitos' (Dance of the Little Old Men) is a traditional folk dance from which country?

    Answer: Mexico

    The Danza de los Viejitos is a traditional Michoacán folk dance from Mexico, where dancers perform wearing masks of old men.

  13. The 'Fiesta de la Candelaria' in Mexico, where the Virgin of Charity is paraded, usually takes place in which month?

    Answer: February

    Celebrated on February 2nd, it blends Catholic beliefs with indigenous traditions, often featuring traditional dance.

  14. The 'Día de la Virgen de Guadalupe' is a major religious celebration in Mexico honoring the Virgin Mary. On which date is it celebrated?

    Answer: December 12th

    The feast of Our Lady of Guadalupe is celebrated on December 12th, honoring the apparition of the Virgin Mary to Juan Diego in 1531. It is one of the most important religious celebrations in Mexico.

  15. In ancient Aztec culture, which festival honored the god Huitzilopochtli and involved ritual ceremonies at the Templo Mayor?

    Answer: Panquetzaliztli

    Panquetzaliztli was a major Aztec festival in December celebrating Huitzilopochtli, the god of sun and war, featuring elaborate processions and rituals.
