Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the name of the Jewish New Year festival?

    Answer: Rosh Hashanah

    Rosh Hashanah, meaning 'head of the year,' is the Jewish New Year. It marks the beginning of the High Holy Days or 'Days of Awe', ending ten days later with Yom Kippur, the Day of Atonement.

  2. Hanukkah, an eight-day Jewish festival, commemorates which historical event?

    Answer: The rededication of the Second Temple in Jerusalem

    Hanukkah celebrates the rededication of the Second Temple in Jerusalem after the Maccabean Revolt. The Exodus is celebrated at Passover, the Ten Commandments at Shavuot, and the destruction of the First Temple is mourned on Tisha B'Av.

  3. During the Jewish festival of Hanukkah, which of the following is traditionally fried in oil to commemorate the miracle of the temple?

    Answer: Latkes

    Latkes (potato pancakes) are a traditional food for Hanukkah, fried in oil to celebrate the miracle of the oil lasting eight nights in the rededicated Second Temple.

  4. What is the main spiritual focus of the Jewish holiday of Yom Kippur?

    Answer: Fasting and atonement

    Yom Kippur, the Day of Atonement, is the holiest day in Judaism, focused on repentance and fasting from sundown to sundown.

  5. What is the main food traditionally eaten during the Jewish festival of Passover to represent the bread the Israelites baked in haste when leaving Egypt?

    Answer: Matzo (unleavened bread)

    Matzo is unleavened bread, eaten during Passover to commemorate the hurried exodus from Egypt when there was no time for dough to rise.

  6. What is the primary symbol of the Jewish holiday of Hanukkah?

    Answer: Menorah

    The menorah, a nine‑branched candelabrum, is the central symbol lit during Hanukkah.

  7. In the Jewish tradition, which event celebrates the deliverance of the Israelites from slavery in Egypt?

    Answer: Passover

    Passover (Pesach) commemorates the Israelites' Exodus from Egypt and is marked by the Seder meal.

  8. What is the Jewish festival of lights called, where a menorah is lit for eight nights?

    Answer: Hanukkah

    Hanukkah lasts eight nights and commemorates the rededication of the Second Temple in Jerusalem.

  9. In Judaism, what is the name of the eight-day festival commemorating the rededication of the Second Temple in Jerusalem?

    Answer: Hanukkah

    Hanukkah, also called the Festival of Lights, lasts eight nights and is celebrated by lighting a menorah, eating fried foods, and giving gifts.

  10. What is the significance of the 'Seder' meal in Jewish tradition?

    Answer: It commemorates the Exodus from Egypt

    The Seder is a ritual feast that begins the Jewish holiday of Passover, commemorating the Israelites' liberation from slavery in Egypt.

  11. In Judaism, what is the name of the week-long festival commemorating the Israelites' exodus from Egypt?

    Answer: Passover (Pesach)

    Passover (Pesach) commemorates the liberation of the Israelites from slavery in ancient Egypt.

  12. What is the significance of the Day of Atonement (Yom Kippur) in Judaism?

    Answer: Fasting and repentance

    Yom Kippur is the holiest day in Judaism, marked by fasting, prayer, and repentance for sins.

  13. Which religious text is considered the central scripture of Judaism?

    Answer: The Torah

    The Torah, comprising the five books of Moses, is the foundational religious text of Judaism.

  14. What is the name of the Jewish ritual bath used for purification, separate from regular bathing?

    Answer: Mikveh

    A Mikveh is a pool of water used for the purpose of ritual immersion in Judaism to achieve ritual purity.

  15. Which religious tradition involves the practice of 'Tzedakah', or giving to the poor?

    Answer: Judaism

    Tzedakah is a fundamental Jewish obligation involving charitable giving, often considered a form of social justice.
