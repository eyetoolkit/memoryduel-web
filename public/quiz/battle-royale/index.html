Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which video game franchise features a battle royale mode with building mechanics and over 100 million players?

    Answer: Fortnite

    Fortnite by Epic Games popularized building mechanics in its battle royale mode and became a global phenomenon.

  2. Which esports title is primarily associated with the 'Battle Royale' genre?

    Answer: PUBG

    PUBG (PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds) popularized the Battle Royale genre in competitive gaming.

  3. What is the most popular game mode in Fortnite that pits 100 players against each other?

    Answer: Battle Royale

    Fortnite's Battle Royale mode is its flagship experience, featuring 100 players competing to be the last one standing.

  4. Which popular video game is famous for the phrase 'Winner Winner, Chicken Dinner'?

    Answer: PUBG: Battlegrounds

    This phrase is the victory quote for PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds (PUBG).

  5. Which video game is credited with popularizing the battle royale genre for mass audiences?

    Answer: PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds (PUBG)

    PUBG: Battlegrounds is widely considered the first major standalone battle royale game to achieve massive global popularity, paving the way for titles like Fortnite.

  6. What is the name of the popular battle royale game developed by Respawn Entertainment and set in the Titanfall universe?

    Answer: Apex Legends

    Apex Legends is a free-to-play battle royale game developed by Respawn Entertainment, set in the Titanfall universe.

  7. What is the term for the final, shrinking safe zone in battle royale games like 'PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds' (PUBG) and 'Apex Legends'?

    Answer: The Zone

    The term 'The Zone' (or Blue Zone) is widely used to describe the area outside the playable safe zone that deals damage to players.

  8. In the video game 'Fortnite', what is the primary objective mode called where players build structures and fight to be the last one standing?

    Answer: Battle Royale

    Battle Royale is the flagship mode of Fortnite, pitting 100 players against each other in a shrinking map.

  9. Which company is the creator and developer of the game Fortnite?

    Answer: Epic Games

    Fortnite, particularly its Battle Royale mode, was developed and published by Epic Games.

  10. In the video game 'Call of Duty: Warzone', how many players typically drop into a single match?

    Answer: 150

    Call of Duty: Warzone supports up to 150 players per lobby, larger than many other battle royale games.

  11. What is the name of the battle royale game where players compete on a tropical island map called 'Erangel' before other maps were added?

    Answer: PUBG (PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds)

    PUBG pioneered the modern battle royale genre, and Erangel was its original and most iconic map.

  12. What is the name of the fictional battle royale island location featured in Season 1 of Fortnite Battle Royale?

    Answer: The original Fortnite island (Athena)

    The original Fortnite Battle Royale map, internally called Athena, was the sole playable map when the game launched in September 2017, featuring locations like Tilted Towers and Loot Lake.

  13. What is the most popular battle royale video game by total registered accounts (as of the 2020s)?

    Answer: PUBG: Battlegrounds

    PUBG: Battlegrounds pioneered the modern battle royale genre and surpassed 100 million registered accounts.

  14. In the video game 'Fortnite', what is the term for the shrinking playable area that forces players closer together?

    Answer: The Storm

    In Fortnite, the shrinking playable area is called 'The Storm', which damages players caught outside of it.

  15. In the video game Fortnite, what is the primary mechanic that distinguishes it from many other battle royale games at launch?

    Answer: Building structures for defense and traversal

    Fortnite popularized building mechanics in the battle royale genre, allowing players to quickly construct walls, ramps, and forts.
