Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which Italian Renaissance sculptor created the statue 'David'?

    Answer: Michelangelo

    Michelangelo sculpted the iconic marble statue of David between 1501 and 1504.

  2. Who sculpted 'David', the marble statue displayed in Florence?

    Answer: Michelangelo

    Michelangelo sculpted 'David' between 1501 and 1504; it is one of the most famous works of Renaissance sculpture and is displayed at the Galleria dell'Accademia in Florence.

  3. Who sculpted 'David' in the early 16th century?

    Answer: Michelangelo

    Michelangelo sculpted the marble 'David' between 1501 and 1504, and it is considered one of the masterpieces of Renaissance sculpture, housed in the Galleria dell'Accademia in Florence.

  4. Which sculptor is known for "The Thinker" and "The Kiss"?

    Answer: Auguste Rodin

    Auguste Rodin created both iconic sculptures, "The Thinker" (1902) and "The Kiss" (1889).

  5. Which French sculptor created the bronze statue 'The Burghers of Calais' that stands at the entrance of the National Gallery in London?

    Answer: Auguste Rodin

    Auguste Rodin's 'The Burghers of Calais' is a celebrated monumental sculpture.

  6. Which sculptor created the bronze statue "The Little Mermaid" that sits in Copenhagen?

    Answer: Edvard Eriksen

    Edvard Eriksen sculpted the Little Mermaid statue, unveiled in 1913.

  7. In which Italian city is Michelangelo's statue of David located?

    Answer: Florence

    Michelangelo's David is housed in the Galleria dell'Accademia in Florence. The marble masterpiece was completed in 1504.

  8. Which 19th‑century French sculptor created the life‑size bronze 'The Age of Bronze', a work that marked his departure from classical idealism?

    Answer: Auguste Rodin

    Auguste Rodin's 'The Age of Bronze' (1876) marks his breakthrough from academic classicism toward modern realism.

  9. Who sculpted 'The Thinker', the famous bronze statue of a man in deep thought?

    Answer: Auguste Rodin

    Auguste Rodin created The Thinker in 1904; originally part of his larger work The Gates of Hell, it became an independent icon of art.

  10. Which artist created the iconic readymade sculpture 'Fountain', a porcelain urinal signed 'R. Mutt'?

    Answer: Marcel Duchamp

    Marcel Duchamp submitted 'Fountain' in 1917, a pivotal piece of the Dada movement.

  11. Which artist sculpted 'The Thinker', originally part of 'The Gates of Hell'?

    Answer: Auguste Rodin

    Auguste Rodin created The Thinker in 1904, originally as part of a larger sculptural group called The Gates of Hell, inspired by Dante's Divine Comedy. It has become a universal symbol of philosophy and contemplation.

  12. The marble statue "The Dying Gaul" is a Roman copy of an original bronze by which ancient Greek sculptor?

    Answer: Epigonus

    The original bronze was created by Epigonus in the 3rd century BCE; the surviving marble version is a Roman copy.

  13. Which artist created the sculpture 'The Thinker' and also designed the Eiffel Tower's metallic skeleton?

    Answer: A) Auguste Rodin

    Auguste Rodin sculpted 'The Thinker' and his studio contributed to the Eiffel Tower's design.

  14. Who designed the internal structure of the Statue of Liberty?

    Answer: Gustave Eiffel

    Gustave Eiffel designed the iron framework that supports the Statue of Liberty.

  15. Which artist created the sculpture "The Thinker" (Le Penseur) and also designed the portal of the "Gates of Hell"?

    Answer: Auguste Rodin

    Auguste Rodin is the sculptor of "The Thinker" and the creator of the monumental "Gates of Hell" from which many of his figures were derived.
