Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which metal is liquid at standard room temperature?

    Answer: Mercury

    Mercury is liquid at standard conditions. Gallium melts just above room temperature (about 30°C), but mercury is liquid at 20-25°C.

  2. Which of these is NOT a noble gas?

    Answer: Oxygen

    Noble gases are chemically inert elements like helium, neon, and argon. Oxygen is a reactive nonmetal.

  3. Which of these is a noble gas?

    Answer: Neon

    Neon is a noble gas located in Group 18 of the periodic table, known for its inertness.

  4. Which of the following is a noble gas?

    Answer: Neon

    Neon is a noble gas located in Group 18 of the periodic table. Noble gases are characterized by their lack of chemical reactivity due to full outer electron shells.

  5. Which of these elements does water (H2O) contain?

    Answer: Hydrogen and Oxygen

    Water molecules consist of two hydrogen atoms bonded to one oxygen atom, hence the chemical formula H2O.

  6. What is the chemical symbol for iron?

    Answer: Fe

    Iron's symbol Fe comes from its Latin name 'ferrum'. Ir is iridium, In is indium.

  7. What is the chemical symbol for the element Sodium?

    Answer: Na

    Sodium's symbol 'Na' is derived from its Latin name 'Natrium'.

  8. What is the most abundant element in the universe by mass?

    Answer: Hydrogen

    Hydrogen makes up roughly 75% of ordinary matter in the universe by mass, followed by helium at about 25%. Most other elements account for only a tiny fraction.

  9. Which of the following is the lightest element in the periodic table?

    Answer: Hydrogen

    Hydrogen, with an atomic number of 1, is the lightest element.

  10. Which of these is the lightest chemical element?

    Answer: Hydrogen

    Hydrogen is the lightest element, with an atomic number of 1 and consisting of a single proton and a single electron.

  11. Which element has the highest melting point of all metals?

    Answer: Tungsten

    Tungsten melts at 3,422°C (6,192°F), the highest of any metal.

  12. Which element has the atomic number 79 and is commonly used in jewelry?

    Answer: Gold

    Gold (Au) has the atomic number 79 and is prized for its luster, malleability, and resistance to corrosion.

  13. What was the first artificial element to be synthesized, and in what year?

    Answer: Technetium, 1937

    Technetium (element 43) was first produced in 1937 by Carlo Perrier and Emilio Segrè. It is the lightest element with no stable isotopes. Francium and the transuranic elements came later.

  14. What is the rarest naturally occurring element in Earth's crust?

    Answer: Francium

    Francium is the rarest naturally occurring element, with an estimated 20-30 grams present in the entire Earth's crust at any given time. It is extremely radioactive and short-lived.

  15. Which of the following is an example of a noble gas?

    Answer: Argon

    Noble gases, including argon, helium, neon, krypton, xenon, and radon, have full outer electron shells and are highly unreactive.
