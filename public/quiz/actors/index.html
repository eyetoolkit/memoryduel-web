Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which famous actor is known for playing the character Jack Sparrow?

    Answer: Johnny Depp

    Johnny Depp portrayed the eccentric pirate Captain Jack Sparrow in the 'Pirates of the Caribbean' franchise.

  2. Which actor played the lead role in the movie "Forrest Gump"?

    Answer: Tom Hanks

    Tom Hanks starred as Forrest Gump in the 1994 film.

  3. Which actor starred as the iconic hero in 'Indiana Jones'?

    Answer: Harrison Ford

    Harrison Ford portrayed Dr. Henry 'Indiana' Jones in the adventure film series created by George Lucas and directed by Steven Spielberg.

  4. Which actress plays the lead role in the movie 'Titanic' alongside Leonardo DiCaprio?

    Answer: Kate Winslet

    Kate Winslet plays Rose DeWitt Bukater opposite DiCaprio's Jack Dawson.

  5. Who played the character of Jack Dawson in the film 'Titanic'?

    Answer: Leonardo DiCaprio

    Leonardo DiCaprio played Jack Dawson, the romantic lead in James Cameron's 1997 blockbuster Titanic.

  6. Which actor has starred in films such as 'Gladiator', 'The Martian', and 'Blade Runner 2049'?

    Answer: Matt Damon

    Matt Damon starred in 'Gladiator' (as Lucilla's lover), 'The Martian' (as Mark Watney), and 'Blade Runner 2049'.

  7. Which actress won an Academy Award for Best Actress for her role in 'The Iron Lady' (2011)?

    Answer: Meryl Streep

    Meryl Streep won her third Oscar for portraying British Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher in 'The Iron Lady'.

  8. In the Marvel Cinematic Universe, which actor portrays Iron Man / Tony Stark?

    Answer: Robert Downey Jr.

    Robert Downey Jr. has played Tony Stark / Iron Man since the 2008 film.

  9. In which film series does the character 'Katniss Everdeen' appear?

    Answer: The Hunger Games

    Katniss Everdeen is the protagonist of *The Hunger Games* series, played by Jennifer Lawrence.

  10. In the Harry Potter film series, which actor played the role of Severus Snape?

    Answer: Alan Rickman

    Alan Rickman portrayed the complex character of Severus Snape across all eight Harry Potter films, earning widespread critical acclaim.

  11. In the TV series 'Game of Thrones', who plays Tyrion Lannister?

    Answer: Peter Dinklage

    Peter Dinklage won multiple Emmy Awards for his role as Tyrion Lannister.

  12. Which actor has been nominated for the most Academy Awards without ever winning?

    Answer: Peter O'Toole

    Peter O'Toole holds the record for the most acting Oscar nominations without a win.

  13. Which actor portrayed the Joker in Christopher Nolan's 'The Dark Knight' (2008)?

    Answer: Heath Ledger

    Heath Ledger gave an iconic, posthumously Oscar-winning performance as the Joker.

  14. Which actor has received the most Academy Award nominations in history (as of 2024)?

    Answer: Meryl Streep

    Meryl Streep holds the record with 21 Academy Award nominations (3 wins), surpassing all other performers.

  15. Which actor is known for playing both the Terminator and the Predator in different franchises?

    Answer: Arnold Schwarzenegger

    Arnold Schwarzenegger played the T-800 in 'The Terminator' films and also cameoed as a Predator-like figure in 'Predator' (1987) as the Dutch commando, while the Predator itself was played by Kevin Peter Hall — this question tests knowledge of his iconic roles across both franchises.
