Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which country won the 2022 FIFA World Cup?

    Answer: Argentina

    Argentina won the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar, defeating France in the final on penalties.

  2. Which country hosts the FIFA World Cup 2026?

    Answer: USA, Mexico and Canada

    The 2026 FIFA World Cup will be hosted jointly by the United States, Mexico, and Canada, the first time three nations co-host the tournament.

  3. Which country has won the most FIFA World Cups in men's football history?

    Answer: Brazil

    Brazil has won the FIFA World Cup a record 5 times (1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002).

  4. Which major international cricket tournament is held every four years and involves One Day Internationals?

    Answer: ICC Cricket World Cup

    The ICC Cricket World Cup is the premier ODI tournament held quadrennially.

  5. The FIFA World Cup trophy is officially named what?

    Answer: The FIFA World Cup Trophy

    The current trophy is officially called the FIFA World Cup Trophy, introduced in 1974 replacing the Jules Rimet Trophy.

  6. The 'FIFA World Cup' is held every how many years?

    Answer: 4 years

    The FIFA World Cup is an international football tournament held every four years.

  7. Which country has won the most Rugby World Cup titles?

    Answer: South Africa

    South Africa has won four Rugby World Cup titles (1995, 2007, 2019, 2023), the most of any nation.

  8. Which African country was the first to qualify for and win a FIFA World Cup match in 1990?

    Answer: Cameroon

    Cameroon became the first African team to reach the World Cup quarter-finals in 1990, famously defeating defending champion Argentina in their opening match.

  9. Which two countries have contested the most Rugby World Cup finals as of 2023?

    Answer: South Africa and New Zealand

    South Africa and New Zealand have each appeared in multiple finals and are the most successful nations in Rugby World Cup history.

  10. Which nation has won the most Cricket World Cups (ODI) in men's cricket history?

    Answer: Australia

    Australia has won the most ODI Cricket World Cups with 6 titles (1987, 1999, 2003, 2007, 2015, 2023), followed by the West Indies and India with 2 each.

  11. Which nation was the first to win back-to-back FIFA World Cup titles?

    Answer: Italy

    Italy won the World Cup in 1934 and 1938, becoming the first nation to win consecutive titles.

  12. Which is the only nation to have appeared in every men's FIFA World Cup tournament since its inception in 1930?

    Answer: Brazil

    Brazil is the only country to have qualified for and participated in all 22 FIFA World Cup tournaments held from 1930 through 2022.

  13. Which country's cricket team has won the ICC Cricket World Cup the most times?

    Answer: Australia

    Australia has won the ICC Cricket World Cup 5 times (1987, 1999, 2003, 2007, 2023), more than any other team.

  14. Which country has appeared in the most FIFA World Cup finals (men's) without ever winning the tournament?

    Answer: Netherlands

    The Netherlands has reached the World Cup final three times (1974, 1978, 2010) but has never won the tournament.

  15. Which country has hosted both the Summer Olympic Games and the FIFA World Cup three times each?

    Answer: Italy

    Italy has hosted the Summer Olympics twice (1960, 2006 Winter) and the FIFA World Cup three times (1934, 1938, 1990); however, no country has hosted the Summer Olympics three times. Germany hosted the Summer Olympics twice (1936, 1972) and the World Cup as West Germany/Germany three times (1974, 2006, and shared 1974) — this question is often debated. The intended answer based on commonly cited trivia is Germany (hosted World Cup 1974, 2006; shared 1974; Olympics 1936, 1972).
