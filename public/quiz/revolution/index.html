Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In what year did the French Revolution begin with the storming of the Bastille?

    Answer: 1789

    The storming of the Bastille on July 14, 1789, is widely considered the flashpoint of the French Revolution.

  2. In which year did the United States declare independence from Great Britain?

    Answer: 1776

    The Declaration of Independence was adopted by the Continental Congress on July 4, 1776, marking the separation of the thirteen American colonies from British rule.

  3. Who was the last Tsar of Russia before the Bolshevik Revolution?

    Answer: Nicholas II

    Nicholas II was the last Tsar of Russia, forced to abdicate in 1917 during the Russian Revolution.

  4. What was the primary cause of the French Revolution that began in 1789?

    Answer: Social inequality and economic crisis

    The French Revolution was driven by widespread social inequality, food shortages, heavy taxation of the lower classes, and Enlightenment ideals.

  5. Which revolution sparked the overthrow of the monarchy in France in 1789?

    Answer: B) French Revolution

    The French Revolution, marked by events such as the storming of the Bastille, led to the abolition of the French monarchy in 1792.

  6. Which leader of the Haitian Revolution is widely regarded as the founder of the first free black republic?

    Answer: Jean‑Jacques Dessalines

    Dessalines proclaimed Haitian independence in 1804 and became the first ruler of the newly independent nation.

  7. In which year did the Russian October Revolution take place?

    Answer: B. 1917

    The Bolsheviks seized power in October 1917 (Julian calendar), marking the start of the Soviet era.

  8. Which event in 1917 precipitated the collapse of the Tsarist government in Russia?

    Answer: February Revolution

    The February Revolution (March 1917) led to the abdication of Tsar Nicholas II and the end of the Romanov dynasty.

  9. The 'Spring of Nations' or 'Revolutions of 1848' were a series of political upheavals that primarily affected which continent?

    Answer: Europe

    The Revolutions of 1848 were a widespread series of liberal, nationalist, and socialist uprisings across Europe, from France to the Austrian Empire.

  10. The Haitian Revolution, the only successful large-scale slave revolt leading to an independent state, began in which year?

    Answer: 1791

    The Haitian Revolution began in 1791 and culminated in 1804 with Haiti becoming the first independent nation founded by formerly enslaved people.

  11. The Decembrist Revolt of 1825 took place in which empire?

    Answer: Russian Empire

    The Decembrist Revolt was an uprising by liberal Russian army officers against Tsar Nicholas I in December 1825.

  12. Which leader of the Haitian Revolution was formerly an enslaved person who became a key general?

    Answer: Toussaint Louverture

    Toussaint Louverture, born into slavery, rose to command the revolutionary forces that fought French colonial rule.

  13. The 1917 October Revolution in Russia was led by which political group, eventually establishing the Soviet Union?

    Answer: The Bolsheviks

    The Bolsheviks, led by Vladimir Lenin, seized power in the October Revolution (November 1917 by the Gregorian calendar), leading to the formation of the USSR.

  14. Which event directly preceded the collapse of the Qing dynasty in 1912?

    Answer: B) The Wuchang Uprising

    The Wuchang Uprising in October 1911 sparked the revolution that led to the abdication of the last emperor.

  15. What was a primary catalyst for the 1917 Russian Revolution?

    Answer: Food shortages and autocratic rule

    Severe food shortages, war exhaustion, and Tsar Nicholas II’s autocratic rule sparked the February and October Revolutions of 1917.
