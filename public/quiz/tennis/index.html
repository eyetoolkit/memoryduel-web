Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In which sport is the term 'love' used to denote a score of zero?

    Answer: Tennis

    In tennis, 'love' means zero. The exact origin is debated, but it has been used since the late 19th century.

  2. Which sport is associated with the term 'Grand Slam' in tennis?

    Answer: The four major tournaments

    In tennis, a Grand Slam refers to winning all four major championships (Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon, US Open) in the same calendar year.

  3. Which country is traditionally credited with inventing modern tennis?

    Answer: England

    Modern lawn tennis originated in England in the late 19th century, building on earlier racket games.

  4. Which sport is played on a court with a net dividing it in half, using a racket to hit a ball over the net?

    Answer: Tennis

    Tennis features a net and players use rackets to hit a ball back and forth over it.

  5. Which sport is contested at the Wimbledon Championships?

    Answer: Tennis

    Wimbledon is the oldest tennis tournament, played on grass courts.

  6. In tennis, what is the name of the tournament considered the oldest in the world, played on grass courts?

    Answer: Wimbledon

    Wimbledon, held in London, is the oldest tennis tournament in the world and the only Grand Slam played on grass courts.

  7. The Wimbledon tennis tournament is held in which country?

    Answer: United Kingdom

    Wimbledon is the oldest tennis tournament in the world and is held in London, England.

  8. What is the maximum number of games in a standard tennis set (before a tiebreak)?

    Answer: 14

    In traditional tennis scoring, a set could theoretically go to 12-10 or higher before tiebreaks were introduced, but 6 games is standard to win a set. If tied 6-6, play continues until one player leads by two games.

  9. In tennis, what term describes a perfect service game where the server wins all points without the ball being returned?

    Answer: Love game

    A 'love game' is when a server wins a game without losing a single point (score of 40-0 or equivalent).

  10. In which sport are 'aces' and 'volleys' common terms?

    Answer: Tennis

    An ace is a serve that wins a point directly, and a volley is hitting the ball before it bounces.

  11. Which tennis player has spent the most weeks at world No. 1 in the ATP rankings?

    Answer: Novak Djokovic

    As of 2024, Novak Djokovic has surpassed 420 weeks at the top of the ATP list, the most of any player.

  12. In professional tennis, which surface is the French Open played on?

    Answer: Clay

    The French Open (Roland Garros) is the only Grand Slam tournament played on clay courts.

  13. The Davis Cup is the premier international team competition in which sport?

    Answer: Tennis

    The Davis Cup, established in 1900, is the world's premier international team event in men's tennis.

  14. In professional tennis, what is the speed of the ball required to trigger the 'Hawk-Eye' electronic line calling system's challenge effectively if it is wrong?

  15. Who was the first female to win a Grand Slam singles title in the Open Era?

    Answer: Nancy Richey

    Nancy Richey won the women's singles at the 1968 French Open, the first Grand Slam event of the Open Era.
