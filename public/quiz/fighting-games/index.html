Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In the fighting game Street Fighter, what is the name of Ryu and Ken's signature fireball attack?

    Answer: Hadouken

    The Hadouken (Surge Fist) is the iconic projectile attack. The Shoryuken is a rising uppercut, and the Tatsumaki is a spinning kick.

  2. Which video game company developed the 'Soulcalibur' series?

    Answer: Namco

    Soulcalibur is a weapons‑based fighting game series created by Namco, originally developed by Project Soul.

  3. Which esports game is famous for its 'Pikachu' mascot character from the Pokémon franchise?

    Answer: Super Smash Bros.

    Super Smash Bros. features characters from Nintendo franchises, including Pikachu.

  4. Which of these is the name for the annual fighting game tournament held in Las Vegas, widely considered the most prestigious in the world?

    Answer: EVO (Evolution Championship Series)

    The Evolution Championship Series, or EVO, is the world's largest and most long-running annual fighting game tournament.

  5. The 'EVO Championship Series' is the world's largest annual event for which type of games?

    Answer: Fighting games

    EVO is the premier annual fighting game tournament held in Las Vegas.

  6. Which esports title is known as the most-watched competitive fighting game at EVO (Evolution Championship Series)?

    Answer: Street Fighter

    Street Fighter has historically been the flagship title of EVO, with Street Fighter II starting the tournament and Street Fighter V/VI drawing massive entrant numbers.

  7. Which fighting game franchise is known for the 'Hadouken' special move?

    Answer: Street Fighter

    The Hadouken is the signature fireball attack of Ryu and other characters in Capcom's Street Fighter series.

  8. In the fighting game community, what is the term for a combo that is inescapable once the first hit connects?

    Answer: True combo

    A 'true combo' is a sequence of attacks where, if the first hit lands, the subsequent hits are guaranteed and cannot be blocked or escaped.

  9. In Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, which third‑party franchise is represented by a playable fighter?

    Answer: Sonic

    Sonic, Sega’s iconic hedgehog, appears as a playable character in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate.

  10. In competitive 'Super Smash Bros. Melee,' which character is widely considered the best in the meta, and which country is most associated with its top players?

    Answer: Fox, United States

    Fox McCloud is widely regarded as the best character in Melee due to his speed and versatility, and the US has produced many legendary Fox players like Mang0 and Leffen (though Leffen is Swedish/TSM).

  11. Which player achieved the first‑ever perfect 30‑0 score in a professional 'Super Smash Bros. Ultimate' bracket at the 2022 'Genesis 8' tournament?

    Answer: MkLeo

    MkLeo, playing as Joker, became the first to go undefeated through a top‑level Ultimate bracket at Genesis 8.

  12. In 'Street Fighter', what is the input for the iconic 'Hadouken' move performed by Ryu?

    Answer: Quarter-circle forward + punch

    The Hadouken is performed by inputting a quarter-circle forward (down, down-forward, forward) followed by a punch button.

  13. What is the name of the fictional city in the fighting game series 'Tekken' that serves as the location for the King of Iron Fist Tournament?

    Answer: Mishima Zaibatsu HQ (Tokyo)

    The King of Iron Fist Tournament in the Tekken series is hosted by the Mishima Zaibatsu, with many tournaments set in Tokyo, Japan. Mishima Zaibatsu is the powerful conglomerate behind the events.

  14. In competitive Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, what mechanic introduced in this game replaced the traditional directional air dodge from previous entries?

    Answer: Directional air dodging with end lag

    Smash Ultimate introduced directional air dodging with significant end lag (recovery frames), replacing the fixed-direction air dodge of Smash 4 and changing competitive movement significantly.

  15. What is the highest possible rank in the competitive 'Street Fighter V' ranking system?

    Answer: Champion

    Champion is the top rank attainable in Street Fighter V's online ranking ladder.
