Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which of these is the fastest land animal?

    Answer: Cheetah

    The cheetah is the fastest land animal, capable of running at speeds up to 112 km/h (70 mph) in short bursts.

  2. What is the process called when an animal sheds its outer layer of skin?

    Answer: Molting

    Molting is the shedding of feathers, fur, or skin to allow new growth.

  3. How many chambers does a human heart have?

    Answer: 4

    The heart has four chambers: two atria and two ventricles.

  4. What is the term for the process by which plants convert sunlight into chemical energy?

    Answer: Photosynthesis

    Photosynthesis is the process used by plants, algae, and some bacteria to convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose.

  5. Which part of the human body contains the strongest muscle relative to its size?

    Answer: Jaw (Masseter)

    The masseter (jaw) muscle can exert a force of up to 200 pounds, the highest relative to its size.

  6. Which part of the brain controls balance and coordination?

    Answer: Cerebellum

    The cerebellum coordinates voluntary movements, posture, and balance.

  7. In genetics, what does DNA stand for?

    Answer: Deoxyribonucleic Acid

    DNA stands for Deoxyribonucleic Acid, the molecule that carries genetic information in living organisms.

  8. What is the primary function of hemoglobin in the human body?

    Answer: Transporting oxygen

    Hemoglobin is a protein in red blood cells that binds to oxygen in the lungs and carries it to tissues throughout the body.

  9. Which process in plants involves the conversion of carbon dioxide and water into glucose using sunlight?

    Answer: Photosynthesis

    Photosynthesis uses solar energy to convert CO₂ and H₂O into glucose and O₂.

  10. Which part of the cell is often called the 'powerhouse of the cell' because it produces most of the cell's ATP?

    Answer: Mitochondria

    Mitochondria generate most of the cell's supply of adenosine triphosphate (ATP), used as a source of chemical energy. This process is called cellular respiration.

  11. Which molecule is primarily responsible for carrying genetic information in most organisms?

    Answer: DNA

    DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid) stores and transmits genetic information in a double-helix structure of nucleotide bases.

  12. Which bird can fly backwards?

    Answer: Hummingbird

    Hummingbirds are unique among birds for their ability to hover and fly backwards thanks to their specialized wing joints.

  13. Which enzyme is primarily responsible for unwinding the DNA double helix during replication?

    Answer: Helicase

    Helicase breaks the hydrogen bonds between base pairs, unwinding and separating the two DNA strands at the replication fork.

  14. What is the largest internal organ in the human body?

    Answer: Liver

    The liver is the largest internal organ, weighing about 1.5 kilograms in adults. The skin is the largest organ overall, but it is considered an external organ.

  15. What is the term for an organism that produces its own food using sunlight?

    Answer: Autotroph

    Autotrophs, such as plants and algae, use photosynthesis to convert light energy into chemical energy (food).
