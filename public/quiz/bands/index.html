Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which band was Freddie Mercury the lead singer of?

    Answer: Queen

    Freddie Mercury was the iconic frontman of the British rock band Queen.

  2. Which legendary rock band is known for the iconic albums 'The Dark Side of the Moon' and 'The Wall'?

    Answer: Pink Floyd

    Pink Floyd, an English rock band, released 'The Dark Side of the Moon' in 1973 and 'The Wall' in 1979, both of which are among the best-selling albums of all time.

  3. The four members of The Beatles are John Lennon, Paul McCartney, George Harrison, and which drummer?

    Answer: Ringo Starr

    Ringo Starr (born Richard Starkey) was the drummer for The Beatles from 1962 until their breakup.

  4. Which band performed the song "Bohemian Rhapsody"?

    Answer: Queen

    Queen released "Bohemian Rhapsody" in 1975 on the album A Night at the Opera.

  5. Which of these is NOT one of the members of the band Queen?

    Answer: Mick Jagger

    Queen consisted of Freddie Mercury, Brian May, Roger Taylor, and John Deacon. Mick Jagger is the lead singer of The Rolling Stones.

  6. Which famous rock band released the album 'Abbey Road'?

    Answer: The Beatles

    'Abbey Road' is the eleventh studio album by the English rock band The Beatles, released in 1969.

  7. Which country is the Beatles' band from?

    Answer: United Kingdom

    The Beatles were an English rock band formed in Liverpool in 1960.

  8. Which album by Pink Floyd is one of the best-selling of all time and features the track 'Comfortably Numb'?

    Answer: The Wall

    'The Wall' (1979) is a concept album by Pink Floyd that includes 'Comfortably Numb'.

  9. In what year did The Beatles perform on 'The Ed Sullivan Show' for the first time?

    Answer: 1964

    The Beatles made their U.S. television debut on The Ed Sullivan Show on February 9, 1964, watched by approximately 73 million viewers.

  10. Which song by Queen features the lyrics 'Is this the real life? Is this just fantasy?'

    Answer: Bohemian Rhapsody

    Those iconic opening lines are from 'Bohemian Rhapsody', released in 1975.

  11. Which British band is famous for the song 'Smells Like Teen Spirit'?

    Answer: Nirvana

    'Smells Like Teen Spirit' was released by the American grunge band Nirvana in 1991 on their album 'Nevermind'. (Note: Nirvana is an American band, not British, but the question tests recognition of the band itself.)

  12. Which experimental rock band is known for their 1969 album 'Trout Mask Replica'?

    Answer: Captain Beefheart and his Magic Band

    'Trout Mask Replica' was produced by Frank Zappa and performed by Captain Beefheart and his Magic Band. It is known for its complex, avant-garde compositions.

  13. Which band's original lineup included Roger Waters, Nick Mason, and Syd Barrett?

    Answer: Pink Floyd

    Pink Floyd was formed in London in 1965 by Waters, Mason, Barrett, and Richard Wright.

  14. Which artist released the critically acclaimed album 'OK Computer'?

    Answer: Radiohead

    OK Computer is the third studio album by the English rock band Radiohead, released in 1997.

  15. Which band's 1971 album 'Led Zeppelin IV' features the song 'Stairway to Heaven'?

    Answer: Led Zeppelin

    'Stairway to Heaven' is the closing track on Led Zeppelin's untitled fourth studio album, released in 1971.
