Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which country is home to the city of Cairo?

    Answer: Egypt

    Cairo is the capital of Egypt and the largest city in Africa.

  2. Which country has Canberra as its capital?

    Answer: Australia

    Canberra is the capital of Australia, chosen as a compromise between Sydney and Melbourne.

  3. What is the capital of Australia?

    Answer: Canberra

    Canberra was purpose‑built as the capital of Australia in 1913, situated between Sydney and Melbourne.

  4. Which is the capital of Canada?

    Answer: Ottawa

    Ottawa is the capital of Canada. Toronto and Montreal are larger cities, but Ottawa serves as the national capital.

  5. Which is the capital of Turkey?

    Answer: Ankara

    Ankara is the capital of Turkey, though Istanbul is the largest city.

  6. What is the capital of Kazakhstan?

    Answer: Astana

    Astana (now Nur‑Sultan) has been the capital of Kazakhstan since 1997.

  7. What is the capital of South Africa?

    Answer: Pretoria

    Pretoria is the administrative capital of South Africa. South Africa uniquely has three capital cities: Pretoria (executive), Cape Town (legislative), and Bloemfontein (judicial).

  8. What is the capital of Argentina?

    Answer: Buenos Aires

    Buenos Aires is the capital and largest city of Argentina. It is located on the western shore of the Río de la Plata estuary.

  9. What is the capital city of Australia's neighbor, New Zealand?

    Answer: Wellington

    Wellington, not the larger city of Auckland, is the capital of New Zealand.

  10. What is the capital of Sri Lanka?

    Answer: Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte

    Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte is the official capital, while Colombo is the commercial capital.

  11. Which capital city is located at the confluence of the Rhine and Main rivers?

    Answer: Frankfurt

    Frankfurt am Main is located where the Main River joins the Rhine, making it a major financial hub.

  12. The Mekong River flows through which of these capitals?

    Answer: Vientiane

    Vientiane, the capital of Laos, sits on the banks of the Mekong River, which is vital to mainland Southeast Asia.

  13. Which is the capital of Kazakhstan, moved from Almaty in 1997?

    Answer: Astana

    Astana (renamed Nur-Sultan 2019–2022, then back to Astana) replaced Almaty as Kazakhstan's capital in 1997.

  14. The capital of Kazakhstan is...

    Answer: Astana

    Astana (renamed Nur-Sultan from 2019-2022, then back to Astana) replaced Almaty as Kazakhstan's capital in 1997, located in the country's northern steppes.

  15. Which capital city is located at the highest elevation above sea level?

    Answer: La Paz

    La Paz, the administrative capital of Bolivia, sits at about 3,650 m, higher than the other listed capitals.
