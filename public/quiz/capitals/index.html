World Capitals Quiz

Capital cities are a strange category of knowledge: everyone can name Paris and Tokyo, and almost nobody can name Naypyidaw or Ngerulmud without help. The gap between those two ends is where this quiz lives.

It runs 22 questions, easy to hard, and deliberately includes the capitals that trip people up — the purpose-built ones like Canberra and Brasília, the ones that moved like Astana, and the ones where the biggest city is not the capital at all.

Each answer comes with the reasoning attached, and the full key is below the quiz. No sign-up, no timer, no regional restrictions — if you are reading this in a country the quiz covers, the capital question still counts.

Answer key

All 22 questions, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the capital of France?

    Answer: Paris

    Paris has been the capital of France since the 10th century.

  2. What is the capital of Canada?

    Answer: Ottawa

    Ottawa was selected as the federal capital in 1857, balancing English and French populations.

  3. What is the capital city of Myanmar (formerly Burma)?

    Answer: Naypyidaw

    In 2005 the capital was moved from Yangon to the newly built city of Naypyidaw to better centralize governance.

  4. What is the capital city of Japan?

    Answer: Tokyo

    Tokyo has been Japan's capital since 1868, when the Emperor moved the court from Kyoto.

  5. What is the capital city of Australia?

    Answer: Canberra

    Canberra was chosen as the capital in 1908 as a compromise between Sydney and Melbourne.

  6. What is the capital of Mongolia?

    Answer: Ulaanbaatar

    Ulaanbaatar, formerly known as Ulan Bator, is the capital and largest city of Mongolia.

  7. What is the capital of Brazil?

    Answer: Brasília

    Brasília became the capital in 1960, replacing Rio de Janeiro to move the political centre inland.

  8. Which European capital is famously built on 14 islands?

    Answer: Stockholm

    Stockholm, the capital of Sweden, spans 14 islands across the Baltic Sea.

  9. What is the capital of Kazakhstan?

    Answer: Astana

    Astana (now Nur‑Sultan) has been the capital of Kazakhstan since 1997.

  10. Which capital city is situated at the highest elevation above sea level?

    Answer: La Paz (Bolivia)

    La Paz’s administrative center lies at about 3,650 m, making it the world’s highest capital city.

  11. Which European capital is built on the River Thames?

    Answer: London

    London, the capital of the United Kingdom, straddles the River Thames.

  12. What is the capital of Palau?

    Answer: Ngerulmud

    Ngerulmud became the capital in 2006, replacing the larger city of Koror.

  13. What is the capital city of Argentina?

    Answer: Buenos Aires

    Buenos Aires has been the capital of Argentina since the 19th century and is one of the largest cities in South America.

  14. What is the capital of South Sudan?

    Answer: Juba

    Juba has been the capital of South Sudan since its independence in 2011.

  15. Which European capital city is located on the River Danube?

    Answer: Budapest

    Budapest lies on both sides of the Danube, with the historic Buda and modern Pest districts separated by the river.

  16. Which of the following capitals lies the farthest north?

    Answer: Reykjavik

    Reykjavik sits at about 64° N, further north than the other listed capitals.

  17. Which city serves as the administrative capital of South Africa?

    Answer: Pretoria

    Pretoria is the executive (administrative) capital, while Cape Town is legislative and Bloemfontein is judicial.

  18. Which of the following is the smallest capital city by area?

    Answer: Vatican City

    Vatican City covers only about 0.44 km², making it the smallest national capital.

  19. What is the capital of South Korea?

    Answer: Seoul

    Seoul, officially the Special City of Seoul, has been the capital since the Joseon dynasty.

  20. What is the capital of Uzbekistan?

    Answer: Tashkent

    Tashkent is the largest city and the capital of Uzbekistan.

  21. What is the capital of New Zealand?

    Answer: Wellington

    Wellington, located on the North Island, is New Zealand's capital city.

  22. Which capital city is located on the island of Luzon?

    Answer: Manila

    Manila, the capital of the Philippines, lies on Luzon island.

What this quiz covers

The quiz leans on the two things that make capitals hard: countries that moved theirs, and countries whose capital is a small city chosen for political reasons rather than a large one that grew on its own.

Roughly half the questions are place-to-capital and half are capital-to-country, because those are separate skills — knowing that Canberra is the capital of Australia does not always survive being asked which country has Canberra as its capital.

  • The classics: Paris, Tokyo, Ottawa, Canberra, Brasília — the ones you are expected to know.
  • The hard end: Naypyidaw, Ulaanbaatar, Ngerulmud, Juba and the capitals of Central Asia.
  • Capitals that moved or were renamed, including Kazakhstan's shift from Almaty and its later renamings.
  • Records: the highest capital, the smallest by area, the northernmost capital, the one built on fourteen islands.
  • Cities identified by their river rather than by their country — the Thames, the Danube, the Nile.

Frequently asked questions

Which country has three capital cities?
South Africa. Pretoria is the executive capital, Cape Town is the legislative capital and Bloemfontein is the judicial capital. Bolivia is often described the same way, with Sucre as the constitutional capital and La Paz as the seat of government.
What is the highest capital city in the world?
La Paz, Bolivia, at roughly 3,600 metres. It is the highest administrative capital; Bolivia's constitutional capital is Sucre, which sits much lower.
Which capital city was moved most recently?
Kazakhstan has changed its capital city's name twice without moving it. Almaty was replaced by Astana in 1997, the city was renamed Nur-Sultan in 2019, and the name reverted to Astana in 2022.
Why are so many capitals not the largest city in their country?
Because capitals are chosen for politics, not population. Canberra, Brasília, Ottawa and Washington were all built or designated to keep government away from an established commercial centre, or to settle a rivalry between two regions.
What is the smallest capital city in the world?
By area, Vatican City. By population, Ngerulmud in Palau has only a few hundred residents, which makes it one of the smallest capitals by population anywhere.
