Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which company developed the iPhone?

    Answer: Apple

    Apple Inc. introduced the iPhone in 2007, revolutionizing the smartphone market with its touchscreen interface.

  2. What does the 'G' stand for in '2G', '3G', '4G', and '5G' mobile networks?

    Answer: Generation

    The 'G' stands for Generation, representing the evolution of mobile network technology over time.

  3. Which of the following is a widely used operating system for smartphones, developed by Google?

    Answer: Android

    Android is the mobile operating system developed by Google, used by many smartphone manufacturers.

  4. Which part of a smartphone contains the display and touch interface?

    Answer: Screen assembly

    The screen assembly integrates the display panel and touch sensors for user interaction.

  5. Which technology allows a smartphone to make contactless payments by briefly touching a terminal?

    Answer: NFC

    NFC (Near Field Communication) enables secure short‑range communication for payments.

  6. In what year was the first iPhone released by Apple?

    Answer: 2007

    Apple released the first iPhone in 2007, revolutionizing the smartphone industry with its touchscreen interface.

  7. Which programming language is most commonly associated with developing Android apps natively?

    Answer: Java

    While Kotlin is now preferred, Java was the primary language used for Android development for many years and remains widely compatible.

  8. Which company created the Android operating system originally?

    Answer: Google

    Android was developed by Android Inc., which was acquired by Google in 2005.

  9. What does the 'i' in 'iPhone' originally stand for according to Steve Jobs' 2007 keynote?

    Answer: Internet

    When introducing the iPhone in 2007, Steve Jobs explained that the 'i' stood for internet, as well as individual, instruct, inform, and inspire.

  10. Which company is known for designing the ARM architecture, widely used in mobile devices and increasingly in laptops?

    Answer: Arm Holdings

    Arm Holdings designs the ARM architecture, which powers the majority of the world's smartphones and is now expanding into laptops and servers.

  11. What does the 'G' in '5G' stand for?

    Answer: Generation

    5G stands for the 5th Generation of mobile network technology. Each 'G' represents a new generation of cellular technology, offering improvements in speed and latency.

  12. What was the revolutionary innovation introduced by the 'iPhone' upon its initial release in 2007?

    Answer: A capacitive multi-touch screen interface

    While not the first smartphone, the original iPhone's key innovation was its capacitive multi-touch screen and intuitive user interface, which fundamentally changed how people interacted with mobile devices.

  13. Which of these technologies is used for contactless payments using smartphones?

    Answer: NFC

    NFC (Near Field Communication) allows two devices to communicate when they are within a few centimeters of each other, commonly used for mobile payments.

  14. Which of these is the highest-grossing mobile game of all time globally, known for its gacha mechanics and anime-style characters?

    Answer: Honor of Kings

    Honor of Kings (Arena of Valor in some regions), developed by Tencent's TiMi Studios, has generated the most revenue of any mobile game worldwide, largely driven by the Chinese market.

  15. Which processing architecture, designed by Arm Holdings, powers the majority of the world's smartphones?

    Answer: ARM

    ARM (Advanced RISC Machines) architecture dominates mobile devices due to its power efficiency, unlike x86 used in most PCs.
