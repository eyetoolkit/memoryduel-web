Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In which ancient civilization did the Great Pyramid of Giza belong?

    Answer: Ancient Egypt

    The Great Pyramid of Giza was built during the Fourth Dynasty of Ancient Egypt around 2560 BCE for Pharaoh Khufu.

  2. What famous wall was built to protect the northern border of China?

    Answer: The Great Wall

    The Great Wall of China is a series of fortifications built across the historical northern borders of China to protect against nomadic invasions.

  3. The Great Wall was built to protect against invasions from the north. In which country is it located?

    Answer: China

    The Great Wall of China stretches across northern China and was built over many centuries to defend against northern invaders.

  4. The Taj Mahal was built by which Mughal emperor?

    Answer: Shah Jahan

    Shah Jahan built the Taj Mahal in Agra as a mausoleum for his wife Mumtaz Mahal, completed around 1653.

  5. What is the significance of the 'Torii' gate commonly found at the entrance of Shinto shrines in Japan?

    Answer: It marks the transition from the mundane to the sacred

    In Shinto belief, a Torii gate marks the transition from the profane (mundane) world to the sacred space of the shrine.

  6. Which dynasty built the Great Wall of China in its most recognizable form?

    Answer: Ming

    While walls were built earlier, the most well-known sections were constructed during the Ming Dynasty (1368–1644).

  7. Which ancient wonder was located in Alexandria, Egypt?

    Answer: Lighthouse of Alexandria

    The Lighthouse of Alexandria, also known as the Pharos of Alexandria, was one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.

  8. What is the world record for the tallest free‑standing structure?

    Answer: Burj Khalifa

    Burj Khalifa stands at 828 m, the tallest free‑standing structure ever built.

  9. Which ancient civilization built the city of Machu Picchu in the 15th century?

    Answer: The Inca

    Machu Picchu was built in the 15th century by the Inca Empire in present-day Peru. It served as a royal estate and was abandoned during the Spanish conquest, remaining largely unknown until 1911.

  10. What is the primary advantage of using a 'fat client' architecture in network design?

    Answer: Offline capability

    Fat clients can perform many operations locally, allowing the application to function without constant server connectivity.

  11. The Great Wall of China, often said to be visible from space, is approximately how long?

    Answer: About 21,196 kilometers

    A 2012 Chinese government survey found the total length of all sections of the Great Wall, including trenches, natural barriers, and newly discovered sections, to be 21,196 kilometers (13,171 miles).

  12. The ancient city of Petra, famous for its rock-cut architecture, was the capital of which civilization?

    Answer: Nabataeans

    Petra was the capital of the Nabataean Kingdom, a wealthy trading civilization located in modern-day Jordan.

  13. Which term describes a system's ability to continue operating correctly despite component failures?

    Answer: Fault tolerance

    Fault tolerance ensures a system can survive and recover from hardware or software faults.

  14. In the Bahá'í Faith, what is the name of the nine-sided temple open to all faiths?

    Answer: House of Worship

    Bahá'í Houses of Worship are characterized by their nine-sided design and openness to people of all backgrounds.

  15. Which dynasty in China was responsible for building the Forbidden City in Beijing?

    Answer: The Ming Dynasty

    The Forbidden City was constructed during the Ming Dynasty under the Yongle Emperor in the early 1400s and served as the imperial palace for nearly 500 years.
