Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Whitney Houston's cover of 'I Will Always Love You' was originally written and performed by which artist?

    Answer: Dolly Parton

    Dolly Parton wrote and first recorded 'I Will Always Love You' in 1973. Whitney Houston's 1992 cover became one of the best-selling singles of all time.

  2. Which artist is famous for painting a series of sunflowers?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh painted several still-life works featuring sunflowers in Arles, France, in the late 1880s.

  3. In which country was the Beatles formed?

    Answer: United Kingdom

    The Beatles formed in Liverpool, England, United Kingdom, in 1960.

  4. Who is known as the 'King of Pop'?

    Answer: Michael Jackson

    Michael Jackson was dubbed the 'King of Pop' for his massive influence on popular music and dance.

  5. Which Beatles album is widely considered to feature the song 'A Day in the Life'?

    Answer: Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band

    'A Day in the Life' is the closing track of the Beatles' 1967 album Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band, often cited as one of their greatest songs.

  6. Who sang the song 'Rolling in the Deep'?

    Answer: Adele

    Adele's 'Rolling in the Deep' was a massive hit from her album '21'.

  7. The Beatles originated from which city in England?

    Answer: Liverpool

    The Beatles formed in Liverpool in 1960.

  8. Which legendary guitarist is known for his 'Star Spangled Banner' performance at Woodstock in 1969?

    Answer: Jimi Hendrix

    Jimi Hendrix's distorted, psychedelic rendition of the US national anthem at the Woodstock festival is considered a defining moment in rock history.

  9. Which composer lost his hearing later in life but continued to write music, including his famous 9th Symphony?

    Answer: Ludwig van Beethoven

    Beethoven composed some of his greatest works, including the 9th Symphony, after becoming almost completely deaf.

  10. Which painter is associated with the paper cut-outs late in his career, such as 'Icarus'?

    Answer: Henri Matisse

    Henri Matisse turned to cut-outs paper technique late in life, creating works like Icarus from the book Jazz.

  11. Which artist is known for the concept album 'The Rise and Fall of Ziggy Stardust and the Spiders from Mars'?

    Answer: David Bowie

    David Bowie released this landmark concept album in 1972, introducing his alter ego Ziggy Stardust.

  12. Which piece is considered one of the most difficult piano works ever written, composed by Franz Liszt?

    Answer: La Campanella

    'La Campanella' (from Grandes études de Paganini) is renowned for its technical difficulty.

  13. Which jazz musician is known for pioneering the 'cool jazz' style and composing 'Take Five'?

    Answer: Dave Brubeck

    Dave Brubeck's quartet recorded 'Take Five' (from the album Time Out, 1959), one of the first jazz singles to go gold.

  14. Which jazz musician, nicknamed 'Bird', revolutionized bebop improvisation?

    Answer: Charlie Parker

    Charlie 'Bird' Parker was a pioneering alto saxophonist who, along with Dizzy Gillespie, developed bebop in the 1940s.

  15. Which artist created the painting 'Guernica', depicting the bombing of a Basque town during the Spanish Civil War?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in 1937 after the Nazi bombing of the town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War; it is one of the most powerful anti-war paintings ever created.
