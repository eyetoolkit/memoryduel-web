US Presidents Quiz

Presidential trivia is the part of American history most people half-remember: you know Lincoln freed the slaves and Roosevelt did the New Deal, but the ordinal numbers, the amendments and the one-term presidents blur together.

This quiz has 20 questions built around the distinctions that actually get tested — who was first, who served longest, who resigned, which amendment stopped a third term. No sign-up, no timer, and every answer carries a one-sentence explanation so the correction sticks.

The full answer key is below the quiz, followed by a short summary of what the 20 questions cover and the answers to the questions people ask most often about the presidency itself.

Answer key

All 20 questions, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Who was the first president of the United States?

    Answer: George Washington

    Washington took office in 1789 and was elected unanimously by the Electoral College in both of his elections.

  2. Which president wrote the Declaration of Independence?

    Answer: Thomas Jefferson

    Jefferson drafted the Declaration in 1776, sixteen years before he became the third president.

  3. Which president issued the Emancipation Proclamation?

    Answer: Abraham Lincoln

    Lincoln issued the preliminary proclamation in September 1862 and the final version on 1 January 1863.

  4. Which president was assassinated in 1963?

    Answer: John F. Kennedy

    Kennedy was shot in Dallas on 22 November 1963, in the third year of his term.

  5. Which president resigned from office?

    Answer: Richard Nixon

    Nixon resigned in August 1974, the only president to do so, after the Watergate investigation closed in on him.

  6. Which president was elected to four terms?

    Answer: Franklin D. Roosevelt

    Roosevelt won in 1932, 1936, 1940 and 1944. The 22nd Amendment was passed after his death to prevent a repeat.

  7. Which amendment limits a president to two terms?

    Answer: The 22nd

    The 22nd Amendment was ratified in 1951, four years after it passed Congress.

  8. Who was the first president to live in the White House?

    Answer: John Adams

    John Adams moved in during 1800, near the end of his single term. Washington never lived there — the building was not finished while he was in office.

  9. Which president served the shortest term?

    Answer: William Henry Harrison

    Harrison died 31 days after his 1841 inauguration, having caught a cold that turned into pneumonia.

  10. Who was the youngest person elected president?

    Answer: John F. Kennedy

    Kennedy was 43 when elected in 1960. Theodore Roosevelt was younger when he took office, but he succeeded a president who died rather than being elected.

  11. Which president was impeached in 1998?

    Answer: Bill Clinton

    Clinton was impeached by the House in December 1998 and acquitted by the Senate in February 1999.

  12. Which president served two non-consecutive terms in the 19th century?

    Answer: Grover Cleveland

    Cleveland won in 1884, lost in 1888, and won again in 1892, which is why he is numbered as both the 22nd and 24th president.

  13. Which president led the Rough Riders during the Spanish-American War?

    Answer: Theodore Roosevelt

    Roosevelt led the volunteer cavalry regiment in 1898, three years before becoming president.

  14. How many presidents served before Abraham Lincoln?

    Answer: 15

    Lincoln was the 16th president, so fifteen men held the office before him, beginning with Washington in 1789.

  15. Which president ordered the Berlin Airlift in 1948?

    Answer: Harry Truman

    Truman ordered the airlift after the Soviet Union blockaded West Berlin, and it ran for almost a year.

  16. Which president signed the Civil Rights Act of 1964?

    Answer: Lyndon B. Johnson

    Kennedy proposed the bill, but it was Johnson who signed it into law on 2 July 1964, months after Kennedy's death.

  17. Which president worked as a peanut farmer before entering politics?

    Answer: Jimmy Carter

    Carter ran the family peanut farm in Plains, Georgia, and used the background deliberately in his 1976 campaign.

  18. Which president had a career as a Hollywood actor?

    Answer: Ronald Reagan

    Reagan appeared in more than fifty films before turning to politics, and was president of the Screen Actors Guild.

  19. Which president was born in Hawaii?

    Answer: Barack Obama

    Obama was born in Honolulu in 1961, two years after Hawaii became a state.

  20. Who was the first president to be impeached?

    Answer: Andrew Johnson

    Andrew Johnson was impeached by the House in 1868 and missed removal from office by a single Senate vote.

What this quiz covers

The questions avoid the two failure modes of president quizzes: asking for a list of ordinals nobody remembers, and asking about whoever is in office this week. Everything here is settled history with a date attached.

If you get fewer than twelve, the useful next step is not another quiz but a timeline — the answer key doubles as one, because each explanation names the year.

  • Term rules: the two-term tradition, Franklin Roosevelt's four elections, and the 22nd Amendment that ended it.
  • The shortest and longest presidencies, and why the numbering runs higher than the number of people who have held the office.
  • Landmark acts and the president attached to each: the Emancipation Proclamation, the Civil Rights Act of 1964, the Louisiana Purchase.
  • Careers before politics — the general, the peanut farmer, the actor.

Frequently asked questions

Who was the first president of the United States?
George Washington, who took office in 1789 and served two terms. He was elected unanimously by the Electoral College in both 1789 and 1792.
How many presidents has the United States had?
Forty-five people have held the office, but the numbering runs higher than that because two presidents served two non-consecutive terms and are counted twice. Grover Cleveland, for example, is both the 22nd and the 24th president.
Which president served the shortest term?
William Henry Harrison, who died of illness 31 days after his inauguration in 1841 and is the first president to die in office.
Can a US president serve more than two terms?
No. The 22nd Amendment, ratified in 1951, limits a president to two elected terms. Before it, the two-term limit was a convention rather than a rule, and Franklin D. Roosevelt was elected four times.
Which presidents were assassinated?
Four: Abraham Lincoln in 1865, James Garfield in 1881, William McKinley in 1901 and John F. Kennedy in 1963.
Whose faces are on Mount Rushmore?
George Washington, Thomas Jefferson, Theodore Roosevelt and Abraham Lincoln — chosen to represent the founding, expansion, development and preservation of the country.
