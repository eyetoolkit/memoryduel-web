Answer key

All fourteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In what year did World War II end?

    Answer: 1945

    World War II ended in 1945, with Germany surrendering in May and Japan surrendering in September after the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki.

  2. In which year did the Berlin Wall fall?

    Answer: 1989

    The Berlin Wall fell on November 9, 1989, symbolizing the end of the Cold War division of Europe.

  3. Which world war lasted from 1939 to 1945?

    Answer: World War II

    World War II lasted from 1939 to 1945 and was the deadliest conflict in human history.

  4. The Cold War was primarily a rivalry between which two superpowers?

    Answer: USA and Soviet Union

    The Cold War was an ideological and geopolitical struggle between the United States and the Soviet Union from 1947 to 1991.

  5. Which conflict was sparked by the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand in 1914?

    Answer: World War I

    The assassination of the Austro‑Hungarian heir triggered a series of mobilizations that escalated into World War I.

  6. Which event is widely considered the spark that started World War I?

    Answer: Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand

    The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary in Sarajevo on June 28, 1914 triggered a chain of alliances that led to World War I.

  7. Which of the following was a direct cause of the outbreak of World War I?

    Answer: The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand

    The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary in Sarajevo triggered a chain of events that led to the outbreak of World War I.

  8. Who was the leader of Nazi Germany during World War II?

    Answer: Adolf Hitler

    Adolf Hitler was the dictator of Nazi Germany from 1933 until his death in 1945.

  9. World War I began in 1914 after the assassination of which political leader?

    Answer: Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria

    Archduke Franz Ferdinand, heir to the Austro-Hungarian throne, was assassinated in Sarajevo on June 28, 1914, triggering a chain of events that led to World War I.

  10. Which event is known as the beginning of World War I in Europe?

    Answer: The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand

    The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary in Sarajevo on June 28, 1914, triggered a chain of events that led to World War I.

  11. Which event triggered the start of World War I?

    Answer: Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand

    The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary in Sarajevo in 1914 triggered the chain of events leading to World War I.

  12. Which event is considered the immediate cause of World War I?

    Answer: Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand

    The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary on June 28, 1914, triggered the chain of alliances leading to WWI.

  13. Who was the leader of the Soviet Union during most of World War II?

    Answer: Joseph Stalin

    Joseph Stalin led the Soviet Union from the mid-1920s until his death in 1953, including through World War II.

  14. Which treaty ended World War I?

    Answer: Treaty of Versailles

    The Treaty of Versailles was signed on June 28, 1919, formally ending World War I between Germany and the Allied Powers.
