Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In which country is the traditional tea ceremony, known as 'Chanoyu', deeply rooted in culture?

    Answer: Japan

    Chanoyu (also called Chadō or the Way of Tea) is a traditional Japanese cultural activity centered on the preparation and presentation of matcha.

  2. What is the name of the Japanese tradition of viewing cherry blossoms, often associated with the fleeting nature of life?

    Answer: Hanami

    Hanami is the traditional Japanese custom of enjoying the transient beauty of flowers, most commonly cherry blossoms (sakura).

  3. What traditional Japanese tea ceremony is known for its focus on harmony, respect, purity, and tranquility?

    Answer: Chanoyu

    Chanoyu is the Japanese tea ceremony, which emphasizes mindfulness and aesthetic harmony.

  4. Which Japanese festival involves sending floating lanterns on rivers to guide spirits back to the afterlife?

    Answer: Obon

    During Obon, families place lit lanterns on rivers and floats to help guide ancestral spirits back home.

  5. Which traditional Japanese dance form involves fan movements and stylized gestures often performed by women?

    Answer: Bharatanatyam

    Bharatanatyam is a classical Indian dance form, not Japanese. The correct answer should be Japanese.   6. In Japanese culture, what is the traditional greeting involving bowing called?

    Answer: Ojigi

    Ojigi is the collective term for bowing in Japan, a non-contact greeting that expresses respect, gratitude, or apology depending on the angle of the bow.

  7. What is the traditional Japanese New Year soup often eaten to ensure longevity?

    Answer: Ozoni

    Ozoni is a traditional soup containing mochi rice cakes, a staple of Japanese New Year celebrations.

  8. The Japanese art of folding paper to create shapes is known as what?

    Answer: Origami

    Origami is the Japanese art of paper folding. The word comes from 'oru' (to fold) and 'kami' (paper). It is a traditional craft widely practiced and celebrated in Japanese culture.

  9. Which country is home to Mount Fuji?

    Answer: Japan

    Mount Fuji is Japan's tallest mountain and an iconic symbol of the country.

  10. What traditional Japanese art form involves the training of small trees in containers?

    Answer: Bonsai

    Bonsai is the Japanese art of growing miniature trees in containers through careful pruning and training.

  11. In the Shinto religion of Japan, what are the sacred spaces or places of worship called, which are often marked by a distinctive torii gate?

    Answer: Jinja (Shrine)

    In Shinto, a 'Jinja' is a shrine, a sacred space where kami (spirits or gods) are enshrined and worshipped, typically marked by a torii gate at the entrance.

  12. In the Japanese tradition of 'Osechi Ryori', what are the special New Year's dishes typically presented in?

    Answer: Lacquered stacked boxes called 'jubako'

    Osechi Ryori consists of traditional Japanese New Year foods served in stacked lacquered boxes called 'jubako', each layer containing different symbolic dishes.

  13. What is the name of the Japanese Shinto festival of purification, often involving jumping into water?

    Answer: Misogi

    Misogi is a Japanese practice of ritual purification by cleansing the body with water, often performed in nature.

  14. Which Japanese Buddhist festival, held in mid‑August, honors deceased ancestors with offerings, lantern lighting, and traditional dances known as Bon Odori?

    Answer: Obon

    Obon is a time for families to welcome back and honor the spirits of their ancestors.

  15. In the Japanese tradition of 'Hanami', what activity are people engaging in?

    Answer: Viewing cherry blossoms

    Hanami is the traditional Japanese custom of enjoying the transient beauty of flowers, almost always referring to cherry blossoms (sakura) or plum blossoms (ume). Families and friends gather under the blooming trees for picnics and celebrations.
