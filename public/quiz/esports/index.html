Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the name of the professional League of Legends championship series organized by Riot Games?

    Answer: League of Legends World Championship

    The League of Legends World Championship is the annual world championship tournament for the game, organized by its developer, Riot Games.

  2. What type of game is 'Fortnite' primarily classified as?

    Answer: Battle Royale

    Fortnite popularized the Battle Royale genre, where players fight to be the last one standing.

  3. Which esports title is primarily known for its 5v5 tactical shooter gameplay and features agents with unique abilities?

    Answer: Valorant

    Valorant, developed by Riot Games, is a 5v5 tactical FPS where players choose from a roster of agents, each with distinct abilities.

  4. Which professional esports game involves 'champions', 'turrets', and a 'Nexus'?

    Answer: League of Legends

    League of Legends (LoL) is a MOBA where teams defend turrets and destroy the enemy Nexus.

  5. In the popular esports game 'League of Legends', what is the name of the company that developed it?

    Answer: Riot Games

    Riot Games is the developer and publisher of League of Legends, one of the world's most popular MOBA games.

  6. Which esports organization is one of the oldest and most recognized, with teams competing in multiple games like Counter-Strike and Valorant?

    Answer: Team Liquid

    Team Liquid is one of the oldest and most successful esports organizations, competing across multiple titles.

  7. In esports, which country has historically dominated competitive StarCraft: Brood War?

    Answer: South Korea

    South Korea has been the dominant force in StarCraft: Brood War esports, with a massive professional league system.

  8. Which famous esports tournament is known as 'The International' and features a massive prize pool funded by community contributions?

    Answer: The International (Dota 2)

    The International is the annual world championship for Dota 2, famous for its player-funded prize pool via the in-game Battle Pass.

  9. What is the term for a competitive video game match played online that is broadcast live to an audience, often with commentary?

    Answer: Esports Broadcast

    An esports broadcast (or stream) is the live online transmission of a competitive gaming event. LAN Party is a local gathering, a speedrun is a race to complete a game, and an MMORPG is a game genre.

  10. Which esports organization is known for its Korean roots and success in StarCraft?

    Answer: T1

    T1 (formerly SK Telecom T1) is a legendary Korean esports organization.

  11. In competitive gaming, what does the term 'MOBA' stand for?

    Answer: Multiplayer Online Battle Arena

    MOBA stands for Multiplayer Online Battle Arena, a genre that includes games like League of Legends, Dota 2, and Smite, where teams compete to destroy the enemy's base.

  12. In 'Street Fighter', what is the input for the iconic 'Hadouken' move performed by Ryu?

    Answer: Quarter-circle forward + punch

    The Hadouken is performed by inputting a quarter-circle forward (down, down-forward, forward) followed by a punch button.

  13. Which esports tournament holds the record for the largest prize pool in esports history as of 2024?

    Answer: The International (Dota 2)

    The International, the annual Dota 2 championship, has consistently held the largest prize pools in esports, exceeding $40 million in some years.

  14. In competitive League of Legends, what is the term for the neutral monster found in the top and bottom side river alcoves that grants a team-wide vision advantage?

    Answer: Scuttle Crab

    The Scuttle Crab is a neutral monster in the river that, when killed, creates a speed shrine and grants vision in an area, providing crucial map control for teams.

  15. In 'Counter-Strike: Global Offensive' (CS:GO), which side must plant a bomb at one of two designated sites?

    Answer: Terrorists

    The Terrorist side must plant the C4 bomb at a bomb site, while Counter-Terrorists must defuse it or eliminate them.
