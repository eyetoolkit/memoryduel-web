Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In which sport is the term 'birdie' used?

    Answer: Golf

    A birdie is a score of one under par on a hole in golf.

  2. In which sport do teams compete to score goals by hitting a small ball into a hole using clubs?

    Answer: Golf

    Golf is a sport where players use clubs to hit a ball into holes on a course in as few strokes as possible.

  3. In which sport do athletes compete to hit a ball into a series of holes in the fewest strokes possible?

    Answer: Golf

    Golf is a sport where players use clubs to hit a ball into a series of holes on a course, aiming for the lowest score.

  4. In which sport would you typically see a 'hole-in-one' achieved?

    Answer: Golf

    A hole-in-one occurs when a golfer hits the ball directly into the hole from the tee in a single stroke.

  5. In golf, what is the score called when a player completes a hole in one stroke less than par?

    Answer: Birdie

    A 'Birdie' is one stroke under par. An 'Eagle' is two under par, an 'Albatross' (or Double Eagle) is three under par, and a 'Bogey' is one over par.

  6. What is the name of the major professional golf tour primarily based in the United States?

    Answer: PGA Tour

    The PGA Tour is the main professional golf tour for male golfers, primarily operating in North America.

  7. What is the maximum number of strokes allowed on a single hole in golf before scoring is capped (for handicap purposes in stroke play)?

    Answer: No cap exists

    In formal stroke play, there is no cap on strokes; however, the 'double bogey' rule or similar net scoring caps can apply in certain formats. The traditional answer is that there is no stroke limit in pure stroke play, though many handicap systems cap scores at net double bogey.

  8. In golf, what is the maximum number of clubs a player is allowed in their bag during a round?

    Answer: 14

    Under the Rules of Golf, a player may carry no more than 14 clubs during a round.

  9. Which sport features a 'grand slam' involving winning four specific tournaments?

    Answer: Both A and B

    Both tennis (Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon, US Open) and golf's majors are called Grand Slams.

  10. Which golfer has won the most major championships in men's golf?

    Answer: Jack Nicklaus

    Jack Nicklaus holds the record with 18 major championship victories, followed by Tiger Woods with 15.

  11. In golf, what is the name of the hole with the lowest par on a standard course?

    Answer: Ace

    An 'Ace' or hole-in-one is scoring the ball in the hole in one stroke, typically on a par-3 hole, though it can happen on any hole.

  12. Which sport originated in Scotland in the 15th century?

    Answer: Golf

    Golf as we know it originated in Scotland during the 15th century.

  13. Which sport's top international competition is the Ryder Cup?

    Answer: Golf

    The Ryder Cup is a biennial golf competition between teams from Europe and the United States.

  14. In professional golf's 'The Masters', which club traditionally hosts the tournament?

    Answer: Augusta National Golf Club

    The Masters Tournament is held annually at Augusta National Golf Club in Georgia, USA.

  15. Which sport's world championship is known as 'The Masters'?

    Answer: Golf

    The Masters is one of golf's four major championships, held annually at Augusta National Golf Club in the United States.
