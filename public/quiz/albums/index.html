Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which famous pop artist released the album 'Thriller' in 1982?

    Answer: Michael Jackson

    'Thriller' by Michael Jackson is the best-selling album of all time, with estimated sales of 66 million copies worldwide.

  2. Which British rock band released the album 'Abbey Road' in 1969?

    Answer: The Beatles

    'Abbey Road' was the eleventh studio album by The Beatles and is one of the most famous album covers in music history.

  3. Which rapper released the album 'Recovery' in 2010?

    Answer: Eminem

    Eminem released 'Recovery' in 2010, featuring hits like 'Love the Way You Lie' with Rihanna.

  4. Which Beatles album features the iconic cover with the band members walking across a zebra crossing?

    Answer: Abbey Road

    The 'Abbey Road' cover is one of the most famous album covers in music history.

  5. Which band released the landmark album 'The Dark Side of the Moon' in 1973?

    Answer: Pink Floyd

    'The Dark Side of the Moon' is the eighth studio album by the English rock band Pink Floyd, released in March 1973.

  6. Which singer-songwriter released the critically acclaimed albums '21' and '25'?

    Answer: Adele

    British singer Adele released '21' in 2011 and '25' in 2015, both of which became global mega-hits.

  7. Which female artist released the record-breaking 2011 album '21', featuring hits like 'Rolling in the Deep' and 'Someone Like You'?

    Answer: Adele

    Adele's album '21' became one of the best-selling albums of the 21st century, earning her multiple Grammy Awards.

  8. Which artist's debut album is titled 'Nevermind', featuring the iconic cover of a baby swimming underwater?

    Answer: Nirvana

    Nirvana's 1991 album 'Nevermind' features the famous 'Nevermind baby' on its cover and includes the hit 'Smells Like Teen Spirit'.

  9. Which album, released in 1982, became the best-selling album of all time worldwide with an estimated 66+ million copies sold?

    Answer: Thriller by Michael Jackson

    Michael Jackson's 'Thriller' revolutionized the music industry with its innovative music videos and blend of pop, rock, and R&B. It produced seven singles and won a record eight Grammy Awards.

  10. Which Beatles album contains the song 'A Day in the Life'?

    Answer: Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band

    'A Day in the Life' is the closing track of Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band (1967).

  11. Which 1971 album by Marvin Gaye addresses social and political issues?

    Answer: What's Going On

    Marvin Gaye's 'What's Going On' is a landmark concept album dealing with themes of poverty, war, and ecology.

  12. Which album, released in 1975, features the hit 'Bohemian Rhapsody' and is by which band?

    Answer: Queen - 'A Night at the Opera'

    'Bohemian Rhapsody' is the iconic six-minute suite by Queen from their 1975 album 'A Night at the Opera'.

  13. Which artist released the album 'Lemonade', which features the song 'Formation'?

    Answer: Beyoncé

    'Lemonade' is a visual album by Beyoncé, released in 2016, which includes the hit single 'Formation'.

  14. Which jazz trumpeter recorded the seminal album 'Kind of Blue' in 1959?

    Answer: Miles Davis

    Miles Davis recorded 'Kind of Blue' in 1959. It is the best-selling jazz album of all time and pioneered modal jazz, featuring musicians like John Coltrane and Bill Evans.

  15. Which album by Pink Floyd is known for its use of sound effects, spoken word samples, and the track 'The Great Gig in the Sky'?

    Answer: The Dark Side of the Moon

    'The Dark Side of the Moon' (1973) is famous for its innovative production techniques, including the use of sound effects and spoken word interviews.
