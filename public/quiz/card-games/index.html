Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In the card game 'Uno', what happens if a player plays a 'Draw 4' card?

    Answer: The next player draws 4 cards and skips their turn

    A 'Draw 4' Wild card forces the next player to draw four cards and lose their turn.

  2. In standard poker, what is the highest-ranking hand?

    Answer: Royal flush

    A royal flush (ace, king, queen, jack, ten, all of the same suit) is the highest possible hand in standard poker.

  3. In the board game Clue, how many suspect cards are there in the deck?

    Answer: 6

    Clue features 6 suspect cards: Miss Scarlet, Colonel Mustard, Mrs. White, Mr. Green, Mrs. Peacock, and Professor Plum.

  4. Which card game often uses a standard 52-card deck and involves betting and bluffing?

    Answer: Poker

    Poker is a family of card games involving betting and individual play, typically played with a standard 52-card deck.

  5. Which card game requires players to avoid taking certain penalty cards, particularly the 'Hearts' and the 'Queen of Spades'?

    Answer: Hearts

    In the game of Hearts, the goal is to avoid winning tricks containing Hearts and the Queen of Spades.

  6. In 'Poker', what hand consists of five cards of the same suit in sequence?

    Answer: Straight Flush

    A Straight Flush is five sequential cards all of the same suit, making it the second strongest hand in poker.

  7. What is the name of the card game where players try to reach 21 without going over?

    Answer: Blackjack

    Blackjack (also known as 21) is a casino card game where players aim to get a hand value as close to 21 as possible without exceeding it.

  8. In 'Magic: The Gathering', which card is considered the most powerful and valuable card from the original Alpha set?

    Answer: Black Lotus

    Black Lotus is an Mox that produces three mana of any color and is banned in most formats due to its power.

  9. What is the maximum number of players allowed at a standard poker table in a casino?

    Answer: 9

    A standard full ring poker table seats 9 or 10 players, though 9-max is common in tournaments and 6-max in online games.

  10. Which Japanese company is credited with creating the modern trading card game (TCG) genre with the launch of Magic: The Gathering in 1993?

    Answer: Wizards of the Coast

    Wizards of the Coast created Magic: The Gathering, the first modern trading card game, which revolutionized tabletop gaming and spawned an entire genre.

  11. In the original Japanese version of the card game Uno, are there any unique rules not found in the international version?

    Answer: Yes, Japanese Uno includes a 'Zero Swap' variant officially

    The Japanese version of Uno has an official 'Zero Swap' variant where playing a 0 forces all players to pass their hands in the direction of play. This is not part of the standard international rules.

  12. Which traditional card game, popular in many cultures, is played with 32 cards and involves taking tricks, commonly associated with German-speaking countries?

    Answer: Skat

    Skat is a trick-taking card game for three players, popular in Germany and other German-speaking regions, using a 32-card deck.

  13. Which of these is NOT a standard phase in the popular board game 'Poker'?

    Answer: The Deal

    In Texas Hold'em Poker, the community cards are revealed in stages called the Flop, the Turn, and the River. 'The Deal' is the action of distributing cards, not a named betting phase.

  14. In the game of Bridge, how many cards are dealt to each player?

    Answer: 13

    In Bridge, each player receives 13 cards from a standard 52-card deck.

  15. How many cards are included in a standard Magic: The Gathering booster pack?

    Answer: 15 cards

    A standard Magic: The Gathering booster pack contains 15 cards, including 1 rare/mythic, 3 uncommons, 10 commons, and 1 basic land or marketing card.
