Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which board game is played on a 15x15 grid and involves capturing territory?

    Answer: Go

    Go (Weiqi/Baduk) is played on a 15x15 or 19x19 grid where players capture territory by surrounding stones.

  2. In the board game Catan, how many victory points does the player with the "Longest Road" receive?

    Answer: 2

    The Longest Road card is worth 2 victory points at the end of the game.

  3. What is the name of the traditional Japanese strategy board game where the goal is to surround your opponent's territory?

    Answer: Go

    Go (Weiqi/Baduk) is one of the oldest board games in the world, originating in China, where players place black and white stones to control territory.

  4. How many players typically play a standard game of chess?

    Answer: 2

    Chess is a two-player strategy board game played on a checkered 64-square board.

  5. Which of these is a real-time strategy video game series set in a futuristic sci-fi universe?

    Answer: StarCraft

    StarCraft is a famous real-time strategy game by Blizzard Entertainment, hugely popular in esports.

  6. In the board game Monopoly, which token is shaped like a dog?

    Answer: Dog (Scottie)

    The dog‑shaped token in Monopoly is the Scottie dog.

  7. Which board game requires players to place colored discs to connect four in a row?

    Answer: Connect Four

    Connect Four, invented by Howard Gardner in 1974, challenges players to be the first to align four of their colored discs vertically, horizontally, or diagonally.

  8. In the board game Catan (formerly The Settlers of Catan), what resource is represented by sheep?

    Answer: Wool

    In Catan, sheep tiles produce Wool, one of the five resources (along with Grain, Brick, Lumber, and Ore) needed to build and expand.

  9. In the board game Ticket to Ride, what is the primary method of scoring points?

    Answer: Completing train routes

    Players score points by claiming railway routes and completing destination tickets that connect cities.

  10. In Civilization VI, which leader leads the Scythian civilization?

    Answer: Tomyris

    Tomyris is the leader of the Scythian civilization in Civilization VI.

  11. In the board game Go, what is the name of the traditional opening sequence of the first few moves?

    Answer: Fuseki

    Fuseki refers to the opening phase of a Go game where players establish their positions across the board. Joseki refers to established corner sequences, Ko is a repeating capture rule, and Tenuki means playing elsewhere.

  12. In StarCraft II, which Terran infantry unit can use Stim Packs and is produced from the Barracks?

    Answer: Marine

    The Marine is the basic infantry unit that can activate Stim Packs to trade health for increased speed.

  13. In the strategy board game 'Go', what is the maximum possible score for a single player in a standard 19x19 game?

    Answer: 361

    A 19x19 Go board has 361 intersections; theoretically one player could control all of them, though in practice the maximum is lower due to the rules of capture and territory.

  14. In the board game 'Go', what is the minimum number of stones needed to surround and capture a single isolated stone?

    Answer: 4

    In Go, a single stone has four liberties (adjacent points), so it takes at least 4 stones to completely surround and capture an isolated stone.

  15. In the complex board game 'Twilight Imperium', what is the primary objective for players?

    Answer: Earn victory points through various means

    Twilight Imperium is a grand strategy space opera game where players earn victory points through controlling planets, completing objectives, and engaging in diplomacy and warfare.
