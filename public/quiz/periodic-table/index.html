Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the chemical symbol for gold?

    Answer: Au

    The chemical symbol for gold is Au, derived from the Latin word 'aurum', meaning 'shining dawn'.

  2. Which of these is a chemical element with the atomic number 1?

    Answer: Hydrogen

    Hydrogen has 1 proton in its nucleus, giving it atomic number 1. Helium is 2, carbon is 6, and oxygen is 8.

  3. Which of these is a noble gas?

    Answer: Argon

    Argon is a noble gas (group 18), known for its chemical inertness. Chlorine is a halogen, sodium an alkali metal, and phosphorus a nonmetal.

  4. Which of these elements is a halogen?

    Answer: Chlorine

    Chlorine (Cl) is a halogen, found in Group 17 of the periodic table. Halogens are highly reactive nonmetals that include fluorine, chlorine, bromine, iodine, and astatine.

  5. Which of the following is a noble gas?

    Answer: Argon

    Argon (Ar) belongs to the noble gases, a group of inert elements that rarely form chemical compounds.

  6. Which element has the chemical symbol 'W'?

    Answer: Both A and B

    The element has two names — Tungsten and Wolfram — and its symbol 'W' comes from 'Wolfram.' Both names are officially recognized.

  7. What is the chemical symbol for iron?

    Answer: Fe

    The symbol Fe comes from the Latin word for iron, 'ferrum'.

  8. What is the chemical symbol for potassium?

    Answer: K

    K comes from 'Kalium', the Neo-Latin name. P is phosphorus, Pt is platinum, Po is polonium.

  9. What is the atomic number of carbon?

    Answer: 6

    Carbon has 6 protons in its nucleus, giving it an atomic number of 6.

  10. What is the chemical symbol for Sodium?

    Answer: Na

    Sodium's symbol comes from its Latin name 'Natrium'.

  11. Which of these is NOT a noble gas?

    Answer: Chlorine

    Noble gases are in Group 18 and include argon, neon, and krypton. Chlorine is a reactive halogen in Group 17, not a noble gas.

  12. Which of the following is the most electronegative element on the periodic table?

    Answer: Fluorine

    Fluorine is the most electronegative element, meaning it has the strongest tendency to attract electrons in a chemical bond.

  13. Which of these elements has the highest electronegativity on the Pauling scale?

    Answer: Fluorine

    Fluorine is the most electronegative element (3.98 on the Pauling scale), followed by oxygen (3.44), nitrogen (3.04), and chlorine (3.16).

  14. What is the chemical symbol for tungsten?

    Answer: W

    The symbol W comes from the German name for tungsten, 'Wolfram'.

  15. Which of the following is an example of a noble gas?

    Answer: Argon

    Noble gases, including argon, helium, neon, krypton, xenon, and radon, have full outer electron shells and are highly unreactive.
