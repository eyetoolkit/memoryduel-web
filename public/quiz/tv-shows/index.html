Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which TV show is set in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana?

    Answer: Stranger Things

    'Stranger Things' is a Netflix series centered on supernatural events in Hawkins.

  2. In which TV series does Walter White become a methamphetamine producer?

    Answer: Breaking Bad

    'Breaking Bad' follows Walter White (Bryan Cranston), a chemistry teacher who turns to manufacturing meth after a cancer diagnosis.

  3. Which TV series is set in the fictional country of Westeros?

    Answer: Game of Thrones

    Game of Thrones, based on George R.R. Martin's books, is set primarily in Westeros and ran on HBO from 2011 to 2019.

  4. Which TV series features a chemistry teacher who turns to manufacturing methamphetamine?

    Answer: Breaking Bad

    Breaking Bad (2008–2013) follows Walter White, a chemistry teacher diagnosed with cancer who begins producing meth, starring Bryan Cranston and Aaron Paul.

  5. Which TV series, set in the American Southwest, follows a high school chemistry teacher turned drug manufacturer?

    Answer: Breaking Bad

    Breaking Bad is set in Albuquerque, New Mexico, and follows Walter White's transformation from teacher to drug kingpin.

  6. Which actor has played the Doctor the most times in 'Doctor Who'?

    Answer: William Hartnell

    William Hartnell originated the role as the First Doctor and had the longest continuous tenure at the time.

  7. In the TV show 'Game of Thrones', which family motto is 'Winter is Coming'?

    Answer: Stark

    'Winter is Coming' is the official motto of House Stark, rulers of the North.

  8. In the TV series 'Breaking Bad', what is the real name of the protagonist who becomes a drug lord?

    Answer: Walter White

    Walter White is a chemistry teacher turned methamphetamine producer.

  9. Which TV series, set in a dystopian future, is famous for the phrase 'Winter is coming'?

    Answer: Game of Thrones

    'Winter is coming' is the motto of House Stark in HBO's Game of Thrones, based on George R.R. Martin's books.

  10. The TV series 'Stranger Things' is primarily set in which decade?

    Answer: 1980s

    'Stranger Things' is set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, and draws heavily on pop culture from that era.

  11. In the TV series 'Game of Thrones', which character is known as the 'Mother of Dragons'?

    Answer: Daenerys Targaryen

    Daenerys Targaryen (Emilia Clarke) is called the 'Mother of Dragons' after she hatches three dragon eggs.

  12. Which TV show, set in a fantasy world, is based on George R.R. Martin's book series 'A Song of Ice and Fire'?

    Answer: Game of Thrones

    'Game of Thrones' aired on HBO from 2011 to 2019 and was adapted from Martin's epic fantasy book series.

  13. In the TV series 'Breaking Bad', what is the chemical element referenced in the title?

    Answer: Bromine

    The title 'Breaking Bad' is a play on the chemical element Bromine (Br) and the phrase 'breaking bad' meaning going astray.

  14. Which TV show features a high school chemistry teacher turned drug kingpin in New Mexico?

    Answer: Breaking Bad

    'Breaking Bad' follows Walter White's transformation from teacher to criminal.

  15. In the TV series 'Breaking Bad', what is the profession of the main character, Walter White, before his cancer diagnosis?

    Answer: Chemistry Teacher

    Walter White uses his expertise as a high school chemistry teacher to cook methamphetamine to secure his family's future.
