Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which subatomic particle has a negative electric charge?

    Answer: Electron

    Electrons carry a negative charge, while protons are positive and neutrons are neutral.

  2. Which particle is responsible for the electromagnetic force?

    Answer: Photon

    The photon is the elementary particle that mediates the electromagnetic force, the force between electrically charged particles.

  3. Which of the following particles carries the strong nuclear force?

    Answer: Gluon

    Gluons mediate the strong nuclear force that binds quarks together. Photons mediate electromagnetism, W bosons mediate the weak force, and gravitons (hypothetical) would mediate gravity.

  4. Which of the following particles is known as the 'carrier' of the electromagnetic force?

    Answer: Photon

    The photon is the elementary particle responsible for mediating the electromagnetic force. Gluons carry the strong force, W bosons carry the weak force, and while gravitons are theorized to carry gravity, they have not yet been observed.

  5. In which year did the Higgs boson discovery at CERN's Large Hadron Collider primarily occur?

    Answer: 2012

    ATLAS and CMS experiments at CERN announced the discovery of a Higgs-like boson in July 2012, confirming the mechanism that gives particles mass.

  6. Which subatomic particle was experimentally discovered in 2012, confirming the mechanism that gives particles mass?

    Answer: Higgs boson

    The Higgs boson was discovered at CERN's Large Hadron Collider in 2012. It is the quantum excitation of the Higgs field, which imparts mass to fundamental particles via the Brout-Englert-Higgs mechanism.

  7. Which subatomic particle was predicted by the Standard Model but not observed until 2012?

    Answer: Higgs boson

    The Higgs boson was confirmed at CERN's Large Hadron Collider in 2012, completing the Standard Model.

  8. Which of the following particles is a boson?

    Answer: Photon

    Photons are gauge bosons responsible for electromagnetic force; electrons and quarks are fermions.

  9. Which subatomic particle, predicted by Higgs theory, was experimentally confirmed at CERN in 2012?

    Answer: Higgs boson

    The discovery of the Higgs boson was announced at CERN in 2012, confirming the existence of the Higgs field, which gives mass to other elementary particles.

  10. Which particle is its own antiparticle?

    Answer: Photon

    The photon is a neutral boson and is its own antiparticle.

  11. Which of the following is NOT a fundamental force of nature?

    Answer: Friction

    The four fundamental forces are gravity, electromagnetism, the strong nuclear force, and the weak nuclear force. Friction is a macroscopic force arising from electromagnetic interactions.

  12. What is the smallest known fundamental particle that makes up ordinary matter?

    Answer: Quark

    Quarks are elementary particles that combine to form protons and neutrons, the building blocks of atomic nuclei.

  13. Which subatomic particle was predicted by Paul Dirac and experimentally discovered in 1932?

    Answer: Positron

    Dirac's equation predicted the positron, the antiparticle of the electron, discovered by Carl Anderson in 1932.

  14. In particle physics, what is the name of the theorized particle responsible for imparting mass to other fundamental particles?

    Answer: Higgs Boson

    The Higgs boson is an elementary particle in the Standard Model of particle physics, associated with the Higgs field, which gives mass to other fundamental particles.

  15. Which subatomic particle was theoretically predicted before its discovery and is responsible for giving mass to other particles via the Higgs field?

    Answer: Higgs boson

    The Higgs boson was predicted by the Standard Model and confirmed in 2012 at CERN; it is associated with the Higgs field that gives mass to other particles.
