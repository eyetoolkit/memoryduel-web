Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the process by which water moves from the ground to the atmosphere and back?

    Answer: Water cycle

    The water cycle (hydrological cycle) describes the continuous movement of water through evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and runoff.

  2. What is the name of the natural protective layer of gases surrounding Earth?

    Answer: Atmosphere

    The atmosphere is the layer of gases that surrounds Earth, held in place by gravity.

  3. Which biome is characterized by very low precipitation and extreme temperature fluctuations?

    Answer: Desert

    Deserts receive very little rainfall and often experience scorching days and freezing nights due to lack of moisture in the air.

  4. What is the process by which water changes from liquid to gas at the Earth's surface?

    Answer: Evaporation

    Evaporation is the liquid-to-gas phase change driven by solar heat.

  5. What gas makes up the majority of Earth's atmosphere?

    Answer: Nitrogen

    Nitrogen makes up about 78% of Earth's atmosphere, while oxygen makes up about 21%.

  6. Which layer of Earth's atmosphere do most weather events occur in?

    Answer: Troposphere

    The troposphere is the lowest layer, containing most of the atmosphere's water vapor and weather.

  7. What is the primary reason for Earth's seasons?

    Answer: The tilt of Earth's axis

    Earth's seasons are caused by the 23.5-degree tilt of its axis, which leads to varying amounts of sunlight reaching different parts of the planet throughout the year, not by its distance from the Sun.

  8. What is the phenomenon where Earth's magnetic poles switch places called?

    Answer: Geomagnetic Reversal

    Geomagnetic reversal is a change in Earth's magnetic field in which the magnetic North and South poles interchanged.

  9. What is the process by which rocks are broken down by weather and rain?

    Answer: Weathering

    Weathering is the breakdown of rocks, soil, and minerals through contact with the Earth's atmosphere, water, and biological organisms.

  10. What is the term for the gradual increase in the Earth's average surface temperature due to human activities, particularly greenhouse gas emissions?

    Answer: Global warming

    Global warming refers specifically to the rise in Earth's average temperature driven mainly by the burning of fossil fuels and the resulting greenhouse effect.

  11. The 'Ring of Fire' is a major zone of tectonic activity that surrounds which ocean?

    Answer: Pacific Ocean

    The Pacific Ring of Fire is a horseshoe-shaped belt around the Pacific Ocean where a large number of earthquakes and volcanic eruptions occur.

  12. What is the name of the hypothesis that Earth's living and non-living components form a single, interacting system?

    Answer: Gaia hypothesis

    Proposed by James Lovelock, the Gaia hypothesis views Earth as a self-regulating organism.

  13. Which geological era are we currently living in?

    Answer: Cenozoic Era

    The Cenozoic Era began about 66 million years ago after the extinction of the dinosaurs and is often referred to as the 'Age of Mammals'.

  14. Which element is most abundant in the Earth's crust?

    Answer: Oxygen

    Oxygen makes up about 46% of the crust by mass, mostly bound in silicates.

  15. Which layer of Earth's interior is liquid and generates the magnetic field?

    Answer: Outer core

    Convection of molten iron in the outer core produces Earth's magnetosphere.
