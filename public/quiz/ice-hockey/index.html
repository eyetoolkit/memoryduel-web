Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In ice hockey, what is the name of the thin disk used to play the game?

    Answer: Puck

    The black rubber disk used in ice hockey is called a puck.

  2. In which sport do teams compete to score goals while players wear skates on ice?

    Answer: Ice hockey

    Ice hockey features two teams using sticks to shoot a puck into the opponent's net while skating on ice.

  3. Which of these sports is NOT part of the Summer Olympic Games?

    Answer: Ice Hockey

    Ice Hockey is a Winter Olympic sport, while the others are Summer Olympic sports.

  4. In ice hockey, how long is a standard professional game (excluding overtime)?

    Answer: 60 minutes

    A standard ice hockey game consists of three 20-minute periods, totaling 60 minutes of play.

  5. In ice hockey, how many players from one team are typically on the ice at one time (excluding the goalie)?

    Answer: 5

    In standard ice hockey play, each team has five skaters plus one goaltender on the ice.

  6. In ice hockey, what is it called when a player receives a two-minute penalty for a minor infraction?

    Answer: Sin bin

    The penalty box is commonly referred to as the 'sin bin', where players serve time for minor infractions.

  7. In which sport is the 'Stanley Cup' awarded to the championship-winning team?

    Answer: Ice Hockey

    The Stanley Cup is the championship trophy of the National Hockey League (NHL).

  8. Which major sport is played with a 'puck'?

    Answer: Ice Hockey

    Ice hockey is played on ice using a flat, disc-shaped object called a puck.

  9. In ice hockey, what is it called when a player scores three goals in a single game?

    Answer: Hat trick

    Scoring three goals in a single game of ice hockey (or soccer) is known as a hat trick.

  10. In ice hockey, what is the term for a game that ends in a tie after regulation time?

    Answer: Draw

    If the score is tied after regulation time, the game goes to overtime (sudden death) or a shootout, but the regulation result is a 'draw' or 'tie'.

  11. In which country did the sport of ice hockey originate?

    Answer: Canada

    Ice hockey is widely considered to have originated in Canada in the 19th century.

  12. Which professional league is considered the top tier of ice hockey in North America?

    Answer: NHL

    The National Hockey League (NHL) is the premier professional ice hockey league in the world, featuring teams from the United States and Canada.

  13. In ice hockey, what is the name of the trophy awarded to the champions of the National Hockey League (NHL)?

    Answer: Stanley Cup

    The Stanley Cup is the oldest professional sports trophy in North America and is awarded annually to the NHL playoff champion.

  14. In which year did the 'Miracle on Ice' occur, when the US ice hockey team defeated the Soviet Union at the Winter Olympics?

    Answer: 1980

    The 'Miracle on Ice' took place at the 1980 Winter Olympics in Lake Placid, New York, where the underdog US team beat the heavily favored Soviet Union.

  15. Which country has won the most IIHF World Championship gold medals as of 2023?

    Answer: Canada

    Canada holds the record with 27 world championship titles.
