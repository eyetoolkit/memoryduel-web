Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which of these sports is NOT part of the traditional Summer Olympic Games programme?

    Answer: Cricket

    Cricket has historically not been part of the Summer Olympics (though it was included in 1900 and returned for 2028), unlike Rowing, Boxing, and Cycling.

  2. Which country is famous for inventing the sport of cricket?

    Answer: England

    Cricket originated in England and spread throughout the British Empire.

  3. In which sport do teams compete to score runs by hitting a ball and running between two sets of wickets?

    Answer: Cricket

    Cricket involves batting to score runs while running between wickets, defended by a fielding team.

  4. Which two countries are traditionally the main rivals in the Ashes cricket series?

    Answer: Australia vs England

    The Ashes is a historic Test cricket series played between Australia and England.

  5. In which sport is a 'six' scored by hitting the ball over the boundary without bouncing?

    Answer: Cricket

    In cricket, a hit that clears the boundary without touching the ground scores six runs.

  6. In cricket, how many wickets make up a team's innings in a Test match?

    Answer: 10

    In cricket, 10 wickets falling ends a team's batting innings, as there are 11 players total.

  7. What color is traditionally worn by the team chasing a target in a cricket match?

    Answer: Colored clothing

    In limited-overs cricket, the team batting second wears colored clothing (e.g., blue, yellow), while the first-innings batting team traditionally wears white in longer formats.

  8. How many runs are awarded for a 'six' in cricket?

    Answer: 6

    A six is scored when the ball leaves the playing area without touching the ground, worth six runs.

  9. What is the shape of the field in a standard game of cricket?

    Answer: Oval

    A cricket field is typically a large grassy area that is oval in shape.

  10. Which country won the first ever Cricket World Cup in 1975?

    Answer: West Indies

    The West Indies won the inaugural Cricket World Cup held in 1975, defeating Australia in the final.

  11. Which nation has won the most Cricket World Cups (ODI) in men's cricket history?

    Answer: Australia

    Australia has won the most ODI Cricket World Cups with 6 titles (1987, 1999, 2003, 2007, 2015, 2023), followed by the West Indies and India with 2 each.

  12. In cricket, what is the maximum number of overs per side in a One Day International (ODI) match?

    Answer: 50

    A One Day International (ODI) cricket match allows each team a maximum of 50 overs.

  13. What is the maximum number of players a single team can have on the field at once in cricket (including the wicket-keeper)?

    Answer: 11

    A cricket team fields 11 players at a time, including the wicket-keeper.

  14. Which two countries have contested the most Cricket World Cup finals?

    Answer: India vs. Australia

    Australia and India have faced each other in multiple Cricket World Cup finals (1987, 2003, 2023), the most of any pair.

  15. The 'Hundred' is a unique format of cricket featuring how many balls per innings?

    Answer: 100

    In The Hundred, each team faces exactly 100 balls.
