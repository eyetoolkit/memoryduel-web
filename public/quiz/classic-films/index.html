Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In which film does the character say 'Here's looking at you, kid'?

    Answer: Casablanca

    This famous line is spoken by Humphrey Bogart's character Rick Blaine in the 1942 film 'Casablanca'.

  2. In the movie 'Titanic' (1997), what is the name of the main female character played by Kate Winslet?

    Answer: Rose

    Kate Winslet plays Rose DeWitt Bukater in James Cameron's epic romance film Titanic.

  3. In which film does the character 'Jack Dawson' appear?

    Answer: Titanic

    Jack Dawson is the character played by Leonardo DiCaprio in James Cameron's 1997 blockbuster 'Titanic'.

  4. Which actor is famous for playing the Terminator in James Cameron's 1984 film?

    Answer: Arnold Schwarzenegger

    Arnold Schwarzenegger's role as the Terminator became his most iconic performance.

  5. In which year was the original 'Star Wars' film (A New Hope) released?

    Answer: 1977

    'Star Wars' premiered in 1977, directed by George Lucas.

  6. Which 1975 film features the famous line 'You're gonna need a bigger boat'?

    Answer: Jaws

    Jaws, directed by Steven Spielberg, popularized the summer blockbuster release strategy.

  7. Who played the character of Katniss Everdeen in 'The Hunger Games' films?

    Answer: Jennifer Lawrence

    Jennifer Lawrence starred as the protagonist Katniss Everdeen in the four-film series.

  8. Which director is known for films such as Seven Samurai and Rashomon?

    Answer: Akira Kurosawa

    Akira Kurosawa was a legendary Japanese filmmaker whose works heavily influenced global cinema.

  9. Which film series features a character named Harry Potter?

    Answer: The Harry Potter series

    Harry Potter is the titular character of the film series based on J.K. Rowling's books.

  10. The iconic opening scene of the 1968 film '2001: A Space Odyssey' features apes discovering what object?

    Answer: A black monolith

    The mysterious black monolith is a recurring and central symbol throughout Stanley Kubrick's 2001: A Space Odyssey.

  11. Which actress won the Academy Award for Best Actress for her role in the 1977 film Network?

    Answer: Faye Dunaway

    Faye Dunaway won the Best Actress Oscar for her portrayal of television executive Diana Christensen in Network.

  12. Which director is known as the 'Master of Suspense' and directed films like 'Psycho' and 'Rear Window'?

    Answer: Alfred Hitchcock

    Alfred Hitchcock earned the nickname 'Master of Suspense' for his pioneering techniques in thriller and suspense filmmaking.

  13. Which 1975 film, directed by Milos Forman, is based on the novel by Ken Kesey and features Jack Nicholson?

    Answer: One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest

    Milos Forman directed 'One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest', which won all five major Academy Awards.

  14. Which of these films was NOT directed by Steven Spielberg?

    Answer: Blade Runner

    'Blade Runner' (1982) was directed by Ridley Scott, not Steven Spielberg.

  15. Which 1972 film, directed by Francis Ford Coppola, features Marlon Brando as Don Vito Corleone?

    Answer: The Godfather

    'The Godfather' (1972) is widely regarded as one of the greatest films ever made, with Brando's portrayal of Don Corleone becoming iconic.
