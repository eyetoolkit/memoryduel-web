Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which artist painted the famous mural 'The Last Supper'?

    Answer: Leonardo da Vinci

    Leonardo da Vinci painted 'The Last Supper' between 1495 and 1498 on the wall of the refectory in Santa Maria delle Grazie, Milan.

  2. Which famous painting features a woman with an enigmatic smile?

    Answer: Mona Lisa

    The Mona Lisa by Leonardo da Vinci is renowned for the subject's mysterious, enigmatic smile which has fascinated viewers for centuries.

  3. The Renaissance began in which country?

    Answer: Italy

    The Renaissance began in Italy in the 14th century, particularly in city-states like Florence, driven by renewed interest in classical Greek and Roman culture.

  4. Who sculpted the statue of David, displayed in Florence?

    Answer: Michelangelo

    Michelangelo sculpted David between 1501 and 1504 from a single block of marble. The 5.17-meter statue is a masterpiece of Renaissance sculpture.

  5. Which artist is known for painting the Mona Lisa?

    Answer: Leonardo da Vinci

    Leonardo da Vinci painted the Mona Lisa, which is now displayed in the Louvre Museum in Paris.

  6. Which Italian painter and architect designed the dome of Florence Cathedral?

    Answer: Filippo Brunelleschi

    Filippo Brunelleschi designed the iconic dome of the Florence Cathedral (Santa Maria del Fiore), completed in 1436, a masterpiece of Renaissance engineering.

  7. Which artist painted 'The Birth of Venus', depicting the goddess emerging from the sea?

    Answer: Sandro Botticelli

    Sandro Botticelli painted 'The Birth of Venus' around 1485, a masterpiece of the Italian Renaissance.

  8. Who painted the ceiling of the Sistine Chapel, a masterpiece of Renaissance art?

    Answer: Michelangelo

    Michelangelo painted the Sistine Chapel ceiling between 1508 and 1512, depicting scenes from the Book of Genesis including the iconic Creation of Adam.

  9. Which Italian painter is considered a master of the High Renaissance and is famous for frescoes like 'The Last Judgment'?

    Answer: Michelangelo

    Michelangelo Buonarroti was a sculptor, painter, architect, and poet of the High Renaissance who exerted an unparalleled influence on the development of Western art.

  10. Who was the first person to circumnavigate the globe?

    Answer: Ferdinand Magellan

    Ferdinand Magellan began the first circumnavigation of the Earth in 1519, though he died during the voyage; his crew completed it in 1522.

  11. Which Renaissance painter created the fresco "The School of Athens" in the Vatican's Apostolic Palace?

    Answer: Raphael

    Raphael painted The School of Athens, a masterpiece of Renaissance fresco art.

  12. Which Renaissance composer is credited with writing the polyphonic chanson 'Mille regretz'?

    Answer: Josquin des Prez

    Josquin des Prez (c.1450-1521) is generally credited with 'Mille regretz,' a famous four-voice chanson that has been attributed to multiple composers over the centuries.

  13. Which German painter of the 16th century produced the series of woodcuts titled 'The Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse'?

    Answer: Albrecht Dürer

    Albrecht Dürer created the woodcut series 'The Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse' around 1498.

  14. Which Renaissance composer wrote the choral work "Missa Pange Lingua"?

    Answer: Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina

    Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina, an Italian Renaissance composer, is famed for his "Missa Pange Lingua," published in 1575.

  15. Which Renaissance painter is best known for 'The Birth of Venus'?

    Answer: Sandro Botticelli

    Sandro Botticelli painted 'The Birth of Venus' around 1485, depicting the goddess emerging from the sea.
