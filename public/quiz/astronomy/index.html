Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which of these is the largest star in our solar system?

    Answer: The Sun

    The Sun is a massive star at the center of our solar system, containing 99.8% of its total mass.

  2. What planet is known as the Red Planet?

    Answer: Mars

    Mars is known as the Red Planet due to iron oxide (rust) prevalent on its surface, giving it a reddish appearance.

  3. Which planet is known as the 'Morning Star'?

    Answer: Venus

    Venus shines brightly in the morning and evening sky, earning the nickname.

  4. What is the closest star to Earth?

    Answer: The Sun

    The Sun, the star at the centre of our Solar System, is the nearest star to Earth.

  5. Which planet has the most moons confirmed by recent observations?

    Answer: Saturn

    Saturn overtook Jupiter in 2023 with over 140 confirmed moons, making it the planet with the most known moons.

  6. What is the name of the boundary around a black hole where nothing, not even light, can escape?

    Answer: Event horizon

    The event horizon is the point of no return; once crossed, escape is impossible.

  7. How long does light from the Sun take to reach Earth?

    Answer: About 8 minutes

    Sunlight travels approximately 150 million kilometers to reach Earth, taking about 8 minutes and 20 seconds.

  8. What is the primary element found in stars?

    Answer: Hydrogen

    Hydrogen makes up about 73% of the mass of the universe and is the primary fuel for stars.

  9. Which planet currently has the greatest number of known natural moons?

    Answer: Saturn

    As of recent observations, Saturn has 83 confirmed moons, surpassing Jupiter’s 80.

  10. What is the approximate age of the universe in billions of years?

    Answer: 13.8

    Current estimates place the age of the universe at about 13.8 billion years.

  11. Which element has the atomic number 1 and is the most abundant element in the universe?

    Answer: Hydrogen

    Hydrogen, with one proton, makes up roughly 75% of the mass of ordinary matter in the universe and fuels stars through fusion.

  12. Which phenomenon is responsible for the Earth's seasons?

    Answer: The axial tilt of the Earth

    Earth's axial tilt of approximately 23.5 degrees causes different parts of the planet to receive varying amounts of sunlight throughout the year.

  13. Approximately how old is the Universe according to the current best‑fit Lambda‑CDM model?

    Answer: 13.8 billion years

    Observations of cosmic microwave background and expansion rate indicate the Universe is about 13.8 billion years old.

  14. What is the specific name for the supermassive black hole at the center of the Milky Way?

    Answer: Sagittarius A*

    Sagittarius A* (Sgr A*) is the compact radio source at the Galactic Center of the Milky Way.

  15. Which of the following is the nearest known exoplanet that orbits within its star's habitable zone?

    Answer: Proxima Centauri b

    Proxima Centauri b, about 4.2 light‑years away, lies in the habitable zone of its red dwarf star.
