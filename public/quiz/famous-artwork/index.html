Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Who painted the famous Mona Lisa?

    Answer: Leonardo da Vinci

    Leonardo da Vinci painted the Mona Lisa, which is widely considered one of the most famous portraits in the world and hangs in the Louvre in Paris.

  2. Who painted 'The Starry Night'?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    The Starry Night is an oil-on-canvas painting by Dutch Post-Impressionist artist Vincent van Gogh, painted in June 1889 and depicting the view from his asylum room.

  3. Which famous painting depicts a group of people having dinner and is attributed to Leonardo da Vinci?

    Answer: The Last Supper

    Leonardo da Vinci painted 'The Last Supper' depicting Jesus and his disciples, on a wall of the Convent of Santa Maria delle Grazie in Milan.

  4. Which animal is most famously associated with Salvador Dalí's surrealist paintings?

    Answer: Elephants

    Dalí is well known for painting elephants with long, spindly legs, particularly in works like 'The Temptation of St. Anthony'.

  5. Who painted 'The Persistence of Memory', featuring melting clocks?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí painted 'The Persistence of Memory' in 1931, and it is one of the most recognizable surrealist works.

  6. Which artist created the famous sculpture 'The Thinker'?

    Answer: Auguste Rodin

    'The Thinker' is a bronze sculpture by French artist Auguste Rodin, created in 1904, originally conceived as part of 'The Gates of Hell'.

  7. Who painted 'Guernica'?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in 1937 in response to the bombing of the Basque town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War.

  8. Which artist is famous for painting a series of water lilies?

    Answer: Claude Monet

    Claude Monet painted approximately 250 water lily works in his garden at Giverny, spanning the last 30 years of his life.

  9. Who sculpted the statue of 'David' that stands in Florence?

    Answer: Michelangelo

    The statue of David was sculpted by Michelangelo between 1501 and 1504 and is considered a masterpiece of Renaissance sculpture.

  10. Which painting features a swirl-like sky and was created by Vincent van Gogh?

    Answer: The Starry Night

    'The Starry Night' (1889) was painted by Vincent van Gogh from memory while in an asylum. It is one of the most recognized paintings in Western art.

  11. Which painter of 'The Garden of Earthly Delights' was a master of the Northern Renaissance?

    Answer: Hieronymus Bosch

    Hieronymus Bosch painted this surreal triptych around 1500; its fantastical imagery continues to fascinate scholars and viewers today.

  12. Which painter, a key figure of the Dutch Golden Age, is known for 'The Night Watch' and numerous self-portraits?

    Answer: Rembrandt van Rijn

    Rembrandt van Rijn painted The Night Watch in 1642. He produced nearly 100 self-portraits throughout his life, offering a remarkable record of his own aging and changing fortunes.

  13. Which artist is credited with developing the technique of chiaroscuro, the treatment of light and shade in drawing and painting, and painted 'The Calling of Saint Matthew'?

    Answer: Caravaggio

    Caravaggio (1571–1610) was a master of chiaroscuro, using dramatic contrasts of light and dark. 'The Calling of Saint Matthew' (c. 1599–1600) is one of his most celebrated works, exemplifying this technique.

  14. Which artist painted 'Guernica', a powerful anti-war masterpiece created after the bombing of a Basque town?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso's 'Guernica' (1937) is a large oil painting on canvas, depicting the suffering of people and animals during the bombing of Guernica, Spain, during the Spanish Civil War.

  15. Which artist painted the fresco cycle known as The School of Athens?

    Answer: Raphael

    Raphael painted The School of Athens between 1509 and 1511 in the Apostolic Palace in Vatican City. It depicts great philosophers of antiquity.
