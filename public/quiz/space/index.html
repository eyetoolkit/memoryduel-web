Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. How many days does it take for the Earth to orbit the Sun once?

    Answer: 365.25 days

    Earth takes approximately 365.25 days to complete one orbit around the Sun, which is why we add a leap day every four years.

  2. What is the most abundant element in the universe?

    Answer: Hydrogen

    Hydrogen makes up roughly 75% of the mass of ordinary matter in the universe.

  3. What is the name of the nearest star to Earth?

    Answer: Sun

    The Sun is the star closest to Earth, located about 93 million miles away.

  4. What planet is known as the Red Planet?

    Answer: Mars

    Mars is called the Red Planet due to iron oxide (rust) on its surface.

  5. Which country was the first to send a human into space?

    Answer: Soviet Union

    Yuri Gagarin of the Soviet Union became the first human in space in 1961.

  6. In which year did the first manned Moon landing take place?

    Answer: 1969

    Apollo 11 landed on the Moon on July 20, 1969, with Neil Armstrong becoming the first person to walk on the lunar surface.

  7. Who was the first person to set foot on the Moon?

    Answer: Neil Armstrong

    Neil Armstrong became the first person to walk on the Moon on July 20, 1969, as part of NASA's Apollo 11 mission.

  8. Which planet is closest to the Sun?

    Answer: Mercury

    Mercury is the first planet from the Sun, with an average distance of about 58 million kilometers.

  9. How many rings does Saturn have?

    Answer: 9

    Saturn has 9 main rings (though many sub-rings exist within them), made mostly of ice and rock particles.

  10. Which is the highest mountain in the solar system?

    Answer: Olympus Mons

    Olympus Mons on Mars rises about 21 km, the tallest known mountain.

  11. Which planet is known for its Great Red Spot?

    Answer: Jupiter

    The Great Red Spot is a persistent high-pressure region in Jupiter's atmosphere, producing a storm larger than Earth that has been observed for centuries.

  12. Which planet has the most moons confirmed as of recent counts?

    Answer: Saturn

    Saturn has the most confirmed moons with over 140, surpassing Jupiter after recent discoveries.

  13. What was the first video game to be played in outer space aboard the Mir space station in 1993?

    Answer: Game Boy Tetris

    Game Boy Tetris was played by Russian cosmonaut Aleksandr A. Serebrov aboard the Mir space station in 1993, making it the first video game played in outer space. The Game Boy was chosen for its portability and long battery life.

  14. What is the fastest speed ever recorded for a human-made object (a Parker Solar Probe flyby) in km/h?

    Answer: About 690,000 km/h

    The Parker Solar Probe reached roughly 690,000 km/h (about 430,000 mph) during a close solar approach, the fastest any human-made object has traveled.

  15. Who holds the record for the longest uninterrupted space flight by a human?

    Answer: Valeri Polyakov

    Valeri Polyakov spent 437 days and 18 hours aboard the Mir space station from 1994-1995.
