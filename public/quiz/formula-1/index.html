Answer key

All thirteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In Formula 1 racing, what does the 'chequered flag' signify?

    Answer: The race is finished

    The chequered flag is waved to signal the end of a race or session.

  2. In Formula 1, what color represents the pole position on the starting grid?

    Answer: Red

    Red is traditionally used to denote the pole position on F1 starting grids, a convention that continues in modern graphics.

  3. In Formula 1 racing, what does the acronym 'DRS' stand for?

    Answer: Drag Reduction System

    DRS stands for Drag Reduction System, a movable rear wing flap that drivers can activate in designated zones to aid overtaking.

  4. In Formula 1 racing, which color is traditionally associated with Italian teams like Ferrari?

    Answer: Red

    Italian racing teams traditionally use red (rosso corsa) as their national racing color.

  5. In Formula 1 racing, which country has produced the most World Drivers' Champions?

    Answer: United Kingdom

    The United Kingdom has produced more F1 World Drivers' Champions than any other nation, including legends like Lewis Hamilton, Jackie Stewart, and Sebastian Vettel (who raced under a British license early in his career contextually, though the UK leads overall).

  6. In Formula 1, which driver holds the record for the most World Championships?

    Answer: Lewis Hamilton

    Lewis Hamilton and Michael Schumacher both hold the record with 7 World Championships each, but Hamilton has more race wins.

  7. In Formula 1 racing, what is the name of the trophy awarded to the winner of the Monaco Grand Prix?

    Answer: Coupe du Prince Rainier III

    The winner of the Monaco Grand Prix receives the 'Coupe du Prince Rainier III', named after the former Prince of Monaco.

  8. In Formula 1 racing, which country's Grand Prix is known for being held on the streets of Monte Carlo?

    Answer: Monaco

    The Monaco Grand Prix is held on the Circuit de Monaco, a street circuit winding through Monte Carlo, Monaco.

  9. In Formula 1, which country's flag features a green cross on a white background and is home to the Monaco Grand Prix?

    Answer: Monaco

    Monaco's flag is a red and white bicolor; however, the question is about the host country of the Monaco Grand Prix, which is the Principality of Monaco itself. Note: Monaco's flag is actually red over white, so the description is a slight misdirection—the correct answer is Monaco as the host nation.

  10. In Formula 1 racing, which country does the famous Monaco Grand Prix take place in?

    Answer: Monaco

    The Monaco Grand Prix is held on the Circuit de Monaco, a street circuit in the Principality of Monaco.

  11. In Formula One racing, what does the 'pole position' refer to?

    Answer: The first position on the starting grid

    Pole position is the first position on the starting grid, awarded to the driver who records the fastest qualifying time.

  12. In Formula 1 racing, which constructor has won the most World Constructors' Championships?

    Answer: Ferrari

    Scuderia Ferrari is the most successful team in Formula 1 history, having won 16 World Constructors' Championships.

  13. In Formula 1, which driver holds the record for the most career race wins as of 2024?

    Answer: Lewis Hamilton

    Lewis Hamilton holds the record for the most Formula 1 Grand Prix victories, surpassing Michael Schumacher's previous record of 91 wins.
