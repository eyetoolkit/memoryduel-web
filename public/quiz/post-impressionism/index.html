Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which painter is famous for the sunflower series?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh painted several still lifes of sunflowers in Arles, France, in 1888 and 1889.

  2. Which famous painting features a starry night sky over a village, painted by Vincent van Gogh?

    Answer: Starry Night

    'Starry Night' was painted by Vincent van Gogh in 1889. It depicts the view from his asylum room window and is one of the most recognized works in Western art.

  3. What is the name of the artist famous for painting swirling starry skies?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh painted 'The Starry Night' in 1889, characterized by its swirling sky.

  4. Which famous painter is known for the quote 'I am always doing that which I cannot do, in order that I may learn how to do it' and has a distinctive cut-off ear?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh famously cut off part of his own ear in 1888 after an argument with Paul Gauguin.

  5. Which artist is known for painting 'The Starry Night' and struggled with mental illness?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh painted 'The Starry Night' in 1889 and is a renowned post-impressionist artist who experienced significant mental health challenges, which influenced his work and life.

  6. Which Dutch painter created 'The Starry Night'?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Van Gogh painted 'The Starry Night' in June 1889 while staying at the Saint-Paul-de-Mausole asylum in France.

  7. Which painting is known for its swirling, dreamlike night sky and cypress tree?

    Answer: The Starry Night

    Van Gogh's The Starry Night features a dramatic swirling sky over a village, painted in 1889.

  8. Which painting depicts a village with a church steeple and swirling sky, created shortly before the artist's death?

    Answer: Starry Night

    The Starry Night was painted by Vincent van Gogh in 1889 while he was in an asylum, a year before his death.

  9. Which painter is known for the theory of painting with dots, a style called Pointillism?

    Answer: Georges Seurat

    Georges Seurat developed Pointillism in the late 1880s, using small dots of color that blend in the viewer's eye. His most famous work is A Sunday Afternoon on the Island of La Grande Jatte.

  10. Which famous painting features a couple sitting at a café in Arles, France?

    Answer: Café Terrace at Night

    Vincent van Gogh painted 'Café Terrace at Night' in 1888, depicting the famous yellow café in Place du Forum in Arles.

  11. Which artist is known for using bold colors and thick brushstrokes in works like 'The Bedroom' and 'Sunflowers'?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh is celebrated for his expressive brushwork and vivid palette. His works like Sunflowers and The Bedroom are among the most recognized in the world.

  12. Which painter is known for the distinctive swirling style seen in his series of self-portraits and landscapes?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh's expressive brushwork and swirling style are hallmarks of his late works, including his many self-portraits.

  13. Which artist painted 'The Starry Night' while staying at an asylum?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    The 1889 painting 'The Starry Night' was created by Vincent van Gogh while he was a patient at the Saint-Paul-de-Mausole asylum in France.

  14. Which Dutch painter cut off part of his own ear after a dispute with fellow artist Paul Gauguin?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh famously severed part of his left ear in Arles, France in December 1888.

  15. Which artist's self-portraits, characterized by intense color and emotional brushwork, are among the most recognized in Western art?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Van Gogh painted over 30 self-portraits, using bold colors and expressive brushwork to convey emotion.
