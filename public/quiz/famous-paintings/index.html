Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which famous painting depicts a woman wearing a pearl earring?

    Answer: The Girl with a Pearl Earring

    'Girl with a Pearl Earring' was painted by Dutch artist Johannes Vermeer around 1665. It is often referred to as the 'Mona Lisa of the North'.

  2. Which famous painting features a swirl of blue and yellow stars and was painted by Vincent van Gogh?

    Answer: The Starry Night

    'The Starry Night' is one of the most recognized works in Western art. Van Gogh painted it in 1889 while staying in an asylum in France.

  3. Which painting by Edvard Munch features a screaming figure and is one of the most recognizable images in art?

    Answer: The Scream

    The Scream by Edvard Munch, created in 1893, depicts an agonized figure against a blood-red sky. It has become an iconic symbol of human anxiety and existential dread.

  4. Who painted the famous artwork 'Starry Night'?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    'The Starry Night' was painted by Dutch Post-Impressionist painter Vincent van Gogh in 1889.

  5. Which famous painting depicts a group of people having dinner, but is NOT 'The Last Supper'?

    Answer: American Gothic

    Grant Wood's 'American Gothic' depicts a farmer and his daughter standing in front of a house, holding a pitchfork.

  6. Which painter is known for works such as 'The Persistence of Memory' featuring melting clocks?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí was a Spanish surrealist artist famous for 'The Persistence of Memory' (1931), which features melting pocket watches.

  7. Which artist is famous for his series of sunflower paintings?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh painted several still lifes of sunflowers in 1888 and 1889 while living in Arles, France. The series is among his most well-known works.

  8. Which artist painted the famous mural 'Guernica'?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    'Guernica' was painted by Pablo Picasso in 1937, depicting the bombing of the Basque town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War.

  9. Which artist painted the ceiling of the Sistine Chapel?

    Answer: Michelangelo

    Michelangelo painted the Sistine Chapel ceiling between 1508 and 1512, creating one of the most iconic works of art in history.

  10. Which painter is famous for his 'Water Lilies' series?

    Answer: Claude Monet

    Claude Monet painted over 250 'Water Lilies' works between 1897 and 1926, inspired by his garden at Giverny, France.

  11. Who painted 'The School of Athens'?

    Answer: Raphael

    The School of Athens is a fresco painted by the Italian Renaissance artist Raphael.

  12. Which artist painted Les Demoiselles d'Avignon, a groundbreaking work of Cubism?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted Les Demoiselles d'Avignon in 1907. Its radical treatment of form and perspective is widely considered a foundational work of Cubism and modern art.

  13. Who painted 'The Garden of Earthly Delights', a famous triptych of the Northern Renaissance?

    Answer: Hieronymus Bosch

    Hieronymus Bosch painted this elaborate triptych between 1490 and 1510. It depicts scenes from the Garden of Eden, a fantastical central panel, and the landscape of Hell.

  14. Which painter created the controversial 1937 work 'Guernica'?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in response to the bombing of the Basque town during the Spanish Civil War, making it an anti-war icon.

  15. Which artist created the 'Campbell's Soup Cans' artwork?

    Answer: Andy Warhol

    Andy Warhol, a leading figure in Pop Art, created the iconic 'Campbell's Soup Cans' in 1962.
