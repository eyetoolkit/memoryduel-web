Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. How many players are on a soccer (football) team on the field at one time?

    Answer: 11

    A soccer team has 11 players on the field, including the goalkeeper.

  2. In which sport would you score a goal?

    Answer: Soccer

    In soccer, a goal is scored when the ball completely crosses the goal line.

  3. What color is traditionally associated with the 'red card' in soccer?

    Answer: It sends a player off the field

    A red card in soccer means the player is sent off and cannot be replaced, leaving their team with fewer players.

  4. Which club won the 2020 UEFA Champions League?

    Answer: Bayern Munich

    Bayern Munich defeated Paris Saint‑Germain 1‑0 in the 2020 final to claim the title.

  5. Which league is considered the top professional football (soccer) league in the world by many rankings?

    Answer: English Premier League

    The English Premier League is widely regarded as the most competitive and financially powerful football league globally.

  6. Which sport is known as the 'king of sports' in many countries due to its global popularity?

    Answer: Football (Soccer)

    Football (soccer) is widely regarded as the world's most popular sport, played and followed by billions globally.

  7. In which sport would you perform a 'bicycle kick'?

    Answer: Soccer (Football)

    A bicycle kick (or overhead kick) is a striking technique in soccer where the player kicks the ball while airborne and upside down.

  8. What is the maximum number of substitutions allowed in a standard FIFA World Cup match?

    Answer: 5

    FIFA allows up to 5 substitutions per team in a match (though only 3 stoppages are permitted to make them), a rule temporarily expanded from 3 during the COVID-19 pandemic and now permanent for competitions that choose to adopt it.

  9. Which major international sport is often referred to as 'the beautiful game'?

    Answer: Soccer (Football)

    The phrase 'the beautiful game' is widely used to describe soccer (football) due to its aesthetic appeal.

  10. Which major international team sport is played with a ball that is inflated and roughly the size of a human head?

    Answer: Soccer

    A standard soccer ball (size 5) has a circumference of 68–70 cm, roughly the size of a human head.

  11. Which country has won the most FIFA Men's World Cup titles as of 2024?

    Answer: Brazil

    Brazil has won the FIFA Men's World Cup 5 times (1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002).

  12. In the FIFA World Cup, which player has made the most appearances in the tournament's history?

    Answer: Lothar Matthäus

    German defender Lothar Matthäus has played in 25 World Cup matches, the most of any player.

  13. Which two countries have met the most times in FIFA World Cup finals?

    Answer: Argentina vs. Germany

    Argentina and Germany (including West Germany) have met three times in World Cup finals (1986, 1990, 2014).

  14. Which sport's highest governing body is the Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA)?

    Answer: Association Football (Soccer)

    FIFA is the international governing body for association football, futsal, and beach soccer.

  15. Which country's national team is nicknamed 'Les Bleus'?

    Answer: France

    The French national football team is known as 'Les Bleus' (The Blues), a reference to the blue jerseys they traditionally wear.
