Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What organ pumps blood throughout the human body?

    Answer: Heart

    The heart is a muscular organ that pumps blood through the circulatory system, supplying oxygen and nutrients to tissues.

  2. Which part of the human body contains the strongest muscle relative to its size?

    Answer: Masseter (jaw)

    The masseter muscle can exert a force of up to 72 kg (160 lbs) on a single molar.

  3. How many legs does an insect typically have?

    Answer: 6

    All insects have six legs (three pairs), which distinguishes them from arachnids like spiders that have eight legs.

  4. How many chambers does a typical human heart have?

    Answer: Four

    The human heart has four chambers: two atria and two ventricles.

  5. What is the hard outer covering of a turtle called?

    Answer: Carapace

    The upper part of a turtle's shell is specifically called the carapace, while the bottom is called the plastron.

  6. What is the main function of a plant's root system?

    Answer: To absorb water and nutrients from the soil

    The primary function of roots is to anchor the plant and absorb water and essential minerals from the soil.

  7. How many bones are in an adult human body?

    Answer: 206

    An adult human body has 206 bones. Babies are born with about 270 bones, many of which fuse together as they grow.

  8. Which part of the human brain is primarily responsible for coordinating balance and movement?

    Answer: Cerebellum

    The cerebellum plays a major role in motor control, coordination, and maintaining balance.

  9. What is the primary function of hemoglobin in the human body?

    Answer: To carry oxygen in the blood

    Hemoglobin is a protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen from the lungs to the body's tissues and returns carbon dioxide from the tissues back to the lungs.

  10. Which part of the plant is primarily responsible for absorbing water and nutrients from the soil?

    Answer: Root

    Roots anchor the plant and absorb water and dissolved minerals from the soil.

  11. Which part of the human body has the highest density of sweat glands?

    Answer: Palms of the hands

    The palms and soles contain the greatest number of eccrine sweat glands per square centimetre.

  12. Which human organ has the highest number of neurons?

    Answer: Cerebrum

    The cerebrum, the largest part of the brain, contains the highest number of neurons, estimated at around 16 billion.

  13. Which human sense relies on the cochlea in the inner ear?

    Answer: Hearing

    The cochlea converts sound vibrations into electrical signals that the brain interprets as hearing.

  14. Which specialized cells in plants are responsible for the exchange of gases, primarily opening and closing to control transpiration?

    Answer: Stomata

    Stomata are tiny pores usually found on the underside of leaves, flanked by guard cells that regulate their opening and closing.

  15. Which human organ contains the islets of Langerhans?

    Answer: Pancreas

    The islets of Langerhans are clusters of cells within the pancreas that produce insulin and glucagon.
