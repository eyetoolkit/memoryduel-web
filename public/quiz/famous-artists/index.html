Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. The 'Four Seasons' is a famous set of violin concertos composed by which Italian musician?

    Answer: Antonio Vivaldi

    Antonio Vivaldi composed 'The Four Seasons' around 1720. It remains one of the most popular and frequently performed works in the classical repertoire.

  2. What instrument is mainly associated with jazz legend Louis Armstrong?

    Answer: Trumpet

    Louis Armstrong was a world-renowned trumpeter and vocalist, a key figure in the development of jazz music.

  3. Which famous pop artist released the album 'Thriller' in 1982?

    Answer: Michael Jackson

    'Thriller' is the sixth studio album by American singer Michael Jackson and is the best-selling album of all time.

  4. Which instrument is primarily associated with virtuoso Paganini?

    Answer: Violin

    Niccolò Paganini was a legendary Italian violinist, composer, and virtuoso.

  5. Who is often referred to as the 'King of Pop'?

    Answer: Michael Jackson

    Michael Jackson earned the title 'King of Pop' through his unparalleled commercial success, groundbreaking music videos, and influence on modern pop music.

  6. Which singer released the album '1989', which marked her full transition to pop music?

    Answer: Taylor Swift

    Taylor Swift's fifth studio album '1989' (2014) represented her shift from country to mainstream pop music and was a massive commercial success.

  7. Which Dutch Post-Impressionist painter is known for works such as 'Starry Night' and 'Sunflowers'?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh was a Dutch Post-Impressionist painter. 'Starry Night' was painted in 1889, and he created multiple versions of his 'Sunflowers' series.

  8. Which composer continued to write music after becoming almost completely deaf?

    Answer: Ludwig van Beethoven

    Ludwig van Beethoven composed some of his greatest works, including his 9th Symphony, after losing most of his hearing.

  9. Which artist painted 'Guernica', a powerful anti-war painting?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in 1937 in response to the bombing of the Basque town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War. It is considered one of the most moving and powerful anti-war paintings.

  10. Who painted the famous artwork 'The Starry Night'?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh painted 'The Starry Night' in June 1889 while staying at the Saint-Paul-de-Mausole asylum in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, France. The painting is now housed in the Museum of Modern Art in New York City.

  11. Which jazz musician, known as 'Bird', is considered one of the most influential saxophonists in history and pioneered bebop?

    Answer: Charlie Parker

    Charlie Parker, nicknamed 'Bird', was a central figure in the development of bebop jazz in the 1940s and remains one of the most influential musicians in jazz history.

  12. Which Mexican artist is known for her self-portraits and surrealistic paintings, often featuring themes of pain and identity?

    Answer: Frida Kahlo

    Frida Kahlo was a Mexican painter known for her deeply personal and symbolic self-portraits. Her work explores themes of identity, the human body, and death, often reflecting her life experiences and physical suffering.

  13. Which artist wrote and composed the musical 'Phantom of the Opera'?

    Answer: Andrew Lloyd Webber

    Andrew Lloyd Webber composed the music for the musical 'The Phantom of the Opera'.

  14. Who painted 'Guernica', depicting the bombing of a Basque town during the Spanish Civil War?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted Guernica in 1937 in response to the bombing of the Basque town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War. It is considered one of the most moving anti-war paintings in history.

  15. Which of these songs was NOT written by Bob Dylan?

    Answer: Hallelujah

    'Hallelujah' was written by Leonard Cohen and released in 1984. The other three songs were written by Bob Dylan.
