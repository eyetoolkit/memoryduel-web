Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which film won the Academy Award for Best Picture in 1994 (1995 ceremony)?

    Answer: Forrest Gump

    'Forrest Gump' won Best Picture, Best Director (Robert Zemeckis), and Best Actor for Tom Hanks.

  2. Who directed the 1994 film 'Forrest Gump'?

    Answer: Robert Zemeckis

    Robert Zemeckis directed Forrest Gump, starring Tom Hanks, which won six Academy Awards including Best Picture.

  3. Who directed the 1997 film 'Titanic'?

    Answer: James Cameron

    James Cameron directed Titanic, which became the first film to gross over $1 billion at the box office and won 11 Academy Awards.

  4. Which actress won the Academy Award for Best Actress for her role in 'The Silence of the Lambs' (1991)?

    Answer: Jodie Foster

    Jodie Foster won Best Actress for playing Clarice Starling opposite Anthony Hopkins as Hannibal Lecter.

  5. Which South Korean film won the Palme d'Or at the 2019 Cannes Film Festival and later made history at the Academy Awards?

    Answer: Parasite

    Parasite (2019), directed by Bong Joon-ho, won both the Palme d'Or and the Academy Award for Best Picture, becoming the first non-English language film to win the top Oscar. It also won Best Director, Best Original Screenplay, and Best International Feature Film.

  6. Who directed the 1993 film Schindler's List?

    Answer: Steven Spielberg

    Steven Spielberg directed Schindler's List, which won seven Academy Awards including Best Picture.

  7. Which actor played the title character in the movie "Forrest Gump"?

    Answer: Tom Hanks

    Tom Hanks won the Academy Award for Best Actor for his portrayal of Forrest Gump in 1994.

  8. Which Japanese animated film won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature in 2003?

    Answer: Spirited Away

    Spirited Away, directed by Hayao Miyazaki, was the first hand-drawn and Japanese-language film to win the award.

  9. Which South Korean film made history by winning the Academy Award for Best Picture in 2020?

    Answer: Parasite

    Parasite (2019), directed by Bong Joon-ho, became the first non-English language film to win Best Picture at the Academy Awards in 2020.

  10. Which actor has won the most Academy Awards for Best Actor all-time, tied at three wins with Daniel Day-Lewis?

    Answer: Spencer Tracy

    Spencer Tracy won back-to-back Best Actor Oscars in 1937 and 1938, plus a third in 1967, tying Daniel Day-Lewis's record of three.

  11. Which film was the first non-English language movie to win the Best Picture Oscar?

    Answer: Parasite

    Parasite (South Korea, 2019) was the first non-English film to win Best Picture.

  12. Which actor won the Academy Award for Best Actor in 2020 for his role in 'Joker' (2019)?

    Answer: Joaquin Phoenix

    Joaquin Phoenix won the Best Actor Oscar at the 92nd Academy Awards for portraying Arthur Fleck in Joker.

  13. Which film, directed by Bong Joon-ho, won the Academy Award for Best Picture in 2020, becoming the first non-English language film to do so?

    Answer: Parasite

    Bong Joon-ho's Parasite (2019) made history as the first non-English language film to win Best Picture at the Oscars.

  14. Which actor won the Academy Award for Best Actor for the 2016 film LION?

    Answer: Dev Patel

    Dev Patel was nominated for Best Supporting Actor for LION; Casey Affleck won Best Actor that year for Manchester by the Sea. The question tests recognition of Dev Patel's acclaimed performance in LION.

  15. Which film won the first-ever Academy Award for Best Picture, awarded in 1929?

    Answer: Wings

    Wings, a silent WWI aviation film, won Best Picture at the 1st Academy Awards ceremony. Sunrise won the separate Unique and Artistic Production award that year.
