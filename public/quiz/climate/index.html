Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which of these is a greenhouse gas that warms Earth's atmosphere?

    Answer: Carbon dioxide

    Carbon dioxide is one of the main greenhouse gases, trapping heat and contributing to global warming.

  2. Which biome is characterized by extremely cold temperatures and low precipitation?

    Answer: Tundra

    The tundra biome experiences perennially cold temperatures and receives little precipitation, creating a frozen, treeless landscape.

  3. What is the process called when water changes from a liquid to a gas?

    Answer: Evaporation

    Evaporation is the phase change from liquid to gas, driven by heat energy.

  4. Which of the following gases is most closely associated with the greenhouse effect and global warming?

    Answer: Carbon dioxide

    Carbon dioxide (CO2) is the primary greenhouse gas emitted through human activities like burning fossil fuels. While other gases contribute, CO2 is the main driver of global warming.

  5. Which of the following is a primary greenhouse gas contributing to climate change?

    Answer: Methane

    Methane is a potent greenhouse gas emitted from agriculture, landfills, and fossil fuels.

  6. What phenomenon describes the warming of ocean surface waters in the central and eastern tropical Pacific?

    Answer: El Niño

    El Niño is a climate pattern that refers to the periodic warming of sea surface temperatures in the central and eastern tropical Pacific Ocean.

  7. What is the primary cause of coral bleaching in oceans?

    Answer: Increased water temperature

    Rising ocean temperatures cause corals to expel the symbiotic algae living in their tissues, turning them white.

  8. What is the main way that deforestation contributes to global warming?

    Answer: By releasing stored carbon dioxide

    Trees absorb carbon dioxide as they grow; when forests are burned or cleared, this stored carbon is released back into the atmosphere, increasing greenhouse gas levels.

  9. Which type of forest is often referred to as the 'lungs of the Earth'?

    Answer: Tropical rainforest

    Tropical rainforests produce about 20% of the world's oxygen and absorb large amounts of carbon dioxide, earning them the nickname 'lungs of the Earth.'

  10. What is the main greenhouse gas released by human activities such as burning fossil fuels?

    Answer: Carbon dioxide

    CO2 from fossil fuel combustion is the largest contributor to human-driven greenhouse gas emissions.

  11. What is the term used to describe the gradual increase in the average temperature of the Earth's atmosphere and oceans?

    Answer: Global warming

    Global warming refers to the long-term heating of Earth's climate system observed since the pre-industrial period, primarily due to human activities like burning fossil fuels.

  12. What is the main driving force behind the global thermohaline circulation (ocean conveyor belt)?

    Answer: Differences in water temperature and salinity

    Thermohaline circulation is driven by density differences caused by variations in water temperature (thermo) and salinity (haline).

  13. What is the 'albedo effect' in climate science?

    Answer: The reflection of solar radiation by Earth's surface

    Albedo is a measure of reflectivity. Surfaces with high albedo (like ice) reflect more sunlight and cool the planet, while dark surfaces absorb more heat.

  14. Which process is the largest source of atmospheric methane derived from human activities?

    Answer: Enteric fermentation in ruminants

    Enteric fermentation in ruminant animals (e.g., cattle, sheep) is the largest human‑related source of methane emissions.

  15. Which term describes the gradual increase in the acidity of ocean water due to increased atmospheric CO2?

    Answer: Ocean Acidification

    Ocean acidification occurs when CO2 dissolves in seawater, forming carbonic acid and lowering pH, threatening shell-forming organisms.
