Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. On which continent is the country of Egypt located?

    Answer: Africa

    Egypt is located in northeastern Africa, though the Sinai Peninsula extends into southwestern Asia, making it a transcontinental country.

  2. Which country is home to the Great Barrier Reef?

    Answer: Australia

    The Great Barrier Reef lies off the northeast coast of Queensland, Australia.

  3. Which mountain range separates Europe from Asia at their conventional boundary?

    Answer: Urals

    The Ural Mountains in Russia are conventionally considered the boundary between Europe and Asia.

  4. Australia is both a country and a ____?

    Answer: Continent

    Australia is unique as it is both a sovereign country and a continent.

  5. In which country would you find Mount Fuji?

    Answer: Japan

    Mount Fuji is Japan's highest peak at 3,776 meters and is an iconic symbol of the country, located on Honshu Island.

  6. Which river flows through the city of Cairo?

    Answer: Nile

    The Nile River flows through Cairo, Egypt's capital. It is the longest river in Africa and one of the longest in the world, flowing northward to the Mediterranean Sea.

  7. Which country has the largest land area?

    Answer: Russia

    Russia covers over 17 million square kilometres, the largest of any country.

  8. Which South American country has the longest coastline?

    Answer: Chile

    Chile stretches over 4,300 km along the Pacific, giving it the longest coastline in South America.

  9. Which country has the city of Marrakech as a major destination?

    Answer: Morocco

    Marrakech is a major city in Morocco, known for its medina, markets, and as a gateway to the Atlas Mountains.

  10. Which of these countries is landlocked (has no coastline)?

    Answer: Czech Republic

    The Czech Republic (Czechia) is a landlocked country in Central Europe. Portugal, Greece, and Norway all have significant coastlines on the Atlantic Ocean or Mediterranean Sea.

  11. Which country contains the world's highest navigable lake?

    Answer: Bolivia

    Lake Titicaca, at approximately 3,812 meters above sea level, sits on the border of Bolivia and Peru and is the highest navigable lake in the world.

  12. Which African capital city was formerly known as Leopoldville?

    Answer: Kinshasa

    Kinshasa, capital of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, was renamed from Leopoldville in 1966.

  13. Which African country is the source of the Nile River?

    Answer: Tanzania

    The source of the Nile is generally considered to be Lake Victoria in Tanzania (though the lake is shared with Uganda and Kenya), specifically at Jinja, Uganda where the White Nile begins.

  14. Which country currently has the greatest number of time zones?

    Answer: Russia

    Russia spans eleven standard time zones, the most of any single country.

  15. Which is the world's longest river that flows entirely within a single country?

    Answer: Volga (Russia)

    The Volga River at approximately 3,530 km is the longest river in Europe and the longest river flowing entirely within one country — Russia.
