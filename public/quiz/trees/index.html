Answer key

All eleven questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the largest living organism on Earth by mass?

    Answer: Giant sequoia tree

    Giant sequoia trees (Sequoiadendron giganteum) are the heaviest living organisms; the blue whale is the largest animal.

  2. What is the tallest type of tree in the world?

    Answer: Coast Redwood

    Coast Redwoods (Sequoia sempervirens) are the tallest trees, with the tallest known living specimen exceeding 115 meters.

  3. What is the outermost layer of a tree trunk called?

    Answer: Bark

    Bark is the protective outermost layer of a tree trunk, shielding it from physical damage, insects, and disease.

  4. What is the largest type of tree on Earth?

    Answer: Sequoia

    Giant sequoias are the largest trees by volume, growing up to 85 meters (280 feet) tall.

  5. Which of these trees is a type of gymnosperm?

    Answer: Pine

    Gymnosperms are plants that produce 'naked seeds,' often in cones. Pines, spruces, and firs are common examples. Oak, maple, and birch are angiosperms (flowering plants).

  6. Which of these trees is known for losing its leaves in autumn?

    Answer: Oak

    Oak trees are deciduous, meaning they shed their leaves annually in the fall. Pine, cedar, and spruce are evergreen trees that retain their needles year-round.

  7. Which of these trees is known for its distinctive peeling bark that reveals a white trunk?

    Answer: Birch

    Birch trees, particularly the Paper Birch (Betula papyrifera), are famous for their white, peeling bark.

  8. Which of these trees produces acorns?

    Answer: Oak

    Oak trees produce acorns, which are an important food source for many animals.

  9. Which of these trees is known for its ability to store massive amounts of carbon and can live for thousands of years?

    Answer: Giant Sequoia

    Giant Sequoias are among the largest and oldest living organisms, capable of storing up to 1,100 tons of carbon per tree and living over 3,000 years.

  10. Which of these trees is known for its extremely long lifespan, with some specimens living over 5,000 years?

    Answer: Bristlecone Pine

    The Great Basin Bristlecone Pine (Pinus longaeva) is the longest-lived non-clonal organism on Earth. The oldest known individual is over 5,000 years old.

  11. What is the oldest known living tree in the world?

    Answer: Old Tjikko

    Old Tjikko, a Norway spruce in Sweden, is a clonal organism aged about 9,550 years.
