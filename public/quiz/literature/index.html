Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Who is the author of 'Harry Potter'?

    Answer: J.K. Rowling

    J.K. Rowling wrote the Harry Potter series, which became one of the best-selling book series in history.

  2. Which novel begins with the line 'It was the best of times, it was the worst of times'?

    Answer: A Tale of Two Cities

    The opening line belongs to Charles Dickens's 'A Tale of Two Cities'.

  3. Which of these is NOT a novel by Jane Austen?

    Answer: Jane Eyre

    'Jane Eyre' was written by Charlotte Brontë, not Jane Austen. Austen authored 'Pride and Prejudice', 'Sense and Sensibility', 'Emma', and others.

  4. Which book begins with the line 'It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a single man in possession of a good fortune, must be in want of a wife'?

    Answer: Pride and Prejudice

    This famous opening line is from Jane Austen's 'Pride and Prejudice' (1813).

  5. Who wrote the novel 'Things Fall Apart', a classic of African literature?

    Answer: Chinua Achebe

    Nigerian author Chinua Achebe published 'Things Fall Apart' in 1958; it portrays Igbo society before and during colonialism.

  6. Who wrote the science-fiction novel 'Dune'?

    Answer: Frank Herbert

    Frank Herbert's 'Dune' (1965) is one of the best-selling science-fiction novels ever written.

  7. Which of these novels was written by Gabriel García Márquez?

    Answer: One Hundred Years of Solitude

    Gabriel García Márquez's masterpiece 'One Hundred Years of Solitude' (1967) is a landmark of magical realism.

  8. Who wrote '1984', the dystopian novel set in a totalitarian society?

    Answer: George Orwell

    George Orwell published '1984' in 1949; terms like 'Big Brother' and 'thoughtcrime' entered global vocabulary from this book.

  9. Which author wrote the novel 'One Hundred Years of Solitude'?

    Answer: Gabriel García Márquez

    Gabriel García Márquez, a Colombian writer, wrote 'One Hundred Years of Solitude', published in 1967.

  10. Which author is the creator of the detective Sherlock Holmes?

    Answer: Sir Arthur Conan Doyle

    Sherlock Holmes first appeared in Sir Arthur Conan Doyle's 1887 short story "A Study in Scarlet".

  11. Who was the author of 'The Divine Comedy'?

    Answer: Dante Alighieri

    Dante Alighieri, the 13th-14th century Italian poet, wrote 'The Divine Comedy', an epic journey through Hell, Purgatory, and Paradise.

  12. Who wrote 'In Search of Lost Time', a seven-volume novel sequence?

    Answer: Marcel Proust

    Marcel Proust wrote In Search of Lost Time (À la recherche du temps perdu), published between 1913 and 1927, considered one of the greatest novels ever written.

  13. Which Japanese author wrote the novel 'Norwegian Wood', a bestselling coming-of-age story published in 1987?

    Answer: Haruki Murakami

    Haruki Murakami's 'Norwegian Wood' became a massive bestseller in Japan and internationally. Unlike his more surreal works, it is a realistic novel dealing with loss, nostalgia, and sexuality in 1960s Tokyo.

  14. Which novel, published in 1962, is considered a masterpiece of post-colonial literature and was written by Chinua Achebe?

    Answer: Things Fall Apart

    Things Fall Apart (1958) by Nigerian author Chinua Achebe is one of the most important works of African literature, depicting pre-colonial life and colonial impact.

  15. Who wrote the epic novel 'War and Peace,' considered one of the greatest works of world literature?

    Answer: Leo Tolstoy

    Leo Tolstoy wrote 'War and Peace,' published in 1869. The novel weaves together the lives of several aristocratic families against the backdrop of the Napoleonic Wars.
