Harry Potter Quiz

There are two kinds of Harry Potter knowledge: the plot you could summarise in a minute, and the details that only survive a second read — which house the Sorting Hat almost chose, what Harry's Patronus takes the shape of, how many pieces of soul Voldemort actually split off.

This quiz is built for the second kind. 22 questions across the seven books and eight films, no sign-up, no timer, and an explanation after every answer so a miss tells you where to look rather than just costing you a point.

The answer key sits directly under the quiz. Contains spoilers for the whole series, including the ending — if you are mid-read, come back later.

Answer key

All 22 questions, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which Hogwarts house is Harry Potter sorted into?

    Answer: Gryffindor

    The Sorting Hat hesitates and considers Slytherin, but Harry asks not to go there and is placed in Gryffindor.

  2. What is the name of the wizarding school Harry attends?

    Answer: Hogwarts

    Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry is in Scotland, and the other three schools appear during the Triwizard Tournament.

  3. Who is headmaster of Hogwarts for most of the series?

    Answer: Albus Dumbledore

    Dumbledore leads the school from the first book until his death, after which McGonagall takes over.

  4. What position does Harry play on his Quidditch team?

    Answer: Seeker

    Harry is a Seeker, the player whose job is to catch the Golden Snitch and end the match.

  5. How many Horcruxes did Voldemort deliberately create?

    Answer: Six

    He intended six Horcruxes, which with the piece left in his own body would have divided his soul into seven parts. The fragment that lodged in Harry was made by accident when the curse rebounded, and Voldemort never knew it existed.

  6. What is the name of Harry's owl?

    Answer: Hedwig

    Hagrid buys Hedwig for Harry's eleventh birthday, and she is killed during the escape from Privet Drive.

  7. Which spell is used to disarm an opponent?

    Answer: Expelliarmus

    Expelliarmus knocks a wand out of an opponent's hand. Harry uses it so often that it becomes his signature.

  8. What is the name of the three-headed dog guarding the trapdoor?

    Answer: Fluffy

    Fluffy belongs to Hagrid and can be put to sleep with music, which is how the trio gets past him.

  9. Which platform at King's Cross leads to the Hogwarts Express?

    Answer: Platform 9¾

    Students run through the barrier between platforms nine and ten to reach the hidden Platform 9¾.

  10. Who teaches Potions during Harry's first year?

    Answer: Severus Snape

    Snape teaches Potions from the first book until Slughorn replaces him, after which Snape finally gets the Defence Against the Dark Arts post.

  11. What is the wizarding prison called?

    Answer: Azkaban

    Azkaban is guarded by Dementors until the Ministry loses control of them during the second war.

  12. Which shop in Diagon Alley sells wands?

    Answer: Ollivanders

    Ollivanders is where Harry buys his wand, and where the twin cores of his and Voldemort's wands are first noticed.

  13. What is the name of Ron Weasley's rat?

    Answer: Scabbers

    Scabbers is later revealed to be Peter Pettigrew in his Animagus form, a rat.

  14. Dobby is which kind of magical creature?

    Answer: House-elf

    Dobby is a house-elf, first belonging to the Malfoy family, later freed by Harry and eventually killed by Bellatrix.

  15. What is the name of the competition held in Harry's fourth year?

    Answer: The Triwizard Tournament

    The Triwizard Tournament pits one champion from each of three schools against three tasks.

  16. How many tasks are in the Triwizard Tournament?

    Answer: Three

    Three: dragons, the lake, and the maze. The final task is where the Portkey takes Harry to the graveyard.

  17. What shape does Harry's Patronus take?

    Answer: A stag

    Harry's Patronus is a stag, echoing his father James, whose Animagus form was also a stag.

  18. What is Voldemort's birth name?

    Answer: Tom Riddle

    Tom Marvolo Riddle rearranged the letters of his name into 'I am Lord Voldemort'.

  19. Which house is Draco Malfoy sorted into?

    Answer: Slytherin

    Draco follows his family into Slytherin, the house that produced most of Voldemort's early followers.

  20. Which teacher turns out to be a werewolf?

    Answer: Remus Lupin

    Lupin is a werewolf, which is why he takes Wolfsbane Potion and why he resigns at the end of the third book.

  21. What is the final book in the series called?

    Answer: Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows

    Deathly Hallows was published in 2007 and adapted into two films released in 2010 and 2011.

  22. Which actor played Severus Snape in the films?

    Answer: Alan Rickman

    Alan Rickman played Snape across all eight films, a role he had discussed with J.K. Rowling before filming began.

What this quiz covers

Every question here is answerable from either the books or the films — the quiz deliberately avoids details that only exist in one version, because a quiz that punishes you for having seen the film instead of read the book is testing the wrong thing.

The difficulty is in the recall, not in the obscurity. Nothing here requires you to remember a minor character's middle name; several questions do require you to have finished the series.

  • Hogwarts: the houses, the headmaster, the platform, and the dog behind the door on the third floor.
  • Characters: Harry's owl, Ron's rat, Hermione's cat, and the house-elf who keeps turning up.
  • Spells and magic: what Expelliarmus does, what a Patronus looks like, and how many Horcruxes were made on purpose.
  • The tournament: how many tasks, and which year it happens in.
  • Book-to-film details: the actor behind Snape, and which book closes the series.

Frequently asked questions

How many books and films are there?
Seven books, published between 1997 and 2007, and eight films, because the final book was split into two parts released in 2010 and 2011.
Do I need to have read the books to do well?
No. All 22 questions can be answered from the films alone, and several are easier if you have only seen the films. The quiz avoids book-only trivia on purpose.
Does the quiz contain spoilers?
Yes. Several questions reference events from the last two books, including the fate of major characters. If you are partway through the series, the quiz will spoil it.
Is this an official Harry Potter quiz?
No. It is an unofficial fan-made quiz on MemoryDuel and is not affiliated with or endorsed by J.K. Rowling, Warner Bros. or any rights holder. Names are used descriptively.
Can I retake it?
Yes, as often as you like, and there is no account needed. The question order is fixed, so a second run is a memory test rather than a fresh set — the answer key is the better study tool once you have played once.
