Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the main gas found in the Earth's atmosphere that is essential for respiration?

    Answer: Oxygen

    Oxygen makes up about 21% of the atmosphere and is vital for cellular respiration.

  2. Which of the following gases is most closely associated with the greenhouse effect and global warming?

    Answer: Carbon dioxide

    Carbon dioxide (CO2) is the primary greenhouse gas emitted through human activities like burning fossil fuels. While other gases contribute, CO2 is the main driver of global warming.

  3. What is the name for the layer of gases surrounding Earth?

    Answer: Atmosphere

    Atmosphere is the envelope of gases that surrounds the planet.

  4. What gas do plants primarily absorb from the atmosphere during photosynthesis?

    Answer: Carbon dioxide

    Plants take in carbon dioxide (CO2) and water, using sunlight to convert them into glucose and releasing oxygen as a byproduct.

  5. Which gas do humans and most animals need to breathe in to survive?

    Answer: Oxygen

    Oxygen is essential for the process of cellular respiration, which provides energy for living organisms.

  6. What is the main greenhouse gas released by burning fossil fuels?

    Answer: Carbon dioxide

    Burning coal, oil, and gas releases large amounts of carbon dioxide, the primary driver of climate change.

  7. What is the primary greenhouse gas emitted by human activities that drives climate change?

    Answer: Carbon dioxide

    Carbon dioxide from burning fossil fuels is the main greenhouse gas driving global warming.

  8. Which layer of the atmosphere contains the ozone layer, which protects life from harmful ultraviolet radiation?

    Answer: Stratosphere

    The ozone layer is located in the stratosphere, roughly 15 to 35 kilometers above Earth's surface, and absorbs most of the sun's harmful ultraviolet radiation.

  9. What phenomenon explains the bluish color of Earth's sky during the day?

    Answer: Rayleigh scattering

    Rayleigh scattering causes shorter (blue) wavelengths of sunlight to scatter more strongly in the atmosphere than longer (red) wavelengths.

  10. Which of the following processes is primarily responsible for the formation of the ozone layer?

    Answer: Photolysis of oxygen molecules

    High-energy UV light splits oxygen molecules (O2) into atomic oxygen (O), which then combines with O2 to form ozone (O3).

  11. Which gas is responsible for the majority of human-caused greenhouse warming?

    Answer: Carbon dioxide

    While water vapor is the most abundant greenhouse gas, carbon dioxide from fossil fuel burning contributes the most to human-driven warming.

  12. Which phenomenon, caused by the loss of stratospheric ozone, results in increased UV-B radiation reaching the Earth's surface?

    Answer: The Ozone Hole

    The Ozone Hole, most notably over Antarctica, is caused by chemical reactions involving chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) that deplete ozone molecules.

  13. Which element makes up approximately 78% of Earth's atmosphere?

    Answer: Nitrogen

    Nitrogen (N2) constitutes about 78% of the atmosphere, while oxygen is about 21%.

  14. Which greenhouse gas is primarily produced by enteric fermentation in ruminant animals like cattle?

    Answer: Methane

    Ruminant animals produce significant amounts of Methane (CH4) during digestion, a potent greenhouse gas.

  15. What phenomenon, first described by the Montreal Protocol in 1987, involves the depletion of the ozone layer caused by chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)?

    Answer: Ozone hole

    The 'ozone hole' refers to severe seasonal depletion of stratospheric ozone over Antarctica, caused largely by CFCs. The Montreal Protocol (1987) phased out these chemicals and is credited with helping the ozone layer recover.
