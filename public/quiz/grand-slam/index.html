Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In tennis, what is the name of the four major annual tournaments considered the sport's most prestigious events?

    Answer: The Grand Slams

    The four Grand Slam tournaments are the Australian Open, the French Open (Roland Garros), Wimbledon, and the US Open.

  2. Which country hosts the Wimbledon tennis tournament?

    Answer: United Kingdom

    Wimbledon is held in London, United Kingdom, and is the oldest of the four Grand Slam tennis tournaments.

  3. In tennis, which tournament is played on grass courts?

    Answer: Wimbledon

    Wimbledon is the only Grand Slam tournament still played on grass courts.

  4. Which sport is played at Wimbledon?

    Answer: Tennis

    Wimbledon is one of the four Grand Slam tennis tournaments and is played on grass courts in London.

  5. Which of these is NOT one of the Grand Slam tennis tournaments?

    Answer: Canadian Open

    The four Grand Slams are the Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon, and US Open.

  6. How many sets must a player win to win a men's singles match at Wimbledon?

    Answer: 3

    Men's singles matches at Grand Slam tournaments are best-of-five sets, so a player must win 3 sets.

  7. What is the maximum number of sets in a standard men's Grand Slam tennis singles match?

    Answer: 5

    Men's Grand Slam singles matches are best of 5 sets, while women's matches are best of 3 sets.

  8. Which country has produced the most male Grand Slam tennis singles champions?

    Answer: United States

    The United States has produced the most male Grand Slam singles champions, led by greats like Pete Sampras, Andre Agassi, Jimmy Connors, John McEnroe, and others.

  9. In tennis, what is it called when a player wins all four Grand Slam titles in the same calendar year?

    Answer: Calendar Grand Slam

    Winning all four Grand Slam titles (Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon, US Open) in a single calendar year is called a Calendar Grand Slam.

  10. In professional tennis, which surface is the Wimbledon Championships played on?

    Answer: Grass

    Wimbledon is the only Grand Slam tournament still played on grass courts.

  11. In professional tennis, which surface is the French Open (Roland Garros) played on?

    Answer: Clay

    Roland Garros is the only Grand Slam tournament played on red clay courts, making it distinct from the hard courts of the Australian and US Opens and the grass of Wimbledon.

  12. Which golfer completed the 'Grand Slam' in a single calendar year, winning all four major championships in 2000?

    Answer: Tiger Woods

    Tiger Woods won the U.S. Open, The Open, PGA Championship, and Masters in 2000.

  13. How many Grand Slam titles did Roger Federer win in his career up to 2023?

    Answer: 20

    Roger Federer has won 20 Grand Slam singles titles, the last of which was the 2018 Australian Open.

  14. Which sport features a 'grand slam' involving winning four specific tournaments?

    Answer: Both A and B

    Both tennis (Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon, US Open) and golf's majors are called Grand Slams.

  15. Who holds the record for the most career Grand Slam titles in men's singles tennis?

    Answer: Novak Djokovic

    Novak Djokovic holds the record for the most men's Grand Slam singles titles with 24 (as of 2024).
