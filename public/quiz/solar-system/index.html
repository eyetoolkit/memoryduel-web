Answer key

All twelve questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which planet is known as the 'Red Planet'?

    Answer: Mars

    Mars appears red due to iron oxide (rust) on its surface, earning it the nickname 'The Red Planet'.

  2. Which of the following planets in our solar system is known for having the most prominent and easily visible ring system?

    Answer: Saturn

    Saturn is famous for its extensive and bright ring system, which is primarily composed of ice particles and rocky debris.

  3. What is the closest planet to the Sun?

    Answer: Mercury

    Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system and the closest to the Sun.

  4. Which planet in our solar system has the largest number of moons?

    Answer: Saturn

    As of recent discoveries, Saturn holds the record with over 140 confirmed moons, surpassing Jupiter's count, thanks to its strong gravitational field capturing many small bodies.

  5. Which planet in our solar system is known for its prominent rings made mostly of ice?

    Answer: Saturn

    Saturn is famous for its spectacular ring system, which is composed primarily of ice particles, rocks, and dust.

  6. Which planet in our solar system has the shortest orbital period around the Sun?

    Answer: Mercury

    Mercury completes one orbit around the Sun in about 88 Earth days, the shortest year of any planet.

  7. Which planet in our solar system has the Great Red Spot, a giant storm that has raged for at least 350 years?

    Answer: Jupiter

    Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a persistent high-pressure storm larger than Earth, first observed in the 1600s. Mars lacks such storms, Saturn is known for rings, and Neptune's storms (like the former Great Dark Spot) are not long-lived in the same way.

  8. Which planet is known as the 'Morning Star' or 'Evening Star' due to its brightness?

    Answer: Venus

    Venus is often called the Morning Star or Evening Star because it is the brightest natural object in the night sky after the Moon.

  9. Which planet is known as the 'Red Planet' due to iron oxide on its surface?

    Answer: Mars

    Mars appears red because its surface is rich in iron oxide (rust), which reflects red light and gives the planet its distinctive color visible even from Earth.

  10. Which planet in our solar system has the most confirmed moons as of 2024?

    Answer: Saturn

    Saturn overtook Jupiter in 2023 with over 140 confirmed moons. Both have far more than Uranus (28) and Neptune (16).

  11. Which planet in our solar system has the shortest day, completing one rotation on its axis in roughly 10 hours?

    Answer: Jupiter

    Jupiter, the largest planet, rotates the fastest, with a day lasting about 9 hours and 56 minutes. This rapid spin causes the planet to bulge at its equator. Saturn is a close second at about 10.7 hours, while Mars takes about 24.6 hours and Mercury about 58.6 Earth days.

  12. Which planet in our solar system has the most moons as of recent astronomical observations?

    Answer: Saturn

    Saturn surpassed Jupiter in moon count after numerous small icy moons were discovered, bringing its total to over 140 confirmed moons.
