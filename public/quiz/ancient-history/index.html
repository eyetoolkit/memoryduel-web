Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. The ancient city of Mohenjo-daro was located in which modern-day country?

    Answer: Pakistan

    Mohenjo-daro was one of the largest settlements of the ancient Indus Valley Civilization, located in present-day Pakistan.

  2. In which ancient civilization did the pyramids of Giza get built?

    Answer: Ancient Egypt

    The Great Pyramid of Giza and the other pyramids were constructed during the Old Kingdom period of Ancient Egypt as tombs for pharaohs.

  3. The 'Silk Road' primarily connected China with which region during ancient and medieval times?

    Answer: Central Asia and the Middle East

    The Silk Road was a network of trade routes linking China to Central Asia and the Middle East, facilitating commerce and cultural exchange.

  4. The Code of Hammurabi, one of the oldest known written legal codes, originated in which ancient civilization?

    Answer: Babylonian

    The Code of Hammurabi was created by King Hammurabi of Babylon around 1754 BC and is a key example of early legal systems in human history.

  5. The Indus Valley Civilization flourished primarily along the banks of which modern-day countries' rivers?

    Answer: India and Pakistan

    The Indus Valley Civilization (also known as the Harappan Civilization) flourished along the Indus River and its tributaries, primarily in modern-day Pakistan and northwest India, from around 3300 to 1300 BCE.

  6. Which ancient wonder of the world was located in Babylon?

    Answer: Hanging Gardens

    The Hanging Gardens of Babylon were one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, located in modern-day Iraq.

  7. Who was the first Emperor of China's Qin dynasty?

    Answer: Qin Shi Huang

    Qin Shi Huang became the first emperor of a unified China in 221 BCE and is famous for ordering the construction of the Great Wall.

  8. Which famous leader crossed the Alps with his army, including war elephants, to attack Rome in 218 BC?

    Answer: Hannibal Barca

    Hannibal, a Carthaginian general, famously crossed the Alps with his army and elephants to invade Italy during the Second Punic War.

  9. Which ancient trade network connected East Asia with the Mediterranean world?

    Answer: Silk Road

    The Silk Road was a network of trade routes connecting the East and West, facilitating the exchange of goods, ideas, and culture for centuries.

  10. Which ancient civilization is known for its extensive road network across South America?

    Answer: The Inca

    The Inca Empire built an extensive road system spanning over 30,000 kilometers, connecting distant regions of their mountainous empire.

  11. The 'Year of the Four Emperors' occurred in which ancient empire?

    Answer: Roman Empire

    The 'Year of the Four Emperors' was a period of civil war in the Roman Empire in 69 AD following the death of Nero, seeing Galba, Otho, Vitellius, and finally Vespasian rise to power.

  12. The Edict of Milan in 313 CE legalized what practice in the Roman Empire?

    Answer: Christianity

    The Edict of Milan, issued by Constantine, granted religious tolerance and effectively legalized Christianity in the Roman Empire.

  13. The Silk Road, a network of trade routes connecting the East and West, was primarily active during which era?

    Answer: Ancient and Medieval periods

    The Silk Road was most active from around the 2nd century BCE to the 15th century CE, facilitating trade and cultural exchange between Asia and Europe.

  14. Which ancient philosophy school was founded by Socrates, Plato, and Aristotle?

    Answer: Classical Greek Philosophy

    Socrates, Plato, and Aristotle are the three central figures of classical Greek philosophy, establishing the foundation of Western thought.

  15. Which ancient civilization is credited with developing the earliest known system of writing, cuneiform, primarily for administrative and economic records?

    Answer: The Sumerians

    Cuneiform, developed by the Sumerians in Mesopotamia around 3400 BCE, began as pictographs and evolved into a complex script used across the ancient Near East for millennia.
