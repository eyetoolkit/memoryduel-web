Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In which year did World War II end?

    Answer: 1945

    World War II ended in 1945 following the surrender of Germany in May and Japan in September after the use of atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki.

  2. Which war was fought between 1914 and 1918?

    Answer: World War I

    World War I lasted from 1914 to 1918, involving most of Europe's great powers.

  3. Who wrote 'The Great Gatsby'?

    Answer: C: F. Scott Fitzgerald

    Fitzgerald's 1925 novel captures the decadence of the Jazz Age on Long Island.

  4. In which country was the painter Frida Kahlo born?

    Answer: Mexico

    Frida Kahlo was born in Mexico in 1907; she is one of the country's most celebrated artists.

  5. Who wrote the dystopian novel 'Brave New World'?

    Answer: Aldous Huxley

    Aldous Huxley published Brave New World in 1932, depicting a future society controlled by technology, pleasure, and genetic engineering.

  6. Which Spanish painter is famous for his 'Blue Period' and the masterpiece 'Guernica'?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso (1881–1973) was a Spanish painter and co-founder of Cubism. His 'Blue Period' (1901–1904) featured melancholic works in blue tones, and 'Guernica' (1937) is his powerful anti-war depiction of the bombing of a Basque town during the Spanish Civil War.

  7. Which art movement is Salvador Dalí most closely associated with?

    Answer: Surrealism

    Salvador Dalí was a prominent Spanish Surrealist, best known for his striking and bizarre imagery, such as in his painting 'The Persistence of Memory' with its melting clocks.

  8. Which Spanish artist is best known for painting "Guernica" and co-founding the Cubist movement?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso co-founded Cubism with Georges Braque and painted Guernica in 1937, a powerful anti-war masterpiece depicting the bombing of the Basque town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War.

  9. Which artist painted 'Guernica', depicting the horrors of the Spanish Civil War?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in 1937 in response to the bombing of the Basque town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War. The monumental black-and-white painting is housed in the Museo Reina Sofía in Madrid.

  10. Which 20th-century composer is famous for 'The Rite of Spring' and was a leading figure in Russian ballet music?

    Answer: Igor Stravinsky

    Igor Stravinsky composed 'The Rite of Spring' (1913), which caused a famous riot at its Paris premiere due to its revolutionary rhythm and dissonance.

  11. Which Russian composer wrote 'The Rite of Spring,' whose premiere in 1913 reportedly caused a riot in the audience?

    Answer: Igor Stravinsky

    Stravinsky's 'The Rite of Spring' (Le Sacre du printemps) premiered at the Théâtre des Champs-Élysées in Paris. Its dissonant harmonies and aggressive rhythms shocked audiences and became a landmark of modern classical music.

  12. Who wrote the play 'Waiting for Godot'?

    Answer: C: Samuel Beckett

    Beckett's two‑act play premiered in 1953 and became a landmark of absurdist theatre.

  13. Which of the following works by Igor Stravinsky contains the recurring 'Augurs of Spring' theme?

    Answer: The Rite of Spring

    The Rite of Spring (Le Sacre du Printemps) famously opens with the 'Augurs of Spring' dance.

  14. Which artist is associated with the Blue and Rose periods before developing Cubism?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Picasso's Blue and Rose periods (1901–1906) preceded his co-founding of Cubism with Georges Braque.

  15. Which composer of the late Romantic period wrote 'Also sprach Zarathustra' and died in 1949?

    Answer: Richard Strauss

    Richard Strauss (1864–1949) wrote 'Also sprach Zarathustra', famously used in '2001: A Space Odyssey'.
