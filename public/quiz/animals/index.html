Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What do bees mainly collect from flowers to make honey?

    Answer: Nectar

    Bees collect nectar, which they convert into honey; pollen is collected mainly as a protein source for larvae.

  2. What is the largest animal currently living on Earth?

    Answer: Blue Whale

    The Blue Whale is the largest animal known to have ever lived, reaching lengths of up to 30 meters (100 feet).

  3. What is a young frog called?

    Answer: Tadpole

    A young frog is called a tadpole. It lives in water, breathes through gills, and gradually develops legs and lungs as it metamorphoses into an adult frog.

  4. Which of these is the largest type of big cat?

    Answer: Tiger

    Tigers are the largest big cats, with Siberian tigers being the largest subspecies.

  5. Which animal is known for its black and white coloring and eats bamboo?

    Answer: Giant Panda

    Giant Pandas are native to south central China and have a diet that consists almost entirely of bamboo.

  6. What is the term for animals that are active primarily during twilight?

    Answer: Crepuscular

    Crepuscular animals, such as deer and rabbits, are most active during dawn and dusk.

  7. Which bird is the fastest when diving, reaching speeds over 240 mph?

    Answer: B) Peregrine falcon

    The peregrine falcon can exceed 240 mph (386 km/h) during its characteristic hunting stoop.

  8. Which of the following is a primary consumer in a food chain?

    Answer: Rabbit

    A primary consumer eats producers (plants). A rabbit is a herbivore and thus a primary consumer.

  9. Which type of animal is an ectotherm, relying on external sources to regulate its body temperature?

    Answer: Reptile

    Reptiles are ectotherms (cold-blooded), meaning they use environmental cues like sunlight to control their body temperature, unlike endotherms (warm-blooded) like mammals and birds.

  10. Which of these animals is known for undergoing metamorphosis?

    Answer: Frog

    Frogs hatch as aquatic tadpoles and undergo a dramatic transformation into adult frogs.

  11. What is the biological process where an organism's genetic makeup changes over successive generations, driven by natural selection?

    Answer: Evolution

    Evolution is the gradual change in the heritable characteristics of biological populations over time, primarily driven by natural selection acting on genetic variation.

  12. Which is the heaviest animal that has ever lived on Earth?

    Answer: Blue Whale

    The blue whale is the heaviest animal known to have existed, reaching up to 200 tonnes (200,000 kg).

  13. Which animal is capable oftrue hibernation, dropping its body temperature close to freezing?

    Answer: Ground squirrel

    Ground squirrels undergo true hibernation; bears enter a lighter torpor where body temperature drops only a few degrees.

  14. What is the fastest animal in the world in a dive (speed)?

    Answer: Peregrine falcon

    The peregrine falcon can exceed 300 km/h (about 240+ mph) in a hunting stoop, the fastest of any animal.

  15. Which term describes a species that is at risk of extinction?

    Answer: Endangered

    An endangered species is one whose population has declined to a level where it is at serious risk of extinction in the wild.
