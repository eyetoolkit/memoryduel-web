Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which Spanish artist is known for painting surrealistic works like 'The Persistence of Memory'?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí was a leading figure in the Surrealist movement, famous for melting clocks in 'The Persistence of Memory'.

  2. Which artist is famous for melting clocks in his surrealist paintings?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí painted 'The Persistence of Memory' in 1931, featuring soft, melting clocks and one of the most iconic images of the surrealist movement.

  3. Which art movement is Salvador Dalí associated with?

    Answer: Surrealism

    Salvador Dalí was a leading figure in the Surrealism movement, known for dreamlike and bizarre imagery such as melting clocks in 'The Persistence of Memory'.

  4. In which country was the painter Frida Kahlo born?

    Answer: Mexico

    Frida Kahlo was born in Mexico in 1907. Her work often explores themes of identity, post-colonialism, gender, class, and race in Mexican society.

  5. Who painted 'The Garden of Earthly Delights'?

    Answer: Hieronymus Bosch

    Hieronymus Bosch painted the triptych 'The Garden of Earthly Delights' around 1510, known for its fantastical imagery.

  6. Which painter is known for works such as 'The Persistence of Memory' and 'Swans Reflecting Elephants'?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí was a Spanish surrealist artist whose work often featured dreamlike, irrational imagery rendered with precise, realistic technique.

  7. In which art movement did Frida Kahlo prominently work?

    Answer: Surrealism

    Frida Kahlo's work is often associated with Surrealism, although she herself rejected the label.

  8. Who painted 'Guernica' and was a Spanish artist associated with Surrealism and Cubism?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso created 'Guernica' in 1937, a powerful anti‑war mural.

  9. Which Spanish painter is known for his surrealistic art style?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí was a leading figure in the Surrealism movement.

  10. Which painter is known for the quote 'I dream my painting and I paint my dream'?

    Answer: Marc Chagall

    Marc Chagall, the Russian-French artist known for dreamlike, poetic works, is often credited with this quote reflecting his surreal artistic style.

  11. Which painter is known for the artwork 'The Persistence of Memory' featuring melting clocks?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí painted The Persistence of Memory in 1931. Its soft, melting clocks draped over a barren landscape are among the most famous images of the Surrealist movement.

  12. Which artwork by René Magritte features the text 'Ceci n'est pas une pipe' (This is not a pipe)?

    Answer: The Treachery of Images

    René Magritte painted The Treachery of Images in 1929. It depicts a pipe with the caption 'Ceci n'est pas une pipe', challenging viewers' perceptions of representation and reality.

  13. Which artist painted 'The Persistence of Memory' in 1931 and was associated with the Surrealist movement?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí painted The Persistence of Memory in 1931, featuring soft melting clocks, and was a leading figure in the Surrealist movement.

  14. Which Mexican artist is known for her self-portraits and surrealistic paintings, often featuring themes of pain and identity?

    Answer: Frida Kahlo

    Frida Kahlo was a Mexican painter known for her deeply personal and symbolic self-portraits. Her work explores themes of identity, the human body, and death, often reflecting her life experiences and physical suffering.

  15. Which director's filmography includes 'Mulholland Drive', 'Blue Velvet', and 'Twin Peaks: Fire Walk with Me'?

    Answer: David Lynch

    David Lynch was a visionary filmmaker known for his surrealist style and exploration of the dark underbelly of American life, creating works that blur the line between dreams and reality.
