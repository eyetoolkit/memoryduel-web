Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which Austrian composer wrote the 'Für Elise' piano piece?

    Answer: Ludwig van Beethoven

    'Für Elise' is a well-known Bagatelle composed by Ludwig van Beethoven.

  2. Who composed the opera 'The Magic Flute'?

    Answer: Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart

    'The Magic Flute' (Die Zauberflöte) is a famous opera composed by Mozart in 1791.

  3. Who sang the hit song 'Rolling in the Deep'?

    Answer: Adele

    'Rolling in the Deep' is a song by British singer Adele, released in 2010.

  4. Which band released the album 'Abbey Road'?

    Answer: The Beatles

    The Beatles released 'Abbey Road' in 1969, featuring iconic tracks like 'Come Together' and 'Here Comes the Sun'.

  5. Which song by Queen is famous for its operatic section?

    Answer: 'Bohemian Rhapsody'

    'Bohemian Rhapsody' (1975) is renowned for its multi-part structure including an operatic passage.

  6. The composition 'The Four Seasons' was written by which Italian Baroque composer?

    Answer: Antonio Vivaldi

    Antonio Vivaldi composed 'The Four Seasons' (Le quattro stagioni) around 1720. It is one of the most popular pieces of Baroque music ever written.

  7. Who composed 'The Four Seasons', a famous set of violin concertos?

    Answer: Antonio Vivaldi

    Antonio Vivaldi, an Italian Baroque composer, wrote 'The Four Seasons' around 1720.

  8. The piece 'Also sprach Zarathustra' by Richard Strauss famously opens which 1968 Stanley Kubrick film?

    Answer: 2001: A Space Odyssey

    The dramatic opening fanfare of Strauss's 1896 tone poem became iconic through its use in Kubrick's '2001: A Space Odyssey', accompanying the Dawn of Man sequence.

  9. What is the name of Beethoven's famous symphony that begins with a distinctive four-note motif?

    Answer: Symphony No. 5

    Beethoven's Symphony No. 5 in C minor is famous for its opening four-note (da-da-da-dum) motif.

  10. Which artist released the critically acclaimed album 'Rumours' in 1977?

    Answer: Fleetwood Mac

    Fleetwood Mac released 'Rumours' in 1977, which became one of the best-selling albums of all time.

  11. Which opera contains the famous aria 'Nessun dorma'?

    Answer: Turandot

    'Nessun dorma' is from Puccini's opera 'Turandot', popularized by Luciano Pavarotti.

  12. Which piece is considered one of the most difficult piano works ever written, composed by Franz Liszt?

    Answer: La Campanella

    'La Campanella' (from Grandes études de Paganini) is renowned for its technical difficulty.

  13. Which piece is considered the first major work of the Romantic era in music?

    Answer: Symphony No. 3 'Eroica' by Beethoven

    Beethoven's 'Eroica' Symphony (1803-1804) is widely regarded as marking the transition from the Classical to the Romantic period, breaking with formal conventions of the time.

  14. Which composer of "The Nutcracker" ballet was born in Russia?

    Answer: Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky

    Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky (1840–1893) composed "The Nutcracker" ballet in 1892, and he was born in Votkinsk, Russia.

  15. Which of these composers is known for 'Clair de Lune'?

    Answer: Claude Debussy

    'Clair de Lune' is the third movement of Claude Debussy's 'Suite bergamasque' for solo piano.
