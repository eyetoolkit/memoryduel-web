Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which esports event is widely regarded as the most prestigious annual tournament for the game 'Dota 2'?

    Answer: The International

    'The International' is the annual world championship for Dota 2, famous for its massive prize pools.

  2. Which esports tournament for Dota 2 held the record for the largest prize pool in esports history for several years?

    Answer: The International 2021

    The International 2021 (TI10) featured a prize pool of over $40 million, crowdfunded through the game's Battle Pass. While other tournaments have grown, TI10 remains a benchmark for massive community-funded prize pools.

  3. In the game Dota 2, what is the name of the genre that describes it?

    Answer: MOBA (Multiplayer Online Battle Arena)

    Dota 2 is a MOBA, where teams compete to destroy the enemy's Ancient structure.

  4. In the game 'Dota 2', what is the term for the character controlled by the player?

    Answer: Hero

    In Dota 2 and other games like Heroes of the Storm, characters are called 'Heroes', whereas League of Legends uses 'Champions'.

  5. In esports, which game has historically hosted 'The International', one of the largest annual prize pools in competitive gaming?

    Answer: Dota 2

    The International is the annual world championship for Dota 2, known for its crowdfunding-driven prize pools that have exceeded $40 million.

  6. Which esports title has consistently been one of the most-watched globally, featuring teams from around the world competing for multi-million dollar prize pools?

    Answer: Dota 2

    Dota 2 is famous for 'The International' tournament, which has featured some of the largest prize pools in esports history.

  7. In Dota 2, what is the term for the act of a team deliberately losing a match to secure better future matchmaking placement?

    Answer: Throwing

    'Throwing' refers to intentionally losing a match. Smurfing refers to playing on a lower-ranked account, boosting is helping another player rank up, and griefing is disrupting teammates.

  8. Which esports tournament is often referred to as 'The International' for its flagship event?

    Answer: The International (Dota 2)

    The International is the annual world championship for the video game Dota 2, organized by Valve Corporation.

  9. What is the maximum number of players that can compete in a standard match of Dota 2?

    Answer: 10

    Dota 2 features 5v5 gameplay, with two teams of five players each competing to destroy the opposing team's Ancient.

  10. In Dota 2's annual championship, The International, what is the name of the primary trophy?

    Answer: The Aegis of Champions

    The Aegis of Champions is the trophy awarded to the winner of The International.

  11. Which country hosts The International, the largest annual tournament in Dota 2?

    Answer: Rotating countries

    The International is held in various host cities around the world each year, unlike many fixed-location tournaments.

  12. The 'The International' is the annual world championship tournament for which esports title?

    Answer: Dota 2

    The International is the premier annual tournament for Dota 2, known for its record-breaking prize pools.

  13. Which esports team won the first-ever 'The International' Dota 2 tournament in 2011?

    Answer: Natus Vincere (Na'Vi)

    Natus Vincere (Na'Vi), led by the legendary player Dendi, won the inaugural The International in 2011 at Gamescom in Cologne, Germany.

  14. In the game 'Dota 2', which organization won 'The International' tournament in 2021 (held in 2021, though tournament was in 2021)?

    Answer: Team Spirit

    Team Spirit won The International 2021 (TI10), held in Bucharest, Romania, defeating PSG.LGD in the grand finals.

  15. Which esports player is widely regarded as one of the greatest 'Dota 2' players of all time, known for his time with team OG and winning two consecutive TI titles?

    Answer: N0tail

    Johan 'N0tail' Sundstein is the captain of OG, which won The International 2018 and 2019 back-to-back, making him a two-time TI champion.
