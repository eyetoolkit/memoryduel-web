Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which painter is known for his 'Blue Period' and 'Rose Period' works?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso went through his Blue Period (1901–1904) and Rose Period (1904–1906) early in his career.

  2. Which Spanish painter is known for co-founding the Cubist movement?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso, along with Georges Braque, co-founded Cubism, revolutionizing 20th-century art.

  3. Which Spanish painter is known for the distorted, fragmented style of 'Guernica'?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Guernica (1937) is Pablo Picasso's powerful anti-war masterpiece depicting the bombing of a Basque town during the Spanish Civil War.

  4. Which artist painted 'Guernica', depicting the bombing of a Basque town?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in response to the bombing of the Basque town during the Spanish Civil War.

  5. Which artist founded the Cubist movement along with Georges Braque?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso and Georges Braque co-founded Cubism in the early 1900s, revolutionizing art by depicting objects from multiple viewpoints simultaneously.

  6. Who is known for the painting 'Guernica'?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in 1937 as a response to the bombing of the Basque town during the Spanish Civil War.

  7. Which painter created 'Guernica', a powerful anti-war mural-sized painting?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in 1937 in response to the bombing of the town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War.

  8. The painting 'Guernica' is widely attributed to which artist?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted Guernica in 1937 as a response to the bombing of the Basque town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War. It is considered one of the most powerful anti-war paintings ever made.

  9. Who painted 'Guernica' and was a Spanish artist associated with Surrealism and Cubism?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso created 'Guernica' in 1937, a powerful anti‑war mural.

  10. Which Spanish artist is known for his unique style and works like 'Guernica'?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso, a Spanish painter, is famous for co-founding the Cubist movement and for works like 'Guernica'.

  11. Which artist painted 'Guernica', a famous anti-war masterpiece?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in 1937 in response to the bombing of the Basque town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War.

  12. Which artist painted Les Demoiselles d'Avignon, a groundbreaking work that helped launch Cubism?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted Les Demoiselles d'Avignon in 1907, widely considered a foundational work of Cubism.

  13. Who painted 'Guernica'?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted 'Guernica' in 1937 as a response to the bombing of the Basque town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War. It is one of the most powerful anti-war paintings in history.

  14. Which artist was a founder of the Cubist movement alongside Pablo Picasso?

    Answer: Georges Braque

    Georges Braque worked closely with Picasso to develop Cubism in the early 1900s; the two were so intertwined that Braque once said their collaboration was 'roped together like mountain climbers.'

  15. Which artist broke from traditional perspective in 'Guernica'?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Guernica (1937) is a powerful anti-war painting by Pablo Picasso, created in response to the bombing of the Basque town during the Spanish Civil War.
