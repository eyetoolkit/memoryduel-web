Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Who is the author of the novel '1984'?

    Answer: George Orwell

    The dystopian novel '1984' was written by English author George Orwell.

  2. What book begins with the line 'Call me Ishmael'?

    Answer: Moby-Dick

    'Moby-Dick' by Herman Melville opens with this iconic line. The novel follows Captain Ahab's obsessive hunt for the white whale.

  3. Who authored 'A Tale of Two Cities'?

    Answer: A: Charles Dickens

    Dickens' historical novel set during the French Revolution was published in 1859.

  4. Which American novelist authored The Grapes of Wrath?

    Answer: John Steinbeck

    John Steinbeck wrote The Grapes of Wrath, published in 1939 and winning the Pulitzer Prize.

  5. Who wrote 'The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes'?

    Answer: Arthur Conan Doyle

    Arthur Conan Doyle wrote the collection of detective stories featuring Sherlock Holmes.

  6. Which American novelist wrote 'The Great Gatsby', a classic depiction of the Jazz Age?

    Answer: A) F. Scott Fitzgerald

    Fitzgerald's 'The Great Gatsby' came out in 1925, epitomizing the Roaring Twenties.

  7. Which author wrote the high‑fantasy series 'The Lord of the Rings'?

    Answer: J.R.R. Tolkien

    Tolkien's trilogy, published 1954‑1955, chronicles the quest to destroy the One Ring.

  8. Which 19th‑century Russian novelist wrote 'Anna Karenina'?

    Answer: Leo Tolstoy

    Leo Tolstoy authored 'Anna Karenina', a sweeping novel of love, family, and Russian society in the 19th century.

  9. Which English novelist authored the classic 'Jane Eyre'?

    Answer: Charlotte Brontë

    Charlotte Brontë's 1847 novel follows the life of the eponymous heroine.

  10. Which author wrote the murder mystery 'Murder on the Orient Express'?

    Answer: Agatha Christie

    Christie's 1934 novel features Hercule Poirot solving a murder on a snow‑bound train.

  11. Which English novelist wrote the modernist novel 'Mrs Dalloway'?

    Answer: Virginia Woolf

    Virginia Woolf authored Mrs Dalloway in 1925, a hallmark of modernist narrative technique.

  12. Who wrote 'Things Fall Apart', published in 1958?

    Answer: Chinua Achebe

    Chinua Achebe of Nigeria wrote 'Things Fall Apart.' It is one of the most widely read works of African literature and has been translated into over 50 languages.

  13. Who wrote the novel 'One Hundred Years of Solitude'?

    Answer: Gabriel García Márquez

    Gabriel García Márquez authored One Hundred Years of Solitude, a cornerstone of magical realism published in 1967.

  14. Who wrote 'The Master and Margarita', a satirical novel blending politics and the supernatural?

    Answer: Mikhail Bulgakov

    Mikhail Bulgakov wrote 'The Master and Margarita' between the 1930s and shortly before his death in 1940; it was first published in serialized form in the 1960s.

  15. Which Irish novelist and poet wrote the novel 'Ulysses', heavily referencing Homer's 'Odyssey'?

    Answer: James Joyce

    James Joyce published 'Ulysses' in 1922, modeled after the structure of Homer's epic.
