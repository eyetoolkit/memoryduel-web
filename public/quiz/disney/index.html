Disney Quiz

Disney trivia has a useful property that most trivia does not: the answers are checkable, because the studio keeps its own archive and the release dates are documented to the day. So this quiz stays away from opinion and sticks to what happened.

Twenty questions across the animated features, the characters, and the business decisions that shaped both — the 1928 cartoon that introduced Mickey, the 1937 feature that everyone said would bankrupt the studio, the 2006 purchase that brought Pixar inside.

Answer key

All 20 questions, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What was the first full-length Disney animated feature?

    Answer: Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs

    Snow White was released in December 1937 and was called Disney's Folly during production because nobody expected a feature-length cartoon to succeed.

  2. In which 1928 cartoon did Mickey Mouse first appear?

    Answer: Steamboat Willie

    Steamboat Willie was the first Mickey cartoon released with synchronised sound, which is why it is treated as the debut even though Plane Crazy was made earlier.

  3. Which Disney film features the song 'Let It Go'?

    Answer: Frozen

    Frozen, released in 2013. The song won the Academy Award for Best Original Song and became the film's commercial engine.

  4. Where did Walt Disney open his first theme park in 1955?

    Answer: Anaheim, California

    Disneyland opened in Anaheim in July 1955. The Florida resort came in 1971, five years after Walt Disney died.

  5. Which film features the lion cub Simba?

    Answer: The Lion King

    The Lion King was released in 1994 and was the studio's first feature built on an original story rather than an existing fairy tale.

  6. How many dwarfs appear in Snow White?

    Answer: Seven

    Seven. Their number was kept at seven so that each could be given a distinct personality that would read on screen.

  7. Which city is Princess Jasmine from?

    Answer: Agrabah

    Jasmine is from the fictional city of Agrabah in Aladdin. Arendelle is Frozen and Atlantica is The Little Mermaid.

  8. Which film introduced Buzz Lightyear?

    Answer: Toy Story

    Buzz arrived in Toy Story in 1995, the first feature made entirely with computer animation.

  9. What is the name of the mermaid in The Little Mermaid?

    Answer: Ariel

    Ariel is the mermaid; Eric is the prince she rescues. Aurora is Sleeping Beauty and Belle is Beauty and the Beast.

  10. Which Disney film is based on a Chinese legend about a woman who takes her father's place in the army?

    Answer: Mulan

    Mulan is drawn from the Ballad of Mulan. The 1998 film Disney-fied the story considerably, and the 2020 remake went back towards the original.

  11. What kind of animal is Dumbo?

    Answer: An elephant

    Dumbo is a baby elephant whose oversized ears let him fly. The film runs just 64 minutes, one of the shortest Disney features.

  12. What is the name of the snowman in Frozen?

    Answer: Olaf

    Olaf is the snowman. Sven is the reindeer, Kristoff is the ice harvester, and Marshmallow is the giant snow guard.

  13. In Ratatouille, what is the name of the rat who wants to cook?

    Answer: Remy

    Remy is the rat. Emile is his brother, Gusteau is the dead chef, and Linguini is the human he works through.

  14. Who is Mickey Mouse's girlfriend?

    Answer: Minnie Mouse

    Minnie has been in the cartoons since 1928. Daisy Duck is Donald's partner, which is the pair people mix up.

  15. Which studio did Disney acquire in 2006?

    Answer: Pixar

    Disney bought Pixar in 2006, then Marvel in 2009 and Lucasfilm in 2012. The order matters if you are asked which came first.

  16. Which film features the song 'A Whole New World'?

    Answer: Aladdin

    Aladdin, sung by Jasmine and Aladdin on the magic carpet. It won the Oscar for Best Original Song in 1993.

  17. Which Pixar film was the studio's first winner of the Best Animated Feature Oscar?

    Answer: Finding Nemo

    The category began in 2001 and Shrek took the first award. Finding Nemo won it for 2003, the first Pixar win.

  18. What is the name of Mickey Mouse's pet dog?

    Answer: Pluto

    Pluto is the dog. Goofy is a separate character who is also dog-like, which is a distinction the films have never explained.

  19. What are the names of Donald Duck's three nephews?

    Answer: Huey, Dewey and Louie

    Huey, Dewey and Louie, in that order — red, blue and green. Their full names are Hubert, Deuteronomy and Louis.

  20. What was the name of Walt Disney's brother, who co-founded the studio and ran it after Walt died?

    Answer: Roy O. Disney

    Roy O. Disney was the business half of the partnership and pushed the Florida park through after Walt's death in 1966.

What this quiz covers

The questions assume you have seen the films rather than read about them, which is why there are no box-office figures. Where a fact is about a date, it is a date that is genuinely part of the story — 1928, 1937, 1955, 2006.

A few are deliberately harder than a family quiz would normally go. The character questions in particular use the near-miss pairs — Pluto and Goofy, Minnie and Daisy — because those are where a confident wrong answer hides.

  • Firsts: the first Mickey Mouse cartoon, the first animated feature, and the first Disney theme park.
  • Characters and where they belong: Simba, Ariel, Olaf, Remy, Buzz Lightyear, Pluto and the three duck nephews.
  • Songs tied to specific films — 'Let It Go' and 'A Whole New World' — and who sings them.
  • Studio history: the 2006 acquisition of Pixar, and the first Pixar film to win the animation Oscar.
  • The people behind the name: Walt Disney, his brother Roy, and what each of them actually ran.

Frequently asked questions

What was the first Disney animated feature?
Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs, released in December 1937. It was nicknamed Disney's Folly while in production because the industry expected a full-length cartoon to fail.
When did Mickey Mouse first appear?
In Steamboat Willie, released in November 1928. It was the first cartoon with synchronised sound to be widely distributed, and it made both the character and the studio.
When did Disney buy Pixar?
2006, in an all-stock deal that made Steve Jobs the largest individual shareholder in Disney. Pixar had been distributing through Disney since Toy Story in 1995.
Where was the first Disney theme park?
Anaheim, California, opened in July 1955. Walt Disney World in Florida followed in 1971, after Walt's death, and was run by his brother Roy.
How many dwarfs are in Snow White?
Seven. The names were settled after a long list of rejected candidates, and the group's number was chosen so the animators could keep each one distinct.
