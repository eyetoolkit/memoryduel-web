Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which famous novel begins with the line 'Call me Ishmael'?

    Answer: Moby-Dick

    'Moby-Dick' by Herman Melville opens with this iconic line. Published in 1851, it is considered one of the greatest novels in American literature.

  2. Who wrote the novel 1984?

    Answer: George Orwell

    George Orwell published 1984 in 1949. The dystopian novel introduced concepts like Big Brother and Newspeak into popular culture.

  3. Which of these is a play written by William Shakespeare?

    Answer: Hamlet

    'Hamlet' is one of Shakespeare's most famous tragedies, written around 1600. The other options are novels by later authors.

  4. In which famous novel would you find the character Harry Potter?

    Answer: Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone

    Harry Potter is the titular character of J.K. Rowling's bestselling fantasy series, which begins with 'Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone'.

  5. Which country was the writer Gabriel García Márquez from?

    Answer: Colombia

    Gabriel García Márquez was a Colombian novelist and Nobel Prize winner, best known for 'One Hundred Years of Solitude' and the style of magical realism.

  6. Which author wrote the Harry Potter series?

    Answer: J.K. Rowling

    J.K. Rowling published the first Harry Potter book, 'Philosopher's Stone,' in 1997. The seven-book series follows the adventures of a young wizard and became the best-selling book series in history.

  7. Which author wrote the play 'Romeo and Juliet'?

    Answer: William Shakespeare

    William Shakespeare, often called the world's greatest playwright, wrote 'Romeo and Juliet' in the late 16th century.

  8. Who wrote the epic fantasy trilogy 'The Lord of the Rings'?

    Answer: J.R.R. Tolkien

    J.R.R. Tolkien wrote 'The Lord of the Rings', a foundational work of modern fantasy literature.

  9. Which literary character lives at 221B Baker Street?

    Answer: Sherlock Holmes

    221B Baker Street is the fictional London address of the detective Sherlock Holmes, created by Sir Arthur Conan Doyle.

  10. Which novel features the character Elizabeth Bennet?

    Answer: Pride and Prejudice

    Elizabeth Bennet is the protagonist of 'Pride and Prejudice', written by Jane Austen and published in 1813.

  11. In which country was the novelist Franz Kafka born?

    Answer: Czech Republic

    Franz Kafka was born in Prague in 1883, which was then part of the Austro-Hungarian Empire and is now the capital of the Czech Republic. Works like 'The Metamorphosis' and 'The Trial' are central to 20th-century literature.

  12. Who wrote 'One Hundred Years of Solitude', a landmark of magical realism?

    Answer: Gabriel García Márquez

    Gabriel García Márquez of Colombia wrote One Hundred Years of Solitude, published in 1967. It is considered the defining work of magical realism and earned him the 1982 Nobel Prize in Literature.

  13. Who is the author of the philosophical novel 'The Stranger'?

    Answer: Albert Camus

    Albert Camus, a French-Algerian philosopher and author, published 'The Stranger' in 1942; it is one of the most celebrated works of existentialist literature.

  14. Who was the author of 'The Divine Comedy'?

    Answer: Dante Alighieri

    Dante Alighieri, the 13th-14th century Italian poet, wrote 'The Divine Comedy', an epic journey through Hell, Purgatory, and Paradise.

  15. Who wrote the epic poem 'Paradise Lost', first published in 1667?

    Answer: John Milton

    John Milton wrote 'Paradise Lost' while blind. The epic poem retells the biblical story of the Fall of Man and is considered one of the greatest works in the English language.
