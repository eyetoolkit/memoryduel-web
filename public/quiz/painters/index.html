Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Who painted the famous artwork 'The Starry Night'?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    'The Starry Night' was painted by Dutch Post-Impressionist painter Vincent van Gogh in June 1889.

  2. The painting 'Starry Night' is the work of which artist?

    Answer: Vincent van Gogh

    Vincent van Gogh painted 'The Starry Night' in 1889 while staying at the Saint-Paul-de-Mausole asylum in France.

  3. Which country was the painter Pablo Picasso born in?

    Answer: Spain

    Pablo Picasso was born in Málaga, Spain, in 1881, though he later worked primarily in France.

  4. Who painted the famous work 'Mona Lisa'?

    Answer: Leonardo da Vinci

    The Mona Lisa, housed in the Louvre Museum, was painted by the Italian polymath Leonardo da Vinci during the High Renaissance.

  5. The abstract painting style known as 'Cubism' was co-founded by Pablo Picasso alongside which French artist?

    Answer: Georges Braque

    Pablo Picasso and Georges Braque co-founded Cubism in the early 1900s, revolutionizing European painting and sculpture by depicting subjects from multiple viewpoints.

  6. Which painter is known for the painting 'The Persistence of Memory' featuring melting clocks?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí painted 'The Persistence of Memory' in 1931; its melting clocks are an iconic image of surrealism.

  7. Which painting by Edvard Munch depicts a figure screaming in existential anxiety?

    Answer: The Scream

    Edvard Munch created 'The Scream' in 1893; its wailing figure has become a universal symbol of modern anxiety.

  8. Which artist is famous for painting 'Guernica', depicting the bombing of a Basque town?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso painted Guernica in 1937 as a response to the bombing of the town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War.

  9. Which artist is famous for his Blue and Rose periods, and co-founded Cubism with Georges Braque?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Pablo Picasso, a Spanish artist, is famous for his Blue and Rose periods and co-founded Cubism with Georges Braque.

  10. Which Spanish artist is known for his surrealist works such as 'The Persistence of Memory'?

    Answer: Salvador Dalí

    Salvador Dalí was a Spanish surrealist painter famous for melting clocks in The Persistence of Memory (1931).

  11. Which painter created the 'Guernica' mural?

    Answer: Pablo Picasso

    Guernica was painted by Pablo Picasso in 1937 in response to the bombing of the Basque town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War.

  12. Which artist created the famous mobile sculptures, including 'Clouds' and 'Black and White'?

    Answer: Alexander Calder

    Alexander Calder was an American sculptor known for inventing the mobile, a type of kinetic sculpture that moves with air currents.

  13. Which artist's 'Campbell's Soup Cans' is a defining work of the Pop Art movement?

    Answer: Andy Warhol

    American artist Andy Warhol created 'Campbell's Soup Cans' in 1962, which became an iconic symbol of Pop Art.

  14. Who painted the ceiling of the Sistine Chapel?

    Answer: Michelangelo

    Michelangelo took four years (1508-1512) to paint the monumental frescoes on the ceiling of the Sistine Chapel.

  15. Which painter of the Dutch Golden Age is famous for 'The Night Watch'?

    Answer: Rembrandt van Rijn

    Rembrandt van Rijn painted The Night Watch in 1642, a masterpiece of group portraiture from the Dutch Golden Age.
