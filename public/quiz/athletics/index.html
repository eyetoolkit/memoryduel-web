Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which Olympic sport involves athletes jumping over a bar using a long pole?

    Answer: Pole vault

    The pole vault uses a long flexible pole to help athletes clear a high bar.

  2. In which Olympic sport do athletes compete for gold in the '100 metres' event?

    Answer: Athletics (Track and Field)

    The 100 metres is one of the most prestigious sprint events in track and field athletics.

  3. In athletics, what is the standard length of a marathon race?

    Answer: 42.195 km

    A marathon is standardized at 42.195 kilometers.

  4. In which city were the 2020 Summer Olympics originally scheduled to be held before being postponed?

    Answer: Tokyo

    The 2020 Summer Olympics were scheduled for Tokyo in 2020 but were postponed due to the COVID-19 pandemic.

  5. In which sport do athletes compete to jump the farthest from a take-off point?

    Answer: Long Jump

    The Long Jump event requires athletes to sprint and jump for maximum horizontal distance.

  6. Which athlete holds the world record for the men's 100m sprint as of 2023?

    Answer: Usain Bolt

    Usain Bolt set the 9.58‑second world record in 2009, which still stands as of 2023.

  7. Which Olympic sport involves athletes throwing a metal ball attached to a wire (the 'hammer')?

    Answer: Hammer Throw

    The Hammer Throw is an athletics event where athletes swing a heavy metal ball attached to a steel wire around their head before releasing it.

  8. In which sport is the 'Fosbury Flop' technique commonly used?

    Answer: High jump

    The Fosbury Flop, introduced by Dick Fosbury in the 1968 Olympics, is the dominant technique for clearing the high jump bar backward over the back.

  9. Which country has historically dominated men's Olympic javelin throwing?

    Answer: Finland

    Finland, known as the 'land of a thousand lakes' and with a strong tradition in throwing events, has historically been very successful in javelin.

  10. Which country has won the most gold medals in the Summer Olympic track and field events (as of 2020)?

    Answer: United States

    The United States leads all nations in Olympic track & field gold medals.

  11. In athletics (track and field), who was the first person to run a sub-4-minute mile?

    Answer: Roger Bannister

    Sir Roger Bannister of the United Kingdom became the first person to run a mile in under four minutes on May 6, 1954, at Oxford.

  12. In which year did the International Association of Athletics Federations (IAAF) officially rename itself to World Athletics?

    Answer: 2019

    The IAAF adopted the name World Athletics in 2019 after a rebranding decision.

  13. In the Olympic decathlon, how many events must an athlete complete?

    Answer: 10

    The decathlon consists of 10 track and field events spread over two days, testing a wide range of athletic abilities.

  14. What is the maximum weight (in kilograms) of a standard men's shot put used in international competition?

    Answer: 7.26 kg

    The standard weight for the men's shot put is 7.26 kg (16 pounds).

  15. In which Olympic Games did the marathon distance become standardized to 42.195 km?

    Answer: 1908 London

    The marathon length was set to 42.195 km for the 1908 London Olympics.
