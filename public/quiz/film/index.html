Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Who plays the title role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe's 'Iron Man' films?

    Answer: Robert Downey Jr.

    Robert Downey Jr. portrayed Tony Stark/Iron Man from 2008 until his character's arc concluded in 'Avengers: Endgame' (2019).

  2. Which actor starred as Jack Sparrow in the 'Pirates of the Caribbean' series?

    Answer: Johnny Depp

    Johnny Depp created the iconic role of Jack Sparrow.

  3. Which movie features the famous line "I'll be back"?

    Answer: The Terminator

    The line "I'll be back" was spoken by Arnold Schwarzenegger's character in The Terminator (1984).

  4. Who directed the film Jurassic Park (1993)?

    Answer: Steven Spielberg

    Steven Spielberg directed Jurassic Park and its sequel The Lost World.

  5. What is the name of the fictional African nation featured in the movie 'Black Panther'?

    Answer: Wakanda

    Wakanda is the advanced, isolationist African nation in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

  6. Which movie franchise features the fictional planet of Tatooine?

    Answer: Star Wars

    Tatooine is a desert planet in the Star Wars universe, famous as Luke Skywalker's home.

  7. Which actor is known for playing the role of Jack Dawson in the movie 'Titanic' (1997)?

    Answer: Leonardo DiCaprio

    Leonardo DiCaprio starred as Jack Dawson opposite Kate Winslet's Rose in James Cameron's Titanic.

  8. Which animated studio produced the film 'Spirited Away'?

    Answer: Studio Ghibli

    Studio Ghibli, founded by Hayao Miyazaki and Isao Takahata, produced 'Spirited Away', which won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature in 2003.

  9. Which film features the quote 'Here's looking at you, kid'?

    Answer: Casablanca

    This iconic line is spoken by Humphrey Bogart's character Rick Blaine to Ilsa Lund in Casablanca (1942).

  10. Which actor has appeared in the most films directed by Christopher Nolan?

    Answer: Michael Caine

    Michael Caine holds the record, starring in six of Nolan’s films including The Dark Knight Trilogy, Inception, Interstellar, and The Prestige.

  11. Which film won the Academy Award for Best Picture in 1994?

    Answer: Forrest Gump

    Forrest Gump won Best Picture at the 1994 Oscars.

  12. Which actor portrayed Hannibal Lecter in 'The Silence of the Lambs' (1991)?

    Answer: Anthony Hopkins

    Anthony Hopkins won the Academy Award for Best Actor for his chilling portrayal of Hannibal Lecter.

  13. Which filmmaker directed both 'The Godfather' and 'Apocalypse Now'?

    Answer: Francis Ford Coppola

    Francis Ford Coppola directed these two acclaimed films in the 1970s.

  14. Which director is known for surrealist and avant-garde films such as 'Un Chien Andalou' and 'The Discreet Charm of the Bourgeoisie'?

    Answer: Luis Buñuel

    Luis Buñuel was a Spanish-Mexican filmmaker known for his work in surrealist cinema, often collaborating with Salvador Dalí early in his career.

  15. Which director is known for the 'Three Colours' trilogy ('Blue', 'White', 'Red')?

    Answer: Krzysztof Kieślowski

    Polish director Krzysztof Kieślowski created the acclaimed 'Three Colours' trilogy (1993–1994).
