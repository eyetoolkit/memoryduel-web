Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. What is the name of the highest standard female singing voice type?

    Answer: Soprano

    Soprano is the highest standard female voice type in classical music.

  2. Which classical composer wrote the famous opera 'The Marriage of Figaro'?

    Answer: Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart

    Mozart composed 'The Marriage of Figaro' in 1786, based on a play by Beaumarchais. It remains one of the most frequently performed operas worldwide.

  3. Which Austrian composer is known for the opera 'The Magic Flute' and was a child prodigy?

    Answer: Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart

    Mozart composed 'The Magic Flute' (Die Zauberflöte) in 1791 and was famously born in Salzburg, Austria, performing for royalty as a child.

  4. Which famous opera singer is known as 'The Three Tenors' along with Luciano Pavarotti and Plácido Domingo?

    Answer: Jose Carreras

    The Three Tenors were a popular supergroup consisting of José Carreras, Luciano Pavarotti, and Plácido Domingo, who performed together from 1990 to 2003.

  5. Which famous opera is known for its 'Bridal Chorus' (often played at weddings)?

    Answer: Lohengrin

    The 'Bridal Chorus' comes from Richard Wagner's opera Lohengrin.

  6. Who composed the opera 'La Bohème'?

    Answer: Giacomo Puccini

    Giacomo Puccini composed 'La Bohème', which premiered in 1896 and remains one of the most frequently performed operas.

  7. Which famous opera begins with the aria 'Nessun dorma'?

    Answer: Turandot

    'Nessun dorma' is an aria from the final act of Giacomo Puccini's opera Turandot, based on the play Turandot by Carlo Gozzi.

  8. Which opera features the famous 'Brindisi' (drinking song) 'Libiamo ne' lieti calici'?

    Answer: La Traviata

    'Libiamo ne' lieti calici' is the famous drinking song from Act 1 of Giuseppe Verdi's opera 'La Traviata'.

  9. Which opera by Giuseppe Verdi features the famous 'Triumphal March' and is based on a play by Shakespeare?

    Answer: Aida

    Aida premiered in Cairo in 1871 and tells the story of an Ethiopian princess held captive in Egypt. The Triumphal March from Act 2 is one of the most recognizable pieces in all opera.

  10. Which opera by Giuseppe Verdi contains the aria 'La donna è mobile'?

    Answer: Rigoletto

    Verdi’s Rigoletto features the famous aria 'La donna è mobile', sung by the Duke in Act III.

  11. Which opera by Giacomo Puccini tells the tragic love story of a Japanese geisha?

    Answer: Madama Butterfly

    Puccini's 'Madama Butterfly' centers on Cio‑Cio‑San, a geisha.

  12. Which composer wrote the opera 'Der Ring des Nibelungen' (The Ring Cycle)?

    Answer: Richard Wagner

    Wagner's four-opera cycle 'Der Ring des Nibelungen', completed in 1876, is a monumental work based on Norse and Germanic mythology.

  13. Which composer wrote the opera 'Tristan und Isolde', which is considered a landmark in Western music for its use of chromaticism?

    Answer: Richard Wagner

    Richard Wagner's 'Tristan und Isolde' is noted for its revolutionary use of chromatic harmony, influencing later composers.

  14. Which composer wrote the opera 'Parsifal', premiered at the first Bayreuth Festival?

    Answer: Richard Wagner

    Richard Wagner composed 'Parsifal,' which premiered at the inaugural Bayreuth Festival in 1882. Wagner designed the Bayreuth Festspielhaus specifically for his works.

  15. Which composer's opera is titled 'The Rake's Progress'?

    Answer: Igor Stravinsky

    Stravinsky wrote the opera 'The Rake's Progress' based on Hogarth's engravings.
