Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. In which modern country would you find the ancient city of Machu Picchu?

    Answer: Peru

    Machu Picchu is a 15th-century Inca citadel located in the Andes Mountains of Peru.

  2. Which country is famous for the annual Carnival celebration in Rio de Janeiro?

    Answer: Brazil

    The Rio Carnival is the most famous festival in Brazil and is widely considered the largest carnival in the world.

  3. Which country do the Andes mountains run through?

    Answer: Chile

    The Andes stretch along the western edge of South America, and Chile lies almost entirely along this range.

  4. Which ancient civilization built the Machu Picchu complex in the Andes mountains?

    Answer: The Inca Empire

    Machu Picchu was built in the 15th century by the Inca emperor Pachacuti as a royal estate high in the Peruvian Andes. The Aztec and Maya were Mesoamerican civilizations, and the Olmec predated them.

  5. Which is the largest river in South America?

    Answer: Amazon

    The Amazon River is the largest river by discharge volume in the world and runs through South America.

  6. Which is the capital of Argentina?

    Answer: Buenos Aires

    Buenos Aires is the capital and largest city of Argentina.

  7. The Amazon River flows through which of these countries?

    Answer: Brazil

    The Amazon River flows through Brazil, as well as Peru, Colombia, and several other South American countries.

  8. Which South American country has the capital Lima?

    Answer: Peru

    Lima, founded by the Spanish in 1535, is the capital of Peru.

  9. What is the capital city of Argentina?

    Answer: Buenos Aires

    Buenos Aires is the capital and largest city of Argentina, located on the Río de la Plata.

  10. Which country contains the source of the Amazon River?

    Answer: Peru

    The Amazon River originates in the Andes of southern Peru (at Nevado Mismi or nearby peaks, depending on the study). While the majority of the river flows through Brazil, its source is in Peru.

  11. The Andes mountain range runs along the western coast of which continent?

    Answer: South America

    The Andes stretch approximately 7,000 km along the western edge of South America, passing through seven countries.

  12. Which indigenous South American culture developed the 'quipu', a complex system of knotted strings used for record-keeping?

    Answer: The Inca

    The Inca Empire used quipus (or khipus) as a method of recording information, using a system of knotted strings whose exact full meaning is still being studied by scholars.

  13. Which is the highest mountain peak located entirely within a single country (not on a border)?

    Answer: Aconcagua

    Aconcagua in Argentina, at 6,961 m, is the highest peak entirely within one country's borders; K2 sits on a border and Kilimanjaro/Denali are lower.

  14. The 'Inti Raymi' festival, a major celebration of the Inca sun god, is still observed today primarily in which country?

    Answer: Peru

    Inti Raymi is an ancient Inca festival honoring the sun god Inti, now celebrated as a cultural reenactment in Cusco, Peru.

  15. The Amazon River discharges how much water into the Atlantic Ocean?

    Answer: About 1/5 of world's river water

    The Amazon River discharges about 20% (1/5) of the world's river water into the ocean, more than the next seven largest rivers combined.
