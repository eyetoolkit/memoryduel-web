Answer key

All fifteen questions above, with the correct answer and the reasoning.

  1. Which of these planets is the closest to the Sun?

    Answer: Mercury

    Mercury is the first planet from the Sun and the closest of the eight planets in our solar system.

  2. What planet is known as the 'Red Planet' due to iron oxide (rust) on its surface?

    Answer: Mars

    Mars appears red because iron minerals in its soil oxidize, or rust, creating the distinctive color.

  3. What planet is known as the Red Planet?

    Answer: Mars

    Mars is known as the Red Planet due to iron oxide (rust) prevalent on its surface, giving it a reddish appearance.

  4. Which planet is known as the 'Morning Star' or 'Evening Star'?

    Answer: Venus

    Venus appears bright in the morning or evening sky, earning the nickname Morning Star or Evening Star.

  5. Which planet is known as the "Red Planet"?

    Answer: Mars

    Mars appears reddish due to iron oxide on its surface.

  6. Which of the following is the largest planet in our solar system?

    Answer: Jupiter

    Jupiter is the largest planet in the solar system, both in mass and volume. Saturn is the second largest.

  7. Which planet is known for its prominent ring system visible from Earth?

    Answer: Saturn

    Saturn has the most extensive and visible ring system in the solar system.

  8. Which planet has the most moons confirmed as of 2024?

    Answer: Saturn

    Saturn overtook Jupiter with 146 confirmed moons as of 2024.

  9. Which planet has the shortest day, rotating in about 10 hours?

    Answer: Jupiter

    Jupiter completes a rotation in about 10 hours, the shortest day among the planets.

  10. Which planet rotates on its axis the slowest?

    Answer: Venus

    Venus takes about 243 Earth days to complete one rotation, the slowest planetary rotation.

  11. Which celestial body is known as the 'Red Planet'?

    Answer: Mars

    Mars appears red due to iron oxide (rust) on its surface.

  12. Which planet has the largest atmosphere, including the Great Red Spot?

    Answer: Jupiter

    Jupiter has the largest atmosphere of any planet, and its Great Red Spot is a giant storm larger than Earth.

  13. Which planet currently has the greatest number of known moons?

    Answer: Saturn

    Saturn holds the record with over 140 confirmed moons, surpassing Jupiter in recent counts.

  14. Which planet has the Great Red Spot, a giant storm lasting centuries?

    Answer: Jupiter

    Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a storm larger than Earth that has been observed for over 300 years.

  15. Which planet has the shortest day in the solar system?

    Answer: Jupiter

    Jupiter rotates in roughly 10 hours, the fastest rotation among the planets.
