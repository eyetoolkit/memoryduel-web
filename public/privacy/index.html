Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

1. What data we collect

MemoryDuel follows a privacy-first principle and collects as little data as possible:

  • Local storage: game records, high scores, and training records are saved in your browser's localStorage and never leave your device.
  • Anonymous battle data: if you play online battles, we generate a random UUID to identify your account for leaderboards and stats. This UUID is not linked to any personal identity.
  • Anonymous stats: we may use privacy-friendly analytics (e.g. Plausible) to collect aggregate visits, with no IP addresses or personal identity.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We don't collect names, emails, phone numbers, or other personal identity (unless you contact us by email).
  • We don't read your contacts or address book.
  • We don't use third-party ads or behavioral tracking.
  • We don't use Google Analytics or other tracking analytics.

3. Cookie usage

We may use one cookie (pid) to store an anonymous account ID for recognizing the same user on the leaderboard. You can clear browser cookies anytime.

4. Data storage & retention

Local training records stay in your browser and are deleted when you clear browser data. Battle leaderboard data is served by Cloudflare edge storage; aggregate stats are periodically purged.

5. Third-party services

We may use these third-party services:

  • Plausible Analytics: a privacy-friendly analytics tool (no cookies, GDPR-compliant). See Plausible's data policy.
  • Cloudflare: web hosting and CDN. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

6. Children's privacy

This site is for all ages. Since we collect no personal identity, COPPA or GDPR-K children's data issues don't apply.

7. Your rights

We collect very little data, mostly on your local device. You can:

  • Clear browser localStorage anytime to delete local records
  • Use incognito/private mode (no local records)
  • Block Plausible Analytics (install an extension like uBlock Origin)

7a. COPPA 2025 Update (US users)

Effective September 30, 2026. This section supplements the policy above to satisfy the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Final Amendments published 2025-01-16, with compliance deadline 2026-04-22.

  • Persistent identifier disclosure — when cloud ELO sync is enabled, the browser auto-generates a UUID via crypto.randomUUID(). This UUID is stored in Cloudflare KV with a 90-day TTL and is used solely for cross-lesson ELO tracking. No IP address, device fingerprint, or Advertising ID is collected.
  • Categories of third parties — Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers Paid, KV, D1, R2, Durable Objects); GitHub, Inc. (source code); Agent Mail (智邮) for the contact email alias. No advertising networks, data brokers, social platforms, or analytics beyond Cloudflare Workers Logs.
  • Purpose limitation — UUID and game data are used solely for: (1) cross-lesson ELO tracking, (2) anti-cheat, (3) engine improvement, (4) session replay. Never for behavioral profiling, advertising, re-marketing, or cross-site identification.
  • Biometric identifiers — we do not collect facial recognition, voiceprints, fingerprints, retina scans, or any biometric data. The 2025 amendment's expanded definition does not apply to our service.
  • DSAR contact — mathduel@agent.qq.com (also the COPPA § 312.6 designated contact). Requests are processed within 48 hours.
